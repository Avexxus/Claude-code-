Eleven plates rendering the whole codex as a visual system — the magic engine, the cosmology, the ladder of being, ten thousand years of chronology, the bloodline, the web of races, the structural skeleton of all four books, the time-war's machinery, and the map of what remains unbuilt. PLATE I

The World-Engine

The whole magic system on one sheet. One undivided act — a being gripping reality — passing three gates and no others. No physics audit, ever. The flavor is named after the act; the external systems run outside the Remnant economy entirely.

PLATE II

The Infinite Regress

The cosmology as a column: dimensions that created dimensions, endlessly in both directions. The Blight are the same kind of being we are — just from higher levels. The bubble is the only wall; the Between sits beside our level, not above it.

PLATE III

The Ladder of Being

Lifespan rises with Remnant — resistance, never immunity. One continuous mortal scale (bars illustrative, not literal years); the true immortals sit off the scale, deathless by mechanism, not magnitude. Hover a rung.

PLATE IV

The Loom of Ages

The full chronology on four threads — the family, Jon, Derrek, and the Blight — from the unchanging deep past, across the anchor, down the ~10,000-year forward run to the finale. The deep history happened once, identically, beneath every loop. Hover any event. Scroll sideways; the loom is wide.

the family / the books Jon's thread Derrek's thread the Blight / the loop

Book 4 does not sit on this axis — it opens BEFORE all of it, on the original timeline (Plate X).

PLATE V

The Bloodline

One elven family, three eras — the monument's material, cherished and spent at once. Solid nodes are drafted; hollow nodes are unbuilt or unnamed. The ghost-line is a love story that exists only in the notes — no reader ever reads it.

PLATE VI

The Elder Web & the Churn

Every race defined by entanglement, not essence — who uses whom, on which clock, behind which mask. Hover a race to isolate its threads. The catastrophe is emergent and authorless; Derrek only tilts an ocean he doesn't control.

PLATE IX

The Time-War Machinery

The tether, the shared jump, and the loop that ran beyond counting — then the single forward step that dismantles all of it. The head start was never temporal; the leash was also a seawall.

PLATE X

The Four-Book Ouroboros

The trilogy runs forward across the eras; Book IV opens where Book III ends — but in the original, unbroken world, the first of many loops, before they shattered reality forever. Finish the saga, turn the page, and you are back at the beginning: the structure enacts the loop.
