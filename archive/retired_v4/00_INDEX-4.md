# 00 — INDEX & READ-ME
### The codex is built in TWO LAYERS. This is the most important thing to understand.

> ## 🔵 WORLD / SYSTEM LAYER — rigid bedrock
> The physics, the magic-system, the forces, cosmology, the mythology, the method, the ability-mechanics. **This is the part that stays rigid.** 🔒 LAWs here are real and load-bearing. We hold these to the letter; they do NOT bend to serve story. This is the engine everything runs on.
>
> ## 🟡 STORY LAYER — fluid / work-in-progress
> Characters, names, plot, book structure, scenes, who-does-what. **Nothing here is fixed.** All names are placeholders (Jon, Derrek, the elf — ALL placeholders). Beats and roles move freely. **No 🔒 applies to story** — story is clay, by design, so new ideas can reshape it without breaking anything. The ONLY constraint on story is that it must not violate the WORLD bedrock. **Story-vs-story "conflicts" are not conflicts — they're just the story being unfinished.**
>
> **Why split this way:** the world must be rigorous (it's the science/magic that has to hold up). The story must be flexible (you never know when a new idea reshapes a character or plot, and if every line were locked, nothing could move). Build the world hard; let the story float on top.

## THE STABILITY TIER SYSTEM (applies to WORLD-layer content)
- 🔒 **LAW** — invariant. Never broken without explicit renegotiation. (WORLD layer only.)
- ⚖️ **FLEXIBLE** — adjustable with care.
- 🌊 **FLUID** — open clay, actively being shaped.
- 🟡 **STORY** — provisional story content; movable by default; never law.
- 🚫 **RETIRED** — a tripwire; an idea we killed. Never reintroduce.

## CORE WORLD PREMISES (the rigid spine, in one place)
- **The One = origin of CONSCIOUSNESS only.** The physical universe (matter, energy, the four forces) pre-exists it and is pure science, untouched.
- **Magic = inherited mastery.** The One *mastered* the universe (wielder, not author); a being's magic is a smaller expression of that mastery. No interface mechanism — the capacity is identity. **REMNANT + SKILL = POWER.** **SPIRIT = the name for active Remnant magic** (a being's faculty to consciously manipulate matter; having it is what separates a being from inert flesh). One undivided system; **the Body/Spirit "two kinds" split is retired.**
- **No steer-only; effort-gated; no physics audit.** (REWORKED this session.) Magic **may add energy** when the scene calls for it (steer-only retired; conservation no longer enforced). The only gates are **Remnant (how much) + skill (how well) + effort/fatigue (the bill)** — never a feasibility check. Rare second branch: **soul magic** (Spirit turned on another's Remnant — will-vs-will, self-risk).
- **Forces & elements = pure descriptive FLAVOR, named after the act — never a power-class or a kind of person.** There are **no force/element "users," no types, no builds.** You watch the act, then call it gravity/fire/EM/weak. Blended freely, no permission check, no paths/trees/reachability. **Magic is EXPRESSION, not derivation:** build the person first; their magic follows; no nature+ability+element+race equation.
- **All BEINGS descend from the One; matter is just physics.**
- **AGING/LIFESPAN (bedrock):** every being = flesh + a piece of the One; more Remnant → more resistance to age (longer life) but **never immunity** — everything ages and dies. "God" = a larger chunk of Remnant in flesh. Ladder: orcs < humans < dwarves < elves < **giants (longest mortal)** < great gods (seem eternal). **NO true immortals among the aging.** True immortality is a separate MECHANISM (only Jon, Derrek, rule-beasts). *(Elves & giants are ANCIENT MORTALS — not "near-immortal"; giants live MUCH longer than elves.)*
- **Design stance:** a **science-FLAVORED, SCENE-FIRST** magic story (UPDATED — *not* "mechanisms rigid, magnitudes bend," which is retired). Magic explains the *can* ("they are users"); physics is **light flavor** on the *what*, never an engine. **The science serves the cool; the instant it makes something less cool or adds homework, it loses.** Mechanism-policing (which force, feasibility, mass-budgets, nuclear-process labeling) is GONE; the forces (fire, gravity, lightning, EM) return as welcome visible texture, never a compass. Rigor lives ONLY in internal consistency, never in matching reality's math. `14_PHYSICS_REFERENCE.md` is a non-binding flavor palette.

## HISTORY (provisional, story-layer)
- **Elven tales: the saga of one elven family across the eras** (elves are the vantage because they live long enough to perceive the time-ripples; a **bloodline** is followable across deep time — elves are long-lived but less than giants, so the throughline is a lineage, not one immortal elf). Three standalone novels, one protagonist each, all ending in Derrek's Tower (the convergence point at the end of time) facing him under a different name. Book 3 shows the convergence (spot earlier protagonists; no required reading order). **Jon = on-page side character revealed at the end as the secret architect; Lirael = light-touch (Book 1 lead's sister, Jon's old love).** The hidden time-war runs beneath the family story. **Final loop, losses stand.** — all 🟡 provisional.
- **THEME = DUALITY** (strict: one subject holding two polar-opposite truths at once — the One, Jon, Derrek, Jon's ending; NOT pairs, NOT synonyms). `09_STRUCTURE.md`.
- **CORE SKELETON (every book):** orc attack → **Jon rescues** → face Derrek (the FLAVOR/era is the disguise; the repeating CORE is the breadcrumb). Per-book Freytag shape with Jon's two appearances. `09_STRUCTURE.md`.
- **The TOWER = one physical place across all time** (same spot, three eras); the three heroes never meet — only the reader sees all three ascents braided. The orc subplot = Derrek's recurring instrument against the bloodline, the trap aimed at the Bk3 protagonist (NOT Jon — Jon doesn't care about orcs). **Jon's arc = a FALL** (love → hardened coldness; the saga is a monument to a dead love). `08`/`09`.
- **Far-future world (Book 3)** fully built — Earth-as-singularity, the Galactic Council/stalemate, race forms, ship-power, dwarf/giant roles, the elven pocket-realm. `07_BEINGS.md`.
