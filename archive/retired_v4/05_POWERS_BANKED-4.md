# 05 — POWERS BANKED (seeded, not yet built)

New power domains we identified as worth developing, with their grounding direction set, awaiting full buildout. All 🌊 FLUID unless noted.

---

## LIFE / DEATH 🌊 (a soul-magic flavor)
- Growth, decay, fertility, disease, the living process; necromancy, ghosts, soul-passage. Sits on the **soul-magic** branch (acting on the living fragment, not on dead matter). Likely the most evocative use of soul magic on the page.
- *Status:* discovered per scene; no pre-spec.

## MIND 🌊 (core soul magic)
- Affecting minds directly — cognition, memory, emotion, perception, will. This **IS soul magic** (Spirit gripping another's Remnant): will-vs-will, two-way, limited by self-risk. Not a separate domain — it's the central case of the rare branch.
- *Status:* discovered per scene.

## MEMORY OF MATTER 🌊
- Reading the **past held in things** (the earth's memory, structural memory, water carrying history). Straddles matter-reading and soul-reading; possibly a unique spin beyond "psychometry."
- *Status:* develop later, per scene.

## LIGHT & DARK (the power) 🚫 RETIRED OUTRIGHT
🚫 **SCRAPPED with cosmic Light/Dark.** Derived from "the two cosmic halves," which no longer exist. Do not rebuild from halves/poles. Any future light/dark-flavored effect is ordinary magic description (a variable), never a derived power. *(Full dead buildout archived: `_archive/retired_light_dark_halves_power.md`.)*

## Phenomena considered and CUT 🚫
- **Space / distance-folding** — too difficult; also partly the threshold-beings' turf (the Between).
- **Entropy** as a wieldable power — too difficult; already a cosmic *direction* (though entropy/death magic as a god-counter survives as flavor — `07_BEINGS.md`).
- **Probability / luck** — too hard to quantify; consistency hazard.
- **Antimatter** (as Dark) — rejected grounding.
