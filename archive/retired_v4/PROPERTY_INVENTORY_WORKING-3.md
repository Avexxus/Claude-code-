# PROPERTY INVENTORY — effects palette (optional flavor — NOT a rulebook)
### A grab-bag of physical effects to pull from when describing magic. The four-force sorting below is descriptive convenience only, never a system anyone belongs to.

> **METHOD (current architecture — `04_MAGIC_SUBSTRATE.md`):** The hand (Remnant) touches reality; forces and other natural processes are reality's *responses*. This sheet sorts phenomena under the **force** that produces them (EM, Gravity, Strong, Weak) where applicable — a useful physics map — but note forces are *tools/responses, not a power-class*, and pressure/spin/etc. are manifestable responses too, not just by-products. Two phenomena that *feel* like one "element" can sit under different forces. Three things are NOT forces and are flagged accordingly:
> - **CURRENCY/STATE** — energy, temperature, momentum, pressure: not forces, but natural responses in the same toolkit — **directly manifestable**, gated by cost/skill.
> - **EMERGENT** — a behavior that is just a force acting in a particular regime (no new mechanism).
> - **SEPARATE** — consciousness-side (Spirit/the One) or deliberately off-map (time, quantum plumbing).
>
> **"How it's magic"** = a consciousness (Remnant) gripping the relevant force and doing the listed thing. Everyday folk-faces noted where useful.

## THE TAKEAWAYS (what the sort revealed)
1. **EM is ~half the list** — confirms it as the broad baseline. Most "elemental" magic is EM aimed at different targets (heat, bonds, charge, light, magnetism, contact/solidity, nerves).
2. **Energy-forms (heat, pressure, momentum, spin…) are manifestable responses like any other** — the old "only the four forces are wieldable" line is 🚫 RETIRED/REVERSED. One act may yield several at once (reality answers holistically). What resolved "Fire = energy": energy is the universal currency, owned by nothing.
3. **The folk elements split cleanly across forces:** "fire" = EM (+Strong at the nuclear ceiling); "earth" = Gravity (mass/heave) + EM (rigidity/fracture); "water" = Gravity (flow/weight) + EM (cohesion); "air" = EM only. Same name, different force, different fighter.
4. **Strong and Weak own only the nucleus** — tiny slice of the list, but the most devastating/exotic, which is why they read as mythic and rare.
5. **Gravity is small but distinct** — pure mass/attraction/bulk-motion; cheap and efficient.
6. **The SEPARATE pile is the consciousness-side + off-map** — life, mind, soul, time. This is the next system to build (the consciousness side), and it is correctly NOT derived from the physical forces.

🌊 Open: a few items could be argued (e.g. is "memory of pressure" worth a dedicated ability, or just EM-reading?) — refine when building specific spells. No item required inventing a fifth force; every phenomenon found a home, which validates the four-force base.
