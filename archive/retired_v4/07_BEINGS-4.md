## Creation generations 🔒 (a SIZE spectrum, not staggered creation — reconciled this session)
> **⚠️ These tiers are fragment-SIZE, not waves of creation.** All races coalesced in the ONE shatter (`03_COSMOLOGY.md`); none were made later. The list below sorts by fragment size (→ body-durability → lifespan → generational distance to the origin), NOT time of creation. "First Coalesced / Latecomers" = *near vs. far from the origin in generational depth*, because big fragments live in long strides and small ones churn through many short lives — same age as peoples, different memory-depth.
1. **Transcendent (largest fragments):** gods and demons. Need no warmth/food, can exist anywhere; some create **pocket dimensions.**
2. **Elder Races (large fragments):** Dragons, Giants, Elves, **and Changelings** (the four elders — see below). Long strides → the beginning is only a few lifetimes back, near and vivid. 🌊 (Door open for more elder races; author currently satisfied with four.)
3. **Latecomers (small fragments):** humans, orcs, dwarves, goblins, gnomes. Short-lived, multiply fast, the origin decayed into dim myth they're too consumed to regard. *(Birth mechanism for ALL races: diffuse unclaimed Remnant channeled into the child at conception — `03_COSMOLOGY.md`.)*
4. **Scraps (smallest fragments):** beasts, monsters, animals. Low Remnant, still genuine fragments.

## Tiers of powers/beings 🔒 (distinct from generations)
- **Tier 1 — Gods & Demons:** coherent fragments inside the universe, each with its own individual will (will lives only with coherence). Demons are independent wills, proxies of nothing (see `06_EXTERNAL_POWERS.md`). A **god** = a fragment powerful enough to act at world-altering scale. Two rough roads: large *born* fragments vs. mortals who *learned* deep mastery over time (pure scale of Remnant × skill — no fusion ladder).
- **Tier 2 — Unified Ones & the Blight:** the **Unified Ones** are the ones who didn't split — sovereign living absolutes, NOT in harmony (Derrek's patron is one). **They created the bubble/barrier that holds the Blight out.** The **Blight** = the varied population of stronger entities a level up (not a maw, no instinct-doctrine — `03_COSMOLOGY.md`).
- **Tier 3 — Threshold Beings ("in-betweeners"):** an **ancient lineage that pre-dates the One and is NOT descended from it** (as old as the One, or so old they've forgotten any higher origin), living in their own **bubble between the worlds.** They **obey physics** (shared substrate) but run the **Rule-OS, not Remnant** (`06_EXTERNAL_POWERS.md`) — they were never the One's consciousness. Lateral to the Unified Ones/Blight — different lineage & OS, not inherently stronger. *(RETIRED: 'they caused the separations / ignore physical law' — they're bound by physics, just running a different, older system.)*
- **Tier 4 — Time Gods:** fragments fused with **time**; exist across all sequence at once; gathered into a **society**. Jon & Derrek are first/most complete.

## THE FOUR ELDER RACES ⚖️

### THE FALL OF THE GIANTS & THE DARKEST AGE 🟡→🔒 (the elder tier's shared root-history)
> **NAMING (ruled):** the giants' age ended in **war, not descent** — and "the Darkest Age" was never the fall itself. **The Darkest Age has always referred to the age of DRAGON RULE that followed** — the same era the dragons name "the Age of Dragons." The only race with a named Age, and everyone else's name for it is *the Darkest Age.* The bitter arc: the coalition fought for freedom — and the two ages that followed the giants were **both worse.**
- **The giant castles were never gifts.** Giants built the megastructures **for themselves** — the halls of the **fallen empire of giants**, when they ruled the world. Dragons live in them now as *occupiers*, not guests.
- **Why giants ruled:** with the largest fragments of the One, they felt a **duty** to maintain order for the world's sake — genuine, iron-handed **compassion totalized into rule that allowed no dissent and no consent.** (Their mask-over-hunger, per the churn model: *pride-as-duty* — the sin of believing your care doesn't need consent. No clean hands, even here.)
- **The revolt:** centuries of efficient, ruthless, well-intentioned rule bred the grinding resentment all long servitude breeds. **The dragons — the giants' seconds, envious of their stature and halls — knew exactly what the giants' rule really was, and believed they would rule BETTER** (their self-justification; not cynicism but the oldest revolutionary lie). Too important to sully their own hands, dragons **manufactured and controlled the coalition narrative.** The accusation had teeth — the giants held **ALL races in forced protection**: enslavement *by several measures of the word* (no consent, no exit, no self-rule), though the **gentlest bondage the world would ever know — love led the giants' hand**, ruling in ways genuinely best for the ruled. "Tender and understanding rulers" — fully visible only *in retrospect*, against what followed. **The LIE was the promise: freedom as equals after victory.** They sent **great columns of human war-users and orc legions** to fight it (humans deceived but *willing* — the lie flattered their pride; **orcs deceived and desperate** — the lie promised the one thing they'd never had). Dragons stepped in personally only when forced (a giant champion). A **key igniting event** exists but is 🌊 deliberately undecided/mythic — cultures tell different versions of why the giants fell, none quite right.
- **The fall:** the coalition **killed the giants' heroes and leaders** — a defeat so total the giants could not survive as the civilization they had been. Not a chosen peace: a **broken vocation.** The survivors withdrew, leaving their mighty halls empty; the dragons took them, and the apex — and began **THE DARKEST AGE.**
- **THE DARKEST AGE (dragon rule) 🔒:** the giants ruled from warped compassion; **the dragons wanted control — to try THEIR way: they believed they could aim every other race to their end.** Races as instruments pointed at dragon purposes. Once the giants gave up on the world, dragons **controlled and used everything below them.** Life was truly miserable — that is *why* the era carries the name. **Humans:** not chattel like the orcs, but an **indentured race** — dragons in their prime led *empires of humans*; humans formed the backbone the dragon order rode on. **Dwarves:** kept their useful status (the neutrality already ancient) yet were **subject to dragon will all the same** (subject, never enslaved; status intact, line unbroken — no contradiction with the unbroken-empire canon, `10`). **Orcs:** re-chained outright, immediately, after bleeding for the coalition — the promise of freedom-as-equals dead on arrival. This shared misery is the deep root of **why the races hate dragons** and why they eventually banded to erode them (later completed by fast-breeding humans — the established recession). *Three apex powers, each toppled by what rose beneath it: giants → dragons → humans — and each master, in the orcs' experience, worse than the last.*
- **The wound:** the giants' grief is **shared across every complicit race**, weighted by role — dragons architected it knowingly; humans and orcs were the manipulated hands (giants *know* this, and it does not read as forgiveness: manipulated-into-genocide still means your hands did it — though the orcs, re-chained by the victors within the hour, paid for the deception like no one else). This is among the **oldest wounds** feeding the long-memory enmity (`10_WORLD.md`) — and why giant bitterness reads as *weary total withdrawal* rather than a dragon's simmering contempt. Giants also carry many later, smaller grievances against human pride on top of it.
- **The elves' first withdrawal:** the fall-war and the Darkest Age that followed are likely when the elves **began separating** — the pocket-realm's origin era; refusing to be any tyranny's subject class. The exodus from Derrek's Earth is their *second* such withdrawal — the same move, eras apart, against different tyrants.
- **Geologic scars:** the war's full-might battles left marks still visible — but read them as the **wreck of a civilization's monuments** (ruined halls, the killing-grounds of heroes), not mere craters. In Book 1 these are just eerie named places with superstitions; the truth is the reader's later reward.
- **The ERASED AGE (resolved this session — one more giant tragedy):** there *was* an era of the giants — and its name was **erased** despite all they did for everyone, while the dragons keep an Age named for them. Two causes braided: **deliberate erasure** (the victors — the dragon-controlled record — unnamed the caretakers they'd destroyed) and **deep time** (the giants' era is vastly older; the Age of Dragons came recently enough to be remembered, the giants' age decayed with the short-lived races' churning generations — `03` generational-depth). The race that built the world's scenery is remembered as scenery: nameless, ancient, assumed. This is part of the giants' wound.

Each race anchored to a cardinal element **symbolically/culturally ONLY** (Law 8 — no mechanical racial lean). The rivalries form one interlocking web.

### GIANTS — symbolically EARTH — the god-like race
- **Mortal titans, NOT cosmological gods** — but indistinguishable from gods to lesser races (oldest religions may worship them). No "Age" named for them, yet they **built much of the scenery** (raised hills, carved valleys, laid the most ancient megastructures).
- **Largest bodies → largest Remnant → most POTENT users** (not most skilled — they can *afford* expressions no one else can pay for). **Signature mastery (added this session): massive-scale TRUE TRANSMUTATION and will-imposed reshaping** — a giant takes matter and forces it to *become* what it wills, holding the form by sheer will, at scales no one else can pay for (this is the engine behind raising hills, carving valleys, the monumental architecture, the grand starships). Distinct from dwarves: **dwarves mine → forge → enchant existing matter** (craft + magical infusion); **giants transmute and will matter into being and shape** (raw large-scale transformation by potency). They are **powerful, ACTIVE users — never passive landmarks.**
- **Longest-lived of ALL mortal races — by far (corrected this session: MUCH longer than elves, not "~as long as").** Required both by the aging law (largest Remnant → most age-resistant flesh → longest life — Constitution 4a) and by the **titan/elemental/god-scale giant apex**: the giant range runs from elementless brutes (low end, shorter-lived) UP through elder giants, god-scale ancients, and **elemental titans** (e.g. a tree elemental) at the apex — and those primordial beings *must* be incredibly ancient to be what they are. So **age/power/Remnant rise together up the giant range.** A single giant can personally span all three eras (the "living bridge" is natural, not a freak — see the original ship-builders still alive as advisors, below). Still **MORTAL** — ancient, not immortal; they age and die (no true immortals among the aging — 4a). *(This is WHY the saga is an elven BLOODLINE, not a giant: elves are long-lived but less than giants, so a lineage carries the eras; giants are the individual deep-time witnesses.)*
- Glacial, patient. **Raised in STOICISM & HUMILITY** — power without pride (mentor/ally source). ⚠️ **Reframed this session: the stoicism is SCAR TISSUE, not innate virtue** — the psychology of the fallen (see THE DARKEST AGE above): once the world's iron-handed caretaker-rulers, broken so thoroughly (their heroes killed by their own wards) that they could not survive in the same form. Not a race that refused to fight for what it had — a race defeated out of its own shape, its vocation broken. Few (slow births, huge upkeep — ordinary biology of large beings, `03`), mostly solitary, acting at long timescales. (Ogier resonance.)

### DRAGONS — symbolically AIR — the First Dominion, apex mortal threat
- **The strongest users we see** outside Jon/Derrek/threshold-beings/great gods. **There was an Age of Dragons** (no other race has an age named for it) — reputation + raw power incarnate. *(Everyone else's name for that era: **the Darkest Age.**)*
- Ancient god-king **sorcerer-tyrants** who ruled the world from the sky **after usurping the giants** (THE DARKEST AGE above — they were the giants' seconds, self-justified as *better* rulers, and took the apex by manufactured coalition and other hands; ruling-through-proxies was how they WON the throne, not a late habit), then **slowly RECEDED** as fast-breeding men overran the ground — now a background power working through **proxies/minions**, with earned impunity in their own domains. *(Their Age sits between two defeats: lieutenant-to-giants before, apex-lost-to-humans after.)*
- **Single sovereign entities**, loose hierarchy, very scarce, each overwhelming.
- **Combat profile:** explosive overwhelming power + predator's patience. **Weakness (🔒):** immense Remnant costs immensely to sustain — supreme in *bursts*, can't fight at peak indefinitely; a frugal, evasive, patient opponent who drags the fight long *drains* them. (Pride is a *character* tool for scenes, never a system rule — avoid the bumbling-proud-fool trap.)
- **Beatability (🔒):** no singular being overpowers a great ancient dragon (survive/flee/outwit); a gifted individual may best a young/weak one; bad matchups blunt any dragon; a coordinated skilled group can fell even an apex at great cost. "The world was named for them" = dread, never a rule that decides fights.
- **The world's foremost transmuters/alchemists — and they wield VARIED breaths.** (REWRITTEN this session — the old force-labeled "two breaths, Miasma=EM / Transmutation=Weak" is RETIRED with the mechanism-policing.) A dragon has a **variety of breaths it WIELDS** — fire, lightning, corrosion/rust, rot/plague, freeze, transmutation/alchemy, poison, and so on — and a single dragon may wield **several.** Breaths are **cool attacks, never categorized by which force achieves them.** The science-nod survives as flavor (rust corrodes, alchemy transmutes, freeze pulls heat) with **zero** feasibility audit. **CRITICAL LANGUAGE (the same cage as "fire user"):** say **"a drake who wields corrosion,"** NEVER "a corrosion drake" — the breath is a wielded ability, not a species/lock; nothing stops that drake learning fire or lightning instead. **Alchemy/transmutation breath transmutes elements — full stop, no asterisk** (the "but heavier elements need fusion" rabbit-hole is dead).
  - **Dragons are thinking, witty, ancient, adaptive** — NOT brainless breath-dispensers (they lead a 4D-chess war vs. giants & changelings; they are an elder race, prolific, foremost transmuters). In a fight they **sense materials and adapt**: e.g. a hero escapes, returns with a dwarven-imbued rust-proof shield; the dragon *senses the metal*, recognizes the dwarven master-work (it has seen these before), is **impressed**, and grants the clever foe the "privilege" of dying to its rarer **transmutation breath** (which defeats the shield by changing *what it is*, not attacking a property). **Variety = a difficulty ladder:** lesser breaths can be prepared against (rust-proof the shield); an elder's transmutation defeats the preparation — which is *why* elders are terrifying. The prepare-and-escalate dynamic can even play out against the *same* dragon.

### ELVES — symbolically WATER — mirror to dragons
- Patient, calm, **ancient but MORTAL** (corrected this session — NOT "near-immortal": they age very slowly and can be killed, but they age and die; no true immortals among the aging — Constitution 4a). **Long-lived, but distinctly LESS than giants** — which is *why the saga follows an elven BLOODLINE* (a lineage overlapping across the eras), not a single immortal elf; the elves are the *family/lineage* throughline, giants the *individual* deep-time witnesses. Plan over millennia. **Born like all races** (🚫 RETIRED this session: "elves awaken instead of reproducing" — the awakening was every race's origin-event, not an ongoing elven mechanism; `03_COSMOLOGY.md`). Resets remain existentially terrifying — and the mechanism is darker than erasure: a jump shifts the tiny upstream variables (a survival, an errand, a chance meeting) that every conception depends on, so the lost are **not deleted — they never were.** Elves, with their reset-texture, are the one race that *feels* the holes: **grief for the unrememberable** — mourning absences with no memories attached, because the mourned never existed in this timeline. Lirael's ferocity is partly this grief. (`08_CHARACTERS.md` — this is what Jon witnessed among them, and what finally stilled his hand.)
- **The bond:** innately **sense one another** (an unfakeable belonging-bond); lean **collective over individual**; rigid codes of ethics/discipline (the code *is* the togetherness). **The bond makes them un-assimilable** — it rejects the foreign "I" like tissue rejects a graft — which is why they are the **one race the human machine could never dissolve** (the exception that proves the churn — `10_WORLD.md`), and why the exodus was **one seedship, chosen together.** 🔒 **This is also why they advance fastest (not a new mechanic — a consequence of what's above):** no knowledge-transfer, no shortcut around individual earned mastery (`04`'s awakening gradient stands untouched) — simply a people who cooperate with zero friction, no ego, no rivalry, no wasted effort re-litigating shared purpose, because the bond makes genuine collective work their natural default rather than something painstakingly negotiated. Contrast humanity's own churn (`10`): *"no shared aim the way an elf-clan... does, so they turn on themselves hardest of all."* Five centuries of frictionless cooperation compounding, against five centuries of a fragmented race fighting itself, is why the elves built and the world stagnated.
- **The exodus (reconciled this session):** the elves' departure was NOT a vague drift — it was a **contested cold-war event** (`09_STRUCTURE.md` B1→B2 bridge). They stood apart from and against human domination since the dawn; **Jon didn't convince them, he gardened them** toward fleeing over centuries (seeding a dread they already half-carried), just before Derrek's blow. The others read it as a power grab and warred to reach the stars first. The elves left together on the seedship; their pocket-realm/empire is built at the new home (hold both). They are the One's **surviving unity** to humanity's **shatter** (`03`).
- **The pole-shifted mirror of dragons** — equal apex peers, opposite temperaments (Individual↔Collective, Explosive↔Sustained, Immediate↔Measured, Impunity↔Discipline). Each sees the other's nature as a flaw; neither objectively right.
- **Uninfiltrable/unsubvertible:** dragons can't subvert them (elves grant no deference), changelings can't infiltrate them (the bond rejects the foreign "I"). The **Council** springs from them (`08_CHARACTERS.md`).

### CHANGELINGS — symbolically FIRE — the reunion pole's dark mirror (REBUILT this session)
> 🚫 **SUPERSEDED (tripwires):** "volatile/restless, abandons long lives on impulse, which prevents consolidating power"; "one continuous perfectly-remembering 'I' beneath every face"; "ultimate individualist"; the single cold ego-less calculator; molecular-reassembly-as-copying; changelings-as-literal-demons. The fire anchor survives *reframed*: fire **consumes what it transforms** — that is now the point.
>
> 🔒🔒 **MANDATORY PRE-WRITE CHECK — read before writing ANY changeling shift, no exceptions:** Ask **"can ANYONE see them?"** If the answer is yes — the shift does not happen on the page at that moment. Full stop. This is not a judgment call weighed against the scene's convenience; it is not waived because the witness is an ally, a dying person, a rival, or **another changeling who doesn't yet know what it's looking at.** From the acting changeling's own point of view, any unidentified witness is exactly the exposure risk this entire race is built to never take — "it'll probably be fine this once" is never a thought a changeling has. **This rule has already been violated multiple times in drafting this race** (shifting mid-chase in view of pursuers; shifting mid-standoff to "reveal" the truth to another changeling) — each time because a visible shift is the easy, lazy way to resolve a scene instead of doing the harder work of resolving it through dialogue, behavior, or a private beat found later. Treat every instinct to write a shift-as-reveal as the signal to stop and find another mechanism.

- **What a changeling IS:** the **founder-soul** — the original changeling, HE/SHE, the first source — **persists and presents**: continuity of character, its own goals, morals, and beliefs (the followable being). Around it, every consumed soul it has ever taken: an ever-muddier **swirl of absorbed tendencies** influencing, shaping, steering the founder (`03` harvesting canon — "increasingly plural/fractured" honored as *erosion of the founder's majority*, the changeling aging-arc: old changelings are more crowded, more shaped by the mass, less purely themselves).
- **Unity by CONSUMPTION (`03` axis):** where the elves are many-who-consent to be one, a changeling is many-souls-forced-into-one-vessel — a microcosm of the unified One, *parodied*. Maximum unity by the profane road. (The bond reads this instantly: what approaches an elf is a **crowd wearing one face** — foreign Remnant, plural where there should be one. Uninfiltrable stands, sharpened.)
- **The two arts (two branches, priced separately):**
  1. **ACQUISITION (soul magic — `04`'s rare branch, their mastery):** a slow, expensive **ritual** — the changeling consumes the victim's spirit (soul-harvesting, `03`: absorbs the Remnant, gains power — *the* canonical way to raise innate Remnant, and why changelings sit at elder-tier strength). The victim's **body is left intact** (real corpse, real funeral — the changeling can attend as someone else). The victim's *self* joins the collective.
  2. **ASSUMPTION (self-reshaping):** expending Remnant to take any form in the library — **at any speed cost allows**: leisurely for modest effort, or **instant for a massive surge** (evasion-viable; a pocketed **reservoir charm** (`06`) can fund one emergency shift — finite, then it's fatigue or nothing). Shifts can be **partial and tactical** (a skull shrunk mid-projectile), not only full identity changes. 🔒 **Every single use of this mechanic, in any scene, is gated by the pre-write check above: the shift itself must always happen unseen — alone, off-page, around a corner, in the dark — never as a witnessed act, never as the moment of reveal.**
- **Not impersonation — WEARING THE PERSON:** the changeling doesn't *play* your old friend; it wears **your friend himself as the costume** — his memories, mannerisms, voice, genuinely present and supplying himself, animated from within by the founder's will. Perfect fidelity because the person is *there*; agency stays the founder's. (Whether the worn soul experiences being worn: deliberately unanswered on the page.) 🔒 **GUARDRAIL:** "agency stays the founder's" is absolute, not a matter of degree — the swirl of consumed tendencies (above) colors and pressures the founder's *own* character over time (the aging-arc), but it never seizes the wheel in a moment, never "comes to the surface" as a separate driver, and never varies in intensity by topic. When a founder draws on a specific worn mind's expertise for a specific subject — reaching for the mask that knows this best, for maximum fidelity — that is the founder *choosing which instrument to play*, not the instrument playing itself. Any visible tell (a flicker, a change in bearing) marks a switch of which mind is being channeled, never a lapse in who is doing the channeling.
- **Cold logic, restated:** their observable patience/coldness is **not** ego-lessness (superseded) — it's the founder's own character tempered by internal noise: a seated ruler who knows it is *influenced*, so it governs carefully. **No appetite for power as an end** — power is the most flexible tool for the founder's *personal goals* (individual, never racial — the one place changelings differ from each other). Always **leverage over force**: force is spent, leverage compounds (nudge two kings into mutual ruin rather than flatten either). But 🔒 **raw power rivals the other elders** — the preference for subterfuge is strategy, never weakness; cornered, the switch to force is instant and costless (no ego invested in "winning cleverly").
- **Institutions, not people:** they outlive every mortal relationship, so their real attachments are to things built to survive death — guilds, houses, crowns — held across **centuries and multiple faces**, including engineered deaths/successions that read as ordinary time. What looks like restless wandering is a single patient multi-generational campaign threading several power centers; no mortal lives long enough to see the pattern. Their warmth toward marks is **performance** — a survival adaptation (dependent for food/shelter/place on the very societies that would destroy them if known), refined into instinct; from inside it reads as competence, not cruelty.
- **Scarcity 🔒:** elder-tier power = elder-tier rarity (a handful of individuals, per the dragons/giants design rule). Cause: **encounter probability** — scattered, unable to recognize one another, embedded apart; two changelings ever knowingly meeting (let alone breeding) is a vanishing coincidence.
- **The changeling CHILD (resolved this session):** born like any other race's child (`03` birth mechanics — two changeling parents; changeling-natured Remnant channels in at conception). **Raised in whatever race/community it was born into**, unaware of its nature; as it ages, its power **awakens along the ordinary gradient** (`04` — involuntary leaks first, loudest under emotion). Eventually the child **figures out it is the thing the stories speak of with hatred** — it hears the fear and loathing of changelings from the very people who raised and love it, then discovers that thing is *itself.* That discovery begins each changeling's **evolution: the deliberate, learned shedding of the burden of love**, so it can assimilate more efficiently (both senses — blend into societies, and eventually consume). 🔒 **The coldness is developmental, not innate:** every cold, calculating elder changeling was once a child raised with love somewhere, who learned what it was and what that meant, and shed the love to survive it. (A young changeling is a founder-soul with an *empty* collective — the swirl begins with its first acquisition, later.)
- **Society's wound:** no one can identify a changeling — no test, no tell; they never reveal themselves to ANYONE (🔒 the mandatory pre-write check at the top of this section is this line's direct enforcement — no page-level exception, ever). The dread is structural, not personal: *your oldest friend could be one, and could be one of the most powerful beings alive, and you will never know until it chooses.* They live inside that fear, hearing what people say when they believe no changeling can hear.
- **The demon distinction 🔒:** changelings are NOT demons (kept separate — `06` pacts unchanged; the Orc King's patron is a real demon). But a changeling can *perform* a demon-pact convincingly — banked comic beat: the reader, who saw the Orc King's real pact, recognizes the ritual… and the changeling cons mark and reader both, granting nothing, vanishing behind a new face before blame can land.
- Closest natural analog to **in-betweener fluidity**; can thread all three eras — could notice Derrek's time-pattern; possibly entangled with Derrek's planted institutions.
- **Lifespan:** elder-tier long-lived; exact rung on the ladder 🌊 open. **Ages per Law 4a/4b** — assumption reshapes flesh but the **founder-fragment's age-clock is its own**; wearing a young form does not make you young. No fourth immortal.

### The Elder Web — two games on two timescales ⚖️
- **Game of the NOW (Dragons vs. Changelings):** fast, for living kings/heroes. **Changelings dominate** via **infiltration**; dragons compete via **proxy.**
- **Game of the FUTURE (Dragons vs. Giants):** slow, across millennia. **Giants dominate** by patience + long waiting traps + **attrition** (earth outlasting air); dragons are capable century-players but outmatched. *(Post-Darkest-Age, this is the broken giants' quiet revenge — see the history below.)*
- **Changelings play BOTH timescales — but invisibly and alone** (⚠️ amended this session; 🚫 retired: "changelings can't play the future / nature forbids"). Their multi-generational institutional campaigns are precisely future-game play — but individual, hidden, uncoordinated, where **elves** remain the true *open, collective* dual-timescale player, running their own currents untouched.
- **Deepest/oldest rivalry: Dragons vs. Giants** (earth vs. air; impunity vs. attrition); the dragons' decline was partly the giants' quiet, unannounced victory (the proud may never know it).
- 🌊 **The giant who lived through all three ages** is a strong recurring figure — the one being who personally watched the world climb from swords to total mastery (a living bridge / second witness; candidate mentor for the Book-3 protagonist). **Now embodied (this session):** *some of the original giants who helped build the first ships (2nd era) are still alive in Book 3, in advisory roles* — ancient even for giants, carrying living memory of the entire history of flight (the hopeful dawn → the warped end-times). Natural under 4a (giants are the longest-lived). A *second witness* alongside Jon — but where Jon is the hidden manipulator who remembers across loops, the giant is a venerable, open ancient-mortal who remembers across this timeline's eras.

## THE FAR FUTURE — Book 3 / the Age of Absolutes 🟡 (built this session; story/world)

> Each race's far-future form = its **locked nature scaled to the stars.** Nothing invented; everything is the established race grown up. The **dead-universe** cosmology caps the design burden: other worlds are lifeless rocks the races settled — **no alien peoples to author** (the galaxy is the known races spread across dead space; alien *life* effectively none, alien *minds* none).

### Race forms (each from its nature)
- **ELVES — the cohesive networked EMPIRE in space.** Left Earth; hold a vast empire in other regions of space. Their cohesion rides **the bond + a pocket dimension they created/control** — a **microcosm of the One's act (creating a world and holding it safe; a fragment doing what the One did).** They physically enter it (a real constructed elsewhere, consistent with existing pocket-dimensions — Transcendents make them, the in-betweeners live in one). The mirror of the core law: a mage expresses the One's *inherited* mastery; an ASI expresses *authored* mastery over the reality humans built for it. Real magic vs. its soulless reflection (rhymes with the orc puppet-device and Jon's monument). A cell-/cage-fight between two ASIs can only happen in the digital arena — the one place these reflections are gods and real mages are blind.
