# 06 — EXTERNAL / ALIEN POWER SYSTEMS

> ⚠️ **POST-RESET:** In-betweeners **pre-date the One and are NOT descended from it** — an ancient lineage in its own **bubble between the worlds**, as old as the One (or so old they've forgotten any higher origin). They **obey physics** (shared substrate) but run the **Rule as their operating system instead of Remnant** (they were never the One's consciousness) — same physics, different OS, which is why a chase inside a Rule is coherent. Demons, by contrast, ARE coherent fragments of the One with their own will.
>
> **⚠️ REWORK (this session):** the external systems are collapsed to **TWO umbrellas plus the Rule.** **(1) PACTS** = every deal-with-a-being for power (demons, gods/blessings, blood rituals, Blight-favor, Unified-One boons — one system, distinguished by patron & price). **(2) ENCHANTMENTS** = power stored in matter (and a learnable craft). **(3) THE RULE** stays separate (the in-betweeners' alien OS — not a deal). **All three are thin FRAMES, fleshed out per scene, never pre-catalogued** (`12_METHOD.md`: build laws, not a spell list — the deity's terms, the brace's bound effects, the shape of a soul-strike are decided when an attack needs them).

Systems that operate **outside** the normal Remnant economy — i.e. powered by something other than your own fragment.

## PACTS — the deal-with-a-being umbrella 🔒 (clarified this session)
**Every "deal with a powerful being for a cut of its power, paid by a tithe" is a Pact** — there is no separate system for blessings vs. blood rituals vs. demon deals vs. divine-domain channeling. They differ only by **patron** and **price**, not by mechanism.
- **The mechanism:** power flows to the mortal through a channel; **something flows back** — Remnant, years, memory, devotion, pieces of self, a kept vow. Lets a weak being punch far above its Remnant (the equalizer); unstable; **someone holds the leash.** Can lend either steady power or **borrowed effects** (an ambient channel — running innate + borrowed at once causes interference; used in bursts).
- **Patrons (flavor variety, one system):**
  - **Demons** — coherent fragments with their own individual wills, agents of no cosmic power; each pursues its own ends. The classic two-way Remnant-siphon (power out, Remnant back — which is *why* demons want pacts). (Orc King's pact, `08_CHARACTERS.md`.)
  - **Gods / blessings / divine domains** — a god loans a slice of its authority over one thing (death, fire, fate) on its terms (a vow or tithe you keep or lose it). Borrowed authority far past your tier, conditional and revocable. (The banked pantheon; the angel sending Jon to kill a demon — `11_OPEN_THREADS.md`.)
  - **Blood rituals** — pay in blood, lives, or time to buy an effect; at root a deal with whatever answers the offering. Slow, transactional, grim.
  - **Blight-things / Unified Ones** — rarer, more overwhelming patrons; every use moves the mortal toward what the patron *is* (more power, less themselves). *(Medieval era too early to introduce the Blight directly.)*
- 🚫 RETIRED framing: "demons are the Dark's proxies playing a long game" (implied a willful Dark). The *mechanism* is unchanged; only the "serving a unified Dark" story is dropped. (🚫 "The Light's touch" as a distinct system → it's just a god-pact/blessing now.)
- 🌊 All pact specifics (a given patron's terms, prices, and granted effects) are **built per character, per scene**, grounded in this frame.

## Threshold-beings / In-betweeners (Tier-3) 🔒
*An ancient lineage pre-dating the One (not descended from it), in their own bubble between the worlds; obey physics, run the Rule-OS, not Remnant.*
**Fuel = transition (change/becoming). Price = coherence** (overuse → dissolve into pure transition = their unique death). They operate **outside** the Remnant/force system (they predate it) — they don't out-power anyone, they **sidestep** the rules. Powers are narrow, specific, **rule-shaped.**

**Universal kit (true in-betweeners):**
- **Phase-step** — transition-based movement (cheap short, costly far).
- **Unfixing** — a flicker of non-presence; defense; can't be sustained; countered by being **pinned** (e.g. a gravity anchor).
- **Threshold-sense** — passive perception of transitions/seams/instability and of other transitional beings (incl. Jon & Derrek); free.
- **Minor reshaping** — cheap small-scale state-change.
- **The Hold** — force something to *stay* mid-transition (their trap/prison signature); bleeds coherence continuously → the encounter clock.

**The Rule** 🔒 — a **binding condition/law imposed on a bounded space that traps everyone inside it — the in-betweener included.** Releases only when its condition is met (e.g. "only one may leave"). The in-betweener is bound by its own law and must win its own game to survive it. The fight is a **puzzle-prison:** deduce the wording, navigate/survive it, defeat or escape the hunter who works the same rules against you.

**Emergency moves (tiered):** Lash-Out (overload unfixing) → Sacrifice-Step (tear free of a pin by abandoning coherence; escape diminished) → Snare-Reversal (hold the attacker's pin mid-transition; coherence-vs-Remnant race) → Anchor-Theft (transition the reference point a pin needs; can't be caught by the same trick twice) → **Cascade.**
- **CASCADE** 🔒 = spend **100% of remaining coherence** to set the Rule's condition to its **most lethal value** ("one"→"zero"). The Rule's final form, not a generic explosion. **Dishonorable** (a sore loser flipping the board to deny an earned win, at the cost of self-erasure). Subtlest counter: resolve the condition against it *before* it panics into Cascade. Dramatic tell: when an in-betweener goes **still / stops trying to escape**, Cascade is coming.

**Honor code (rooted in nature):** only a **fair, genuinely-uncertain contest** produces rich transition (their food). So they demand **equal duels** (1v1, 2v2, always matched), bind themselves by their own Rules to keep it fair, and **cultivate worthy opponents** — imbuing mortals with Rules and setting them to fight, watching for any mortal worthy of a personal duel. Clean loss = honorable; Cascade = shameful. Jon & Derrek (living transition) = the ultimate coveted opponents.

**Two categories of Rule-bearer** 🔒 (the second was added for the Book-3 protagonist):
- **The AFFLICTED** — a mortal *given* a Rule unasked; can't aim/master it (transition is alien to a Remnant soul — no learning curve, only scarring); pays unpredictable, often harmful costs; can't safely Cascade. Anchored by mortal coherence (blurry/shaken but rarely fully dissolves). Each is a flawed *preview* — a demi-boss implying a true in-betweener "master" who granted the Rule. (Two-stage villain/ally template: the haunted amateur, then the alien master.)
- **The VICTOR** — a mortal who **defeated a threshold being in fair contest** and was *awarded* the Rule as the honorable prize. Because they *won it through understanding*, they can **wield it with intention and skill** (the afflicted cannot). Legendarily rare. *(This is the Book-3 protagonist — see `08_CHARACTERS.md`.)*

🌊 Open: do true in-betweeners' grand Rules differ in *category* from mortal-gifted ones, or are they just grander? Individual Rules are built per-being as needed (the **specific Rule for the Book-3 protagonist is deferred** to when we build the full threshold-being system).

## Other secondary-ability sources → now folded into PACTS ⚖️
The old separate entries (Unified-Ones-derived elevation, Blight-derived power, "the Light's touch") are **all pacts now** — deals/boons from a powerful being, differing only by patron and price (see PACTS above). A great being briefly raising a mortal's power beyond its ceiling = a god-pact/blessing; drawing on the Blight = a Blight-pact (every use moves you toward what it is). No separate system; no cosmic doctrine attached.

## ENCHANTMENTS & Imbuements — power stored in matter 🔒 (expand per scene)
Foundational rule: **Remnant (consciousness) can be impressed *into* physical matter and persist there** — a stored grip on reality that a being's mind set up and left behind, which can later be drawn on or which keeps running on its own. (Grounding is consciousness-side; matter is just physics — what's stored in it is a *configured bit of a mind's force-grip*.) The counterweight to the live-Remnant economy; the historical dragon-slaying toolkit; the **equalizer for low-Remnant and non-mage characters** (you spend the object's stored charge, not your own fragment — finite, can't improvise, but devastating until spent, with a hard ceiling and no fatigue).

**Three types:**
1. **Reservoirs (stored fuel):** an external Remnant battery; lets a small-Remnant being afford big effects. **Potent reservoirs are stolen Remnant** (harvested = murder; wielder likely Dark-leaning/marked). Cleaner sources may exist (freely given, slowly accumulated, drawn from rip-sites) but the potent stuff is stolen. *(Derrek's Bk2→3 escape: power locked in ancient stones, channeled through rings — a one-time reserve.)*
2. **Attunement objects (skill/control amplifiers, not fuel):** wands/staves/foci/braces that make a discipline easier/sharper/more efficient. Better at *spending*, not richer.
3. **Bound effects (unique enchantments):** a specific working locked permanently into matter (unbreakable blade, sound-dampening cloak, primed transmutation stone, a brace's bound abilities). The proper magic-item category.

- **Enchantment is also a LEARNABLE CRAFT** — a discipline studied like potions, lore, or smithing, not a "kind of mage." A master enchanter need not be a powerful Remnant-user; they're someone who went deep on a craft. Gives the world schools/trades/depth and gives non-prodigies somewhere to be excellent. **Dwarves** the candidate master-enchanter race (🌊 to lock?); their oldest art is fusing magic into metal (which, far-future, becomes the first digital apparatus — `07_BEINGS.md`). Greatest items = **lost-art elder relics.**

> 🌊 Exact mechanism (how consciousness imprints on inert matter and persists) is open for the consciousness-side buildout.
