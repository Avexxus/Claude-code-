# 12 — METHOD (How We Work)

The design discipline behind the whole project. These are 🔒 LAWs about *process* — they protect the work's quality and were hard-won (mostly by the author correcting Claude's reflexes).

---

## The No-Formula / Multi-Scale Law 🔒
- Characters are **not computed** from their positions (no x+y=z).
- Every scale (morality, temperament, favored expressions, loves, duties, …) is **independent, full-range** (with a real middle and a real neutral), and does **NOT** blend into "types" or "corners."
- **Most beings rest in the soft middle of most scales;** the poles are rare. Nobody lives in a corner. ("Dark" is a *tendency that slides by degree in context*, never a category that makes all your acts evil — the cartoon-villain failure mode.)
- Depth lives in the **subtle, often non-aligned** positions a person holds at once — including genuinely **contradictory** ones held whole (Lirael: love AND grief AND duty-above-feeling AND desire, none reconciled; she acts from duty *while* loving). **Stop computing characters; hold their contradictions.**
- The cosmic poles are **bare directions only.** Any meaning/feeling/morality (belonging vs. suffocation, liberty vs. isolation) is *individual*, never definitional. Never assign a pole a tidy thematic value.
- **Tell that I'm violating this:** if I start enumerating "the four combinations are…" or drawing a grid, stop.

## Rigor on the substrate; silence on the soul 🔒
Two modes, and using the wrong one is destructive:
- **Substrate (physics: elements, forces, Remnant, the soul-substrate, costs, interactions):** be the rigorous engineer. Press for hard rules, hunt contradictions, demand consistency.
- **Soul (who a person is and how they therefore express magic):** build the **space of possibility** and then **go quiet.** Never populate it with types. The system must stay *almost arrogantly silent about who people are* — it defines the substrate and stops; the people are sovereign over how they meet it.
- **Why:** the moment a system encodes "this kind of person gets this kind of power," it quantizes individuality and caps versatility. The deepest-character systems keep the rules on the *physics* side of the line and the people on the *character* side. Every "no racial/temperamental lock," "morality is independent," "Inward/Outward is a property of the ability," etc. is this line being held.

## Build laws, not a spell list 🔒
- We build **the laws the spells must obey**, never a lookup table of techniques. A spell list pre-solves every fight (characters become loadouts; prose narrates a calculation — dead).
- Test for anything proposed: *"Is this a law the world obeys, or a catalog of outcomes?"* A law *generates* infinite moments ("gravity can borrow energy from existing mass → cheap, low-ceiling"); a catalog *forecloses* them ("here are the six gravity techniques, ranked").
- **Characters' specific powers emerge from the story** and are explained *after* (as Lirael's gravity-well was recognized, then built on). In a live moment (e.g. Jon vs. an inversion-tornado) we ask: what is it made of? its traits? relative power here? what can the defender perceive? what do they reach for? — invented in the moment, grounded in the laws, never pulled from a menu.
- The groundedness in the **laws** is exactly what *earns* total improvisational **freedom** on top. Freedom above, bedrock below.

## The skeleton & build order 🔒 (REWORKED this session — the rigorous engine was retired)
The original six-joint Sanderson-mirrored build (SOURCE → ACCESS → UNITS → PROGRESSION gates → COST/LIMIT → INTERACTION) was scaffolding for a **rigorous physics-engine magic system that the author deliberately abandoned** this session for **science-flavored, scene-first, loose magic** (`04_MAGIC_SUBSTRATE.md`). So:
- **Kept & solid:** SOURCE (the One → Remnant; Spirit = active Remnant magic), ACCESS (the awakening gradient; Remnant ceiling), UNITS (principle-work + reproducible spells).
- **Retired as pending bones:** PROGRESSION gates, COST/LIMIT charts, INTERACTION laws — these were the rigorous-engine homework. The only standing limits are **Remnant + skill + fatigue**, and all specifics are **discovered per scene**, never charted.
- **Sanderson's Laws still make a fine QC lens** (understand-it-for-earned-solutions; limitations > powers; deepen-before-expanding) — but as *taste*, not a build checklist.

## Magic is expression, not derivation 🔒 (added this session — the "silence on the soul" line, applied to magic)
Build the **person** completely first (motives, wounds, history, who taught them, what they'd take to); **then** their magic follows as an expression of that person, with the force/element flavor named *last* to describe the output. **No equation in either direction** — not ability→character, not character→ability, not race→element. There are **no force/element "users" or types**, only beings who do magic. This is the same discipline as "rigor on the substrate; silence on the soul" below — now the system stays silent even about *abilities* having an identity, let alone people.

## Stability-tier discipline 🔒
- Tag everything 🔒 LAW / ⚖️ FLEXIBLE / 🌊 FLUID (default 🌊; ask when unsure). Mark dead ideas 🚫 RETIRED as tripwires.
- When something changes, **update it in the one file it lives in**, hunt down downstream references, and leave a RETIRED note where confusion could recur. Never leave stale next to current unflagged. (This is *record, not memory* — read the file before working in its domain.)
