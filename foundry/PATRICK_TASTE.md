## 3. House voice and prose rules

**Patrick's rules (quoted or ruled):**
- Heavy wording, suffocating air, grit, nothing clinical (BANKp 11-10).
- Plain, standard writing; no AI-isms; don't over-spell (BANKp 11-07).
- Show, don't tell; no exposition; reveals land suddenly, not telegraphed (BANKp 11-16; 13 jul08 t223, t227).
- Dialogue must read as coherent from a human, not as exposition (BANKp 11-16).
- Traits shown "without becoming blank exposition or weird ham fisted" (13 jul08 t223).
- The numbers stay off the page; describe the act; never label "gravity", "EM" or "strong force" in narration (13 craft; t0–5).
- How gods fight: show what the working looks like (R038).
- Madness is subtext; behaviour, never clinical labels (12 wk W70).
- No real-world racial analogies in text (12).
- Author knowledge isn't page text: "We dont have to write down in words jon manipulated everything for it to be true" (12/13 t402).
- Cost and characterize, never titillate; tenderness is the setup, violence the punchline; the warmth must be real enough to hurt, never a death flag (13 grimdark).
- But/therefore and real-world logic before a scene goes to him (12 jul08 t225).
- More room per beat: "giving him freedom to expand each section one by one" (BANKp 11-16). *(The phrase "expand with scenes, not connective tissue" is a fresh Claude's outside read that Patrick passed on, BANK 11-17; it isn't his own wording.)*
- Soul-sensing is rare and never "video gamey" (12 wk W09; t223, t225).
- Variety in word choice (BANK 11-17).
- His own lines are protected word for word; Claude drafts from his ideas (R106; BANK 11-09).
- Short replies for a phone (CLAUDE.md; BANKp 11-08).

**The house voice as built in v3 (inferred: Claude's practice that Patrick approved by locking ch1–6, not his own words):**
- Close third, tight on the POV; the world arrives through what the POV handles, sees and pays for (`v3/voice_notes.md`; v4 rebuttals).
- Short, plain sentences carry weight; speech is clipped, often with no question mark; working people talk in dialect ("You're stood in the road", "give it him back") (`v3/voice_notes.md`).
- People talk to objects instead of each other (Teo to the sink, Seffa to the stove and the hull) (`v3/voice_notes.md`).
- Hard things said flat, then left alone ("They're all dying. That's what the line is.") (`v3/voice_notes.md`).
- End a beat on an action or object, never on a gloss; half of scenes end flat or mid-business (RI §9, §14).
- Caps: no gesture or phrase more than twice a chapter; no two sentences in a row with the same two-word opening; place phrases ("the edge of the") ≤2 (RI §1, §15, §16).
- Banned telling shapes: "mostly from", "couldn't read", "in a different way", "a long way off", purpose clauses, explaining a look (RI §9).

## 6. Scoring rubric (10 questions, 1–5 each; 50 max)

Score 1 = Patrick would send it back; 3 = workable; 5 = "grimdark excellence". **Any 1 on Q1, Q4 or Q10 fails the chapter, whatever the total.**

1. **Grimdark weight.** Is the air heavy, the cruelty concrete and specific, with few glimmers, and the glimmers at risk? (L1–L3, H11–H12)
   1 = polite or vague darkness · 3 = dark but tidy or clinical · 5 = suffocating, grotesque where it counts, never titillating.
2. **Showing, not telling.** No named feelings, no explained looks, no glosses on callbacks, no exposition dumps. (H14, H20; RI §9)
   1 = narrator explains · 3 = a few glosses · 5 = every beat ends on action or object.
3. **Dialogue.** Would a human send these lines? No one tells another what both know; no epigram buttons; speeches broken by body or listener (except Patrick's lines). (H19–H20; RI §2)
   1 = recap and ripostes · 3 = mostly natural · 5 = every voice distinct and true to `voice_notes.md`.
4. **Calibrations held.** Eli loving and absent at once; Jon warm, cruel and uninterpreted; Doc free and unexplained; orcs as people. (§4; H1–H3, H8–H10, H29)
   1 = a calibration broken · 3 = held but flat · 5 = the gap shows itself on the page.
5. **No convenience.** Every arrival, find and rescue was planted earlier, with friction; every character acts for their own reasons. (H13; RI §7–8)
   1 = quest markers · 3 = one convenience · 5 = nothing arrives on cue.
6. **Sense and logic.** But/therefore holds; geography, timeline, objects and injuries track; rules already on the page are kept. (H21, H27; RI §4–6)
   1 = a stated rule broken · 3 = minor slips · 5 = airtight.
7. **Seed and clue discipline.** About 6 Book 1 seeds plus 3 light future seeds at most; 2–3 pointers per secret; overdue seeds handled. (H7; RI §11–12, §22)
   1 = a seed parade or telegraphing · 3 = at the cap · 5 = under it, every pointer with a plain surface reading.
8. **Pacing and turns.** Every scene turns; travel carries a clock; no two flat scenes in a row; scenes, not connective tissue. (H24, H28; RI §21)
   1 = travelogue · 3 = one sag · 5 = pressure throughout.
9. **Originality and word variety.** No stock mentor, smuggler or psychic images; no tics over cap; varied openings; no polysyndeton engine. (H22–H23; RI §1, §14, §17)
   1 = a trope on autopilot or a tic flood · 3 = a few repeats · 5 = this world's own images throughout.
10. **Canon and provenance.** Nothing breaks the World Kernel; no early op tech (e.g. the Returned ship); Patrick's lines are intact and word for word; nothing AI-added is passed off as his. (H4, H35–H37, H44)
    1 = a canon or provenance break · 3 = a soft drift · 5 = clean.

**Bands:** 45–50 ship to Patrick · 38–44 one light pass · 30–37 a fix round · under 30, or any failing 1, redraft.
