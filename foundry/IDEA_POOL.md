# Idea pool: Patrick's unruled seeds

🌊 Built by Claude, 2026-11-22, from the five export miners (`work/export_mine/m1–m5`) and `foundry/PATRICK_VOICE.md` §3. **Every item is 🌊 not canon.** Patrick floated these and never ruled on them. A story may use one as story-local clay. Nothing here goes into `codex_v5/` without a ruling. Before using one, check it against the World Kernel and the 🚫 lists.
