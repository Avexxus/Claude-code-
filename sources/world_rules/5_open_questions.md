# Appendix: Open questions

### Magic

- Whether overreach past what the body can pay is a named mechanic, or only a description of doing more than a body survives.
- Whether over-reaching through a pact ends in burnout or only in exhaustion.
- Whether soul-sensing is felt rather than reported.
- Whether a soul-magic override that leaves the victim's fragment in place should be called "domination."
- Whether a self survives death, and where it goes.
- Whether objects hold souls without anyone concentrating, while ghosts need a holder.
- Which vessels (a corpse, an amulet, armor) need a holder's concentration and which do not.
- Whether the dense unclaimed Remnant at great-death sites is a usable resource for soul mages, changelings and reservoir builders.
- How concentration as the cost of soul-binding fits with soul magic being limited by self-risk rather than fatigue.
- How changelings' limited lifespan fits with Remnant being ageless and with the transfer road.
- Whether the older account of Convergence still stands in full: its start as a consensual funerary rite, its warping into a tactic in the dragon war, its later monetization, and its use for giant halls and cathedral ships.
- How Convergence relates to the three types of enchantment.
- How the elven bond's monitoring of disposition fits with the bond not tracking individuals.
- What a demon or other non-god patron gains from a pact, given that no Remnant flows back to it, and how the pact tether feeds a god.
- Whether non-soul tithes (years, memory) are still a possible pact price.
- Whether blood rituals and Blight patrons are strong enough to support a pact link.
- Whether losing attunement also costs a pact-holder coherence.
- Whether borrowed pact effects interfere with innate magic and are used in bursts.
- How "a god asks for devotion; a demon offers a deal" fits with gods, angels and demons sitting on one blurred continuum.
- Who the Orc King's patron is, and whether the Orc King is a changeling.
- Whether entropy reaching the Orc King through a pact falls inside or outside the cut on entropy as a domain.
- Whether the psychic road to godhood is the soul-magic road.
- The exact mechanism by which a soul is held in inert matter, beyond the rule that body-like substrate holds best.
- How bound effects (a working locked into matter) fit the definition of enchantment as a soul held in matter.
- Whether beings who cannot do magic can use reservoirs and enchanted items, and whether enchantments degrade.
- How the amplifier principle applies case by case: the orc chips, reservoirs, a captured dragon in a focusing chamber, and the forge world's slave population.
- What happens to a captured dragon held in a focusing chamber, including whether it dies.
- Whether dwarves are the master-enchanter race.
- Whether the Returned, as a dwarven faction, are the specific makers of the bond-spoof chip; when the chip is built; and how it affects the far-future stalemate.
- Whether the human slaving company that supplies elven captives is also the organ trade's supply chain.
- Which era focusing-chamber implants and augmented humans belong to.
- Whether true in-betweeners' grand Rules differ in kind from the Rules given to lesser beings, or are only grander.
- Whether a victim sees Chain's chains as chains.
- Whether a Rule stays anchored to a place if its keeper leaves, and whether a Rule costs its keeper upkeep.
- Whether an in-betweener's physical form can be killed outside its Void.
- Whether a particular Victor, a being carrying a won Rule, exists.
- Whether any of the Returned revere the maze or its keeper, and what to call the technology they built from what the maze taught them.
- "Keeper" names both the fractal-level title and the in-betweener at the center of a maze; the two are not yet separated.
- Whether the cut on folding space is lifted, given the Returned compressing and phasing through warped space, each race's faster-than-light method, the elves' pocket dimensions and the elves' rift device.
- Life and death, mind, and memory of matter are recognized as power domains but not built out; only their soul-magic rules are settled. Reading the past held in things is undeveloped.

### Cosmology and Laws

- Whether "seeks maximum dissolution" still describes the Blight, now that Blight is a title with individual motives and the Blight are beings who rule what they take.
- Why and how the in-between exists outside the regress's chain of events.
- How "Jon and Derrek borrow the puppeteer's magic" fits with "their only given power is time; everything else was learned."
- Whether Jon's time power, lent by the keeper, counts as power from beyond the regress, and so how "Jon can kill Derrek" fits the rule that only a god armed with such power could threaten Derrek.
- Whether the limits on killing Derrek also apply to Jon.
- Whether the pull of reunion is a pull rather than a decree, leaving the openers fully responsible.
- Whether the pull of reunion revives reunion and separation as opposed poles, or stays a background force with no pole positions or motives.
- What dissolution's light face is, matching unity's light and dark faces.
- The puppeteer's motive (guardianship, self-protection from the Blight, or beyond comprehension), and whether it is still a coherent entity that can want, deal and grant.
- Whether "hell" as the human warp route and "hell" as the demons' home are the same place as the clone-pocket the elves call hell.
- "Tether" names both the time-god tether and the pact tether; names are undecided.
- What Jon carries across resets.
- Whether the Tower rift is the only place the Blight can reach in, given accounts of horrors spreading from several rifts in the sky.
- Whether a deep-past catastrophe drove all life to converge on Earth, explaining the lifeless universe.
- Whether each Tower is a door to the time-god society.
- How the time-god society works; who sides with whom in its civil war; whether the founding pair still faces its own window; and which of the founding pair died.
- Whether the details of heaven and the dark prison, and the time-god society itself, are fixed world facts or still provisional.

### Beings

- What elementals positively are.
- Whether more elder races exist beyond dragons, giants and elves.
- Whether a giant's transmutation is fired and left, or must be held by will.
- Whether a soul held inside a changeling experiences being worn, and whether the dread of a soul bound in matter extends to it.

- The exact size of the dragon population: fewer than other races, but more than a handful.
- The real names of the dark elves and of the Returned, and the era of the first escapes from hell.
- The giants' own name for the forge world.

### World

- How the authorless churn fits with the claim that Jon and Derrek have controlled the world so thoroughly that nothing is not manufactured.
- Whether far-future Earth as a grey ruin is a later state or outdated, given that Earth is also the living seat of the Empire and the Tower.
- How Derrek's continuous conquest fits with his time powers seizing him at random.
- Whether "humanity's every flaw and motive concentrated into one being" still describes Derrek.
- How the age that holds Derrek relates to his arrival from the future.
- Whether Derrek persuaded the dragon who struck on betrayal day.
- What key event ignited the giants' fall; cultures tell different versions.
- How Jon leading Mika and the elves away fits with the exodus being a royal directive.
- Whether the modern era's augmented humans and the seedship's approach fit the rest of the timeline.
- Where dragon warships come from: whether dragons stole and reverse-engineered a cathedral ship to build their own, buy ships from the giants, or both.
- Whether the Emperor's son is a will-less pawn with no name, or has a built-up nature of his own.
- Whether "dark elves" names a sect of medieval elves or only the exiles of the dark prison.
- Whether dwarves treat elves as weaker beings to use.
- Whether family is what dwarves love and continuity what they serve, and whether consistency is their one defining trait.
- Whether the dwarven mini-age has a named loss; dwarven faith, underclass and daily life.
- Whether the elven mask is vainglory: the belief that their way is universal.
- Whether the legal organ market's stock is clones, convicted elves and stateless dark elves.
- How giants travel faster than light after their first, slow crossing; whether the Returned have their own method; whether dragons make exotic fuel for dwarven drives; and whether changelings ride inside others' ships and orcs travel as cargo.
- The roster of races is thin, and the Galactic Council needs more seated races.
- Unbuilt: human daily life and specific human polities; goblin and troll detail; gnome exemplars; demons; beasts; elementals; geography and chronology across races.
