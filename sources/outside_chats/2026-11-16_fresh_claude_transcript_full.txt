## Structural problems (fix these first)

1. **Eli is a camera.** Jon rescues him, Jon deflects his questions, the master trains him, the Queen beats him, and Doc heals him. In the finale he's thrown into a wall in the first minute and spends the rest on the floor while the Queen, the dark elf, Doc and Jon do everything. 2. **The book's real story is Jon and the Emperor.** The only relationship with real charge in the final chapters is the two of them: "It's just us," the hand stopping halfway toward the door, sitting shoulder to shoulder. Eli has no relationship with either that matters on the page, so the protagonist is displaced by the villains' story.
3. **Eli's want is thin.** "I'm going to take the chair" arrives in one beat in chapter 2, off a wanted poster. Nothing before it shows ambition. 4. **The ending answers nothing.** Still open at the last line:

5. **Jon is too legible.** The deflections (peaches, "Elves.", "Teo, is there anything to eat?", "You're doing beautifully", the hand on the shoulder) repeat so often that by chapter 3 the reader knows he's hiding something and by chapter 5 knows he's dangerous. The gag outlives its mystery.
