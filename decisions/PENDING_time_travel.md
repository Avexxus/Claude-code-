## What the doc records (Patrick's words in quotes)
- **Why he's considering it:** time travel is "killing" him, and things are "so much easier and less messy without time gods and resets". He worries the Tower nexus plus multiverse plus loop is too close to *The Dark Tower*. He doubts the keeper could grant time powers. And: "This was so many changes and idk if I even like it."
- **Options considered:**
  1. Cut it entirely.
  2. Keep it offstage (Claude's lean then). Patrick rejected it: it "just makes it fun author lore… may as well not exist."
  3. Keep only Derrek's sight.
- **The keeper mechanism, if kept:** the keeper lives outside the universe, so it can grant time powers ("I love that actually… he could give powers from outside thus its ok"). Its charge: "You ripped open the barrier… now you must forever hold it shut by hand."
- **The latest no-time-travel direction:** Jon and Derrek are mortals in one timeline. The portal is torn in Book 4, where they become gods and fight new portals leaking deities. Books 1–2 follow other heroes: Book 1 is young Mika in the Darkest Age (a candidate), Book 2 has no story yet, and Book 3 is Jon and Derrek, mortal, in the far future, with **Eli** (Derrek's son, named). Further points:
  - Derrek is the teacher and Jon the student.
  - Jon's arrogance gets Eli killed, and grief makes Derrek the villain.
  - Jon is an anti-hero and the narrator.
  - Lirael is alive in the far future, heads the Inquisition, and Jon kills her with her own Collapse. She has no fragments.
  - The elves own their own history: Jon doesn't garden them.
