# Rulings ledger (Claude Code era)

The record of every decision Patrick makes in this workspace, from 2026-10-31 on. It continues `WEEK_RULINGS.md` (W01–W70, which is not in this repo).

## How an entry works
- **ID:** `R001`, `R002` … Cite as `(cc R###)` in codex text.
- **Codex markers for changes made from a ruling:** `[NEW v5-cc]`, `[REV v5-cc] (was: …)`, `🚫 RETIRED v5-cc`. These outrank every earlier marker: v5-cc > v5-wk2 > v5-wk > v5 > v5-jul > v4.
- **Every entry records:** the date, the question (with its register/L#/NP# IDs), Patrick's words (verbatim where possible), what was decided, its stability (🔒 only if Patrick says lock; otherwise ✅), which files changed, and what it closed or opened.
- Claude proposals Patrick didn't pick are **not** recorded here. They stay in `work/`.
