## 2. Being tiers & generations (as they stand)

### Creation generations 🔒 — a SIZE spectrum, not creation order (L19–24)
All races coalesced in the ONE shatter; none made later. Sorted by **fragment size → body durability → lifespan → generational distance to the origin**. "First Coalesced / Latecomers" = near vs far from origin in *generational depth* (big fragments live long strides; small ones churn many short lives — same age as peoples, different memory depth).
1. **Transcendent (largest):** gods. Need no warmth/food, exist anywhere; some create **pocket dimensions**. (v5: demons no longer a born order here — angels/demons are made, t666/t674.)
2. **Elder Races (large):** **Dragons, Giants, Elves — the three true elders** (t116–t118). Beginning is only a few lifetimes back. 🌊 door open for more elder races. **Changelings not in the list** — elder-*tier* power but made, from any race (t118, t484, t680).
3. **Latecomers (small):** humans, orcs, dwarves, goblins, gnomes. Short-lived, multiply fast, origin decayed to dim myth. **Birth mechanism for ALL races:** diffuse unclaimed Remnant channeled into the child at conception (`03`).
4. **Scraps (smallest):** beasts, monsters, animals; low Remnant, still genuine fragments. **Elementals** demoted here out of the giants (t78, t102 ruling 3); positive definition **un-ratified** (🌊).

### GIANTS — symbolic EARTH (L84–136, L251–254, L275, L286–299)
**Nature.** A born race (t78), fully separate from elementals. **Mortal titans, not cosmological gods**, but god-like to lesser races (oldest religions may worship them). **Form:** roughly dragon-comparable proportions, human-like shape (t74, t76); "a true giant is smart, cunning and focused" (t76). 🚫 no size chart / square-cube math (t72 "no math"). Largest bodies → largest Remnant → **most POTENT** (not most skilled). **Signature: massive-scale TRUE TRANSMUTATION / will-imposed reshaping**, holding form by will (hills, valleys, megastructures, grand starships). Distinct from dwarves (mine → forge → enchant existing matter). Active users, never passive landmarks. 🌊 "fired and left" vs held. **Grown architecture:** seamless, no chisel marks; complex chain bonds so works stand forever; "your power was the source used to grow the walls" (t34). Symbolic element is cultural only (Law 8).
**Longevity:** longest of all mortals by far; rests on 4a alone (elemental apex retired, t102 r3). A single giant can span all three eras; survivors of betrayal day remember it (t272/t273). Slow births, huge upkeep. **Stoicism & humility = SCAR TISSUE**, not innate virtue. After the genocide they **became cold** (t46) — covering an **active war of attrition** (t56).
**Convergence** ("when soul and matter become one," Patrick's name, t54):

- Old, cultural, predates dragon wars; **planning commissions** ready for a king's funeral (t42). Dragons sit inside the remains of the giant family that built the hall (t32).
- General technique: works with harvested Remnant from any being as **"stealing their production"** (only that phrase is Patrick's, chat t83–t85); mechanism 🌊 — "drives the taken soul's grip" (Claude t84, unconfirmed) vs "using an amplifier to touch a wider pool" (t83, "Im not sure"). ## 10. Must-know for an idea agent (key constraints)

1. **Tiers and generations are rigid; named individuals and plots are clay.** Generations are fragment SIZE, not creation order — every race coalesced in the one shatter. Only three true elder races (dragons, giants, elves). Changelings are elder-*tier* but made, never born, and not a race.
2. **Remnant can't be burned or spent** (W10). Taking a soul is "stealing their production" (W11, mechanism 🌊). Don't write souls as fuel, batteries that "use up" Remnant, or "burning souls."
3. **Changeling pre-write check 🔒🔒:** a transfer or taking never happens on the page if ANYONE can see it. Changelings are possessors, not shapeshifters. Every taking kills the host. One driver holds only at a sustainable pace (1–2 souls); past that come decay or rupture. Changelings are largely monsters now, with only a few clever ones at court; "infiltrate every delegation" 🚫. Never write "a changeling child".
4. **Angels = demons = soul mages on one blurred continuum with changelings.** A pact is a harvest run in reverse. There's no graduation, and good/evil doesn't sort angel from demon. But changelings ≠ singular gods (W32). All gods are born of flesh on one world. Humans reach godhood only by time-fusion or theft; dragons, giants and elves can grow singular. No forced triads.
5. **Dragons are a capable feudal empire** with families and houses (raised vs dispossessed), second only to giants. They build. Never write them as solitary takers or bumbling proud fools. Breaths are wielded, never a species label ("a drake who wields corrosion"). They are burst-strong but drain over a long fight 🔒. No singular being beats a great ancient dragon 🔒; groups, bad matchups and augmented humans can.
6. **Giants:** a born race, separate from elementals, the longest-lived mortals, most potent (not most skilled). Their stoicism is scar tissue over an active war of attrition. They build cathedral ships from converged dead, and now from bought orcs, so that dragons kill dragons. Convergence works on bodies, and the funerary version releases the soul. Their age was erased by dragons. The moral line between giants and dragons is deliberately murky.
7. **Betrayal day is fixed:** the giant emperor's closest advisor (not a dragon ruler) made a deliberate self-sacrifice with a prepared stone, plus a coup inside dragonkind. The Tower IS the converged giant emperor (a compressed city, still faintly feeling). It is the nexus of time gods. "Darkest Age" = the dragon era as others name it, and the name is unfair to dragons.
8. **Elven bond:** innate, collective, unique to elves. It does NOT track individuals; an elf who dies far away dies alone. It doesn't cross into pocket dimensions. On the page soul-sensing is rare, felt and unreliable. Changelings can't infiltrate elves from outside, except through the dwarf-made bond-spoof chip. Elven society is authoritarian, with reunion as its mask.
9. **Heaven and hell** are lifeless clone-pockets. "Elves built heaven" is the in-world belief only. Heaven is empty and hell is full. The crime that sends an elf to hell is stealing a fellow elf's soul or body (drift toward dissidents 🌊). Dark elves are a large population with many born outside. "Drow" is banned.
10. **In-betweeners** pre-date the One and don't descend from it. They obey physics, run the Rule-OS not Remnant, and are NOT "outside time and space." Their powers are Chain, Void and Chain Void, plus splitting at the cost of decoherence. The Returned use their own new Remnant magic, NOT stolen Rule magic (Law 8).
11. **Symbolic elements (earth, air, water, fire) are cultural only** (Law 8); there's no mechanical racial lean. No race is a monolith: factions, not races (t596). Sin isn't a racial attribute ("All races… greedy prideful jealous").
12. **Far future = Book 1 setting** (book numbers are placeholders). The galaxy holds no alien minds, only the known races grown up. Derrek is the open, faceless human Emperor, not a hidden hand. Derrek engineered the forge-world strike (W64). "The final run" is Jon's lie to himself: about 10,000 years per run, 1,000+ runs.
13. **Ship and power parity:** focusing chambers and giant hulls are general tech. The power source differs by race: dragon burst (≈5 beams, never on the page, swarmable), elven sustained bond-pool, human gun-crews. Each race has its own FTL road. Mechanical and Remnant-augmented modes coexist.
14. **ASI has no Remnant and no magic.** It is sovereign only in the digital arena. A brain is not a soul.
15. **Process rules:** don't invent names (placeholders only). Use no real-world ethnic or national analogies. Prefer closing the 🌊 items listed in §8 over opening new ones. Check every idea against the 🚫 list in §6 (especially shapeshifting, souls as fuel, elves awakening, changeling children, four elder races, dragon "take only", individual bond-tracking, and the elves building heaven as fact).
