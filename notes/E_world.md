## 1. What this file is for

- **Two layers in one file (header, lines 1–3):**
  - **Immutable (🔒) = the NATURE of the races and the world.** Lifespans, core magical/physical character, the strife-constant texture, the world's origin as a protected place for the One's expression. Sits beside cosmology as rigid bedrock.

Labels like "Book 2's modern era" are legacy placeholders. Era-specific *plot* belongs to the story layer (`09`).

## 11. Must-know for an idea agent (key constraints)

1. **Nature is law, politics is clay.** Don't change lifespans, core magical character, the strife texture, or the races' natures; alliances, wars and arrangements are ⚖️ and can move.
2. **No assigned racial sin.** Every race carries the full vice-set (greed, pride, jealousy, wrath); describe *most common expression* and its cultural/historical logic. Never "dwarves are greedy," "orcs are greedy," "dwarves are neutral" (🚫).
3. **Conditions, never blood.** Every trait is produced by clock, history and position (Law 8). Orcs are "short-horizoned, not stupid"; "they earned it" is always the world's lie, never the author's.
4. **No monoliths, every race fractured.** Any race has several powers and a named schism (orcs: inside each orc). The elves can't come apart but keep distinct realms.
5. **The churn is authorless.** No single villain caused the catastrophe; a god stepping in is "another expression of our system." (But note the open #59 tension and Derrek's forge-world strike.)
6. **Human hunger is open** (want vs necessity, #11). Don't write ideas that silently pick one; human supremacy is theologized as "anointed children of the One," and humans are the religious, zealous race worshipping the puppeteer.
7. **Derrek is the open emperor (faceless "the Emperor" in the far future); Jon is the elves' hidden god** who asks for devotion. Derrek is humanity's apex avatar, not the embodiment of human sin. He engineered the forge-world strike; his ultimate goal is proving humans were never lesser (possibly a cover).
8. **Elves are un-absorbable** (bond rejects grafts); only the bond-spoof chip cracks them. Authoritarian, united, never under the giants. "Heaven built by elves" is in-world belief only. Jon led Mika and the elves away on the seedship. Dark-elf name is open ("Drow" banned as D&D's).
9. **Dwarves win by entanglement, elves by separation**; they alone (with elves) "never fell." Dwarves sell to every side, carry a family face, but sold their own to the maze (the Returned).
10. **Orcs: three masters, grievance without archive, culture of HATE, pact-craft as poorer-patron religion; far-future cattle.** The reader-trap is the point: never write them as brutes who deserve it.
11. **Small races: gnomes happy only in remote medieval villages (gone by the end), indentured to dwarves in the far future; goblins are pets of orcs and counted as "orcs"; trolls get small details only. No goblin city or troll fortress ever shown.**
12. **One capital, every age**; Tower at its heart; first and largest rift there. Rifts are reservoirs, never the source. Betrayal day was a deliberate sacrifice (not a blunder). Dragons held the Tower before Derrek.
13. **Far-future order:** Earth is everyone's homeworld and human capital; Galactic Council in human buildings; official stalemate, humans winning functionally; each race its own FTL road (no shared hell-shortcut).
14. **Late-October systems are ✅ not 🔒:** religion = name vs act; clones have souls (world pretends not); organ trade = government market + black market, dwarven-human enterprise, orcs excluded, captured dark elves included — don't treat Claude's stock list or supply chain as canon (L32). Remnant is never burned as fuel (wk W10).
15. **Process:** madness/the One is subtext only; no real-world racial or ethnic analogies (UN/Dutch East Indies comparisons are pending #9); names Claude invents are placeholders; prefer ideas that close listed open items (§9) and say so when an idea opens a new one; check every idea against the 🚫 list in §7.
