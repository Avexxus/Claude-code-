## 1. What these trackers are and how they relate

**11_OPEN_THREADS (the story-layer open list).** A 🟡 story-layer file under the "nothing here is fixed" banner. It holds what still has to be *built*, and when: the active pending decisions (A), the deferred-questions log grouped by trigger ("when we build the Orc King…", B), reconciliation flags (C), banked threads (D/E), threads closed by v5 canon (F), new v5 threads by trigger (G), housekeeping (H), and the late-October open questions (I). Most of its live items are pointers: "→ NP #n" or "→ L#". It carries `[REV v5]`, `✅`, `🚫 MOOT v5` and `[v5-wk2]` markers, and nothing is deleted, so a reader has to follow the latest marker on each line.

**NEEDS_PATRICK (decisions only Patrick can make).** The conflict register: each item is a clash between two sources, two of Patrick's own turns, or a Patrick turn and an existing law. NP #1–#100 come from the v5 physics/July passes. #36, #88 and #95 were settled by the cross-file pass and removed, and the old numbers are kept so cites still hold. L1–L35 come from the late-October fold and raw-chat audit, with L1–L10 marked "most load-bearing". Settled numbers are marked **RESOLVED v5-wk2**, and the "Already settled" list sits at the end. Files carry both sides or a 🌊 until he rules.

**FLAGGED_ADDITIONS (Claude-proposed, awaiting his word).** F1–F182: material that came up in chat but is **not canon**. That covers Claude's or Gemini's proposals, questions never answered, offers left hanging, and lines the verifiers traced to Claude's wording rather than Patrick's. It is grouped by destination file. Each item needs a "yes", a "no", or an answer. F174–F182 are the Claude wording the audit found inside WEEK_RULINGS. A 🚫 declined/moot list at the bottom stops items being re-proposed.

**How they interlock.** 11 says *what is still unbuilt*, NP says *where sources disagree*, and FLAGGED says *what Claude proposed that Patrick hasn't ratified*. One real question often appears in all three. For example, "stored Remnant" is 11-A, 11-I, NP#1, L1, F17 and F31. That is why this register merges rows.
