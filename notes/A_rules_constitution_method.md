## 8. Must-know for an idea agent

1. **Only Patrick makes canon.** Label every idea 🌊 Claude proposal; never give it 🔒/✅; route it toward FLAGGED_ADDITIONS. No codex writes unless Patrick calls the pass.
2. **Every line of prose is Patrick's.** Offer beats, options, consequences, never prose passages.
3. **World is rigid, story is clay.** Never bend a world law to fit a story idea; story-vs-story clashes are fine.
4. **Physics is flavor.** No math, no feasibility audits, no budgets or stat tables; the science serves the cool; rigor = internal consistency only. Never bridge physics and consciousness except via magic.
5. **Remnant is identity: never fuel, never burned, never spent, never transferred.** Only wholesale absorption moves it (mechanism of "stealing production" is open). The only bill for magic is effort/fatigue; magic may add energy.
6. **Three dials: Remnant, control, inner sense.** The grip is an appendage (mechanized); the inner sense stays mysterious; basic magic needs only control, deeper work needs finer sense (gradient, not gate).
7. **No users, types, builds, element/force classes, grids, triads or formulas.** Magic is expression: build the person first, name the force-flavor last. Everyone is "just a skilled user."
8. **Everything is graded.** No god/mortal divide (only scale), no monolithic races, no group is THE anything, no racial sins, don't harden tendencies into conditions, don't flatten blurs (changeling ↔ angel/demon ↔ god is one continuum). Humans become gods only by time-fusion or theft. Don't use "mortal" as god's opposite.
9. **Matter does no magic; a soul held in matter does** (enchantment). Inanimate matter never *gains* a soul. Machines are not beings.
10. **Aging: resistance, never immunity.** Ladder orcs < humans < dwarves < elves < dragons < giants < great gods. True immortality only by mechanism: Jon, Derrek, rule-beasts. Only a god with beyond-the-regress power (who outsmarts him) can threaten Derrek.
11. **Cosmology**: the regress (planes are beings that came apart; some part always clings); the One is background only; Unified Ones hold the barrier; keeper/consumer/Blight are titles with individual motives; the puppeteer is the keeper; the portal breached the shield and each reset widens it; Derrek never supported the Blight. In-betweeners pre-date the One, run the Rule, obey physics.
12. **Pacts grant technique, never Remnant**; the tether fades only on the holder's side; the holder pays fatigue from their own Remnant; taught abilities remain. Angels = demons = changeling-kind soul mages. A changeling is a crime, not a race; soul theft belongs to no race.
13. **Jon and Derrek's only given power is time** (Derrek sees time, Jon jumps, Derrek is tethered to the jumps). "The final run" is Jon's lie; each book ends at the window with two men; earlier loops never happened; at the window Jon never answers. Book numbers and eras are placeholders; scenes float.
14. **Names Claude invents are placeholders; deeds before names. No real-world racial/ethnic analogies, no clinical labels; madness is subtext; tone is grimdark.**
15. **Before presenting, check** against 02, every file's 🚫 list (§4 above) and 12; name the law touched; prefer closing open items (NEEDS_PATRICK L1-L35, `11` section I) over opening new ones, and say so if an idea opens one. Give bite-sized numbered options; expect "c, both."
