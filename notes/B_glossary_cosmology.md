Innate, fixed in the living, raisable only by harvesting (which fragments you). **NOT** fuel, mana, or spent per cast; cost is effort/fatigue only. 4. **Soul magic is a rare, taboo branch** (will vs will, self-risk, belongs to no race). Pacts teach **technique, never Remnant**; tether is asymmetric ("can't stop being findable"); the tether feeds the god.
