## 2. The magic system as it stands now (current state + stability)

### 2.1 Design frame (🔒)
- **The MAJOR REWORK** (04 L14) has three retirements: **steer-only is dead** (magic may *add* energy), **the Body/Spirit split is dissolved** (no "two kinds" of magic), and **force/element mechanics are pure flavor** (no clusters, ladders, pathways, or "kinds of user").
- **No physics audit, no math, ever** (04 L39; t72 "your doing math and we agreed no math"; t73 dragons are the standing proof that no mechanism is owed). The only limits are the soft gates (2.4).
- **Magic = science, in-world one word** (04 L23). Humans call it "science" because dragons were humanity's first teachers (wk W63). Faith, magic, and science are one act under different names (wk W57).
- **Built-in reader mystery** (MSE §12): medieval folk-elements give way to the reveal of one undivided power. How it plays out for a reader is now undecided: book numbers and era order are placeholders (wk W52).

### 2.2 Remnant (🔒, with v5/jul/wk additions)
- **Remnant = the chunk of the One you ARE.** It is your consciousness and your capacity, meaning how much reality you can grip at once. It is innate and fixed in the living. It can be raised only by harvesting, which fragments you. It is **identity, not fuel**: no mana, no pool, nothing spent per cast (04 L20). **Soul = Remnant** (wk W07).
- **[v5-wk] Remnant cannot be burned or spent:** "we cant burn remnant" (wk W10). This is the single most consequential late ruling (see §6).
- **Ageless** (t678): the fragment doesn't age, flesh does. More Remnant makes flesh *resist* age (Law 4a), but never grants immunity. This retires the changeling "founder's clock" (routed to 07).
- **[v5-jul] Not transferable** (jul23 t108, t114). It can't be given, lent, or slowly siphoned. Only knowledge or technique passes. **Wholesale absorption is the one exception** (jul23 t116).
- **Rifts are reservoirs, never the source** (t186–188): they leak power from the level above and people fight over them, but every being's magic is its own soul.
- **Spirit = the name for active Remnant magic.** It means consciously manipulating matter (and rarely, consciousness). It is the system itself, not half of a two-part system (04 L22).
- **No "mana"/pool language for a being's Remnant** (🚫, 04 L181). Rift "pools of remnant" are allowed because they describe *outside* reservoirs. Patrick's placeholder technique name containing "mana" is flagged for renaming.

### 2.3 The appendage and the inner sense (v5-wk, unlocked)
- **THE APPENDAGE (wk W01, W02):** Remnant extends an appendage that grabs the universe. Its length, size, shape, and intensity scale with **Remnant, skill and focus**. Flavor list (never math):
  - shaking particles = **heat**
  - pulling charges apart = **lightning**
  - crushing or ripping the nucleus = **explosions**
  - **turning a quark = transmutation, the hardest act**; a botched transmutation rips the nucleus
  - denting space = **gravity**
  - Dragons stay four-legged; the appendage is their hands (wk W63).
- **"The grip is now mechanized; the sense stays mysterious"** (wk W06). This retired "There is no interface mechanism to explain" (🚫 v5-wk).
- **THE INNER SENSE (wk W03, W05, W06):** an unexplained perception from Remnant, **separate from the appendage**, both trainable and innate. Patrick: "you mostly feel with a second inner sense." "Feel" returns as a skill dial, not a gate (W05).
- **Sensing other beings' Remnant** is a rare master-level skill: "Some very talented masters have learned to sense other beings remnant. This is rare" (wk W07). On the page it must never be "video gamey", with no constant scanning (W09). **Caveat (05 L31, v5-verify-wk, L35):** "felt rather than reported" and "unreliable" are **Claude's specifics (chat t226), unconfirmed**. Only rarity and anti-"video gamey" are Patrick's (t223, t225). 04 L25 and MSE L139 still state all four as rulings.
- **Elven bond (wk W07, W08):** innate, constant, collective, **unique to elves**. Elves are the premier sensors, and the elf queen's "vigilance" comes from intense use of the bond. **It does NOT track individuals**: an elf who dies far away dies alone.

### 2.4 The three dials + the bill (🔒 section; reworked v5-wk)
- **Three independent dials** (wk W03), which replace "Remnant + skill = the entire mechanical statement" (🚫 v5-wk):
  1. **Remnant (how much):** the natural ceiling.
  2. **Skill / mastery, which the rulings call control (how well):** grip, shape, and **blend**. Two beings with equal Remnant produce utterly different results.
  3. **Inner sense (how finely you perceive):** "Basic magic needs only control; deeper levels need finer sense. **This is a cost gradient, not a gate**" (wk W04). Attribution (v5-wk2): the substance is Patrick's ("To start actually interfering with actual physics you must be able to feel small things", chat t65), but the *words* "cost gradient, not a gate" are Claude's (t60, t66). Example: the **"numb titan"**, with huge Remnant and poor sense.
- **Effort/fatigue = the bill.** "Exhaustion is the universal soft limit." Overreach past what body and Remnant can pay is dangerous. **🌊 Whether "overdraw"/burning the self is a named mechanic is OPEN.** But since v5-wk, **overdraw can no longer mean burning Remnant** (W10).
- **Power** = a single graded continuum of **Remnant × skill × time-to-learn** (04 L159).

### 2.5 Forces/elements = descriptive flavor (🔒)
- The four forces (Strong, EM, Weak, Gravity) and four elements are a **vocabulary applied after the fact**, never constraints. Unbinding an atom gets called nuclear, collapsing matter gets called gravity, reshaping gets called EM, and warping what a thing *is* gets called weak.
- **No users, no types.** "As with all our characters she is just a skilled user" (t612). The "Strong-user" label is retired: "Hes just a master" (t136). **Strong-flavored feats remain the rarest, found only at the very top** (t136, t83–84; Law 7b: mythic by scale, never a class).
- **Blending is free** (fire + gravity + spin = a fire-gravity tornado). The only structural residue is that the forces never fuse into a fifth thing, and even that gates no one.
- Showpiece image (MSE §10): holding matter in a gravity well while igniting a star at its heart. **Mastery is combination.**
- **Giants' stone** (t29–30, t34): grown, willed, seamless, built through chain-bond mastery. This is description, not mechanism. 🚫 EM does not actively hold a giant up (t72).

### 2.6 Expression, not derivation (🔒, the core character principle)
- Build the person completely first. Magic flavor is assigned **last**. There is **no derivation in either direction** (ability→character or character→ability), and nothing like race→element or temperament→affinity.
- The same effect can mean opposite things: one being's gravity is grief, another's is dominance.
- People are **individuals, not types** (the Wheel of Time comp). They have a learned repertoire, natural inclination (culture can *color* it, never wall it in), and a chosen specialty. Crafts such as potions, enchantment, and lore are where low-Remnant characters can excel.
- **[v5-jul] Every character develops their own powers; gods are the ultimate expression** (jul23 t106). A god's magic is the same Remnant magic. Patrick rolled back "gods practice magic different to remnant" (jul23 t104).

### 2.7 Awakening gradient & spells (🔒)
- Ability is a continuous gradient: involuntary leaks (loudest under emotion) → crude intent → control through understanding → engineered reproducible workings → mastery. Remnant sets the ceiling. Ability dulls with neglect. It is individual, never racial (only statistically race-flavored).
- **Spells** = specific, complex, **reproducible** workings, like a repeatable experiment. They are not slotted. They are capped by Remnant and reached through skill, with no cap on how many one knows. This is why schools, lineages, and lost spells exist. There are two layers: raw working (most people stop here) and spells (MSE §10).

### 2.8 Machines vs magic (v5-jul)
- "Machines can mimic magic's effects — never do magic" (jul08 t339–340). Tech such as an EM field generator produces a **parallel effect described in the same words, never the same act**, because "Magic is implicitly stated as your conscious manipulating the world." Anything with no Remnant (a machine, an ASI) does no magic.
- Related devices (all in 06): **focusing chambers** (dwarven, built onto dragon warships), **focusing-chamber implants** (augments), and the **bond-spoof chip**. These are hybrids. A chip "works only for someone already capable of soul magic" (jul23 t151–152), so it amplifies a Remnant-bearer rather than doing magic itself.

### 2.9 Soul magic, the rare second branch (🔒 header; subsections ✅)
- Spirit turned on **another fragment of the One**. **Same Remnant and effort** (v5-wk revised "same fuel"), different rules: **will against will, coherence against coherence**, no forces or elements. It is two-way and intimate: **"the limiter is self-risk, not fatigue"**. Overreach bleeds the other's nature into you, fractures you, or costs you pieces of self.
- It is rare, **forbidden and scarcely known** (t544), and belongs to no race (t484, Law 8). It is **tempting and taboo**, because everyone knows souls can be stolen (t680).
- **[v5-jul] It can override a living Remnant and take its body** (jul08 t329). Claude's "domination" naming was never confirmed.
- **Specifics are discovered per scene**, never pre-catalogued.

**Echoes / ghosts / changelings (✅, t498–502):**
- **Echoes:** loose Remnant left by great death, **impressions only, no person there**. They fade back "into the pool" over centuries. 🌊 Their role as a resource ("a massacre site is a mine," t499) is tied to the open reservoir question.
- **Ghosts:** "remnant which was bound together by magic" (t500). **Every true ghost is a soul-binding crime; a haunting is a crime scene.** Ghosts may be bound as guardians, servants, power sources, punishment, or out of love.
- **Changelings:** many souls bound into one living driver (07).
- 🌊 Whether a self survives death, and where it goes, is **open by design** (t499).
- **What it is like to be held:** "some type of experience," but full experience "takes biology" (t42). **Ruled (wk W13): a bound soul feels dread, without a brain to understand it** (cf. jul08 t341).
- **[v5-wk] Undead types** (W69): plague (biotech), animated flesh, souls bound to corpses, souls in machines, changeling leftovers. **Resurrection is possible in principle** (W51): through decoherence nothing is destroyed, but the odds are near zero, and Derrek, who sees time, could trace the fragments.
- **Not every walking corpse is a ghost:** the Orc King's dead are **empty** animated flesh (t522).

**Binding = concentration; the holder pays (✅, t504–505):**
- "What if the cost is a constant effort to hold the magic still" (t504). **A binding is never finished.** If the holder is killed, the binding lapses and the held soul is freed or destroyed (the brothers example). That makes killing the holder a tactic.
- This reads on changelings too, as one driver holding a crowd by constant will. **[v5-wk] The guardrail holds only for changelings who keep a sustainable pace** (W22).
- **Scope (wk W14): "ghosts need a holder; objects hold souls without a holder's concentration."** This is **[v5-wk2] 🌊 PROPOSED, UNCONFIRMED.** It was Claude's proposal (chat t82); "Patrick moved on and never addressed it."

**Warden-objects (placeholder term) (✅ concept / 🌊 mechanism, t506–509):**
- A millennia-old ghost needs "an even sicker use of soul magic meets enchantment… bind someone's soul permanently to an object and force its only purpose to be maintaining concentration on the ghost" (t506). So there are **two victims**, and **breaking the object frees both**. The mechanism is deliberately hand-wavey ("ghost magic isnt easy to quantify anyway," t508).
- [v5-wk] Under W12 the object *holds* the warden-soul, which is enchantment. The matter does no magic; the held soul does.

**The vessel varies (✅, t510–513):**
- "We happened to use his body this time. But we could lock him into an amulet like the son. Or armor like al" (t512). **Necromancy = the holding + the vessel.** [v5-wk] **Body-like substrate holds best** (W12). 🌊 Which vessels need a holder under W14 is not stated.
- 🟡 Example (t510): a dark Returned necromancer forges the son into an amulet bound to the dead father's chest. When the mother dies, the father is left cohered by his dead son. Touchstone: binding a loved one's soul into armor (t502).

**Two roads of soul theft (✅, t676):**
- "There are 2 ways to become a changeling. You move body or consume them."
  - **TRANSFER:** force your soul into a younger body (an old man would).
  - **CONSUME:** take souls into your own body (a dragon, or someone with a bloodline trait, would).
- **A crime, not a race** (t680): "Its your father its your uncle its the priest at the church." **Only the powerful can do it** (t544): "ALL CHANGELINGS ARE INCREDIBLY POWERFUL OFF RIP."
- **[v5-wk]** The pace is **1–2 souls at a time** (W16). **The tell is a soul–body mismatch**; smart changelings shield it or hop to bigger bodies (W18). Failure modes: the giant's **decay** and the young dark elf's **rupture** (W20). Changelings are now largely monsters (W19), and only a few clever ones hide at court (MSE L160). They live about as long as dragons and giants (MSE L158).

**Wholesale absorption (⚖️ Patrick-stated, never locked; jul23 t116–120):**

- It is soul-harvesting from the taker's side. It raises the taker's Remnant and pluralizes them (Constitution 14). The consume road is its changeling form.

### 2.10 Power scale and gods (🔒 continuum)
- "Demigod"/"god" are descriptive points on the scale, not tiers. A weak god ranks low among gods. **Gods are what happens when ANY user becomes incredibly powerful** (t668), by matter or soul paths (t670). **No graduation**: changeling → angel/demon → god are blurred regions (t674). **Angel and demon are one kind of being** (t674), and they are soul mages like changelings (t666, t668).
- **[v5-wk]** All gods are born of flesh, on one world (W30). **Humans reach godhood only by time-fusion (Jon, Derrek) or theft.** Dragons, giants, and elves can grow **singular** (W31). Singular gods revile changelings, though power earns respect regardless. **There is no formula tying god vs. demon to method of ascent.** A changeling who ascended is not a "god" in the singular sense (W32).
- Usage rule: **never use "mortal" as the opposite of "god"**. "careful not to call them mortal gods are mortals" (jul23 t118). Say "pact-holder."

### 2.11 Pacts (🔒 header, reworked v5; approved t630)
- **One umbrella** covering demons, god-blessings, blood rituals, Blight-favor, and Unified-One boons. They differ only by **patron and price**.
- **A pact grants TECHNIQUE, never Remnant** (t629/630, t633–634).
  - **The pact-holder pays for every use from their own limited Remnant, meaning the same effort/fatigue every act of magic costs** (jul23 t108). The old "own soul pays/spent" wording is 🚫 retired v5-verify.
  - A **soul-magic tether** binds holder to patron. It is **fragile and fading on the holder's side (it needs attunement), and costs the patron nothing**, so "You can stop attuning. You can't stop being findable" (jul23 t115). The patron can always collect. What collecting looks like (a demand, a curse, a geas) is decided per patron.
- **Each patron's technique is its own signature** ("kekegenki," jul23 t106).
- **"A pact doesn't make you strong; it makes you unrepeatable"** (jul23 t109). **"A user in book 1 using a gods remnant would be undefeatable. A user using a gods power with their own limited remnant keeps them grounded"** (jul23 t108).
- **Taught abilities remain** as the link fades (jul23 t114).
- **A patron must be strong enough to support the link** (jul23 t106). Under the physics chat, patrons are superior soul mages (t666). Whether blood rituals and Blight-patrons meet this bar was never ruled.
- **A pact is a harvest reversed:** "Giving a pact would be the reverse of when they take a soul. Instead connecting to them and opening that flow" (t666).
- **"A god asks for your devotion. A demon offers you a deal"** (t548). Patrons are reached through **attunement** (they appear "in the ether as the bhudda," jul23 t112). A user "can literally be weakened by losing enlightenment." 🌊 Whether lost attunement also costs coherence is open (Claude, jul23 t113). **No binary** between patron types (jul23 t110).
- **[v5-wk] Religion as recruitment:** a religion is a portrait of its god and a network of **pact champions**, and **the tether feeds the god** (W57). There are faith priests, pact priests, and One-intuiting mystics. 🌊 What a **demon** gets out of a pact remains open, as does whether non-soul tithes (years, memory) survive.
- **Patron flavors:**
  - **Angels/demons:** one kind of being, soul mages.
    - 🟡 **The monk** (W24): made a demon pact, conquered parts of hell, became a demon, **severed and inverted the contract**, and fuses with demons and dark elves.
  - **Gods:** a level of power that teaches its signature technique.
  - **Blood rituals:** pay in blood, lives, or time.
  - **Blight-things / Unified Ones:** every use moves the holder toward what the patron is. **[v5-wk]** Keeper / consumer / Blight are titles per fractal level, with individual motives (W36). Our keeper is the patron/puppeteer who maintains the bubble and lends the time powers (W35). The medieval era is too early for the Blight.
- **Will-control & the avatar** (jul23 t122–126): a strong patron can puppet a thrall, "not always but could be fun dues ex machina." The thrall also acts as the god's avatar. This does not break the authorless churn ("A being making a choice so itself"). Craft rule: **"Its not the narrative saving a character. Its the narrative saving a character as a plot point."**
- **Orc King's pact:** a pact over **entropy** (t520). His dead are empty flesh (t522). He is small-souled and casting far bigger than he is, paid from his own limited Remnant. 🌊 **His patron is OPEN** (t667; handoff §6 Q8). 🌊 **The Orc King is open for full rework** (W23).
- Pre-rework detail kept pending: borrowed effects used alongside innate magic interfere and are used in bursts (06 L29).

"Objects hold without concentration" (W14) is 🌊 proposed only.

## 8. Must-know for an idea agent (key constraints)

1. **Remnant is identity, never fuel. It cannot be burned, spent, given, lent, or siphoned** (W10; jul23 t108, t114). Any idea that "powers" an attack by consuming souls, rift Remnant, or one's own Remnant violates W10. Only **wholesale absorption** (power-gated, by coherence) moves Remnant between beings. "Stealing production" exists as a name, but its mechanism is open, so don't invent one as canon.
2. **The only limits are three dials (Remnant, control, inner sense) plus fatigue.** The sense is a cost gradient, not a gate. **No physics audit, no math, no energy ledger.** Magic may add energy (steer-only 🚫).
3. **No types, ever.** No "gravity user," no element temperaments, no race→element. Labels name acts. Build the person first and assign flavor last (04 expression 🔒; t612; t136).
4. **Forces/elements are post-hoc vocabulary.** 14_PHYSICS and PROPERTY_INVENTORY are non-binding palettes: use them to *describe*, never to adjudicate.
5. **Soul magic is rare, forbidden, two-way, and limited by self-risk.** It belongs to no race. Specifics are discovered per scene. On the page, sensing souls is rare and never "video gamey" (W09).
6. **Binding = concentration; the holder pays; kill the holder and the binding lapses.** Every ghost is a crime. Echoes have no one there. A warden-object haunting has two victims. "Objects need no holder" (W14) is **unconfirmed**, so don't lean on it.
7. **Changelings: two roads (transfer/consume), a crime not a race, all incredibly powerful, a pace of 1–2 souls, the soul–body mismatch tell.** The one-driver rule holds only at a sustainable pace.
8. **Pacts teach technique, never Remnant.** The holder pays effort/fatigue for every use. The tether fades on the holder's side but the patron can always collect. Taught abilities remain. "Unrepeatable, not strong." Never call pact-holders "mortal" as opposed to gods.
9. **Enchantment = a soul held in matter** (body-like substrate holds best, and the soul feels dread). The old reservoir/charge/"until spent" model is **unreconciled**. Any enchantment, reservoir, or drain idea should say how it fits W10/W12 or mark itself as touching the open seam (06 L111).
10. **Gods are a level of power, not a kind.** Changeling → angel/demon → god is one blurred continuum. Humans reach godhood only by time-fusion or theft. There is no formula linking ascent method to god vs demon.
11. **In-betweeners run the Rule, not Remnant**, and predate the One (🔒 lineage). Void/Chain/Splitting are canon. The kit, Rule mechanics, and Cascade are ⚖️ adjustable. The Returned's powers are **learned Remnant magic, not stolen Rule-magic** (t148).
12. **Machines mimic effects and never do magic** (jul08 t339). Devices such as chips and implants amplify a Remnant-bearer and never raise anyone's Remnant.
13. **Tower set-pieces need mechanics:** the apprentice's technique and death, and the Tower changeling's attack, are overturned. The **master's black hole "feeds… his remnent" is an unflagged W10 tension**. Ideas that close these loops are high-value (prefer closing over opening).
14. **Cut domains stay cut unless Patrick lifts them:** space-folding, entropy as a power, probability, Light/Dark. The Returned, FTL, and the Orc King sit on the seams. Don't add new wieldable domains.
15. **Names Claude coins are placeholders**: warden-objects, forgetting horizon, Rule-engines, the Returned, drow, "domination." Don't promote them to canon.
