## 2. Jon

## 3. Derrek

- **A far-future wizard-scientist** of few peers, with extreme magical and technological augmentation, renown and wealth.

  - That stands beside "a murderous tyrannical dictator. Theres no real forgiveness" (t558) and "the greatest evil the world had ever known" (t182). Held as duality, motive (warped love, t694) vs deeds (monstrous). Unruled (**NP #63**).

- **A god to the past twice over** (jul23 t26): far-future top-tier human plus time god. His first goal on arriving was "to control all of human history and shape them in his image."

### 3.2 The curse and the tether

## 11. Must-know for an idea agent

1. **The whole file is 🟡 clay and every name is a placeholder.** Never invent names; Claude names are placeholders (t630). The world layer (02, 04, 06, etc.) is the only hard wall.
2. **Jon is the true villain and the worst person in the series** (W44). He is warm, charming and **openly sincere**, and hides nothing **except the Lirael secret**. His narcissism shows in choices, never in manner (t215). Don't write him arrogant (that's Derrek's lane) or cold-as-rude on the surface. Write the says-kind/does-cruel disconnect (t748) and never let a narrator confirm it. No clinical labels.
3. **The Lirael secret**: she is Mika's sister, and Jon killed her **on purpose, repeatedly across loops**, because "Mika required a dead sister." Only Derrek knows (W45–46). She and Derrek are the only people who ever moved Jon (chat t243, t301). Keep her light in Book 1: statue, borrowed Collapse, last flashback.
4. **Derrek is the open, faceless emperor of humanity** (t492, t364), not a hidden gardener. **Jon** is the hidden god of the elves, who unknowingly hunt him (t490). Don't swap these back.
5. **Derrek's motive is love and guilt; he holds the world against the Blight he and Jon let in** (t180, t730). The window's "reset or reality ends" is true. He chose to be the greatest evil (t182). His core drive is to be seen once by Jon as an equal (chat t235). He **only ever loved Jon** (t358). Never use the end-everything plan or self-deception (🚫).
6. **Mechanics**: Jon jumps and resets; Derrek sees time, can't reset, is tethered to every Jon jump, and aims them (W39; jul08 t369). Derrek's methods escalate force → horrors → **the son**, whom he planned as his lever for a non-jumping Jon (W41).
7. **Window rules**: every book ends at the Tower window with only two men. **Jon never answers or "refuses"**; the reader decides (W50). **Book 1's window has no plea and shows no time travel** (W53); the plea comes in a later return to the window (W54).
