## 2. Prose-craft principles (the craft law)

All 🟡 in 13 (a story file: no 🔒). Grouped as 13 groups them.

### 2a. The numbers are scaffolding, never on the page 🟡 (t0–5, t72)
- Remnant/skill/fatigue mechanics are an engineering tool for adjudicating what's possible; the reader feels only the **result** (one fighter deep/patient, another fast/hot). Never write "her two parts Earth," "my fire 6."
- There are no force/element "types": describe the **act**, name it in flavor after the fact. "Forces as flavor, not law"; no math, no auditing.
- **No character has a "type"** — "just a skilled user" (t612; Patrick stopped Claude calling the dark elf a "gravity" anything; her Collapse is a tribute, not a specialty).
- [v5-wk] **What the act is, in flavor (W01–W06):** Remnant extends an **appendage** that grabs the universe; reach and intensity scale with Remnant, skill, focus (W01). Acts: shaking particles = heat; pulling charges apart = lightning; crushing/ripping the nucleus = explosions; turning a quark = transmutation, the hardest act (botched, it rips the nucleus); denting space = gravity (W02). Three dials: **Remnant** (strength), **control** (skill), **the inner sense** (unexplained perception from Remnant, trainable and innate; the "numb titan" = huge Remnant, poor sense) (W03). Deeper work costs finer sense — "a gradient, not a gate" (W04; phrase is Claude's, L35). Feel is a dial, not a gate (W05). **The grip is mechanized; the sense stays mysterious — never explain it on the page** (W06).
- [v5-wk] **Remnant is never burned or spent (W10).** Taking others' Remnant = "stealing their production"; [v5-wk2] mechanism 🌊 open: the thief driving their grip (Claude's t84 framing, never confirmed) or "using an amplifier to touch a wider pool" (Patrick chat t83: "Im not sure") (W11; L2). **Don't write souls or Remnant as fuel**; where Patrick's drafts say "burns," a bracketed note flags it.
- On the duel: "describe the acts and sensations; never label 'EM,' 'gravity,' or 'strong force' in narration" (l.82).

### 2b. The grimdark register 🟡 (full detail in 09)
Brutality is a **forge**, not decoration: it manufactures the hardness that makes the showpieces parse (the master's indoctrinated self-sacrifice; Jon's coldness). "my work truly fits the label of grimdark" (t658).
- **Cost-and-characterize, never titillate:** does this reveal character/world, or is it just being watched?
- **Contrast is the delivery system:** tenderness is the setup, violence the punchline [RESTORED v5-verify]; guard the ratio; warmth real enough to hurt, not an obvious death-flag.
- **Simultaneity:** a small funny human moment while a war kills millions offstage. The light lives in the protagonists.
- **Nothing left to cheer for (t380):** "They all die… when you watch Jon maybe choose to end everything." Everyone the reader loves dies in the Tower; "no room for redemption" (t354).
- **No corny goodness (t338):** the "good" changeling is still "in every way a murderous hungry user of other people."
- **Emotional blow, not moral verdict (t558):** "Derrek is clearly a murderous tyrannical dictator. There's no real forgiveness."
- **What makes a killing land (t708):** "Everyone in this story has killed… It's the intensity and coldness of the killings. How useless the killing was. How cruel were their acts."
- **Thesis (t466):** "it doesn't matter in the grand scale but everything matters to someone."
- [v5-wk/wk2] **Madness is subtext only (W70):** base layer is the One dissociating, but no in-world metaphysical cause ever visible; nondiscriminate — "good and bad all are mad" (chat t365). The clause "choice still carries the moral weight" was Claude's (chat t360/t366) and removed; Patrick t361: "No you forget the one is subtext." **Write behavior, never clinical labels** (W44, W70; specific disorders per character held).
- [v5-jul] **Levity, dark jokes, more violence (jul08 t24):** humor "can be dark jokes and… punctuated by more violence." The gnome's "inappropriate jokes at the wrong moments" (t762) embodies it.
- [v5-jul] **An intervention is a plot point, not a rescue (jul23 t122–126):** a stronger being may step in for its own reasons — "Its not the narrative saving a character. Its the narrative saving a character as a plot point" (jul23 t126). The god cast was reworked later (t666–674); rule stands.
- [v5-jul] 🌊 **Tone, open:** 40k — "I dont think grimdark is enough… draw heavy inspiration" (jul23 t96); "our grimdark 40k setting" (jul23 t154). Not a register law (NP #83).

### 2c. The prose-cleaning rule 🟡 (t252–255)
- "cleaning means add syntax or grammar or punctuation. Make it easier to read. Not any actual story cleaning or word choice." Punctuation, grammar, syntax, spelling, paragraph breaks; words/sentences/story untouched; **no suggestions unless asked**; touch a word only where grammar strictly requires it. Patrick: "That's what I need."
- [v5-wk2] Every line of prose is Patrick's (chat t257). (NP #97 asks whether his quoted drafts in the codex should be lightly cleaned or raw; 13 currently cleans spelling only.)

### 2d. Drafting a scene 🟡 [v5-jul] (jul08 t223–227) — written for Claude's drafts; scope under L34

- **Traits without exposition** ("without becoming blank exposition or weird ham fisted"); reveals arrive suddenly, not telegraphed (t227).
- **Show, don't state** (the dwarven-districts example, t225).
- **But/therefore and real-world logic** (t225).
- **Stay inside the codex** — "be careful not to add details"; one part at a time (t223). Process notes (build in chat first t237; structure before surface t245) belong in 12.
