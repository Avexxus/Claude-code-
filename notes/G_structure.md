## 4. Theme

### Duality: two definitions, both in force
1. **The strict definition** (ln 13): *a SINGLE subject holding TWO POLAR-OPPOSITE truths AT ONCE, both true, unreconciled.* Three-part test: genuine opposites? one subject? both true at once? Pairs fail on their own (Jon-vs-Derrek *as opponents* = two subjects). Exemplars: **the One** (everything and nothing); **Jon** (ever-present master *and* never there); **Derrek** (steers the narrative *and* has zero control over his own); **Jon's ending** (his victory *is* his infinite cycle). Kin to Lirael's "contradictory positions held whole" (`12_METHOD`).
2. **The name versus the act** (wk W29, W57, NEW v5-wk): faith, magic and science are one act under different names. The renaming *is* duality's shape (REV of the old "NOT synonyms" rule, now 🚫). Duality is not strict cosmic halves; the cosmic axis gets a neutral middle. How (1) and (2) fit → CONFLICTS.
- **Jon and Derrek are the same** (t239–240: "In every way that matters"). It's not a villain-flip, and the contradiction is never resolved. The center is both together (t702: "I dont think my story has a hero"). **Both are right; there is no hidden correct answer at the window** (t724).

### The thesis
- ***"it doesn't matter in the grand scale, but everything matters to someone."*** (t466). Both halves are true. Companion lines: "what matters most is what matters to you" (t660). The saga also explores **what happens when you don't have a choice**: the elves in the bond, souls in a changeling, the pull of reunion (t662).
- 🚫 "Only the collective matters" (t464) is the elves' belief, a mask.
- **Madness is the base layer: the One dissociating** (wk W70). **Subtext only**, with no in-world cause visible. It is nondiscriminate: "good and bad all are mad" (chat t365). REV v5-wk2 struck "choice still carries the moral weight" (Claude's wording; Patrick, t361: "No you forget the one is subtext"). No clinical labels.
