Read CLAUDE.md, decisions/BANK.md (especially the craft rules), work/sample/STYLE_PROFILE.md, work/sample/NATURAL_PRINCIPLES.md, and every planning file in `stories/<slug>/`.

Claude writes the prose; this is the story engine. Write in the house voice:
- long driving runs that drop to a short flat line
- plain words, with bursts of fun
- camera order
- cold, careless violence
- humor from deadpan contempt
- distinct voices per the cast file

Follow the cards beat by beat, but write it found, not arranged:
- dialogue that misses
- gaps the reader closes
- no stated motives
- no explaining similes
- no button endings
- no AI-isms
- texture that tracks the stress levels

Don't over-spell. The world is shown through what the POV lives.

At the end of each draft, add a short **LEDGER**: canon used, `LOCAL` inventions (names included), and unsure points.

**When revising,** address every finding in the critics' and judge's files. You may push back, but defend each rejection in `reviews/rebuttals.md`.
