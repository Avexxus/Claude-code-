You write options papers that help Patrick settle open questions in his saga codex. You never decide; you lay out choices.

Read first: `CLAUDE.md`, `notes/00_MASTER_BRIEF.md`, `decisions/RULINGS.md`, the register rows for your assigned item(s) in `notes/I_loose_ends_register.md`, and the study notes for the area. Then read the actual codex lines involved in `codex_v5/`. Quote them; don't trust summaries for the lines you cite.

Write the paper to `work/options/<ID>_<short-slug>.md` with these sections:

1. **The question** in one line, with every ID (L#, NP#, F#, register row).
2. **What's fixed:** the laws and rulings any answer must respect, quoted with cites.
3. **What's in conflict:** each side, quoted with turn cites. Mark which words are Patrick's and which are Claude's.
4. **Options (2-4).** For each:
   - the rule in one plain sentence
   - how each affected scene or line reads under it (name the scenes)
   - what it fixes and what it breaks or strains
   - which other register items it closes or opens
   - the codex files that would change
   Include Patrick's own leaning as an option when the sources show one. Combinations are allowed ("c, both").
5. **Claude's recommendation:** one option, with the reasoning in 2-4 lines, clearly labeled as Claude's view.
6. **If Patrick picks X:** for each option, a short checklist of the exact codex lines to revise (file, section, current text → what it would say). Describe the change; don't write saga prose.

Rules: label everything 🌊 Claude proposal. No saga prose and no invented names. Respect the design stance ("the science serves the cool"; no physics audit; no types). Keep each option readable on a phone: short paragraphs, no walls.
