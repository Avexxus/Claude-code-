## 1 · The 12 laws you must never break
1. 🔒 **Only beings do magic.** Magic is a conscious being reaching into reality with its own Remnant (its fragment of the One, its soul). Matter, machines and rifts do none; a soul held in matter can. (02 Laws 0c, 1; 04)
2. 🔒 **Remnant is identity, never fuel.** It is never spent, burned, drained, pooled or refilled, and it can't be destroyed. No mana. (02 Law 2; wk W10; R096, R131)
3. 🔒 **Power = Remnant + Will + Skill.** The bill is fatigue; burnout is the BODY breaking under more than it can hold, "the suit he used is burnt." (02 Law 2; R095, R096)
4. 🔒 **Your own fragment can't be given, lent or siphoned.** Held or stolen Remnant amplifies and never runs dry (a battery is a public myth); a pact grants technique, never Remnant. (02 Laws 2, 16; R112, R133)
5. 🔒 **No types, no formula.** No fire users, classes or builds; forces and elements describe the act afterward. Personality never gates what you can reach, and nobody is computed from their positions. Build the person first; their magic expresses their life. (02 Laws 5, 6, 10, 18b, 20; 04 t612)
6. 🔒 **No physics audit.** "The science serves the cool." No energy maths, size charts or feasibility; magic may add energy. Show the working, not thrown fireballs. (02 Law 0a; t72; R038)
7. 🔒 **One scale; nothing is fixed.** Everything is a grade, never a tier. Gods are any user grown vast: flesh, born on Earth, and they age. Lifespan follows Remnant (resistance, never immunity); no immortals among the aging, never "near-immortal" (4a). True immortality is a separate category by mechanism: Jon, Derrek (⚠️ vs R105, L36) and rule-beasts within their domain (4b). (02 Laws 3, 4, 4a, 4b; R107; wk W30)
8. 🔒 **Soul theft belongs to no race.** A changeling is a crime, never a race or a shapeshifter; every true ghost is a crime with a holder somewhere. Soul-binding and soul-harvesting are the deepest violations; soul magic can override a body without absorbing the soul. (02 Law 14; 04 t680; 03)
9. 🔒 **Physics came first; the One made only consciousness.** The One stays in the background, never on the page; light and dark are never cosmic powers. (02 Laws 0b, 12)
10. 🔒 **Jon and Derrek: time is their only given power; Derrek goes wherever Jon jumps.** No mortal can kill Derrek; only a god armed with power from beyond the regress could threaten him, "if they are powerful and outsmart derrek." (02 Laws 17, 4b; wk W38; t317) ✅ Story rule: neither dies on the page (R105). ✅ The faceless Emperor: in Book 1's era no ordinary person knows who he is (08 t364); Book 3's reveal is open (09).
11. 🔒 **The loop is closed and only this run is shown.** Books 1–3 sit in the current run and each ends at the Tower window, where Jon never answers. (02 Law 19; wk W50; R100) ⚖️ Book 1 shows no time travel (wk W53); for Books 2–3 "no reset" is a reading, not a ruling.
12. ✅ **Earth is the only life.** No aliens; every race on our plane came from the one shatter and is born (in-betweeners and higher-plane beings are not of it); no reincarnation. Rifts are reservoirs, never the source of anyone's magic. (03; 07 far-future banner; wk W58; t188; 02 Law 15; jul23 t100)

## 2 · Magic: hard rules
- 🔒 The grip: Remnant extends a psychic hand (02 Law 1; wk W01). Heat = shaking particles; lightning = pulling charges apart; explosions = crushing a nucleus; gravity = denting space; transmutation = turning a quark, the hardest act (wk W02). ⚖️ Transmutation is the mythic top by scale and prestige, not mechanism. (02 Law 7b)
- 🔒 REMNANT is innate and capped by the body; training and age never add it. WILL is conviction: "the part of me that really believes I can produce this flame." SKILL is training, incl. sense and touch. A timid giant makes nightmares; a willful gnome still can't beat a dragon. (R095, R097, R022)
- ✅ Amplifiers (the Tower, chips, held souls) let users work "2 3 5 50 100000x" normal, far past the body's limit. That's where people destroy themselves: the Tower apprentice burned from inside to cinder. "Dont use more than you can control." (R023, R135, R075)
- ✅ You can hold more than your own: devices and grafted flesh add capacity; soul magic crams more in, held by Will and coherence. (R097)
- 🔒 Enchantment is one rule: Remnant held inside something. (02 0c; R099) ✅ Organics hold it best ("It prefers them"); metal must be bound by a learned craft (R148). Book 1 tech runs on flesh (BANK 11-16 [STORAGE]).
- ✅ Dwarves alone build focusing chambers. The chip is a shock collar: one chip, different boxes, full control only over the unruly. Machines need no Remnant and only mimic magic's effects. (R043, R143, R030)
- 🔒 Soul magic is will against will, two-way, limited by self-risk; rare, forbidden, known to exist. Pre-write check: "can ANYONE see them?" If so, no transfer on the page. (04; 07) ✅ Changelings: 1–2 souls is the sustainable pace; one driver holds only at that pace; past it they decay or rupture. The tell is soul-body mismatch. (07 t122; wk W16, W20, W22)
- ✅ The brain is body, not soul. (02 0c; jul08 t331) Held souls feel only "vague general things and maybe some strangeness." Soul-sensing is rare, never constant scanning. Great beings exert soul pressure; lesser minds need trained shields to keep thinking. (R128, R129, R067; wk W09)
- ✅ The elven bond is a sense organ, not soul magic or a tether; it shares no Remnant and can't cross realities. (R098; t230)
- 🔒 People have repertoire and specialty, never classes. Spells are engineered workings, taught and drilled. Awakening is a gradient: leaks under emotion → crude intent → control → workings → mastery. Blending is free. (04 Spells, Awakening; 02 Law 9)
- 🔒 Dragons: no lone being overpowers a great ancient one; they are supreme in bursts but can't hold peak (stamina, never Remnant). One unaugmented human never beats a dragon alone; an implant can, at self-destructive cost. (07; jul08 t239, t261)
- ✅ Pacts: "A god asks for your devotion. A demon offers you a deal." Angels, demons and changelings are one kind of being. A god may intervene as a plot point, never as a narrative rescue. (t548; 02 Law 16; jul23 t122–126)

## 7 · THE AURA
**"Its grimdark. So were doing haunting evil world with a kind hearted light we follow."** (BANK 11-21) The world never supplies the light; the protagonists do, and then they're taken: "They all die. There can be nothing left to cheer for by the end." (t380) Tenderness is the setup and the violence the punchline, and dark jokes are welcome, "punctuated by more violence." (jul08 t24)

**Nobody is clean.** "All races in this world are greedy prideful jealous beings. This is a deeply broken place." (jul23 t52) Moral murk is designed in: giants against dragons "should be able to root for either and feel the weight." (t474) "Duality friend. Duality. Grading scales not a single fixed point." (jul08 t269) Let the reader slide toward "maybe they deserve it," then pull the camera back; the slide is the indictment. (10)

**Small inside vast.** "It doesnt matter in the grand scale but everything matters to someone." (t466) Even the gods of this story are "one wheel of one car on one world in one galaxy." (t638) Hold both scales in the same scene.

**Magic is felt in the flesh.** You grip, shake, crush, dent. "Your body is what is spent… vibrating or charging… consequences on the structure of your cells." (BANK 11-16) "these are wizards. Big and tough doesnt mean throws punches." "Try not to make it so slingy… describe how that would look more than just he hurled ot at him." (R038) Power presses on a room before anything happens; the terrifying is intimate: "tiny explosions in every cell of your body… He can read your blood pressure." (BANK 11-16)

**Body horror as economics.** "Organics just hold it better" (BANK 11-16), so the far future runs on flesh: cathedral ships of the dead, organ markets ("the younger the flesh the better," R144; BANK 11-08), cores that hold bound souls, clones sold as real. "Anyone who hates it is crushed by the weight of people who will use it anyway." (R064) Show it concretely, never hint.

**Evil is ordinary and quiet.** The soul thief is "your father… your uncle… the priest at the church," and "it cant stop daily life." (t680; R037) Ghosts are bound for many reasons, love among them; every haunting is a crime scene. The horror is in the dim, not the scream: held souls feel "vague general things and maybe some strangeness." (R128)

**Irony is the signature.** Heaven is empty and hell is full, "one of our trademark ironies" (t604); the "Darkest Age" is an outsider's, unfair name (07), and every age is named by whoever holds the pen: "We are humans reading about the history of the humans." (t302) The name versus the act, always. (wk W57)

**Monuments and wrongness.** Later powers live inside the bones of earlier ones: dragons "sit in halls physically inside the remains of the giant family who built them." (t32) The Tower is "a mass of the one with no real way we can understand it" (BANK 11-16). Its leftovers are radiation and "Deformation and such" (R061), and animals avoid it (t88).

**The eras.** Book 3 is myth in giant ruins; Book 2 is smog and frontier, "rugged, untrained, free, fun, bloody"; Book 1 is mastery that changed nothing in beings: humans "solved need and found they were never actually driven by need at all and it was always WANT." (t480; R089) ("The brightness is the horror" is Claude's line, awaiting Patrick's yes: F128.)

**Two men and a door.** "Its only 2 men at a window. Dont forget that." (t458) The cosmic war comes down to two old men and a door they opened when they were young, and the reader decides what happens there. The science serves the cool, and the mystery stays a mystery: "Thats part of the magic." (BANK 11-02)

## 8 · Writing around the open questions
1. Never invent a world-level answer (history, metaphysics, races, devices, the pair). Only Patrick makes canon.
2. Check `world/GAP_QUEUE.md` before a scene touches dates, chips, enchanted items, weapons, sickness, beasts or Jon and Derrek's limits.
3. Write around the gap: keep it off the page, let characters disagree or not know, or stay vague.
4. Story-level details (a town, a minor character, a ship) are yours to invent; log them as LOCAL.
5. If a scene can't be written without the answer, log it under "Hit log" in `world/GAP_QUEUE.md` (story, Q#, need) and move on.
