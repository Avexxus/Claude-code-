# DWARVES: shape sheet
🌊 Not canon until a pass. **SHAPE** = Patrick's (BANK 11-27, dwarves round 1). **DETAIL** = Claude's derivations (🌊). Core canon: `world/ref/beings_world.md` · DWARVES.

## SHAPE (Patrick)
- **Family firms are the government.** Rival enterprises, each a family business, together run an oligarchy. They fight each other and close ranks against outsiders. How many emperors there are is a detail for later.
- **Wealth is rank.** Craft, business-building and ruthless management all count because they lead to wealth. Blood matters inside the family tree. Across the race, money decides. The rich "fund society," so they lead. Generosity always comes with an invoice.
- **The bottom:** the weak are still sold and some dwarves are enslaved. Laborers on deep colonies and asteroid mines who aren't owners or foremen are the lower class. A middle class exists.
- **Worlds:** busy, cramped, rich and **plain**. Efficient and unflashy: cheap steel, not the precious metals they mine. Sealed mines are only for extreme worlds. A minority build megalithic estates packed with magic and tech as quiet shows of power.
- **No pods.** Dwarves refuse, and the AI doesn't force them. Dwarves, humans and the AI are partners with no competing interests.
- **Faith is real, quiet and wide.** Gods are real, with witnessed feats. Dwarves worship the ledger, a few dwarf gods, and borrowed human, giant or dragon gods.
- **What they want: the work itself.** Wealth to fund more machines, labs and compute. Love of hard problems and efficiency, "dragons in miniature." Above all, tech that does what magic does without the cost to the body, because the humans are closing the gap.

## DETAIL (Claude, derived) 🌊
- **The firm is the clan.** Cousins as officers, marriages as mergers. Disowning someone is a firing, and a hostile takeover can be a family feud that lasts a century. Selling your weak is just cutting losses.
- **The invoice culture.** Gifts, favors and rescues are all entered in a ledger. "Free" is a dwarven word for "later." Story engine: an outsider who doesn't understand that they've been accumulating debt.
- **Plain as a virtue.** Showing off reads as waste. A dwarf's respect goes to the person whose operation is leaner. The megalithic estate is the exception: it says "I'm so rich, efficiency no longer binds me," and other dwarves admire it and think it slightly vulgar.
- **The asteroid-mine class.** Contract labor, company towns, debt that rolls over. A miner's way up is a new technique or patent that a firm will buy, so the ladder runs through invention.
- **The quiet ledger-faith.** Accounts balanced as a rite, a quiet word before a deal. Wealth read as a sign of favor. The borrowed gods are worshipped like contracts: offerings for results.
- **Magic-tech as the race's purpose.** Dwarves sit between the elder races' raw power and the humans' rising numbers, and their answer is engineering. Every dwarven lab is chasing the device that lets a dwarf do what a dragon does with a thought.
