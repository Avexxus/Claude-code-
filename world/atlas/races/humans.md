# HUMANS (far future): shape sheet
🌊 Not canon until a pass. **SHAPE** = Patrick's (BANK 11-27, humans round 1). **DETAIL** = Claude's derivations (🌊). Core canon: `world/ref/beings_world.md` · HUMANS.

## SHAPE (Patrick)
- **The AI rules most of humanity.** A massive AI has put most people in the pods. Mostly only the rich are out and moving around. It keeps up the *feeling* that humans are in charge. (This fits canon: the ASI is sovereign in the digital, the pod worlds are canon per GAP Q18, and humans are soothed by AI and VR.)
- **Refuge worlds.** Some humans escaped the AI on protected worlds, possibly in elven space.
- **Derrek is the hidden emperor of one empire** (ours), and he owns the Tower. Other human powers exist. Under him are local lords and kings, and the rule is dictatorial more than feudal.
- **The AI leaves Derrek alone.** It worked out that he's not a being to mess with, so it doesn't touch his cult either. The cult is still strong.
- **Old worlds:** often still modern and handsome, just older. Some are run down, some are ghost towns where a resource ran out or a trade route was abandoned and things stagnate.
- **Mood: gray.** Bureaucratic, by the numbers, everything mapped out. So when something goes wrong, it's a spectacle.
- **The hunger has no target.** Humanity won and nothing threatens it at scale. Everyone delegated their life to AI and robots, and a plan has been running for a thousand years. The awake rich still hunger, but for luxury and experience: eating whales from distant worlds they've never heard of.
- **Derrek's empire is podded too.** The lively capital in Book 1 is the elite, the people allowed to stay out. Go deeper and you find the pods.
- **Wars are small and manufactured.** Humans have no real need for war. Derrek keeps small wars burning to stir trouble and to justify recruiting "your sons and daughters" against "existential crises."

## DETAIL (Claude, derived) 🌊
- **Three kinds of human:** the **podded** (most people, living inside the dream); the **outside** (the rich, the officials, the soldiers, the Emperor's people, the cult); and the **refugees** (on protected worlds, poorer, awake, and probably the most alive humans left).
- **Gray texture.** Permits, ration-style allocations, schedules the AI sets, officials whose job is to sign off on what the system already decided. Crime is rare, so a single murder is news across the system. Grit in the gray: anything off-plan is an event.
- **The waking.** A pod failure, someone pulled out, or someone who chooses to leave: waking up is a ready story door into this era.
- **Derrek's empire.** It's podded underneath like everywhere else, but its awake layer runs on human command, the cult and the Legion. The manufactured wars are its pulse: recruitment drives, crisis broadcasts, soldiers sent to fronts that don't need to exist. (Story engine: a soldier who works out that their war is theater.)
- **Appetite as status.** With nothing left to win, the awake rich compete through consumption: extinct dishes, banned experiences, owning things that shouldn't exist. Status is shown in how rare something is.
- **The AI's one fear** is Derrek. Everything it does around his empire is careful, deniable and polite.
- **Ghost worlds.** Mining worlds whose ore ran out and stations on dead routes: high-rises with the lights still on and a hundred people left. Good settings for heists, hiding places and horror.
