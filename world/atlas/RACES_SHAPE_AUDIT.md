# Race shape audit
🌊 Claude, 2026-11-27. Not canon. Built from `world/ref/beings_world.md`, `world/KERNEL.md` §3, `world/GAP_QUEUE.md` and BANK 11-27.

**Purpose:** for each race, show what we already have at the **shape** level and what's still missing. Patrick fills the shape (texture, flavor, structure, motive). Claude derives the detail (food, dress, slang, fleet colors) from it. These are open questions for Patrick to riff on, not menus.

**What every race needs before Claude can make "two different dragons":**
1. **Core**: hunger, mask, clock, what it's woven into (mostly done)
2. **Internal factions**: the rival powers inside the race
3. **Class ladder**: who's at the top, who's at the bottom (their "slum"), and how you climb or fall
4. **Government**: who rules, and how
5. **Worlds**: what kind of planet suits their body, where they live in each era, and what it feels like there
6. **Wants now**: what the race as a whole is reaching for in each era

## DRAGONS: shape questions (round 1)
Have: scholars, cities and universities; houses vs dispossessed; hoards as status; they design and others build; radiation-tolerant; eggs and families; the empire has had no peace since the coup.

1. **Who rules dragons?** One dragon at the top, a council of great houses, the universities, or no one (each house a state)? Does it change across the eras?
2. **The ladder.** What makes a dragon "great born" vs "slum born"? Is it bloodline, the house, Remnant, breath, hoard, or scholarship? What's the dragon slum actually like?
3. **The factions inside.** Beyond houses vs dispossessed: what are the big rival camps in the far future? (Old palace-dwellers vs the young dragons in human commerce and law is one hint.)
4. **Their worlds.** What does a dragon world *feel* like? Where do they live in the far future, and how do they relate to those worlds?
5. **What do dragons want now?** In the far future, with humans winning by numbers: are they declining, adapting, plotting, or in denial?
6. **The lesser kin.** The wingless two-legged kin and the reptilian peoples they erased: do they still exist as a people, or only as servants?
