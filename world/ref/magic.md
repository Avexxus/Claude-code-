## A. HARD RULES

### The stance (any magic scene)
- 🔒 Scene first, science-flavor after. "The science serves the cool." When science makes a scene less cool or adds homework, science loses. (02 Law 0a)
- 🔒 No physics audit, ever: no energy budgets or ledgers, no "needs fusion, not weak", no feasibility math, no stat tables, size charts or square-cube reasoning. "We agreed no math." (02 Laws 0a, 2; t72)
- 🔒 Rigor means internal consistency only: never contradict our own canon; reality's math doesn't matter. (02 Law 0a)
- 🔒 Magic may ADD energy; conservation isn't enforced; steer-only is dead. A weak being can't crack a planet because they lack the Remnant and it would kill them, not because math forbids it. (02 Law 2; 04)
- 🔒 The four forces and four elements are words for describing an act after it happens. Watch the act, then name it. (02 Laws 5, 6; 01 FORCE)
- 🔒 Science, in any era, can explain WHAT mages do; it can never explain the CAN. (02 Law 1)
- ✅ Magic and science are one word in-world; faith, magic and science are one act under different names. Humans say "science" because dragons were their first teachers. (04 wk W57, W63; 02 Law 1)
- ✅ Real physics is an optional palette for describing effects (heat, magnetism, light-bending, nerve disruption, binding, transmutation), never for ruling on them. (14; PROPERTY_INVENTORY)

### What magic is
- 🔒 Magic = a conscious being reaching into reality with its own Remnant and making it do what it wants. One undivided act; fire, gravity, a shove are descriptions applied afterward, never kinds of magic. (04 What magic IS; 02 Law 1)
- 🔒 Remnant = the chunk of the One you ARE: soul, capacity, "a measure of how much of the one is held together". Soul = Remnant. (R021; 04 wk W07)
- 🔒 Remnant is identity, never fuel: never a pool, never "mana", never spent per cast, never burned. "We cant burn remnant." (02 Law 2; 04 wk W10; R131)
- 🔒 Remnant cannot be destroyed; when the body can't hold it, it releases back into the environment. (R024, R096)
- ✅ Remnant is ageless; only flesh ages. (04 t678; 02 4a)
- 🔒 "Spirit" = actively using your Remnant: one undivided system; soul magic is its rare branch. (04; 02 Law 18)
- 🔒 Only beings do magic. Matter does none. A soul held in matter can. (02 Laws 0c, 1)
- ✅ A soulless machine does no magic; it can only mimic effects mechanically (an EM-field generator throws what looks like EM magic), described in the same words, never the same act. (02 Law 1; jul08 t339)
- ✅ An ensouled machine is a held soul; with receptors that soul can work magic. Simple machines run on electricity and need no Remnant; complex machines need flesh with Remnant held inside. (R130, R030)

### The grip (the appendage)
- 🔒 Remnant extends an appendage, a psychic hand, that grabs the universe (02 Law 1). Length, size, shape and intensity scale with Remnant, Skill and focus. Fine work with it is tiring. (04 wk W01; 01 APPENDAGE; R036)
- ✅ Grip vocabulary (flavor, no math): shaking particles = heat; pulling charges apart = lightning; crushing or ripping a nucleus = explosions; denting space = gravity; turning a quark = transmutation, THE HARDEST ACT; a botched transmutation rips the nucleus. (04 wk W02; 02 7b)
- ⚖️ Transmutation, gold-making and the worst releases are the mythic top: rare arts of the mightiest, never a class. "Transmutation transmutes, full stop." Strong-force-flavored feats appear only at the very top. (02 Law 7b; 04 t136)
- ✅ Dragons stay four-legged; the appendage is their hands, and building or holding tools with it is tiresome, so dragons design and others build. (04 wk W63; R036, R090)

### The three dials (the only gates)
- 🔒 Power = Remnant (how much) + Will (how much you mean it) + Skill (how well, incl. sense and touch). Plus the bill: fatigue, and burnout past it. Nothing else gates magic. (02 Law 2; R095, R096)
- 🔒 REMNANT: innate, capped by the body's physical capacity. Training and age never add Remnant: "unlike some games you dont gain mana over time". (R097, R022)
- 🔒 WILL: conviction, moral weight, wanting it. "The part of me that really believes I can produce this flame." (R095)
- 🔒 SKILL: training. "How many times have yiu sat down and tried to find the nucleus of an atom… How many boulders have you lifted." Covers sensing and touch. Two beings of equal Remnant produce utterly different results. (R095; 04)
- 🔒 A timid, untrained giant throws nightmare fireballs he's too scared to make obey; a trained, willful gnome still can't beat a dragon in raw power. (02 Law 2; R095)
- ✅ Basic magic needs only control; deeper workings need finer sense. That makes deep work costlier for the dull-sensed, never forbidden (the "numb titan": huge Remnant, poor sense). (04 wk W03–W04)
- 🔒 Personality shapes how someone learns and how their magic looks, never what they can reach. (02 Law 10)

### Costs, fatigue, burnout
- 🔒 The everyday bill is fatigue: a mage tires like a laborer or a grandmaster. Exhaustion is the universal soft limit. (02 Law 2)
- 🔒 Burnout = the BODY breaks when made to carry more than it can hold, usually through amplifiers. Magic vibrates and charges the matter of your own cells; overdo it "and you literally destroy your own body". The Remnant is fine and releases. "Imagine god channeling his magic and the suit he used is burnt." (R096; BANK 11-16 [REMNANT · never spent])
- ✅ Bodies normally hold about what their owner can control. Hooking up to external Remnant (Tower, chip, held souls) lets a user work at "2 3 5 50 100000x" normal ability, far past the body's breaking point. That's where people destroy themselves. (R023; BANK 11-16 [THE MASTER])
- ✅ Canon burnout death: the Tower apprentice, amplified from the Tower's soul-mass, burst into fire from inside and went to cinder. The master warned chipped students: "Dont use more than you can control." (R135, R075; 05)

### Capacity: holding more than yourself
- 🔒 Your own fragment can't be given, lent, shared or siphoned. Only knowledge or technique passes between beings. (02 Law 2; jul23 t108, t114)
- ✅ You CAN hold more than your own: devices and grafted flesh add capacity; soul magic crams extra Remnant inside you, kept together only by your Will and your own coherence. (R097)
- ✅ Held Remnant is an AMPLIFIER, never a battery: it boosts your own source, or the held soul is forced to do the work. Nothing drains; an item never runs dry. The public believes it's a battery; that's their myth. (R112, R035, R133, R034)
- ✅ Taking another's Remnant = "stealing their production", worked as an amplifier. (R132; 04 wk W11)
- ✅ Giants, the largest living bodies, have the most capacity; strong users naturally have bodies that can hold what they control. (R023)
- ✅ Loose Remnant drifts everywhere and gathers in latent pools mined "like oil reserves"; rifts are the richest sites (see `cosmos.md` · rifts). Reservoirs, never the source of anyone's magic. (R025, R014; 04)
- ✅ A soul cannot be sold and is not a currency (economy: `beings_world.md`). (R033)

### Enchantment, flesh vs metal, devices
- 🔒 Enchantment is one rule: Remnant held inside something, in many uses and forms. Imbued gear, living metal, chips, the Tower: "It is all the same thing." Reservoirs, attunement objects and bound effects are uses, not kinds. (R099, R074; 06 ONE RULE; 02 0c)
- ✅ Organics hold Remnant better: "It prefers them. So itll stay in it." Metal must be bound by a learned magic. That's why Book 1-era tech uses flesh. (R148; BANK 11-16 [STORAGE])
- ✅ Body-like substrate holds best; younger flesh is higher quality; another elf is very high-quality material. (06 wk W12; R028)
- ✅ Flesh augments keep the hold on your Remnant: "They make your augmentation from flesh so it retains ability to hold your remnant." (R032)
- ✅ Grafted flesh adds capacity. The Orc King: grafted, grotesque body (mismatched skin, bone pushing out, metal plates), ONE chip only, so he holds more. Body horror, never identity. (BANK 11-18; R097)
- ✅ Focusing chambers channel and amplify; only dwarves can build them (a monopoly); dragons buy them. A captured soul can be bound into a core, never drained. (R043, R134)
- ✅ Implant form: a human-sized chamber implant lets a user "project their entire force into a single powerful attack"; the max one-shot is self-destructive. Other uses: longer draws, strength and durability, cognitive speed, magnetic fields against EM attacks. Strength rises only relative to the user's own baseline. (06 jul08 t259–265)
- ✅ The chip is a shock collar: one universal chip sold in different boxes for different classes; full control only when the wearer is unruly; can control anyone chipped. Not full mind control. Derrek led its creation as a mandate. (R143, R017)
- ✅ The bond-spoof chip is a miniaturized focusing chamber, made by dwarves, perfected by dissecting live and dead elves. A key, not a teacher: it works only for someone already capable of soul magic, and lets them pass as an elf or break a bond-protected elf. (06 jul23 t151–156)
- ✅ Cathedral ships are built by giants (via Convergence) from dead flesh: less Remnant, but they hold a lot. (R027, R045)
- ⏳ **Flesh metal** is Patrick's name for Remnant-infused organic material: "they trap the soul within the metal and flesh is the best material to hokd remnant so they make flesh metal which has the properties of both." (chat 11-12 scifi P92–P93) pending pass
- ✅ Dwarves made the Book-3-era masterwork gear (magic swords, enchanted necklaces). Enchantment is a learnable craft; a master enchanter need not be a strong user. (BANK 11-16 [DWARVES]; 06)
- ✅ Drones can't feel souls in general; only specific spoofing or reading chips can. (R031)

### Soul magic
- 🔒 Soul magic = Spirit turned on another fragment of the One (mind, memory, emotion, will, soul). Same Remnant, different rules: will against will, coherence against coherence, no forces or elements. (04 Soul magic; 02 Law 18)
- 🔒 It is two-way: what you grip grips back. The limit is self-risk, not fatigue: you bleed the other's nature into yours, fracture, lose pieces of self. (04)
- 🔒 Rare, forbidden, scarcely known; belongs to no race. Everyone knows souls can be stolen, which makes it tempting and taboo. (04 t484, t544, t680)
- 🔒 Soul-binding (interrupting a soul's natural death) and soul-harvesting (absorbing another fragment) are the deepest violations; harvesting fragments the thief. (02 Law 14)
- ✅ It can override a living soul and take the body. (04 jul08 t329)
- ✅ Two roads of soul theft: TRANSFER/MOVE (force your soul into another, often younger, body, leaving a corpse and taking all the souls) or CONSUME (take souls into your own body). A crime anyone can commit: "Its your father its your uncle its the priest at the church." (04 t676, t680; t120)
- ✅ All changelings are incredibly powerful from the start ("OFF RIP"); no weak ones. (04 t544–545)
- ✅ One driver holds only at a sustainable pace of 1–2 souls at a time (the guardrail itself is 🔒 in 07). Past that, failure: the giant's decay into a house of horrors, the young dark elf's rupture. The tell is soul-body mismatch; smart ones shield it or hop bodies. (t122; 04 wk W16, W18, W20, W22)
- ✅ A healthy elder can't be taken; a broken, dying one can be scavenged. Gods are untouchable. Elves can't be infiltrated from outside. (t113, t543)
- ⚖️ Wholesale absorption: a weaker soul loses coherence in the swirl of a far greater one. Needs at least changeling-level power; a god-scale being can do it to many at once. Jon and Derrek can. (04 jul23 t116–120; 02 Law 14)
- ✅ Binding a ghost is constant concentration: the holder pays every day. Kill the holder and the binding lapses; the soul is released, never destroyed. (04 t504–505; 03; R096)
- ✅ Echo, ghost, changeling are three different things. Echo = impression of loose Remnant left by great death, no one there, fading over centuries. Ghost = Remnant bound together by magic: every true ghost is a crime, every haunting a crime scene, a holder somewhere. Changeling = many souls in one living driver. (04 t498–502; 03)
- ✅ An ancient ghost needs a warden: a second soul bound into an object to hold the first. Two victims; breaking the object frees both. Mechanism deliberately hand-wavey. (04 t506–508)
- ✅ The vessel varies (own body, amulet, armor); necromancy = the holding + the vessel. (04 t510–513)
- ✅ A held soul feels only vague, general things, maybe some strangeness. "Sensation comes from sensory organs." Dread is flavor, not the rule. (R128)
- ✅ Not every walking corpse holds a soul: the Orc King's dead are empty animated flesh. (04 t522)
- ✅ Resurrection possible in principle, odds near zero; no one can trace a dead soul's scattered Remnant: "vague specs of remnant long since swirled back into the world." (R122; 03)
- ✅ Pressure fields: a powerful being exerting itself presses on everyone near; to keep conscious thought you need trained shields against types of pressure ("soul pressure"). Physical immunity is a continuous psychic force barrier needing massive Remnant and control. (R067)
- ✅ Top-tier fine control reaches body chemistry: tiny explosions in every cell, reading hormones, pheromones and blood pressure as mood, nudging them. Defenses resist it. (R066)
- ✅ The elven bond is an extrasensory organ, NOT soul magic, not a tether, shares no Remnant. Reach scales with Remnant and distance: one far elf = a pinprick, the homeworld = a beacon, up close they feel each other plainly. Elven crews coordinate; they don't pool. (R098)
- ✅ Sensing scales with the source: powerful users feel the Tower "like the sun"; the mildly psychic feel ghosts; sensing one being's Remnant is a rare master's skill. (R129; 04 wk W07)
- 🚫 Never soul-sensing as constant scanning or detection ("video gamey"). (04 wk W09)

### Convergence
- ✅ Convergence ("when soul and matter become one") works on bodies. The funerary rite releases the soul; grown towers and hulls are Convergence. The emperor's version used his city's bodies as material and trapped their Remnant in the stone (the Tower: `cosmos.md`). (04 wk W15; 01; R029)
- ✅ Soul mortar: the lost secret of god-forging Convergence, a crowd of souls silenced into unison; lost before the dragon coup. (wk W17, t377)

### External powers: pacts, angels, demons
- 🔒 Two umbrellas plus the Rule: PACTS (any deal with a being), ENCHANTMENTS (held Remnant), THE RULE (in-betweeners: `cosmos.md`). Thin frames, specifics built per scene. (06)
- 🔒 A pact grants TECHNIQUE, never Remnant. The holder pays fatigue from their own limited Remnant. "A pact doesn't make you strong; it makes you unrepeatable" (Claude's line, accepted). (06 jul23 t108–109; 02 Law 16)
- ✅ Each patron's technique is its own signature. Taught abilities remain as the link fades; the tether fades on the holder's side, never the patron's: "You can stop attuning. You can't stop being findable" (Claude's line, accepted). (06 jul23 t106, t114–115; 02 Law 16)
- ✅ "A god asks for your devotion. A demon offers you a deal." A tendency, not a formula. A pact is a harvest reversed; for gods the tether feeds the god; a religion is a recruitment network of pact champions. (06 t548, t666; wk W32, W57)
- 🔒 Angels and demons are one kind of being, the same as changelings: superior soul mages. Good and evil aren't the line between them. Changeling → angel/demon → god are blurred regions; no graduation. (06 t666–674; 02 Law 16)
- ✅ A patron strong enough can puppet a lesser being's will; on the page that's a plot point, never framed as an earned rescue. (06 jul23 t122–126)
- ✅ The Returned's powers (phasing, decoherence, compression) are new Remnant magic learned from the maze, not stolen Rule-magic. (06 t148)

### Who people are, and how magic expresses
- 🔒 No types: no fire users, gravity users, EM mages, classes, builds, loadouts. The label names the act, never the person. "As with all our characters she is just a skilled user." (04 t612, t136; 02 Laws 5, 6)
- 🔒 Build the person first (wounds, teachers, history); magic is expression of their life, assigned last. No derivation either way (not race→element, not temper→element). The same effect can mean opposite things in two people. (04 Magic is EXPRESSION; 02 Law 18b)
- 🔒 Blending is free: fire + gravity + spin = a fire-gravity tornado, no permission check. Forces are wielded side by side and never fuse into a fifth force; mastery = how much of that space you can weave. (04; 02 Law 9)
- 🔒 People still have repertoire, inclination and specialty, just not classes. Spells = reproducible, engineered workings, taught and drilled, never slotted. (04 Spells)
- 🔒 Awakening is a gradient: involuntary leaks (loudest under emotion) → crude intent → control → engineered workings → mastery. (04 Awakening)
- ✅ Every character develops their own powers; gods take this to the ultimate expression with the same Remnant magic. (04 jul23 t106)

### Power scale
- 🔒 One continuous scale (Remnant, Will, Skill × time). "Demigod" and "god" are points on it, not tiers; everything is a grade on a scale. Gods: `cosmos.md`. (04; 02 Laws 3–4; R107)
- ✅ Lifespan follows Remnant (resistance, never immunity); the ladder and each race's place on the three dials: `beings_world.md`. (02 Law 4a; R093, R068–R070, R149)
- ✅ Reference points: a timid untrained giant's uncontrollable fireballs terrify other races. (R095)
- ✅ The dragon king's breath: nuclear fuel made with magic, "a literal nuke fired out of a dragons mouth", that destroyed the giant capital; its radiation poisoned the land for hundreds of years. (BANK 11-16 [BETRAYAL DAY]; R061)
- ✅ The master, near the top of lesser beings: amplified by the Tower, he held his cohesion with his whole body to press a single point until it became a black hole. (BANK 11-16 [THE MASTER])
- ✅ Derrek: microscopic clarity, cell-level explosions, chemistry reading; pours out soul pressure (his full profile: `characters_eras.md`). (R066; BANK 11-16 [DERREK · unleashed])
- 🔒 Time is off-map: Jon's and Derrek's time powers came from the Unified One; it is not a learnable force. (R109; PROPERTY #70; 02 Law 17)

### On the page
- ✅ Wizards, not brawlers: "Big and tough doesnt mean throws punches." "Try not to make it so slingy… describe how that would look more than just he hurled ot at him." Show the working (compressing matter, ripping space), never thrown fireballs. (R038)

- ✅ Don't write souls or Remnant as fuel; overreach is shown in the body. (13 wk W10; R096)

## B. FLAVOR / AURA

**The core feeling**
- Magic is a physical act, the body reaching into matter: you grip, shake, pull, crush, dent. Felt in the flesh, never cast from a menu. "Your body is what is spent. As you harness magic you are doing things like vibrating or charging. This would have consequences on the structure of your cells" (BANK 11-16).
- Power isn't spent, it's carried, and the danger is the body that carries it. The signature image is a scorched suit: "Imagine god channeling his magic and the suit he used is burnt" (R096). The body is a garment the soul can scorch; overreach ends in flesh rupturing or burning from inside, never in an empty gauge.
- Science and wonder are one thing. A reader who knows physics recognizes nuclei and charges under the folk words, but nobody does the math: "we agreed no math" (t72). Words like quark, field, decoherence and resonance are poetry, never homework; folk eras call the same act fire or air (02 Laws 6–7).
- Grimdark economics: Remnant is "stored into an organic or inorganic form. Organics just hold it better" (BANK 11-16). So the far future runs on flesh: grown augments, cores holding bound souls, cathedral ships of dead flesh, clones sold as real. "Anyone who hates it is crushed by the weight of people who will use it anyway" (R064). Horror at industrial scale, shown concretely, never hinted at (BANK 11-08 grimdark standard).

**How it looks in a fight**
- "These are wizards. Big and tough doesnt mean throws punches." "He is compressing matter ripping space sure but describe how that would look" (R038). Show space bending, matter compacting, light going wrong, heat shimmer, charge. No thrown balls of fire.
- Pressure as atmosphere: great beings press on a room and lesser minds struggle to keep thinking; power presses on the air before anything happens. Patrick's touchstones: "haki" and HxH-style shields to "certain types of pressure" (BANK 11-16). "He continues to pour out his soul pressure… He really turns up the pressure" (BANK 11-16 [DERREK · unleashed]). The power is his own, with no stolen souls inside him: "derrek doesnt allow people into him" (chat 11-13 PHY P373–P375, CATCHUP §4 [P]) ⏳ pending pass.
- The terrifying is intimate as well as huge: "tiny explosions in every cell of your body… read each hormone or feremone… He can read your blood pressure" (BANK 11-16).
- Top-end imagery: a black hole pressed into being by a man holding himself together with "all of his body" (BANK 11-16 [THE MASTER]); "a literal nuke fired out of a dragons mouth" (BANK 11-16); holding matter in a gravity well while igniting a star at its heart (MSE §10); the Tower felt "like the sun blazing in the center of our solar system" (BANK 11-16).
- Joy in power, for the right monster: "Derrek loves this. He is having the most fun he has ever had… marvels… about the sheer beauty of the destruction he has sown" (BANK 11-16).

**Soul magic's register**
- Quiet, domestic and wrong. The changeling is "your father… your uncle… the priest at the church" (t680); the village healer "sucked out their soul and lied" (R145). The threat is "so obscure and also inevitable and noone protects against it… it cant stop daily life" (R037).
- Love as crime: ghosts bound "out of love"; a son forged into an amulet to keep his dead father cohered because the mother couldn't let go (04 t510); a brother whose death drops both brothers at once (04 t504). Every haunting is a crime scene.
- Held souls aren't screaming. They feel "vague general things and maybe some strangeness", because "sensation comes from sensory organs" (R128). A brainless body still holds its person, "locked inside as a vague state. They feel whats happening" (t341), which Patrick took as "the beautifully grimdark interpretation" (t343). Near the Tower the mildly psychic catch "vague otherworldly feelings they can't explain" from voices with "no brain or mouth to speak" (BANK 11-16 [CAPITAL · wrongness]). Eerie, not loud: the horror is in the dim, not the scream.
- Soul-sensing stays rare and unreliable, never "video gamey" (wk W09).

**Taste markers (what makes it Patrick's)**
- No classes, ever. "As with all our characters she is just a skilled user" (t612). "Hes just a master" (t136). A label strips the person of the mastery and dedication it took (04 t136, codex wording).
- The dials are emotional, not only mechanical. Will is "the part of me that really believes I can produce this flame" (R095). Cowardice limits a giant; conviction lifts a gnome, and that's still not enough to beat a dragon.
- Races differ in *how* they wield, not in what they're allowed: brute giants, scholarly dragons with "deep schools of magic" (R068), human swarms and chips, dwarves who "built systems for building tools that used remnant" (BANK 11-16).
- Pacts are teachers, not batteries: "A user in book 1 using a gods remnant would be undefeatable. A user using a gods power with their own limited remnant keeps them grounded" (jul23 t108). Patrons appear in the ether as a meditating figure to those who attune (jul23 t112; he used a real-world religious name for the image: keep it off the page. inferred).
- Comps he names: Wheel of Time (one Power, individual strengths, MSE §7); One Piece and HxH for pressure (BANK 11-16); Warhammer 40k for tone, "draw heavy inspiration" without copying (13 jul23 t96; 🌊 not a register law).
- The flesh-weapon instinct: "Yea I like flesh weapons. Lots of fun there" (BANK 11-01; the weapons question is open: GAP_QUEUE).
- Body horror with power behind it: the Orc King as "a more grotesque shape. Flesh and bone sticking out. Metal patches. Grafted skin not the same color" (BANK 11-18). Power you can see bolted on.
- Recurring contrasts: identity vs fuel; flesh holds vs metal must be trapped; huge-and-clumsy vs small-and-trained; the universe-scale working vs the cell-scale one; a god's power in a body that burns like a suit.
- Restraint as wisdom: the master teaches Eli "to listen and be slow" because he watched chipped students destroy themselves (BANK 11-16). In his last stand, caution is what he finally lets go of. (inferred: the drama of magic lives in the decision to exceed the body)
