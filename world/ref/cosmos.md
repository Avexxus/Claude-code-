## A. HARD RULES

### The bedrock: two domains
- 🔒 Physics (matter, energy, the four forces) existed before the One and has no author. The One created consciousness only, never matter. (02 Law 0b; 03 The One)
- 🔒 The One lives inside physics and obeys it. Even the divine is inside the physics. (03)
- 🔒 Physics and consciousness touch at exactly one point: magic. (02 Law 0b)
- 🔒 Between planes, nesting by scale is simply real, "part of the magic", and is never explained. Inside a plane, the 0b wall holds. Both images are allowed as flavor: a universe inside every iota of Remnant, and our universe as "1 tiny particle in something much larger." (02 0b; R101)
- 🔒 Only beings descend from the One. A being = a physical body + a fragment of the One; a rock holds no fragment. (02 Law 0c)
- 🔒 The One can be described *like* a quantum field (a soul as a resonance on it), but it's a separate, non-physical field, never a bridge to physics. (02 0b; 03)
- ✅ The brain is body, not soul. A destroyed brain doesn't free the fragment; the fragment leaves only at death, an event. A brainless living body still holds its person, "locked inside as a vague state." (02 0c; 03; jul08 t331–343)

### Birth, death, the shatter
- 🔒 All races coalesced in the one shatter; none were made later. Long-lived races stand a few lifetimes from the origin, short-lived ones thousands of generations away. The "generations" are fragment size, not creation order. (03 One shatter; 07 Creation generations)
- ✅ New beings are born when diffuse Remnant gathers into the child at conception. No reincarnation: no character is secretly a past self. (03 How new beings are born; 07)
- ✅ When a body dies, its Remnant releases back into the world (soul-binding, ghosts, resurrection odds: `magic.md`). (R024)
- ⚖️ Our universe is dead beyond Earth: no native life anywhere else, ever. Far-future races settle empty worlds; Earth is everyone's homeworld. No aliens. (03 DEAD UNIVERSE; wk W58; 07 far-future banner)

### Gods and immortals
- 🔒 No god/mortal divide. A god is any user grown vast, the same kind of being as everyone else; a weak god still dwarfs lesser beings. (02 Law 4; t668)
- ✅ Any being of any size can become a god with enough time and practice; theft and the keeper's pact are only shortcuts. (R107)
- ✅ All gods are born of flesh, on Earth. Gods age; great gods seem eternal but aren't. (wk W30; 02 4a)
- ✅ Usage: "gods are mortals." Don't use "mortal" as the opposite of "god"; say "lesser being." (02 Law 4; jul23 t118)
- ✅ "God" = any user grown vast; "singular" = a god in the full sense (an ascended changeling isn't one). (01)
- 🔒 The only true immortals are the rule-beasts (inside their own domain they "cannot die, but can feel") and Jon and Derrek, deathless "by mechanism, not magnitude." ⚠️ Jon/Derrek's half is contested by R105 (bodies can be destroyed; the story never lets them die): see `characters_eras.md` · fate and GAP_QUEUE. (02 Law 4b; R105)
- ✅ Gods appear at a set presence level (front and center, on stage, through an avatar, by rumor, in shadow). A god may step in as a plot point, never as the narrative saving someone. (wk W34; 09 jul23 t122–126)

### The regress and the level above
- 🔒 The Law of the Regress: every plane is a being that came apart so far it forgot it was one and became a new plane, and some part always clings together. Same story at every level, infinite and beginningless. (02 Law 11; t172)
- 🔒 Our One came apart completely and can't pull back together. The pieces that held on are the **Unified Ones**. The pieces that let go became their own Ones: the **sibling worlds**, siblings, not copies. (02 Law 11; 03)
- ✅ Every world calls its own One "the One," "much like we say the sun." (03; t192)
- 🔒 The level above is a varied population (predatory, benevolent, indifferent), made of the same Remnant, only larger and older. (02 Law 13; jul23 t100)
- 🔒 The Unified Ones hold together and built the barrier (the bubble, the shield) that keeps our level safe. (02 Law 13)
- ✅ **The Blight** is a placeholder name for the enemies of our reality, those who want to consume or destroy it; never a single maw of hunger. (R102; 03)
- ✅ A Unified One that decoheres becomes a whole reality; one is strong enough to handle a kin's fragments. (R072)
- 🔒 The One stays in the background, never followed on the page. It shows up only as texture: a raving street-priest, a villain's monologue, human zealots calling themselves the One's anointed. (02 Law 12; 03)
- 🔒 Light and dark are variables (morality, aesthetics), never cosmic powers or beings. (02 Law 12)
- ✅ Madness is the base layer, the One dissociating: subtext only, no visible metaphysical cause, touching good and bad alike. (03; wk W70)

### The keeper (the puppeteer) and the breach
- ✅ The puppeteer is a Unified One at universe scale, "a potential universe who never decohered." Unnamed on purpose: call it "the keeper" (plain word) or "the unified one." Never name it. (03; t196; R102)
- ✅ The keeper could reset the world itself; forcing Jon and Derrek to do it is part of their punishment. (R010, R117)
- ✅ Jon and Derrek are beyond every god in the story: "0 beings in our story who come close." Derrek is quiet and respectful only before a Unified One. (03; t315)
- ✅ The portal (Derrek's) didn't create the One. It tore open the Unified Ones' shield (the breach), and every reset rips it wider. (03 THE BREACH; t184)
- ✅ Derrek never supported the Blight; he holds the world together against the breach his portal opened. (02 Law 13; t180)
- 🔒 Resets do two opposite things: they widen the breach, and they rewind the invaders back out. Derrek's "reset or reality ends" is literally true. (03 reset's two functions; 08)
- ✅ How a reset works: nobody inside time-travels. The universe is rewound from outside like a recording. Jon's soul is carried through while his body and brain are rewound; Derrek's sight is the vantage. (R012)
- ✅ Breaks are local and vary by universe: many had no pair, some had singles, some had groups. (R103)

### Rifts
- ✅ A rift forms wherever Jon jumps, even off Earth. Rifts leak because the level above bleeds through; that isn't water or any substance and can't be drained. (R013, R002; t186)
- ✅ Once the bleed is in, it stays. Only Jon and Derrek can undo it, by reversing time; others must seal fast. (R003)
- ✅ Rifts are reservoirs people fight over, never the source of magic: every being's magic is its own. (03; t188; R025)
- ✅ The time gods' shared toolkit: sense (find a rift), slice (open it), hold a portal, stitch (close), brace (steady the area). Never "drain." (R001)

### The outer region and the time-god society
- ✅ The time-god society's Citadel stands in the outer region between realities; both founders built it. Its law: watch, never join, or lose the society's protection. No one enters Jon and Derrek's war. (R127, R113; t650)
- ✅ In the outer region every pair's power is limited; Derrek's sight broke there. (R008)
- ✅ The Arcavist, the Historian and Barnaby are one man, the fourth time god (his profile: `characters_eras.md`). (R104)

### The Tower
- ✅ The Tower is the giant emperor's last Convergence on betrayal day: he drew his city's bodies into himself and dissolved; everything outside was vaporized; the city's Remnant is trapped in the stone. (R136; wk W15–W16)
- ✅ It is mindless: "no brains or eyes or skin. Its not thinking. It isnt feeling. Its not speaking." (R111)
- ✅ It's the largest store of Remnant on Earth, a giant channeling device and amplifier where everyone is at their absolute peak. Living stone made from fresh Remnant (cathedral ships are the opposite: dead flesh). The powerful feel it "like the sun." (R026, R027, R129)
- ✅ It sits on the first and largest rift, Derrek's portal, the most torn by Jon's jumps, and leaks "next dimensions size chunks of raw floating remnant." (R014; t66)
- ✅ It's fused into all eras ("It was always that spot and derrek always chose it"), and the window sits at its center. It's the nexus of *their* family of universes, not of the regress. (01 THE TOWER; t456, t636–638; 09 t68, t102)
- ✅ Derrek built additions (elevators, laser arrays), not the walls. The city never moved, only its ruler: giants, then dragons, then Derrek. (t62, t64; wk §J)
- ✅ The land around it was radioactive for hundreds of years (the capital: `beings_world.md`). (R061, R137)

### The three worlds
- ✅ Each dimension has three worlds: ours, plus two lifeless clone-pockets the elves call heaven and hell. Pocket dimensions, never "universes." (wk W27; 03)
- ✅ "Elves built heaven" is in-world belief. The elves own the rift device, like electricity: found and harnessed. (wk W28–W29)
- ✅ Heaven is a utopia wrapped in light and empty, because entering cuts you off. There's one hell: the elves' prison world, "a normal world like ours. They just name it such," full, with whole societies and "angry noise." No demons' plane. (R120; 07 t602–606)
- ✅ The way in: in the older age, a crude hand-opened gate manned on both sides; in the far future, a ship drive. (R015)
- ✅ The elven bond can't cross the seal between realities: inside a pocket dimension, warp included, an elf is cut off. (03; t230, t600)

### In-betweeners and the Rule
- 🔒 In-betweeners are a lineage older than the One and not descended from it. They run the Rule, not Remnant, and still obey physics. Fuel = transition; price = coherence. (02 Law 15; 06)
- ✅ They never stand in our world: they lay their Void (the pocket they carry) over real places and set binding conditions. (07 Tier 3, t130–164)
- ✅ A rule-beast has no true form inside its Void. It can split into many forms, but split too far and it can never remember itself and return: its only true death. (02 4b; t132, t164)
- ⚖️ Their kit (Void, Chain, Chain Void, Phase-step, Unfixing, the Hold, Cascade) is an adjustable foundation. A Rule traps everyone in its bounded space, the in-betweener included, and releases when its condition is met. (06; 02 Law 15; jul08 t365)
- ⚖️ Inside a severed Rule-domain you fight with only what you are and what you brought in. (02 Law 7c)
- ✅ The dwarves sold the dwarves they considered weak to an in-betweener; their descendants are the Returned (`beings_world.md`). (t146)

## B. FLAVOR / AURA

- **Grimdark with one light**: the cosmos is the haunting part of "haunting evil world with a kind hearted light we follow" (BANK 11-21; full line in `beings_world.md`): vast, indifferent, and stocked with things that eat realities.
- **Cosmic smallness and human weight, together.** "Among our spoke of the wheel we are the central point where still only being one wheel of one car on one world in one galaxy etc etc" (t638). The thesis: **"it doesnt matter in the grand scale but everything matters to someone"** (t466). Scenes should hold both at once.
- **The divine is just bigger.** "gods are what happens when ANY user becomes incredibly powerful" (t668). No holy glow that makes them a different kind of thing; awe comes from scale, never from kind (inferred).
- **Duality, not halves.** "Duality friend. Duality. Grading scales not a single fixed point" (jul08 t269). "Your giving it to much flattening" (t674). Patrick pushes back whenever things sort into neat categories.
- **The theme line of the One:** "The greatest one wished to be everything and that includes things which try to kill each ither" (BANK 11-01). A god that wanted to know what it wasn't, and got murder too (inferred).
- **Madness under everything.** "Madness is base layer kts nondiscriminate good and bad all are mad" (t365). Felt as subtext, never explained.
- **Wonder that refuses to be explained.** On nesting: "Its one of those things we just have to say is real. Thats part of the magic" (BANK 11-02). Keep the mystery; don't footnote it.
- **Names that carry dread.** "The name the blight is a dark fettid term that implies malice and harm. Blights kill. They leave things barren" (t178). Cosmic words should smell of rot and threat, not wonder-park (inferred).
- **The keeper is too big to name.** "So the being is unnamed intentionally to grand to be understood by people in our universe" (t196). Before it, "the world cannot look away," and even Derrek goes quiet (t321).
- **The Tower:** a mindless mass of a dead city's souls in living stone, "just a mass of the one with no real way we can understand it" (BANK 11-16). Radiation sickness and animals that avoid the land around it (t88; R061). Sacred, toxic and coveted, all at once (inferred).
- **Fate as a leash.** "the tether does not care why they jumped. derrek goes with jon. end of story" (jul08 t369). The cosmic layer is a punishment, never a reward (R117).
- **Heaven and hell as propaganda.** Heaven is "Utopia. A perfect place wrapped in light," yet "Beautiful and empty" (t228, t604). Hell is "full and abundant… But its angry noise" (t604), and really "a normal world like ours. They just name it such" (BANK 11-07). "One of our trademark ironies" (t604): the world keeps undercutting its own myths, the name versus the act (wk W57).
- **A dead universe.** Planets everywhere, life nowhere but Earth; far-future settlers find it "weirdly dead" (03). Loneliness at galactic scale (inferred).
- **The infinite above.** "this is happening infinitely and were only 1 level of this happening infinitely" (t464).
- **What makes it Patrick's:** consciousness is the only magic, and physics is left alone; the soul is a measurable chunk of a shattered god; souls are an industrial material while selling a soul stays impossible; gods are flesh; the cosmic war comes down to two old men and a door they opened as young men (inferred from 02, 03, R033, R027).
- Burnout, pressure and "wizards, not brawlers" images: `magic.md` aura. Eldritch Jon and Derrek and the window: `characters_eras.md` aura.
