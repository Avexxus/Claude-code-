# Magic design v1 (🌊 Claude proposal, agreed in chat 2026-11-22/24, Patrick's direction)
Canon for the engine (Remnant, Will, Skill, grip, burnout, amplifiers) stays as in `world/KERNEL.md`. This file is how *expression* works on top of it.

## 1. Engine (fixed, canon)
- Remnant = scale, innate, never added or drained. Skill = precision, trained per verb. Will = direction and conviction.
- Burnout = the body breaks. Higher rungs cost more of the body.

## 2. Foundation (everyone needs these to control magic at all; expand as needed)
Sense · Touch · Read (structure and history of a thing) · Design (plan and write a working) · Steady (hold attention through a running working) · Feel (how much force a thing takes before it gives) · Anchor (keep your own body from burning under load) · Name (a word, sign or gesture that shapes a working, for those who need one).
Foundation is not a ladder. It's what every expression is built from.

## 3. Matter ladder (expression, not separate powers)
| Rung | Force (loose label) | What it is | What it requires |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hold | gravity | move and grip matter | the basic grip. The floor. Clumsy: can carry, cannot build. Store (containing Remnant) lives here. |
| Rearrange | electromagnetism | hold the rigid structure while reshaping it | Hold. Mend (flesh) is Rearrange applied to a body. |
| Shatter | strong force | split atoms | Rearrange, for control. Wildfire (uncontrolled nuclear burst) comes first. |
| Change form | weak force | alter a thing's nature: spin, tilt, protons | Shatter, for fine enough control to reach inside. |

- Rungs are a general progression, not strict. Skipping is possible.
- **Rule:** a rung is only controllable with the rung below it. Skipping gives wildfire (crude, costly, uncontrolled), not craft. Craft needs the rung below.
- **Direction comes from below:** a split with no Hold is a blast; a split held by Hold is a beam or a point.
- Learning arc on each rung: fail (cannot grip) → wildfire (over-pull) → clean (exact split) → shaped (steered from the rung below).
- Atrophy: Skill decays without practice. Remnant does not (canon). A master who stops using Hold loses it; he can still design what he can't hold, through servants.

## 4. Expression dials (how a person uses the rungs)
- **Reach:** which rungs they go deep in. Repertoire, never class.
- **Mode:** wildfire / craft / steered.
- **Precision:** wide or exact. Huge Remnant gives scale, not precision.
- **Sustain vs burst:** long steady work or short huge push.
- **Tell:** what a witness sees first.
- **Shape by life:** trade, body, wound, culture (e.g. a dragon designs, a dwarf builds into chambers, an orc's reach comes with a sound). Mechanics fixed; look personal.
