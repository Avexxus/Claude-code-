**Method:** beat by beat, in story order. Each beat goes through the writer, then sense-checker, payoff-keeper, dialogue-editor, line-editor (natural) and story-guard, then Patrick approves it before the next (skill Stage 3). Each finished chapter goes to an outside reader as a STORY_ONLY copy.

## Fixed spine [P]
1. **Eli** was raised outside among rich kids, thanks to his father's reach. He is naive and kind, not corrupt. His flaw: he's devoted to the *idea* of his friends and misses the real people. He doesn't listen, and the story shows it without saying so. At first he sees orcs the way you'd see a dog, with his orc friend the exception. Meeting unchipped orcs turns that into horror, and the horror becomes the crux of going for the throne.
2. **The opening:** Eli, Teo and the orc friend in the undercity. The orc has a premium chip with its control removed, bought from a Returned dealer they met earlier. He fights the Orc King (whose nature stays open); both draw on stolen Remnant. The orc loses but buys time. Eli freezes; Teo stays cool. The orc's speech: a boy once defended him from bullies, and he's proud to die for the man that boy became. **First death.**
3. **The Inquisition hunts the "terrorist cell".** A joint operation: two elite assassins and a human special-forces squad, with Jon the most wanted. The inquisitor won't wait for backup. Eli fights his father's elite while the drow fights the Inquisition beside him. Backup arrives late.
4. **The old dwarf** owns the ship. He's a rich merchant who wants adventure, and the only one who understands its imperfect engine. He talks brave and moral, but he's afraid, selfish and always asking for something more. At the Tower he "stays with the ship" and takes off, stranding them, and is never seen again. ("Ok sure, that's fair.")
5. **The Tower:**
   - The master dies in the rule-beast room.
   - The drow dies soon after. Derrek's mind-trap (bond-spoof tech) breaks her brittle mind; she sees Eli as her tormentor and uses Lirael's Collapse on him. Eli's training turns it back on her by instinct.
   - Then Eli's own collapse: "I'm ok", while the crew goes quiet and Jon barely notices.
   - Jon steps in only when he has to. His encouragement asks for more: a warden pushing a ward up the executioner's stair.
6. **The throne room:**
   - Derrek kills Teo on entry, mocks him ("You brought this?") and spits on the body.
   - "Did you forget my letter, boy?" / "Do better." / "This is certainly not better."
   - The apprentice defends his master.
   - The Queen's grief charge. She and Teo were in love; she softened only with him. She is stunned, then red rage like a banshee, then calm, tears and resolve, and every eye is on her, even Jon's.
   - The apprentice dies of overload.
   - Eli's final attack is words, to his father.
   - Jon drains and restrains Derrek. At the window: "just enjoy the show, old man". Jon never answers.
7. **It was all in vain.** Both men know it. The cast's reasons are real, and nobody is performing.
