# v1 logic attack: Book 1, "The Emperor's Son"

> Claude proposal, story layer, not canon. Hostile logic read of `drafts/v1.md` (story lines 1–1741), judged on what is on the page. Plan files (`00`, `02`, `03`, `04`, `05`) and `decisions/BANK.md` were read only to know what was promised. Fixes are beats, not prose. Line cites are `v1 L###`.

## Verdict

The draft does not have a queue problem any more, and most arrivals and absences now have a cause. It has five holes a stranger will hit, and three of them sit under load-bearing scenes: the Emperor's idle left hand, the guard who announces the warrant Eli later "discovers", and a dead crew that unstraps the prisoners. Fix 1–7 before any reader sees it.

**The three departures, ruled:**
- **Reach at the grip, no dais crack: KEEP, and update `02`/`03`.** It is better motivated than cracking the dais (rescue, not attack) and sits closer to Gate 2 4b. But it opens a new hole: nothing on the page empties his reach (finding 5). Whether a reach at the grip counts as "no physical reach first" is still Patrick's call (`05` R3.5 Q2).
- **"Proud?" spoken to the door: KEEP.** It is consistent with the eye rule and makes "embarrassed me" land on Jon. It leaves one dead beat and one uncaused hand (finding 12). The words are Patrick's and are unchanged; the moved look should be shown to him.
- **"He had the title right. You said it with him.": KEEP the wording, it is now the accurate one.** "For him" would be false. But the unison itself no longer shows foreknowledge (finding 11).

## Findings, most damaging first

### 1. The Emperor's left hand is free for the whole fight, and killing costs him nothing
- **Quote:** "lifted his left hand a little off the arm of it, without looking, and closed it." (L1463) Then the knife: "His left hand came off the arm of the chair and met it... Nothing moved behind the chair after that." (L1505–1509) Then, for the whole duel: "The Emperor was yawning and resting his head on his hand." (L1513)
- **Why it breaks:** "He'll keep a hand for me" explains the right hand under his head. It does not explain the left. The page shows the left kills at range, without a look, at no cost. So a petition gets death, a thrown knife gets a parry and its thrower is ignored, Doc kneels in the open for the length of a duel, and Eli (the only one who actually reached at him) is merely thrown. Brief fixes 1 and 4 ("why doesn't a god end it") are only half landed: the reserve is explained, the restraint is not. It also reads as son-preference, which breaks "drywall".
- **Minimal fix (beats):**
  - Make the rule physical: he only answers what is in his line to the door. Teo walked into that line. The dark elf is behind the chair; to find her he would have to turn his head off Jon.
  - After the knife: the left hand lifts, half closes toward the space behind the chair. At the door Jon shifts his weight. The hand goes back to the chair arm. He has not turned his head.
  - Eli's flick stays one motion with the release (already there, L1469), so it is the dropping of Teo, not a choice to spare.
  - One visible cost on Teo's death (a breath through the mouth, the fingers slow to open) so killing is not free.

### 2. The guard shouts the warrant in line 3, so Eli "discovers" at the post what he was told at the fence
- **Quote:** "Stop! By imperial warrant!... Return what you took!" (L11). The guard "was backing off, talking fast into his collar." (L51) Then the post: "It had gone up already. The man against the fence had died for nothing, and Jon had known." (L185)
- **Why it breaks:** Eli heard that a warrant existed, from the man he then killed. He also watched that man report by collar before he reached for his throat. So the kill was visibly useless at the time, and the month-four revelation is no revelation. Worse, the inference is false either way: a guard who has located you is worth silencing whether or not a poster exists. This is the foundation of C1 and of the whole Eli–Jon ladder (brief fix 3).
- **Minimal fix (beats):**
  - Chase: cut "By imperial warrant". Keep only "Return what you took!", which Eli hears as a thief-taker's formula.
  - Yard: the guard backs off with the slate held to his chest. He has not called it in yet. Cut the collar.
  - Jon's pitch claims the report would start the hunt: nobody is looking yet; once he writes it up, they are.
  - Post: the same sentence, printed, dated two days before the yard. Recognition comes from the words. Now the hunt provably pre-existed, and Jon's line was a lie Eli can name.

### 3. A crew that is all dust unstraps the prisoners
- **Quote:** "Dust at every station." (L1097) "They unstrapped him." (L1105) "Somebody came along the ring and unbuckled the rest." (L1113)
- **Why it breaks:** The god's boast ("Your crew is dead"), the lord's sweat and Eli's whole bargain rest on nobody being left. There is no "they" and no "somebody". If spare hands exist, the bargain is worth nothing.
- **Minimal fix (beats):**
  - The dark elf's strap already folded away under her hand (L1075). She is the one who gets free.
  - She frees herself, walks the ring, unbuckles each of them, Eli last, without looking at him. It also sharpens "Next time ask me first".
  - Eli is still strapped when he makes the offer. The lord's head turning front is the answer; only then does she reach his buckle.

### 4. The dragon lord lets his hunter's killers, his cradle hands and two sellable elves walk off
- **Quote:** "Take us as far as your house port. Your house will have hands waiting there... The voyage. That's all." (L1109) "The lord's head turned back to the front. That was the answer." (L1111–1113) "The lord never asked their names." (L1121)
- **Why it breaks:** Indifference covers "doesn't kill the witnesses". It does not cover three things the page itself set up. (a) "A dragon's dead went home" (L1031): his pride is on the page, and they killed his hunter, which nobody mentions again. (b) The cradles are "most of them empty, the straps... worn pale" (L1025): he is short of exactly what they are. (c) He buys collared elves, he has just lost all of his, and two elves are standing on his quay. Eli's argument ("you won't want to feed strangers") answers none of it. The crew walks because the plot needs them at Earth.
- **Minimal fix (beats):**
  - Eli prices the hunter in the bargain: it was young, it lost to cargo, the shame is its own; the voyage settles it.
  - At the port the house's hands come up the ramp with a manifest and collars. They count the dust. The six are on no list, so nobody owns them and nobody looks. Drywall with an institutional cause.
  - Or the grimmer option, in pattern with the tug: Jon on the quay has already paid someone. One trace only (a port clerk who looks at Jon, not at them). Eli "puts it away with the rest".
  - Either way the dark elf leaves the ramp with her hood up between Eli and the Queen, and one of the house hands looks at her throat for a collar.

### 5. Eli's reach is gone for the rest of the book and nothing took it (departure 1)
- **Quote:** "it got to Eli before his reach got anywhere." (L1469) "He felt for his reach and there was nothing in his hands." (L1473) Later, as she is folded: "He said her name. There was no air behind it." (L1539)
- **Why it breaks:** In `02`/`03` he emptied himself cracking the dais. In the draft the reach never lands, so it was never spent. The master pushes through blood; Eli held a ceiling "in his teeth" an hour earlier. Broken ribs do not explain a null reach, and "words only" (Gate 2 4b) depends on it. It also makes him a spectator by fiat: the man who took two broken fingers for her does not try once when she is taken.
- **Minimal fix (beats):**
  - The flick comes back down his own reach, like a hand up a sleeve. What is wrung is the thing he reaches with (echoes the Emperor's arm "wrung like a cloth" later).
  - He tries once from the floor, at the fold closing round her. White at the edges, blood, nothing. Doc's hands push him flat. Then her name.
  - Then update `02` §1.1 and `03` §4.1 stage 9 / §4.3 / E8a: no crack, the side wall takes it.

### 6. The Queen hears "we don't kill him" and says nothing
- **Quote:** "My king has sworn to kill the man in that Tower... my king would like me to walk behind him." (L629) Lander: "'so we don't kill him?' 'So we don't.'" (L1211–1213). The master and Eli answer. She does not.
- **Why it breaks:** Her order is the man dead. Jon states, in front of her, a plan that forbids it. A woman who put a blade to Eli's eye over an untold fact lets this pass. She cooperates with the scene. (Brief fix 2.)
- **Minimal fix (beats):**
  - She corrects one fact, in her voice: her order says dead.
  - Jon agrees pleasantly and changes what it means: then that is her best thing; he will have to answer it.
  - She lets the silence stand. Nobody resolves it. It pays when she goes "straight at the chair" (L1487) and when she throws.

### 7. The ceiling room can be passed alone, so "He went alone" proves nothing
- **Quote:** "There's a slot by the door that stays open while something holds the roof." (L1323) "Let it down slow. Don't drop it. Walk out from under." (L1341) "He did."
- **Why it breaks:** Eli holds the roof, walks to the slot still holding it, and lets it down behind him. One strong person passes. The master's realisation, and the meaning of his walking into the beast's room so the others do not go alone, is built on a room that needs two and does not.
- **Minimal fix (beats):**
  - The slot begins to close the moment the holder moves his feet.
  - The master stands in it (he already "stopped in the slot", L1335) and keeps it open with his hand on the stone edge, blood on his lip, while Eli walks out.
  - Then "He went alone." No other line needed.

### 8. The dark elf attacks the human Emperor and the page never says why
- **Quote:** "A knife, short and curved, thrown flat at the side of the man's neck." (L1503), which comes before the sword breaks. Then the Collapse (L1541).
- **Why it breaks:** On the page her enemies are the elves who named her, the farmers, the Inquisition, the dwarves and the pit. The Emperor is nobody to her. `02` says "the man who owns everything", but that sentence never reaches the draft. And the knife makes her attack predate her planned trigger (the sliver), so the sliver is not a cause, only a cue. She climbs the Tower and dies because the cast list says so.
- **Minimal fix (beats):**
  - Galley, the pit night (L553): the crews who came down to bet flew his seal; the chain that left the rings had the oval stamped in it. She shows the wrist. Nothing else.
  - Pays twice for free: "The buyers are ours... Empire money" (L429) and the seal with no face.
  - Throne room: keep the knife as her own blow. The sliver then turns a failed attack into the Collapse, which is a cause.

### 9. The crater moon contradicts itself, and Krell lives a month on one ration
- **Quote:** "There was nowhere on that moon to run to. The station had the only air and water" (L543). But the raid is fought with no suits (Teo "sat in the black grit with both hands clamped round his thigh", L461). "Leave him a water ration... Not till the ninth." (L523)
- **Why it breaks:** (a) Either the moon has air or the station has the only air. (b) If the station has water and every grey coat is dead, the freed prisoners and Krell simply walk in, and the refused ration, "That's slower" and the missing ration all mean nothing. (c) The next ship is a month away; one ration does not keep a dwarf alive a month. The chain Krell → faces → cutter (brief fix 8) rests on this.
- **Minimal fix (beats):**
  - "The only water", not air.
  - The station seals itself when the last coat dies on its step (she killed them "at the door", L487). It opens for the next ship.
  - The Inquisition's own supply run is days off, not a month; the dwarven buyer's ninth stays monthly. Doc's line names the nearer date.
  - Krell: before the water, Teo says he has seen their faces. Eli looks at his hand. He will not kill him. Then "No water." Now the heels-on-slag fragment (L525) reads the right way round.

### 10. The cutter: nobody says why Ilvaen lets the prize float away, or why two strong reaches leave a cable alone
- **Quote:** "The hull, and the hell-born you carry." (L867) "They don't board what they can starve." (L881) The master "looked at it a long time. Then he looked at the lifeboat hatch." (L875)
- **Why it breaks:** Starving explains not boarding. It does not explain not shooting a lifeboat that carries the woman he demanded and every witness to his bribe. And after the reader has seen the master bend dragon breath and Eli bend gun barrels across a crater, one grapple cable at a ship's length needs a spoken reason. The plan has both reasons (`03` P8); neither is on the page.
- **Minimal fix (beats):**
  - The master's verdict gets a second clause: cut the line and he fires on a hull he no longer needs.
  - In the lifeboat, the Queen or the master: he stamped them through his own gate; he will not put that in a firing log.
  - Eli is on the deck with new-broken ribs when the choice is made; let one line tie his not reaching to that.

### 11. The unison title is not foreknowledge (departure 3)
- **Quote:** "The man in the chair said it with him. Every word, in Teo's rhythm, a hair ahead of him by the end" (L1457). "He had the title right. You said it with him." (L1649)
- **Why it breaks:** A title is a fixed formula. Any emperor knows his own. Saying it along is boredom, not impossible knowledge, so BANK 11-01's third time hint (Derrek's foreknowledge) is carried only by the beast's hearsay ("thousands of times"). In v10 he said Teo's own words.
- **Minimal fix (beats), pick one:**
  - Teo gets one word wrong, as he did rehearsing (L1227), and the Emperor says the wrong word with him, a hair ahead. Eli's line becomes "He had the title wrong."
  - Or the Emperor runs on into the first words of the ask, before Teo's mouth has made them, and then the hand closes. Eli's line can stay as drafted.

### 12. "Proud?" to the door: one dead beat and one uncaused hand (departure 2)
- **Quote:** "'Proud?' said the Emperor, to the door." (L1569) "He looked at Jon." (L1575) And the catch: "With the right, the hand that had been under his head." (L1529)
- **Why it breaks:** Jon is standing in the door. A man who has spoken to the door and then "looked at Jon" has not moved his eyes; the beat implies he had been looking elsewhere. Separately, the left parried a knife easily, so nothing on the page forces the reserve hand out for the sword.
- **Minimal fix (beats):**
  - Replace "He looked at Jon" with something that changes: he waits; at the door nobody laughs.
  - The throw: the left comes up first with hard air. The living blade flexes and slides round it, as it slid round Eli's wall in the corridor (L727). Only then the right comes out from under his head. That pays a chapter-five setup and makes the reserve break for a reason.

### 13. Doc throws away every soul and the page gives a death-terrified hoarder no reason
- **Quote:** "I've wanted a look inside ever since." (L1583) He is already inside. Then he opens his arms.
- **Why it breaks:** The barrage is Patrick's scene and stays. But the draft added a motive engine (the god dying last, the day of silence) and then does not connect it: what does Doc want from the man? As written it is a suicide.
- **Minimal fix (beats), needs a canon check against `08 wk W51`:**
  - His lips are already "counting something" (L1499). Let the count be for an opening: the healer judging when a patient is weak enough.
  - The barrage is a break-in. He wants the one body that does not die. The Emperor's line of never letting anyone into his body is what he hits.
  - No new words in the protected litany. One beat before it (his palm flat toward the man, as on the Tower wall).

### 14. Gates and holds that look the other way
- **Quote:** "The poster had the chin scar. It didn't have the cheek. He waved Eli on." (L921) "They went through hell once, on a human transit hull" (L941), after "They count what they carry... They sell what they find at the next port." (L557)
- **Why it breaks:** An extra scar does not clear a man who matches jaw and chin. And the crews she could never ride with now carry her, silver-eyed, through the place she escaped, with no cost. Both are the world cooperating.
- **Minimal fix (beats):**
  - The guard does the giant's arithmetic: the bounty pays at an imperial post, and the nearest is a war away. He waves him on. (Drywall again, and cheaper than a scar.)
  - The transit hull: she crosses as the Queen's property (a borrowed collar, or the Queen's one sentence to the purser). It costs her, it uses "Pardoned", and it loads the eleven hours and the hand.

### 15. The beast's room is "not attached to your universe" and the master draws the Tower into it
- **Quote:** "It isn't even attached to your universe any more." (L1381) "Something went past him toward the pane. It came out of the walls" (L1409)
- **Why it breaks:** The oath covers leaving, not entering, which is the trick. But the beast's own boast says the room is cut off, and Eli then feels power cross the pane. The oath text is v10 and protected, so the fix is outside it.
- **Minimal fix (beats):** show one-way before the draw. Grit or Teo's chalk dust slides to the pane and through; nothing comes back. Then the draw.

### 16. Smaller items
- **"A year of her putting him on the deck" (L1501), "every day for a year" (L1563).** She joins at the Dry; the Tower is seven or eight months on, four of them with broken ribs and nineteen days in a lifeboat. Say "months", or drop the count.
- **Earth burns for half a year.** The relay has the fleets hitting Earth three days after the strike (L799); the drop is seven months later into the same assault. One clause that it has not stopped since.
- **The plan is asked for on the last night** (L1203). A prince who interrogates has waited twenty months to ask what they do when they get there. Move the first ask earlier and let Jon turn it; the lander is the first straight answer.
- **Three dock arrivals with peaches.** Thirty-One (L337, "as if they'd arranged it") has no cause. It blunts the quay, which is the one meant to be impossible. Give Thirty-One a mundane cause (word left at the transit platform).
- **Jon moves a cathedral-ship wreck with one hand** (L1261) and Eli, who has only seen the older-wizard act, registers only the spar. One look is enough.
- **Why jump?** They leave a working lander a mile up. One clause (the roof guns track hulls, not bodies).
- **Eli never raises the Dry with the Queen.** Her king's fleet erased the colony he bled for (L917). He turns the feed off and that is all. In character he would say one sentence to her and get nothing.
