## A. Breaks (fix before v2)

### 1. The master's death drains the Tower into a sealed room, and Eli senses it
- **Quote (L1409):** "Something went past him toward the pane. It came out of the walls, a long slow draining, and under it there was a thing he had felt once before... Here he couldn't find the bottom of it."
- **Rule:**
  - BANK 10-31 L1/L2 (revised): held souls are amplifiers only, "nothing drains"; "battery implies draining". Tripwire 1.
  - `wk W10`: Remnant is never spent or transferred.
  - `13` L110: the beast's room is "severed from the universe entirely". The draft's own oath says so at L1381.
  - `13` L120: the friends "still sense nothing"; their whole view is the beast's picture.
- **Why it matters:** this is Patrick's scene. The draft shows Remnant flowing out of the walls, across the corridor and into a room that is not attached to the universe, and has Eli feel the master's push through the seal.
- **Fix (beats):**
  1. Keep the shudder and the lights dimming all together (the same tell as the apprentice, L1517).
  2. Cut the current: nothing "goes past" Eli, nothing "drains", nothing moves toward the pane.
  3. Eli recognises it from the picture and the shudder (the rim, the loader), not by feeling the push.
  4. Keep "No sound came through, and nothing else."

### 2. The world remembers what the dragons erased
- **Quotes:**
  - L341: "grown whole like the old giant cities but finer" (Eli's point of view, ch 3).
  - L699: Eli pitches "the giants' old quarrel with the dragons" before the giant has told him anything.
  - L779: a dragon ship-master on an open broadcast: "who died on betrayal day."
- **Rule:** `07` L106–107 / `10` L181 (`wk W61`): "The world doesn't remember, because dragons erased the record during the Darkest Age." The giants' age is "deep time".
- **Why it matters:** the giant's history at L707–709 is meant to be news (O5). Eli already has it two chapters early, and a dragon uses the giants' name for the dragons' own coup in public.
- **Fix (beats):**
  1. L341: drop the giant comparison. Seluvane is grown, finer than anything human.
  2. L699: Eli asks for a ship against the man in the Tower, with no "old quarrel". The giant supplies it.
  3. L779: the dragon names his dead without the giants' term ("who burned in the old capital"). Jon or the giant supplies "betrayal day" afterwards.

### 3. The narrator uses the elves' slur, and silver eyes mark the hell-born as a different kind
- **Quotes:**
  - L563: she tells Teo "They named it hell. Then they named us after it," and "he didn't use the word again."
  - The narrator keeps using it: "The dark elf" throughout (L869, L973, L1071, L1115, L1127), and L941 "They went through hell once".
  - L439: the chained prisoners' "eyes caught the station lights and gave them back silver."
  - L509: "Her eyes were silver, like the eyes in every story the nurses..."
  - L1555: "Her silver eyes".
  - No Seluvane elf, and not the Queen, is given silver eyes.
- **Rule:**
  - BANK 11-07 #1: "Where ever an elf is born they are just an elf"; "dark elf" "isnt even accurate".
  - BANK 11-07 #2: "Its not even hell."
  - Tripwire 10: narration must not agree with the label.
  - Tripwire 34: "dark elf" in elven mouths only. Jon (L423) and the narrator both use it.
- **Why it matters:** silver as a trait only the hell-born show is a blood marker. It also undercuts the Queen's secret, that the woman feels like any elf.
- **Fix (beats):**
  1. Give one Seluvane elf and the Queen the same silver eyes, in passing, or cut silver as a marker.
  2. Up to L563 the label is Eli's borrowed word. From L563, or at the latest from the name at L953, narration uses "she" or a neutral tag.
  3. L941: "through what the elves call hell", once. After that, "down there" or "the crossing".
  4. L423: Jon says "the ones they call dark elves."
- **Note:** `02` and `04` use "the dark elf" as her handle. That is a plan-level collision, not only a draft slip.

### 4. A fourth time-travel hint channel: Jon's memory
- **Quotes:**
  - L1133–1139: "I knew you would." / "How?" / "I don't know."
  - L1233–1235: "Your master—" / "Don't. You didn't know him."
  - L1265: "Jon's eyes went to it. They went away again."

- **Fix (beats):** keep one, and I'd keep the quay. Give the others plain covers: "Your master—" becomes a condolence the master cuts off, and the spar becomes a budget choice with no look. Or take the question to Patrick as a fourth channel.
