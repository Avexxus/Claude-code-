# Voice notes: Book 1 v3 (The Emperor's Son)
🌊 Story clay. Kept by continuity-keeper for the dialogue-editor. Habits **as they appear on the page**; cites are `ch01:L###` (draft_v6).

## Eli
- Warm, quick and commanding without noticing it: he issues orders as kindness ("Sit down… Teo, move.").
- Repeats himself instead of listening.
- Laughs at things that should worry him (the phone call, the dwarf's price).
- Under pressure he has a "clear and reasonable" steward voice, and it breaks into fragments when it fails.
- Inner speech in crisis: clipped, broken self-coaching.
- Says "Look at him" to Teo, showing Vosk off.
- Samples:
  - "Sit down. You haven't eaten. There's the end of the bird. Teo, move." (L139)
  - "It's paid for. If there's some sort of fee, I'm happy to—" (L391)
  - "Eleven hundred and a watch. Teo. Look at him." (L351)

## Teo
- Few words. Questions in two words ("From who?", "Whose cut?").
- Flat statements that are warnings, and he doesn't push past one.
- In danger: single-word commands, repeated ("Bridge.").
- Dry when shaken ("She'll want to know what happened to her brush", said to the sink).
- Samples:
  - "He didn't ask you to come." (L111)
  - "Every stall, Eli. Nobody's looking at him. They're looking at the coat." (L239)
  - "He's under there. You want to be up here with us when he comes out?" (L573)

## Vosk (dead; for flashback or echo only)
- Terse, one-word answers ("Wall."). Plain statements.
- Dialect touches: "He asked was you mine"; "You come off the step".
- Dying speech is in breath-broken fragments.
- Samples:
  - "It's quicker on my own." / "I know the way. I go down there." (L155–159)
  - "Teo. Take him up the stair." (L409)
  - "I picked it." … "Nobody." … "Spent me." (L543–547)

## Velk
- Curt, transactional, and she repeats a number for emphasis.
- Answers questions with one word ("Out." "Tax.").
- Gives orders as conditions ("Unless you'd rather do the knife.").
- Ends on a hedge or a disclaimer.
- Samples:
  - "He's paying. Course he is. You're late." (L255)
  - "Depot takes a third now. A third. Then his cut." (L265)
  - "It's lit. It'll settle. Or it won't." / "And I didn't fit it." (L343, L365)

## The King's crew (the smiling one)
- Mocking, uses "Terrace" as a name; drops grammar ("King don't need his ears").
- Samples:
  - "You're up late, Terrace." (L21, the Pole Street one)
  - "Lit you up like a lamp, Terrace." (L389)
  - "Hand down. Put it down or he goes up the stair without his ears. King don't need his ears." (L431)

## Fixed voices
- **The Terrace gate:** "Good evening." Soft, never a name (L187, L601).
- **The service slot:** "Late. Logged." In a pleasant voice (L71).
- **The orc farewell:** "Walk well." (L37)
