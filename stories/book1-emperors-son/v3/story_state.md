## World facts shown on the page
- **Readers:** a disc of dark glass at every landing, at a human's eye height and an orc's chest. For a human: "only a light, a name maybe, a soft chime". For an orc: "the chip in the neck woke up and answered it", cold down both arms. "The readers could also tell the chip to hurt you, and you didn't get used to that." (ch01:L41)
  - If an orc stands too long, the reader "had gone on looking": the cold "at the wrists… turned round and started back up", with a faint taste of foil (ch01:L45).
  - On the climb up, humans get green; orcs flinch at the cold (ch01:L597).
- **Terrace gate:** a ring of dark glass in a white arch. It gives Eli green and greets by the hour, "Good evening" going out and "Good morning" coming back, "without a name", when he is bloodied too; the same for Teo. It "didn't read orcs. It bit them." (ch01:L185–187, L607–613)
- **Service slot:** one orc wide; it reads the chip and punishes lateness. The shock starts in the jaw ("like biting on foil"), then the face, neck and back; "Late. Logged." (ch01:L67–69)
- **Counts:** the household has a night count and a morning count, rung by the barracks bell and read off a slate by number (ch01:L5, L67, L627). Missed counts are "lates", and Eli can have them taken off (ch01:L145).
- **Fobs:** a Terrace fob makes a chipped yard orc's feet jerk off the grass (ch01:L73). The Ilder father's fob made the child Vosk dance (ch01:L531).
- **Chips:**
  - the chip sits under a raised scar at the nape, "a pale ridge like anyone's" (ch01:L19, L159, L307); it is grey, with threads "into the meat like roots"; the old one is fingernail-sized (ch01:L311, L317);
  - the Legion chip is bigger, darker and ridged, its mark filed: "Soldier's. Leash is out." It runs hot when it takes (ch01:L321–325);
  - with it, Vosk felt the Tower ("Sun in the next room") and "them. All of them… It's loud" (ch01:L335–351);
  - fitting it "Lit you up like a lamp, Terrace" (ch01:L397).
- **Reach:** some humans have it; tutors test children with "a feather on a plate" (ch01:L115). A big effort makes the reacher sick (ch01:L453).
- **The King's draw:** chipped orcs nearby sag, "whatever was in them went out through the backs of their necks"; the air "went thick and bright" (ch01:L427). The old woman: "He's eating tonight" (ch01:L29).
- **Drawing's cost:**
  - palm split and steaming; knuckles and the backs of the hands split; blood from the nose, ears and eyes; steam; fingers bent back;
  - the skin "dark and tight and shiny"; "something in his chest had gone" (ch01:L433, L481, L503, L513).
- **Old ground:** warm all night; the heat has a direction ("like a fire burning on a high shelf"). The sick of the old ground: a jaw grown over the eye, fused fingers (ch01:L3, L11, L205).
- **Drift:** seed-sized light, no shadow; "You didn't touch drift with skin"; children net it with wrapped hands; it rises from the cut (ch01:L7, L223).
- **Mid-levels:** the headache for everyone; a blue pill on every landing that never works; floors that lean toward the Tower; light in the gutters that slides from a stare; sideways plants; "Dogs didn't last" (ch01:L49, L193). Eli's headache: "the faint fullness they'd had for years" (ch01:L345).
- **The Tower:** pale and seamless; clouds break round it; the lanes bend away; buildings keep a mile back; "The rich said it was warm on your face"; Terrace windows face it (ch01:L61).
- **Flesh market:**
  - "CLONE STOCK — NO SOUL" with a grade;
  - "Real" costs more;
  - an orc arm sells for "less than a goat's" (ch01:L51–57);
  - Hookline's sawn side hangs with a blank tag, "not even a grade" (ch01:L231).
- **The lift core:** a loaf-sized grey lump in glass that twitches "the way cut meat twitches". Vosk goes round it to the culvert (ch01:L59). *(v6's "Orcs didn't ride the lift" is no longer on the page.)*
- **The bridge at Hookline:** one curved girder, "ribbed like a spine", "Warm like a horse"; Vosk: "Don't touch it" (ch01:L225–229, L591).
- **Money:** marks and imperial. The chip costs 1,100 marks, plus "two" for being late, hot and three of them, paid with the watch (ch01:L5, L283–293). Vosk saved 41 marks in nine years. "Depot takes a third now." (ch01:L273)
- **Orc custom:**
  - the thumb pressed to the scar, given back (ch01:L27, L209);
  - "Walk well" (ch01:L37);
  - kneeling face-to-wall with hands shown when the King is near (ch01:L15–17, L385);
  - the Hurrak song (ch01:L35).
- **The Scald crowd** opens a gap round a Terrace coat (ch01:L205).
- **Humans in the Scald:** clean coats, foreheads to the old walls, paying orc children to mind their shoes (ch01:L9); climbing up "with their shoes in their hands" (ch01:L597).
