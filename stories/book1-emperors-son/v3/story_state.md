## World facts shown on the page
- **Readers:** a disc of dark glass at every landing, at a human's eye height and an orc's chest. For a human: "only a light, a name maybe, a soft chime". For an orc: "the chip in the neck woke up and answered", cold down both arms. "The readers could also tell the chip to hurt you." (ch01:L41–43)
- **Terrace gate:** a ring of dark glass in a white arch. It gives Eli green and "Good evening", "without a name", both ways and when he is bloodied; the same for Teo. It "didn't read orcs. It bit them." (ch01:L187–189, L601)
- **Service slot:** one orc wide; it reads the chip and punishes lateness. The shock starts in the jaw, then the face, neck and back; "Late. Logged." (ch01:L69–71)
- **Fobs:** a Terrace fob makes a chipped yard orc's feet jerk off the grass (ch01:L75). The Ilder father's fob made the child Vosk dance (ch01:L519).
- **Chips:**
  - the chip sits under a scar at the nape and is grey, with threads "into the meat like roots"; the old one is fingernail-sized (ch01:L303, L309);
  - the Legion chip is bigger and ridged: "Soldier's. Leash is out." It runs hot when it takes (ch01:L313–317);
  - with it, Vosk felt the Tower ("Sun in the next room") and "them. All of them… It's loud" (ch01:L333–343);
  - fitting it "lit [him] up like a lamp"; "the whole market felt it go in" (ch01:L389).
- **The King's draw:** chipped orcs nearby sag, "whatever was in them went out through the backs of their necks"; the air "went thick and bright" (ch01:L419). The old woman: "He's eating tonight" (ch01:L29).
- **Drawing's cost:**
  - palm split and steaming; knuckles and the backs of the hands split; blood from the nose, ears and eyes; steam; fingers bent back;
  - the skin "dark and tight and shiny"; "something in his chest had gone" (ch01:L425, L467, L491, L501).
- **Old ground:** warm all night; the heat has a direction toward the King or the Tower. The sick of the old ground: a jaw grown over the eye, fused fingers (ch01:L3, L11, L205).
- **Drift:** seed-sized light, no shadow; "You didn't touch drift with skin"; children net it with wrapped hands (ch01:L7, L223).
- **Mid-levels:** the headache for everyone; a blue pill on every landing that never works; floors that lean toward the Tower; light in the gutters that slides from a stare; sideways plants; "Dogs didn't last" (ch01:L49, L195).
- **The Tower:** pale and seamless; clouds break round it; the lanes bend away; buildings keep a mile back; "The rich said it was warm on your face"; Terrace windows face it (ch01:L63).
- **Flesh market:**
  - "CLONE STOCK — NO SOUL" with a grade;
  - "Real" costs more;
  - an orc arm sells for "less than a goat's" (ch01:L51–57);
  - Hookline's sawn side hangs with a blank tag, "not even a grade" (ch01:L231).
- **The lift core:** a loaf-sized grey lump in glass that twitches "the way cut meat twitches". "Orcs didn't ride the lift." (ch01:L59)
- **Money:** marks and imperial. The chip costs 1,100 marks, plus "two" for being late, hot and three of them, paid with the watch (ch01:L5, L277–285). Vosk saved 41 marks in nine years. "Depot takes a third now." (ch01:L265)
- **Orc custom:**
  - the thumb pressed to the scar, given back;
  - "Walk well";
  - kneeling face-to-wall with hands shown when the King is near (ch01:L15–17, L27, L37, L209, L377);
  - the Hurrak song (ch01:L35).
- **The Scald crowd** opens a gap round a Terrace coat (ch01:L205).
- **Humans in the Scald:** clean coats, foreheads to the old walls, paying orc children to mind their shoes (ch01:L9).
