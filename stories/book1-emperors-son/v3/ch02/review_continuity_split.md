## 1. Timeline and plot logic

**T1. The flyer "over the sumps" against "never below Ancel Row" (Low to Medium)**
- ONE 1:L403: "You've not been below Ancel Row in your life."
- TWO 2:L31: "Eli bent to the glass to look at it" (the core, which he is seeing for the first time). TWO 2:L53 places the sumps underground: "black water sliding past in a channel as wide as a street, with lamps on the far wall".
- TWO 2:L369: "as Teo had breathed the time the flyer lost a fan over the sumps."
- The problem: Eli was there to see Teo's breathing, so Eli has been "over the sumps", which lie below Ancel Row and are enclosed.
- **Fix (one word):** "over the sumps" → "over the lake". That also lets it be the same flyer Eli "pulled… up out of the lake" at fifteen (2:L399). Or, more neutrally, "over the lanes".

**Checked and clean:**

- **The clock.** ONE 1:L643 has "just after eleven", and 1:L637 has the shaft change "at half past eleven… nobody due… until two". TWO has "a few minutes later" (2:L11) and "At half past" (2:L41).
- **The first line.** ONE 1:L643 ("They left the house") leads straight into TWO 2:L3 ("Going down the white path"). The flashback at 2:L9 ("Eli had said in the yard") is in the pluperfect and doesn't jump.
- **The flesh market.** It is opening at Day 1 dawn (1:L53), shut at night with "the evening hosing" (2:L31), and "hosing down the slabs" at Day 2 dawn (2:L557).
