## Scene 1 · The old pit

- **Type:** ACTION (goal: reach the door; conflict: the relay and the chain; disaster: the relay dies and the door is a warm dark seam).
- **POV:** Eli.
- **Place:** the old pit behind the wire fence, down the lane past Pask's gate, Ithe. Water in the bottom with grey ice; a cart's wheel sunk spokes-up; the far lip is bare rock.
- **Time:** first light, the ninth. Frost on the wire. Red sun low, giving no warmth.
- **Present:** Eli (shirt, no coat; the bad hand "partway"), Teo (the coat, buttoned to the neck, good hand in pocket), Seffa (canvas bag, the big pot tied to its strap), Doc (leather bag at the back of his knee), the tall boy (right forearm in Doc's rag, brown at the crease).
- **Enters on:** the fence, Eli's hand on the wire. **Leaves on:** the seam closing behind them with the relay silent.

**Beats**
1. Eli at the fence, wants to go down now. Teo says wait; Teo watches the crow on the far lip, which does not go up.
2. The gate: Pask's chain lies off the post in the frost, its ends bright. Nobody unhooked it, or someone did it long ago. Doc looks at the ends and says nothing.
3. A brass horn on a bracket on the pit rail, the cable running down the wall into the water. It hisses. Eli shouts "Jon" into it. Teo does not look at the horn.
4. The relay gives one line, Jon's voice, flat: "Walk in front, son. Don't stop." Click. Hiss, long and even, like a weir. Eli asks what he means. Nothing.
5. Who goes first. Eli moves to the ladder. Teo stops him with the bad-hand side of his body and takes the first rung himself. (Hard choice for Eli: stand back and let the one who's been in the rows lead.)
6. Descent by rung ladder to the water's edge. The pit's back wall is cut into a stone arch with no leaf, a dark seam in the rock. The chain-off gate of the old pit stands open against the bank.
7. Doc puts his palm flat near the seam. "It's warm on this side." Seffa: "Don't." He takes the hand back, looks at it, says "Hm."
8. Threshold: the frost stops on Eli's face. Inside the seam the air is close and warm, wet stone, a smell of lye with something sweet under it. The cold changes sides on the step.
9. Eli turns to the sky and the pit's rim. The rim is still there; the relay is not. He says "He'll ring" once. Teo goes through first, then Seffa, then the tall boy, then Doc. Eli is last. He steps in because the seam is closing behind the last of them.

**Value shift:** Eli, from *someone is running this and will ring us through* → *no one is coming to say what this is; the way back is cold and the way on is warm.* (Start: hope in a voice. End: no voice.)

**Stress:** moment 5 (the seam and the relay dying). Baseline Eli 3. End calm 3 (false: Eli's talk fills the warm dark).

## Scene 2 · The count-house

- **Type:** REACTION (dilemma: stand still and be read, or cross the line to be quick; decision: the tall boy crosses; the rule answers). Failure: the line takes his finger.
- **POV:** Teo.
- **Place:** a stone room at the foot of the first stair, the count-house. One table, no chair. A wall of names. A lintel over the far arch cut with rules. A groove across the floor in front of the far arch (the line).
- **Time:** shortly after scene 1. Lamp-less; the seam's warm light comes from low wall slots. Dank heat.
- **Present:** Teo, Eli, Seffa, Doc, the tall boy. (Nobody reads the count; no reader present.)
- **Enters on:** the table already in view. **Leaves on:** the boy's hand going into the groove, the stone click, the arch opened to the next stair.

**Beats**
1. Teo counts the room, not the names: one table, one ledger open on it with a pencil on a string (the hut's pencil again), one arch, one line, one lintel.
2. The lintel, cut in square capitals: "STAND. THE COUNT IS READ STANDING." Under it, smaller: "CROSS ONLY WHEN READ." Eli reads it aloud and laughs; nobody joins him.
3. The wall of names: columns of tally, names cut in several hands, some with a line through, one blank at the foot of a column with no name. Doc walks along it and touches no name.
4. Eli wants to write "the account" in the ledger; the pencil is tied short. Teo says no. Eli says he only wants to see it's writable.
5. The tall boy is bored and stiff-armed, with a sharp edge to his talk. He says the rule is for people who are slow, and steps to the groove ahead of the rest "to be quick."
6. Seffa: "Stand where I can see you." (P8-02 set.) He says "You said." He steps across the line before anyone is read.
7. The lintel does not read him. The stone takes the top joint of his left little finger. A click like a tally mark; the joint lies in the groove, then is gone from it.
8. He stands with his hand against his chest and looks at the stump and says "I was quick." Doc has the rag off the right arm and the left hand in both his own, not looking at the boy's face; the bleeding is clean.
9. Teo watches the lintel. The line only costs a body that crosses unread. He says the rule aloud, flat, as if reading a bill. Eli says "We'll be fine, we just stand." Teo: "It doesn't say stand. It says read." (Hard choice: the party must stand on a line no one can see the end of, and Teo keeps them standing.)
10. Seffa will not carry the boy and will not leave him. She holds him by the good arm at the groove edge until the arch opens (the lintel reads the party through as read, once the cost is paid).
11. They go through. The arch closes on the table, the ledger, the open pencil.

**Value shift:** Teo, from *unwatched; I see the room and nobody sees me* → *the rule has read us, and it picks.* (Start: the watcher who stays unseen. End: counted, and the count has chosen.)

**Stress:** moment 7 (the click). Baseline Teo 4. End calm 4 (false: the boy stops bleeding; the arch is open).

## Scene 3 · The self-counting corridor and the lock

- **Type:** ACTION (goal: reach the iron gate at the corridor's far end; conflict: the corridor pulls back a step for each step forward; disaster: the lock takes nothing but a gift, and only Eli's reach counts as one).
- **POV:** Eli.
- **Place:** a long stone corridor below the count-house, pillars every ten strides, each cut with tallies. At the far end an iron gate with a lock-plate; the plate reads "GIVEN, NOT TAKEN" in the same square capitals.
- **Time:** the first hour under. Still no sun, still warm.
- **Present:** Eli, Teo, Seffa, Doc, the tall boy (one hand bound, one hand bare).
- **Enters on:** the corridor's first pillar, Teo counting. **Leaves on:** the gate closing behind them on its own with the lock turning back and a tally notched on the last pillar.

**Beats**
1. Eli leads, talking about the cut of the stone (the stonework is the cleanest thing he has seen since the cranes). Teo counts strides under his breath: "Nine. Ten." At ten, the pillar.
2. They walk. At eleven they are at the pillar again: the floor takes a step back for each step forward. Teo says it. Doc says "We're not going anywhere."
3. Seffa runs three strides. The corridor takes three back. The tall boy stops dead and the corridor stops with him; he stands still for a while and nothing happens, and then Doc says "Don't stop, you'll read yourself"; nobody answers that.
4. Teo sees the rule's shape: each forward step is one back, and a step taken while nobody is looking doesn't count. He stops talking. Eli says "Teo's got it."
5. The gate. Doc tries the hinge with his knife. Seffa pulls at the bars. The tall boy has no bar and nothing to lever with. The plate takes no pry.
6. Eli: "It says given. Nobody's given it anything." He opens his good hand, then the bad one, which won't open all the way. "I can give it." Teo says "Don't." Eli: "It's a lock, Teo."
7. Eli's reach. He puts the bad hand flat on the plate and pushes his thing into the iron: a grip first (Hold), then the tumblers reshaped while he holds them (Rearrange). First push overshoots: the plate hums and the bolt jumps, and he is through the grip into the tumblers. He gets the bolt on the third push. The gate opens a hand's width, then swings.
8. The cost lands. The bad hand locks shut white, the fingers curled to the palm and will not go back. A nosebleed down the left side of his mouth. He sits down in the corridor on the wrong side of the pillar and is sick between his knees.
9. Doc comes and kneels. Looks at the hand, then at the blood, then says "Hm." He does not touch the hand. "Leave it shut. It'll open or it won't." Eli laughs and says "It'll open."
10. Teo lifts him by the coat-less shoulder and says "Stand up." Eli stands. He says he's fine; they all go through the gate. (Hard choice, Eli: he has the reach to spend on the next lock and says he'll keep it "ready"; Teo says he'll keep the count.)
11. Behind them the gate swings closed on its own. The bolt turns back. The tally on the last pillar notches once more.

**Value shift:** Eli, from *my reach is a thing I can give, and it's a gift* → *my reach is a thing taken out of my hand, and the lock took it as a gift.* (Start: generosity with no price. End: the price is in his hand, shut.)

**Stress:** moment 8 (the hand locks; blood). Baseline Eli 4 (up from 3: the hand and the cranes). End calm 5 (false: "I'm fine").

**Promises**

- **Paid:** P8-05 at beat 8 (the gate opens).
- **Seeds set:** S-124 (Eli's reach is the only key in the party; the lock reads a gift, and only a gift). Book 1. Not paid in ch08.
- **Seeds paid:** none.

**Who resists whom**

## Scene 4 · The split

- **Enters on:** the fork, the pot knocking the rail. **Leaves on:** Seffa's hand on the last grille, the old man's cough on the other side.

**Beats**

2. Seffa wants up: the rail's cells are quiet. The tall boy holds his finger-stump against his chest and says the upper way is the way he came in, which is not true.
3. Teo says Eli should not go alone. Eli says Teo should not be near him in case the count reads him. Teo goes down with Eli anyway; Doc goes with them: "I'll go with the one who's bleeding." (Doc's choice, not argued.)
4. Seffa has the pot in her arms. The lid knocks. She has to put it down to climb the rail's first ladder with the boy. She leaves it at the fork, under the landing lamp. (Hard choice: the last thing from the yard, or the boy's balance on the ladder. She chooses the boy.)

9. The rail ends at a grille, a larger cell. Through the bars: an old man on a sack, knees up, a cough that goes on and on; a thin woman kneeling with her palm flat on his chest. Her eyes come up. Silver. She says, flat: "Don't put a hand on the grille."
10. Seffa's hand is on the grille anyway, and comes off it.

## Scene 6 · The count

- **Type:** ACTION (goal: the count must finish; conflict: the counter reads every name and the old man's blank is last; disaster: it reads his teeth and will read the blank last, and nothing stops it but a break that costs).

- **Present:** Ysvet, Ondry (the old man), the counter, Teo at the lower grille, Eli (hand shut), Doc, Seffa, the tall boy (stump bound), all standing as the lintel requires.
- **Enters on:** the bell in the lintel, Ysvet's breath held. **Leaves on:** the counter going back into the wall with a new bead on its cord, the party breathing, the old man's tooth-gap bleeding.

**Beats**
1. The bell. Ysvet's breath stops. She puts her back to the wall and her knife in her left hand. Ondry hums the lintel rule under his breath until the cough takes it.
2. The counter comes through the back wall: a frame that was a desk, too many joints, the arms folded at the wrong elbows, one doubled back on itself. The face is set sideways in the chest at the height of the grille. The skin is tally paper ruled in pencil-grey.
3. The count begins: names in a voice made of many voices, each a little late. Every party member's chest number goes up. The party stops breathing on the count without being told. Ysvet counts the breaths she holds.
4. It reads the living first (the party is in the count; they are "heard"). It reads Teo's name back from the lintel and his right hand. Ysvet sees his finger move.
5. It reads the old man's teeth. Four of them come out of the gum at once and are on its cord before he can shut his mouth. Ondry does not cry. He starts "Twenty-" and the cough takes the rest.
6. The count reads the blank at the foot of the column: the last name, not given. The counter waits for it. Ondry is the blank. The count cannot finish without a name, and the rule says it must finish what it has begun.
7. Ysvet tells Teo, through the grille, when asked: "Before the blank. Across the sill, before it reads the last. Cross it and it starts over from the top." (P8-06 paid.) The rule: it takes the nearest counted thing from whoever breaks the count, and starts again with that person first.

9. The counter takes the top joint of Teo's right index finger. It goes onto the cord. The count starts again from the top with Teo's name first. It reads him, finishes him, goes on. The grille on the cell opens a hand's width (it opens to the one it has taken from; the lintel now reads Teo as given). Ysvet does not move to it.
10. The counter goes back into the wall. The cord across its chest has one more bead.
11. Teo is on his knees at the grille with his hand held out and the finger gone from it. Eli says "Teo." Teo says "Right." Doc comes and takes the hand in his own and says nothing; his fingers are cold.
12. Ysvet counts, under her breath, with her knuckle: "Two. You missed one." Teo: "One." Ysvet: "One. I said." She taps her knuckle on the grille bar, once for each.
13. False calm: Eli laughs. "Well, that's it, isn't it. It's gone back. We're— Right." Nobody agrees. The old man's breath is ragged. The grille is open a hand's width, and the party is standing outside it, and she is not.

## Scene 7 · The door

- **Type:** REACTION (dilemma: stay, or go with the party because she is the only one who has counted this rule; decision: she goes, and leaves the old man; failure: the carry fails at the seam and the old man stays because he has been read).

9. Ysvet does not answer. She puts her palm on his chest, breathes up what is in it into her own lungs, and coughs blood into her sleeve. Her grief is physical, short. She holds the cough as long as it takes to be sure it's hers.
10. He says "Don't count me." She takes her palm off him and stands. She does not turn round.
11. She walks through the seam. The cold changes sides on the step (S-126 reinforced). The tall boy and Seffa, Teo, Doc, Eli ahead of her, Ysvet last: she is the one who does not look back.
12. On the far side the relay horn in the pit hisses, and Jon's voice says one line: "That's you, then." Nothing else. Eli talks; the others don't answer. (Teo's bound hand in his coat; Seffa's bag knocks; Doc's bag knocks at the back of his knee.)
13. The seam closes. Behind the door, the old man's cough goes on, and then stops.
