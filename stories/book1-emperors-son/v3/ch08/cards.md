## Scene 1 · The old pit

- **Type:** ACTION (goal: reach the door; conflict: the relay and the chain; disaster: the relay dies and the door is a warm dark seam).
- **POV:** Eli.
- **Place:** the old pit behind the wire fence, down the lane past Pask's gate, Ithe. Grey ice on the water at the bottom; a cart wheel sunk spokes-up; the far lip is bare rock.
- **Time:** first light, the ninth. Frost on the wire. Red sun low, giving no warmth.
- **Present:** Eli (shirt, no coat; the bad hand "partway"), Teo (coat buttoned to the neck; left hand in pocket; right palm still shows the ladle cut, pink and closed at the edges), Seffa (canvas bag; the big pot tied to its strap), Doc (leather bag at the back of his knee), the tall boy (right forearm in Doc's brown-creased rag).
- **Enters on:** the brass horn on its bracket, hissing; Eli is already shouting "Jon" into it. **Leaves on:** the seam closing behind the last of them, the relay silent.

**Beats**
1. The horn hisses. Eli shouts into it. Teo does not look at the horn. He watches the crow on the far lip, which does not go up.
2. The relay gives one line, Jon's voice, flat: "I jumped from here. Walk in front, son. Don't stop." Click. Then a hiss, long and even, like a weir. Eli asks what he means. Nothing comes back. Jon does not explain.
3. Pask's chain lies off the post in the frost, its ends bright. Nobody unhooked it, or someone did it long ago. Doc looks at the ends and says nothing.
4. Eli moves to the ladder. Teo stops him with his shoulder, on the bad-hand side, and takes the first rung himself. Hard choice for Eli: stand back and let Teo go first, or go first with his own reach and no one to read the rungs for him.
5. Descent by rung ladder to the water's edge. Eli holds with his good hand. The bad hand will not close on the rail; it stays partway. The pit's back wall is cut into a stone arch with no leaf, a dark seam in the rock. The chain-off gate stands open against the bank.
6. Doc puts his palm flat near the seam. "It's warm on this side." Seffa: "Don't." He takes the hand back, looks at it, says "Hm."
7. Threshold: the frost stops on Eli's face. Inside the seam the air is close and warm, wet stone, lye with something sweet under it. The cold changes sides on the step.
8. Eli turns to the rim. The rim is still there; the relay is not. He says "He'll ring," once.
9. Teo goes through first, then Seffa, the tall boy, Doc. Eli is last. He steps in because the seam is closing behind the last of them.

**Value shift:** Eli, from *someone is running this and will ring us through* → *no one is coming to say what this is; the way back is cold and the way on is warm.* (Start: hope in a voice. End: no voice.)

**Stress:** moment 5 (the relay dies; the seam closes). Baseline Eli 3. End calm 3 (false: Eli's talk fills the warm dark).

**Promises**

## Scene 2 · The count-house

- **Type:** REACTION (dilemma: stand still and be read, or cross the line to be quick; decision: the tall boy crosses; failure: the line takes his finger).
- **POV:** Teo.
- **Place:** a stone room at the foot of the first stair, the count-house. One table, no chair. A ledger open on it, a pencil on a string (short). A wall of names. A lintel over the far arch. A groove across the floor in front of the far arch: the line.
- **Time:** the first hour under, after scene 1. No lamp; warm light comes from low wall slots.
- **Present:** Teo, Eli, Seffa, Doc, the tall boy. No reader present.
- **Enters on:** the tall boy's good hand going out over the groove's edge. **Leaves on:** the arch opening on the next stair; the table, the ledger and the open pencil closing away behind them.

**Beats**
1. Teo counts the room, not the names: one table, one ledger, one pencil on a string, one arch, one groove, one lintel.
2. The lintel, in square capitals: "STAND. THE COUNT IS READ STANDING." Under it, smaller: "CROSS ONLY WHEN READ." Eli reads it aloud and laughs. Nobody joins him.
3. The wall of names: columns of tally, names cut in several hands, some with a line through, one blank at the foot of a column with no name. Doc walks along it and touches no name.
4. Eli wants to write "the account" in the ledger; the pencil is tied too short. Teo says no. Eli says he only wants to see that it's writable.
5. The tall boy is bored and stiff-armed, with an edge to his talk. He says the rule is for people who are slow, and steps toward the groove "to be quick."
6. Seffa: "Stand where I can see you." (P8-02 set.) He says "You said." He steps across the line before anyone is read.
7. The lintel does not read him. The stone takes the top joint of his left little finger. A click like a tally mark. The joint lies in the groove, then is gone from it.
8. He holds the hand against his chest and says "I was quick." Doc takes the left hand in both of his, not looking at the boy's face. "I've no bottle left. Keep it clean and keep it still." Doc has nothing to treat it with and does not pretend to. The bleeding is clean.
9. Teo says the lintel aloud, flat, as if reading a bill. Eli: "We'll be fine, we just stand." Teo: "It doesn't say stand. It says read." Hard choice: the party must stand on a line no one can see the end of. Teo keeps them standing.
10. Seffa will not carry the boy and will not leave him. She holds him by the good arm at the groove's edge until the arch opens. The lintel reads the party through as read, once the cost is paid.
11. They go through. The arch closes on the table, the ledger, the open pencil.

**Value shift:** Teo, from *unwatched; I see the room and nobody sees me* → *the rule has read us, and it picks.* (Start: the watcher who stays unseen. End: counted, and the count has chosen.)

**Stress:** moment 7 (the click). Baseline Teo 4. End calm 4 (false: the boy's bleeding is clean; the arch is open).

**Promises**

- **Paid:** none.
- **Seeds set:** S-123 (the lintel line: cross unread and the rule takes a counted part, the top joint). Book 1. Paid at scene 6.
- **Seeds paid:** none.

**The cost:** the tall boy's left little finger, top joint, to the rule. Clean; no splinter. No magic used.

## Scene 3 · The self-counting corridor and the lock

- **Type:** ACTION (goal: reach the iron gate at the corridor's far end; conflict: the corridor pulls back a step for each step forward; disaster: the lock takes nothing but a gift, and only Eli's reach offers one).
- **POV:** Eli.
- **Place:** a long stone corridor below the count-house. Pillars every ten strides, each cut with tallies. At the far end an iron gate. The lock-plate reads "GIVEN, NOT TAKEN."
- **Time:** the first hours under. No sun. Still warm.
- **Present:** Eli, Teo, Seffa, Doc, the tall boy (left hand bound, right hand bare).
- **Enters on:** Teo at the second pillar, the corridor taking him back. **Leaves on:** the gate swinging shut behind them on its own, the bolt turning back, and a tally notched on the last pillar.

**Beats**
1. Eli leads, talking about the cut of the stone (the cleanest thing he has seen since the cranes). Teo counts strides under his breath: "Nine. Ten." At ten, the pillar.
2. They walk. At eleven they are at the pillar again. The floor takes a step back for each step forward. Teo says it. Doc says "We're not going anywhere."
3. Seffa runs three strides. The corridor takes three back. The tall boy stops dead and the corridor stops with him. He stands still and nothing happens. Doc says "Don't stop, you'll read yourself." Nobody answers that.
4. Teo sees the shape: each forward step costs one back, and a step taken while nobody is looking doesn't count. He stops talking. Eli: "Teo's got it."
5. The gate. Doc tries the hinge with his knife. Seffa pulls at the bars. The tall boy has nothing to lever with. The plate takes no pry.
6. Eli: "It says given. Nobody's given it anything." He opens his good hand, then the bad one, which won't open all the way. "I can give it." Teo: "Don't." Eli: "It's a lock, Teo."
7. Eli's reach. He lays the bad hand flat on the plate and pushes his reach into the iron as a gift. The first push is wildfire: the plate hums, the bolt jumps, the reach runs through the tumblers and burns out the grip. The second overshoots the same way. On the third he steers by the one tumbler the lock gives him, and the bolt goes. The gate opens a hand's width, then swings. (Wildfire that happens to open it. He can carry a grip; he cannot build a rung. MAGIC_DESIGN §3.)
8. The cost lands. The bad hand locks shut, white, fingers curled to the palm, and will not go back. A nosebleed runs down the left side of his mouth and drops on the plate. He sits down on the wrong side of the pillar and is sick between his knees.
9. Doc kneels. He looks at the hand, then at the blood on the plate, and says "Hm." He holds out his own hand for Eli's. Eli offers it. Doc takes his hand back: "Not you. You've bled on the lock." "Leave it shut. It'll open or it won't." Eli laughs: "It'll open."
10. Teo lifts him by the shoulder: "Stand up." Eli stands. He says he's fine, and that he'll keep his reach "ready" for the next lock. Teo says he'll keep the count. Hard choice for Eli: he has the reach to spend on the next lock and wants to spend it. They all go through the gate.
11. Behind them the gate swings closed on its own. The bolt turns back. The tally on the last pillar notches once more.

**Value shift:** Eli, from *my reach is a thing I can give, and it's a gift* → *my reach is a thing taken out of my hand, and the lock took it as a gift.* (Start: generosity with no price. End: the price is in his hand, shut.)

**Stress:** moment 8 (the hand locks; the blood). Baseline Eli 4 (up from 3: the hand and the cranes). No calm. "I'm fine" is said, not believed, and Teo hears it as a lie.

**Promises**
- **Set:** P8-05 (Eli: "I'll open it, Teo. You'll see.").
- **Paid:** P8-05 at beat 7 (the gate opens).
- **Seeds set:** S-124 (Eli's reach is the only key in the party; the gate reads a gift, and only a gift). Book 1. Not paid in ch08.
- **Seeds paid:** none.

**Who resists whom**

- Doc resists nobody. He looks, says "Hm," declines Eli's hand because Eli has bled on the lock, and leaves the hand alone.

## Scene 4 · The split

## Scene 6 · The count

- **Type:** ACTION (goal: the count must finish; conflict: the counter reads every name and the old man's blank is last; disaster: it reads his teeth and will read the blank last, and nothing stops it but a break that costs).
- **POV:** Ysvet.
- **Place:** the cell and its grille. The counter comes out of the back wall at the ledger, through the cut columns, and stands taller than the cell door should allow.
- **Time:** the bell. The count. No sun.
- **Present:** Ysvet, Ondry (the old man), the counter, Teo at the lower grille, Eli (bad hand shut), Doc, Seffa and the tall boy on the upper rail. All standing, as the lintel requires.
- **Enters on:** the bell in the lintel, Ysvet's breath held. **Leaves on:** the counter going back into the wall with one new bead on its cord, the party breathing, the old man's gum bleeding.

**Beats**
1. The bell. Ysvet's breath stops. She puts her back to the wall with the knife in her left hand. Ondry hums the lintel rule under his breath until the cough takes it.
2. The counter comes through the back wall: a frame that was once a standing desk, too many joints, the arms folded at the wrong elbows, one doubled back on itself. The face is set sideways in the chest at grille height. The skin is tally paper ruled in pencil-grey.
3. The count begins: names in a voice made of many voices, each a little late. Every party member's chest number goes up. The party stops breathing on the count without being told.
4. It reads the living first. It reads Teo's name back from the lintel and his right hand. Ysvet sees his finger move.
5. It reads the old man's teeth. Four come out of the gum at once and are on its cord before he can shut his mouth. Ondry does not cry. He starts "Twenty-" and the cough takes the rest. Ysvet catches the catch in her own chest with one flat hand: a single held breath, not more.
6. The count reads to the blank at the foot of the column, the last name, not given. The counter waits for it. Ondry is the blank. The count cannot finish without a name, and the rule says it must finish what it has begun.
7. Teo asks through the grille where it runs. Ysvet: "Before the blank. Across the line, before it reads the last. Cross it and it starts over from the top, with the one who crossed first." (P8-06 paid.)
8. Teo's hard choice: cross the line and pay with the top joint of his right index finger (the ladle-cut hand, pink and closed at the edges), or let the count finish and read the old man out. He crosses. (S-123 paid.)
9. The counter takes the top joint of Teo's right index. It goes onto the cord. The count starts again from the top with Teo's name first. It reads him, finishes him, and goes on. The grille opens a hand's width: the cell opens to the one it has taken from. The lintel now reads Teo as given. Ysvet does not move to it.
10. The counter goes back into the wall. The cord across its chest has one more bead. It has not finished. It will finish at first light.
11. Teo is on his knees at the grille with his hand held out, the finger gone from it. Eli says "Teo." Teo says "Right." Doc comes and takes the hand in both of his. He says nothing. His fingers are cold.
12. Ysvet holds up her right hand: one finger short at the top joint. "The corridor's line took it. Not my own lintel. I crossed that line unread once, years back, to reach the lintel. I never crossed my own." She says so once, and she does not look at the others while she says it. Teo: "How many more?" Ysvet: "One less than it wants. It wants his." She taps the grille bar once with her knuckle.
13. False calm: Eli laughs. "Well, that's it, isn't it. It's gone back. We're— Right." Nobody agrees. The old man's breath is ragged. The grille is open a hand's width. The party is standing outside it, and she is not.

**Value shift:** Ysvet, from *nothing breaks the count; I keep him alive under it* → *the count can be broken, and someone who is not me paid it.* (Start: settled despair. End: a crack in the only law she knows, and a debt to a boy who is not hers.)

**Stress:** moment 9 (the teeth; the blank; the finger). Baseline Ysvet 5. End calm 6 (false: the counter is gone, Eli laughs).

**Promises**
