**Status:** draft_v5, fixes from the outside reads taken. About 7,800 words by machine count, over the 6k target. Chapter 7 ran about 5,200.

**What happens:** Eli's party reaches the old pit on the ninth. Jon's relay says the door is where he jumped. Inside: the count-house lintel, the self-counting corridor, Eli's reach opens the gate (it costs him a locked hand and a nosebleed), the party splits, Ysvet (the dark elf) keeps the old man Ondry alive by taking his cough. **Strongest:** the counter as tally paper; "Only a joint, he thought… was not ashamed for long"; "The cold stayed on the left. The warm stayed on the right. Neither of them moved over."; the ending with the cough going on behind the door.

**Weakest:**
1. **The rules are still hard to hold.** Read, count, blank, gift. The first clear definition is in the lintel scene; the outside readers still stalled on the count.
2. **Repetition and length.** The pillar loop is still long. The chapter is about 1,500 words over its target.
3. **Convenience.** The reversals (the lock opens when he stops holding it) still arrive on cue.
4. **Eli's voice.** Warm and absent, as calibrated, but "She's got him sorted" and "I'll open it. You'll see" read as the writer telling us he's naive.

**Fixed in this pass:** the grille's contradiction (one rule now: a gift is taken only if given); Doc's coat stays folded; Jon is on the surface, relay only; the arch count is honest; "Sitter" is explained or cut; "no one answered" is down to one; the tall boy is named Kell once.
