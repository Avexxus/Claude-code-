## Departures

1. **Doc and the tall boy's stump (scene 2).** Cards beat 8 has Doc take the left hand in both of his. Audit says Doc does not touch the stump in the prison, he has no bottle left and says so. Audit wins. Doc looks, does not touch, says "I've no bottle left. Keep it clean and keep it still."
2. **The counter's rule, stated by Teo (scene 3).** Cards give the rule as "a step taken while nobody is looking doesn't count." Draft has Teo say "It takes back what it sees" and the party go the last forty strides with their faces down. Same mechanism, plainer words, and it gives the party a physical act to take.
3. **Ysvet's "Who carries him" (scene 5).** Cards write it as a question. Draft drops the question mark. Voice notes and cast say she never asks. The line is a trap, not a question.
4. **Teo's cross (scene 6).** Cards say the counter "reads him, finishes him, and goes on." Draft has it read him through and then go back into the wall. "Finishes him" would read as a death on the page, which the brief rules out. The counter's cost stays at the finger.

6. **Ending (scene 7).** Brief shape 7 says end on the cough "stopping." Audit B1 and the cards say the cough goes on behind the door. Draft follows the audit: "It went on. / It went on."
7. **The carry (scene 7).** Cards put Eli's reach on the arch as a gift and say the arch "goes flat." Draft keeps it but softens the image to "slackened out toward the edges" to stay inside the similes cap, and keeps the flat-arch outcome in the next sentence.
8. **The gate reference (scene 7).** Cards allow a farewell callback to the yard gate in ch07. Draft leaves it out. Ysvet was not at the yard gate, so she could not see it. The tall boy's farewell is only the rail.
9. **Two stock similes cut.** Several cards-level images ran past the two-per-scene cap ("like a splinter", "the way you'd hold one out for a coin"). Both were rewritten into plain action.
10. **Name use.** "The dark elf" appears in scene 4 (Seffa's POV) and in scene 5 and 6 as "she" and "the dark elf". Her name is not printed. The old man is "the old man" throughout; Ondry is not printed. SIRRIN is printed, as a cut name on the wall, not as hers.
