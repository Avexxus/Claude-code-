## Numbered flags (ranked by how much they break belief)

1. **HIGH · L503–517 · the seam's rule contradicts the crossing.** Ysvet: "It reads only what is already read. He's read." But Teo was read too (his name read back, his finger taken, L421–441), and the seam let Teo through (L503–541 is the party crossing in order). The page gives no reason why Ondry, read in the same way, is refused. Category: broken on-page rule, missing step.
   Fix: make the blank the cause. Ondry's name is never read through, so he is unfinished; the seam takes only bodies whose names have been read in full. Put that in Ysvet's line at L511.

2. **HIGH · L443–467 · the dark elf's confinement and her reason to leave are never shown.** The grille "opened" to Teo (L443) and "She did not move to it" (L445). The reader sees a free woman refusing an open grille. The brief and audit say she is kept in by her own count, which she never finished, and that she needs the door. Neither is on the page. Category: missing step; reason to join not natural.
   Fix: one line at L443 or L467 that her cell opens only to the one it took from, and it took nothing from her cell (her finger went in the corridor, L453). Add one line of want: the door is the first open thing in thirty years (L413 gives the thirty years; nothing says she wants out).

3. **HIGH · L209–215 · the gate opens on a taken catch, against its own plate.** The plate says GIVEN, NOT TAKEN (L191). On the third try Eli "felt for the one catch… and he gave the reach to that and to nothing else." That is a precise, aimed draw with no training (R-027: an untrained draw can't be aimed; the master's "Don't use more than you can control," 5:L245). Category: broken on-page rule; cool moment with no cause. The moth lesson (7:L169) was about not looking, not about aiming.
   Fix: keep it a gift on try three, given whole into the catch, so the lock takes all of it and the hand locks harder (R-014). The aim comes from listening (R-043), and that is the cost.

4. **HIGH · L81–83, L429–431, L441 · the blank has three owners.** Doc says the blank is "Nobody's" (L83). The counter says the old man is the blank and the count cannot finish without his name (L429–431). The audit says the blank is Ysvet's own, and she taps twenty-nine and leaves her own space empty (audit, Count and names). Category: broken rule; count math. The reader cannot tell whose name stops the count.
   Fix: take the audit's version. The blank at the foot is hers. She cannot tap her own name, so the count waits on it, and Ondry's teeth are the price of the wait. Change L83 and L431 to match.

5. **HIGH · L435–441 · Teo crosses the groove with no reason on the page.** He has just said "It reads the ones that cross" (L131) and watched the boy lose a finger for crossing. Then he puts his foot over the line on Ysvet's word, to restart a count that is taking the old man's teeth. Category: a person acts for the plot; leap.
   Fix: one line of his own reason before L437: he has counted the old man's breaths, or the old man has said "Twenty—" and Teo knows the next bead is his. Better: Teo asks Ysvet what it costs, and she says "a joint," and he does it.

   Fix: say in ch08 that Pask hung the horn on the post (one line at L3), so the device is old and local. Patrick must rule R-050 before the horn is used again. Note for Patrick: ch07 FINAL does not need changing, but the ledger citation does.

## Dark elf: are her reasons for joining natural?
No, not as drafted. Four things are missing, in this order:
- Her cell is never shown as shut to her, so she looks like she is refusing an open door (L443–445).
- She is never shown wanting out. The thirty years are there (L413), but not a want.
- Her condition "Nobody puts a door on me" (L471) is accepted in one exchange (L475–481), and she walks through a door (L537) with no beat of her own choosing it. Audit N12 asks for one beat where she accepts the seam once.
- Her cost is real (Ondry, L533–537), but the turn to leave is pushed by his line ("Go. Or you're counted with me," L529). Let her decide on her own terms first, then let Ondry's line make the price fall on him.

## Verdict
**FIX FIRST.** Top three:
1. The seam's rule contradicts Teo's crossing (flag 1, L503–517): give the blank as the cause.
2. The dark elf's cell and want are never shown, so her joining is not earned (flag 2, L443–467).
3. The gate opens on an aimed, taken catch against its own plate (flag 3, L209–215): make it a gift.
