# Splitting v9 into two chapters (Patrick, 2026-11-17: "split this into 2 parts and fill them in. Some of the pacing is still to fast… chapter 1 as a slower introduction and chapter 2 as the sort of quickening part into the underground")

## ONE · The Gift (the slower introduction, about 8–9k words)
- **Base:** v9 from the opening to the end of the kitchen section, ending "They left the house just after eleven." That's about 3,600 words now.
- **Expand with scenes, not connective summary.** Every added scene must turn something: a want, a relationship, or a piece of knowledge.
  - **Vosk's working day** on the Terrace: the chip prods and the Ilders' yard. He is a person with his own life, the hero tale and the sock.
  - **Eli's day among the rich kids.** Show his flaw on the page: he's devoted to the *idea* of his friends and misses the real people. He doesn't listen. His dog-kindness toward orcs, with Vosk as the exception. Never state the thesis.
  - **Teo:** a private scene of his own (texture, not competence), and his first warning.
  - **The household under his father's reach:** the account, the steward, the gate, and the unopened letter he keeps refusing.
  - **How the gift forms:** when Eli decides, and what Vosk actually wants against what Eli hears.
- **End:** they leave the house. The tension rises from the start of this chapter, but the danger hasn't begun.
- **Seeds:** about 4–5 Book 1 seeds and 1–2 future-book seeds.

## TWO · The Scald (the quickening, about 8–9k words)
- **Base:** v9 from "They left the house just after eleven." to the end.
- **Expand the descent and the market.** Take the time the pacing needs, with the pressure building and the market watching.
- **Keep everything else:** the King, the pinned arch, the speech, the climb, and the morning count.
- **Seeds:** the remaining Book 1 seeds (the King lives, Teo's cool head) and 1–2 future-book seeds.
