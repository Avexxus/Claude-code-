## Flags, ranked by how much they break belief

### 1. Doc walks out on a full ward within a minute of one shove
> "Well," he said. "That's my mind made up." (L435)

- **Category:** leap, with a risk of reading as a quest marker.
- **Why it reads wrong:** a man who has sat with the dying for decades, and who just stood and took the shouting without moving, drops a room of patients and a wall of the dying because one grieving son shoved a stranger. Nothing on the page shows the room itself turning on him, so the "why now" is only in the notes.
- **Smallest fix:** in his slow look round the room (L433), let one face withdraw from him. For example: "The woman with the bucket set it down and didn't pick it up again." Then the room has decided, and Doc is answering it.

### 2. Teo's look at Doc is compared to a chip scar
> "steadily, as he'd looked at the square behind the big loader's ear on the pad at Ithe." (L511)

- **Category:** telling, plus a POV slip.
- **Why it reads wrong:** this is the only line in the chapter that steers the reader toward "something is in him". It also puts that thought in Eli's head, two lines after his "Everyone likes you", which Eli wouldn't think.
- **Smallest fix:** cut the clause after "steadily,". "He looked at him for a long time… steadily" is enough.

### 3. Vosk's coins are left under the pallets with no beat
> "the coins in the sock went across the floor under the pallets, rolling, every way." (L393)

- **Category:** clipped join.
- **Why it reads wrong:** this is Vosk's forty-one, the money Eli logged a claim to keep, and Doc has just said "give it him back". Eli walks out and the coins are never thought of again.
- **Smallest fix:** add one line at L457: "The sock was in his pocket, light. He hadn't gone under the pallets for the rest." That spends S-009 here, at a cost.

### 4. Doc's offer to travel has no visible cause
> "I've had a mind to travel," he said. (L343)

- **Category:** a person acting for the plot (minor).
- **Why it reads wrong:** he offers to come with three strangers right after the work. The wrist "Hm" (L231–233) is the only cause, and it's twenty lines back.
- **Smallest fix:** at L341, have him look at Eli's bad hand before he looks "all the way up". That one glance gives his reason without saying it.

### 5. Which night is it?
> "It had hummed all day and all the first night" (L17)

- **Category:** clipped join (low).
- **Why it reads wrong:** the boat left at the end of Ithe day 3 and lands "in the morning". "All day" suggests a full day has already gone by, so the reader can't tell if this is the first night or the second.
- **Smallest fix:** cut "all day and"; or, if it's the second night, say "since Ithe, a day and a night".

### 6. "He's not on anything" is ambiguous
> "No reader," she said. "He's not on anything." (L115)

- **Category:** clipped join (low).
- **Why it reads wrong:** Teo has just run out of pills, so "he" reads as Teo, not Doc's unlogged house.
- **Smallest fix:** "He's not on any list."

## Strict checks

**Is Doc's joining a quest marker?** It passes, narrowly.
- **For it:** Eli says no (the no is shaped as a promise, which fits the calibration). Doc doesn't ask twice. He pays his own passage out of a tin. The cost is on the page: the apron on the nail, one bottle to one woman, and the woman with the quiet baby looking down and up. The trouble is earned: Garro is dying behind the curtain from the moment they walk in, and the son is right by his own lights.
- **The weak point:** the why-now is under-built (flag 1). It's the classic shape of "the healer heals you, then joins". Only the room's reaction breaks that pattern, so give the room one beat.

**Is his nature explained?** No. Narration never links the curtain to the healing. "Three" is said to the stove and never asked about. The changeling saying stays a saying, and nobody takes it up. Seffa is only seen from outside.
- The one line that crosses from planting into steering is the chip-scar comparison (flag 2). Cut it.
- After that cut, the clues are the tide, the curtain, "Three", "one minute", the old women, Seffa's look, Teo's look and the number in his sleep. That's at the upper edge, but each one has a plain surface reading.

## Rulings on the writer's doubts
1. **Seffa's "She was behind there… Mam":** **KEEP.** It's flat and spoken, in her voice, and it only tells us the curtain is where the dying go. The reader needs that to hear the breath stop. Eli's "Oh. I'm sorry." and then the lamp is exactly right.
2. **The son's "you were in there one minute":** **KEEP.** On its face it accuses Doc of neglect (he gave my dad one minute), which is what grief would say. The timing link is the reader's to make, not the text's.
3. **The sock coins scattering:** **KEEP the scatter.** Eli's money proving the son's point is the best cost in the scene, and the child's coin is good. Add the one line in flag 3 so the loss is registered and S-009 is spent.
4. **Ammet's red sun, the same as Ithe's:** **KEEP.** FIVE says Ammet is "next world over", so one sun is the natural reading. Neither world is in the codex, so there's no clash. Log it as LOCAL.
5. **Teo's look compared to a chip scar:** **CUT the comparison** (flag 2). Keep the look and "Right."

**For Patrick:** no canon clash, and no protected line touched.
