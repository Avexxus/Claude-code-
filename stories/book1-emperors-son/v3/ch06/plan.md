## SIX · The Healer (about 7–8k words)
**Job:** get Teo to the gnome on Ammet in time, meet Doc, and plant what Doc is without saying it. Doc joins by his own choice. Seffa's place in the group firms up.

1. **The ore boat to Ammet.** Teo is worse: the red line has passed the elbow and he's raving. Seffa nurses him practically, while Eli plans and narrates (Patrick: the softness is the point). One beat where Seffa does what Eli can't, and Eli thanks her as if he arranged it.
2. **Ammet,** a working world, maybe a quarry, or a hospice town along a cold sea. The gnome "who sits with the dying" has a clinic with a queue out the door: the dying come to him, and they go quiet.
3. **Doc.** Small even for a gnome, round in the face, enormous ears. A free man with his own pride.
   - He thumbs up eyelids. He takes the worst behind a curtain and sits with him. "Three." The count of the dead.
   - The goat story: his brother's goat that ate his herb garden.
   - **He heals Teo too well:** the red line simply goes back. Eli takes it as a doctor being good. Seffa looks at Doc a beat too long.
   - **The clue, never explained:** someone dies quietly behind the curtain while Teo gets better. The narration doesn't connect them.
4. **The price.** Doc asks for nothing for himself: "I've had a mind to travel." Eli refuses him ("no money and no bunk"), the same shape as before but softer, because Eli likes him. Doc comes anyway, by his own choice, when trouble arrives at the clinic: an Inquisition notice, or the dying's families. Nobody owns him, and no one has to defer to him.
5. **End:** Teo sitting up and eating. Doc on his bag. Seffa deciding to stay for now ("Somebody's got to feed you"). They head back toward the master: "Bring him back breathing." Eli is pleased with himself.
