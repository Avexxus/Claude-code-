# Ch06 "The Healer": originality and trope check

Sources checked: CLAUDE.md, `codex_v5/WORLD_OVERVIEW.md`, `07_BEINGS` (changelings, THE GNOME CHANGELING), `10_WORLD` (gnomes), `01_world_slice.md`, `02_cast.md` §1.6.

## Ranked findings

### 1. The old women's "neighbour" saying (l.141–145): trope on autopilot. FIX
- **Text:** "Forty year… Never a cold." / "You never know who your neighbour is. Might be two."
- **Problem:** "You never know who your neighbour is" is the stock line for a hidden monster, the one every body-snatcher story uses. It also comes straight after "never a cold," which points at the healer, so two clues land on Doc in a row, before he has even appeared. The reader is nudged rather than left to notice.
- **What the codex already gives you:** "Might be two" is good and could only come from this saga. Changelings are possessors, so a body holds a crowd, not a disguise (`07` t110–t116). BANK 11-01 also says the dread is "obscure and inevitable… cant stop daily life," with no folk tests (cc R037, slice rule 18).
- **Fix:** Cut the stock first half. Keep the possession joke as everyday grumbling about something else (a husband, a landlord), e.g. "Him? There's two of him in there, and neither one's any use." Or move it away from "never a cold" so the two clues don't stack.

### 2. "Garro" (l.435): name close to a known work. Passing nod, FIX (optional)
- Nathaniel Garro is a lead character in the Horus Heresy books (*Garro*, Black Library) and well known to Warhammer readers. It isn't a copyright problem, but it's a name that fandom recognises, and Garrosh (WoW) is nearby.
- **Fix:** Give him a quarry-folk LOCAL name in the same family as Ammet, Seffa and Oskel. Log it as LOCAL.

### 3. The healer who takes a life to save a life (l.265–313): watch, keep
- **Risk:** The order reads as a one-for-one trade. The breath stops behind the curtain, Doc goes in, comes out, says "Three," and the infection drains. That's the stock "only death can pay for life" device (the best-known example is Mirri Maz Duur in *A Game of Thrones*). It's recognisable as a trope, but no specific work is copied: there's no ritual, no bargain, no price spoken.
- **Why it stays:** This is canon L24 (BANK 11-01, cc R145). The village healer eats the sick slowly and says "I wasn't able to save him." Doc's healing is skill boosted by the souls he holds, and nothing is used up (BANK 10-31). "Three," said to the stove, already shows it's a running total, not a single swap. Keep "Three" so the trade never reads as cause and effect.

### 4. "Doc" and "Ammet": passing nods, note only
- **Doc:** A small being called Doc echoes Disney's dwarf. The nickname is generic and already logged as a LOCAL alias (L-E2). No action.
- **Ammet** (named in ch05, called back here): it's close to Ammit, the Egyptian devourer of the dead. That's mythology, not anyone's IP, and it fits a soul-eater's island darkly well. Keep it, or change it if Patrick doesn't want the wink.
- **Seffa** and **Ithe**: no matches found. Clean.

### 5. Stock devices, done specifically: keep
- **Healer distracts the patient with a rambling story** (the goat). The device is stock, but it carries the brother plant (L-E3, `02` §1.6: it's the brother's goat) and the party gnome's "jokes at the wrong moments" (`07` t762). That's earned.
- **Healer refuses payment.** It's turned around: Doc pays his own fare "out of a tin." That serves the brief's rule that Doc is never a commodity and comes by his own choice. Earned.
- **Crowd turns on the privileged outsider.** It's rooted in the flesh economy ("No reader. He's not on any list"), the coat with yellow cuffs, and the sock. Earned.
- **Mysterious healer.** Avoided. Doc is garrulous, comic and plain, and the menace sits in the gaps, not in a hooded stranger. Good.

## Clearly original (protect)
- "I wasn't able to save him" spoken to Garro's son: Patrick's own canon line (L24), used exactly as banked.
- "Three," said to the stove. The cold fingers straight out of steaming water. The ears that flatten and turn toward a cough before the rest of him does.
- The infection draining "like a tide going out" while a man's breathing stops behind the blanket, and Teo's eyes going to that blanket.
- Seffa lying full length on Teo through the fit, and her steady look at Doc's face while her own face "did nothing at all."
- Eli's "I'll send a boat… a whole wing," then "Suit yourself," then "I'm not in your bunk, I'm on my bag." Doc chooses to come, and Eli's patronage is shown up without anyone saying so.
- The sock of coins (spilt, a child's palm on one), the bread broken in three, "Somebody's got to feed you," Teo's sausage end by Doc's boot.
- Ammet as a quarry world: the stepped cliff with men on its ledges, grey snow, the barge with a lid and a horn count.
