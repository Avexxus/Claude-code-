**Verdict:** v2's dialogue already passes Patrick's test. There are no "as you know" speeches, the voices can be told apart with the tags covered, and most questions get missed or dodged. So this is a light pass: 6 changes.

## Changes
1. **L85, Eli after the fit:** cut "That was exactly right." It said the same thing as "That's it" a third time, and "exactly" was already his word in the Jon spin. The stammer, then "Well done. Thank you.", lands harder without it.
2. **L165, the old woman:** "…before someone has it off you, and you with it." → "Before someone has it off you." She had two sharp ripostes in a row ("Ask him", then this). Cutting the tail breaks the neatness.
3. **L389, Garro's son:** "And you took him first, in front of us. In front of my dad. Since Thursday we sat out there. On that wall." → "And you took him. In front of my dad. Since Thursday, on that wall, my dad—". Grief doesn't make a tidy case. It breaks off on his father. "Walked in this morning" (K1) and "Since Thursday" are kept.
4. **L435, Seffa:** cut two of the four "Come on"s and the repeated "Come and sit with him". She's practical, so it's one ask, one reason, then "Come on" (which the narration repeats at L437).
5. **L481, the ramp man:** cut the spoken "One at a time." His fingers already open one at a time in the narration, so saying it explained the gesture.
6. **Teo's "Right" restored** after the sausage, before Eli's yard speech. It's a protected line, and v2 had cut it. It isn't the closer: the chapter still ends on the cheer through the iron, so v2's payoff concern (no second "Right" closer after FIVE) still holds. Eli talks straight over it, which is on design.
