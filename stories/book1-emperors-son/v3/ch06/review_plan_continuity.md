## Must change before drafting

### P1. Teo's line is past the shoulder now, not the elbow (timeline / factual)
- **Plan §1:** "Teo is worse: the red line has passed the elbow and he's raving." **§3:** "the red line simply goes back."
- **Page:** "the line went up past the elbow and on" / "It's past the shoulder." / "Under the collar on that side, against the neck, the skin was pink" (5:L391–397). Day 3: "the pink went up the neck now under the jaw" (5:L559). On the boat Seffa pulls the coat "to the neck, over the pink" (5:L609).
- **Fix:** §1 "the pink has gone up his neck to the jaw". §3 "the pink goes back down off his neck". The elbow was FOUR (4:L401).

### P2. Seffa "staying" has nothing to stay against (plot logic / characterization)
- **Plan §5:** "Seffa deciding to stay for now ('Somebody's got to feed you'). They head back toward the master."
- **Page:** she is already going back. "Four days… There, and him seen, and back. Four. They'll have to make their own dinner… They'll eat the onions raw." (5:L599). She cooks for the master's yard: "Ten years I've fed you." (5:L371). Oskel expects her: "Bring the pie Thursday" (5:L435). If all three head back to the master's yard, where she lives, she can't "decide to stay".
- **Fix (pick one):** (a) make the choice about **coming on with them past Ithe** (it pays off later, when they leave the master's yard), so in SIX it's only a line like "Somebody's got to feed you" said about the road back, not a decision; or (b) move "Seffa staying" out of SIX's seed list and plant it in the chapter where they leave Ithe. Keep "Seffa's place in the group firms up" as the job for SIX.

### P3. Eli's "no money" goes against what he believes and says (characterization)
- **Plan §4:** "Eli refuses him ('no money and no bunk'), the same shape as before but softer."
- **Page:** Eli offers money every time and believes he has it: "I can pay. I'll pay you. Whatever Pask's boys paid, double, three times… there's an account, I'll have it rung through" (5:L263); "There'll be a way to ring from here, there always is, and they'll fly someone" (5:L489). He still has the 41 marks in his trouser pocket (5:L335); the ore-boat man refused them (5:L595). Voice note calibration: he "narrates, promises and arranges futures".
- **Fix:** either give the refusal a reason in Eli's own frame ("there's no room", "it's not safe for you", "I've a teacher waiting") while he still talks of the account; or, if "no money" is the beat, show the account failing on Ammet first (R-040 only covered the beater: "Nothing rings out of here", 4:L411). Also say which "before" the shape echoes: on the page the refusal shape is the master's to Eli ("No.", 5:L237–265).

## Carry into the draft

### C1. No pills, and what Seffa carries (factual)
- **Plan §1:** "he's raving… Seffa nurses him practically."
- **Page:** whites gone, "None" (5:L481); yellows: two given, "one went in the mud and he left it" (5:L543). After them Teo's "eyes had come back… they were his own" (5:L587). Seffa has "a bag… canvas", "bread", "the cup" (5:L563, L597). Nothing else is listed.
- **Fix:** raving must come back on its own after the yellow wears off; nobody has "Yellow if he goes off his head" left. Seffa nurses with water, the cup, the coat, cold cloths: what's in her bag or on the boat.

### C2. The trip has a clock: four days (timeline)
- **Plan:** no time given for the boat or Ammet.
- **Page:** "Four days… There, and him seen, and back. Four." (Seffa, 5:L599). Ammet is "Next world over, where the ore boats go" (5:L511).
- **Fix:** keep the crossing short (about a day each way) so "back" fits four days, or let Seffa say the clock slipped. Thursday's pie (5:L435) can be a light tick.

### C3. Eli has no coat; the letter is soft (factual)
- **Plan:** silent.
- **Page:** Eli "in his shirt, in the cold. His teeth were going." The coat is over Teo, the blanket over the coat (5:L597, L603). "The letter had gone soft and taken the curve of his ribs, and one corner had come to pulp where the wet got in." (5:L603). The sock is in his trouser pocket (5:L335).
- **Fix:** Eli stays coatless until he takes it back or Teo gives it. The coat-as-price-tag (R-028) now sits on Teo. The letter is still owed at the throne room ("Did you forget my letter, boy?"); if it gets wetter, decide now whether the words survive.

### C4. The hand's pace (factual)
- **Plan:** silent.
- **Page:** ends at "it went partway, and stopped, the fingers curled" (5:L611). Cold sets it back to "white and stiff" (5:L373); a big reach locks it shut (4:L311; 5:L195–197).
- **Fix:** carry "partway" as the baseline; the cold on the boat may stiffen it. **Decide whether Doc touches Eli's hand.** If he heals it as he heals Teo, the hand clock ends in SIX and should be a choice, not a side effect.

### C5. Jon's line: Eli never had a name (characterization / R-034)
- **Plan Must:** "Jon gets one light line (Eli wondering where he is)."
- **Page:** "There was meant to be a man. Who'd find us, he'd—" There wasn't a name to put to him." (5:L225). Eli assumed Pask was the man: "That'll be him." (5:L93). Nobody confirmed it.
- **Fix:** keep the line inside what Eli has: Jon, "a man there'll find you", Pask dead. Don't let Eli or anyone say Jon's man *was* Pask, or why Jon was wrong.

## Author calls

### A1. Doc's place: "clinic" against "He's not a doctor" and the reader rule (world rules)
- **Plan §2:** "The gnome… has a clinic with a queue out the door."
- **Page:** "He's not a doctor. Some of them get up." (Seffa, 5:L515). The clinic at the arch has "a reader on the door"; Teo: "They log it." (5:L119–121, L399–401; R-017).
- **Call:** don't call it a clinic in Seffa's mouth or the narration, or Teo will refuse it on the pattern. Show **no reader** at the door. "Doc" as a name against "He's not a doctor" can be a deliberate turn; make it a choice.

### A2. Seffa has been there before (characterization)
- **Plan §3:** "Seffa looks at Doc a beat too long."
- **Page:** "I took my mother. Last winter… She died well. He was good to her… Some of them get up." (5:L511–515).
- **Call:** Doc may know her, and she knows his work. Her long look has to be at something **new** (the speed, the line going back, the death behind the curtain), not a first sight. One beat of recognition, or one line about her mother, keeps it straight.

### A3. An Inquisition notice would name the Inquisition early, and may show faces (plot logic / reveal schedule)
- **Plan §4:** "trouble arrives at the clinic: an Inquisition notice, or the dying's families."
- **Page / schedule:** "the Inquisition" is not yet on the page (state, Names). The squad is planned for EIGHT / mid-book (ch04 plan; brief §3). FOUR's notice: TERRORIST CELL, the face boxes "TO FOLLOW" (4:L107).
- **Call:** prefer **the dying's families**. If a notice is used, keep it unnamed (TERRORIST CELL wording) and decide whether the faces are filled. Filled faces would make Eli and Teo known on Ammet, which drives SEVEN and EIGHT.
