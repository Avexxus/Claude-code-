# SIX: character sim (v3)
🌊 Story clay. This is a sim only, not the scene's dialogue. Sources: ch06/plan.md (with its fixes), story_state (end of FIVE), voice_notes, BANK 11-01 (the healer who took the sick: "I wasn't able to save your loved one"), 11-01 (changeling paranoia: nobody guards against it), 11-08 G-items, 11-10 (no quest markers), 11-11 (Doc: never owned, proud, "just not a slave").

**The trouble I picked: the dying's families.** It comes from the man behind the curtain. His son (LOCAL, unnamed) has queued for days with his father. Doc takes Teo out of turn, and the father dies while Teo gets up. The son doesn't accuse Doc of anything strange. He says only what anyone would: *you took the off-worlder first.* That puts the canon line on the page, "I wasn't able to save him," and nobody looks behind it. There's no reader and no official, so it doesn't step on EIGHT.

## Doc's voice (new; for voice_notes once drafted)
- **He rambles to deflect.** When anyone gets near *how* or *what*, he's off into a story, usually a goat, and then lands on a flat, practical order: "...and it ate the lot, roots and all. Lift his head. No, the other way."
- **He restarts with "Now."** Sometimes twice: "Now. Now then."
- **He asks questions and doesn't wait.** He answers them himself, wrong on purpose, and lets you correct him.
- **He talks to the sick as if they were old acquaintances,** never "patient" and never "love" (love is Seffa's word): "There you are. Took your time." He says it even to strangers.
- **He counts.** Under his breath, to nobody: "Three." He never explains it.
- **He never says sir, never thanks anyone for coming, and never bows.** He's proud, so he insults the work instead of the person: "Who taped this? A blind cobbler?"
- **His tells are physical:** he thumbs up eyelids, sits on the bag, and his ears move before his face does.

**Samples (voice only):**
- "My brother had a goat. Did I say? Ate my whole herb garden in a night: feverfew, the lot, and the fence. Then it lay down in the hole and looked at me like I'd planted it there for him. Now. Hold that lamp."
- "Off-world, are you? I can smell it. No, I can't. It's the coat. Out of the way, coat."
- "Some of them get up. That's the whole trick. Some of them do."

## Scene 1 · The ore boat (Seffa, Eli, Teo)

**TEO**
- **Want:** for the cup to go back where it belongs and to be on the step at nine. When he's lucid, he wants nobody fussing.
- **Would do:** rave in broken orders about home. Push the coat off. In a clear second, look at Eli and say one flat true thing. He won't let Eli see him need Seffa.
- **Would never:** say "I'm scared," or ask Eli for anything. He doesn't explain the line; Seffa can see it.
- **Voice:** "—the step. He's to—" / "It's at my jaw." / "Right."
- **Plan friction:** none. The fix holds: the line is at the jaw, not the elbow, so the opening has to read as a race against the hum of the boat.

**SEFFA**
- **Want:** to keep him under the blanket, keep him drinking, and get there.
- **Would do:** hold Teo's head and get water into him a thimble at a time. Use her own body heat. Talk to the hull ("Come on, you rusty—"). Give Eli a job to keep him out of her way.
- **Would never:** tell Eli he's useless, or comfort him at Teo's expense. She doesn't pray.
- **Voice:** "Hold that. Not like that. Like it's a baby." / "He's hot, love. That's all right. Hot's alive."
- **The beat where she does what Eli can't:** Teo has to go flat and be held down through a fit, and Eli's hand won't grip. She lies across Teo. Eli says "Good, yes, that's it. That's what I'd have—" and thanks her as if he'd sent her. In character on both sides. Let her say nothing. That silence is her answer to "He said yes" at the end of FIVE.

**ELI**
- **Want:** to arrive with Teo alive and the gnome already won over. He wants to be the one who fixed it.
- **Would do:** narrate the future: the clinic, the gnome, the window room. Plan aloud what he'll say to the gnome. Count the horns. Try the hand and hide it.
- **Would never:** go quiet and just hold Teo. He'd sooner talk.
- **Voice:** "We'll be there by the next horn. I'll talk to him; you just lie there. He'll be marvellous. Seffa says he's marvellous."
- **The Jon line goes here:** in a lull, "He'd know what to do. Wherever he's got to." Then he moves on. No interpretation.

## Scene 2 · Ammet and the queue (Doc, Seffa, Eli)

**SEFFA**
- **Want:** to get Teo in. **Underneath:** this is the room where her mam died last winter.
- **Would do:** know the way without looking. Stop at the door one breath too long. Say the hard thing flat: "No reader. He's not on anything." That's the fix's one mention. Join the queue, because she queued last time.
- **Would never:** jump the queue herself, or tell Eli about her mother again.
- **Voice:** "Back of the line, love. They're all dying. That's what the line is."

**ELI**
- **Want:** Teo seen *now*.
- **Would do:** his social voice: "Good morning. I'm hoping— He's very bad, you see." He offers money (the 41, or the account). He asks for the best. He doesn't see the queue as people.
- **Would never:** wait his turn quietly. He doesn't threaten either; he charms and pleads.
- **Voice:** "It's not that I think we're more— It's only that he's young. Honestly. Ask anyone."
- **⚑ Flag:** Eli pushing in is in character. Doc taking Teo *because* Eli asked is not. That would make Doc servile to the coat. **Fix:** Doc chooses for his own reasons. He comes down the line thumbing eyelids, looks at Teo's jaw, and says "That one." He ignores Eli's pitch completely, which is where the comedy is. On the page his reason reads as triage. The real reason stays in the notes (below).

**DOC**
- **Want (notes only, never on the page):** he has a dying man behind the curtain tonight and a boy who'd be dead by morning. He also wants off Ammet. His count here is getting long, and the families have started remembering faces.
- **Would do:** come out wiping his hands. Walk the line. Thumb up eyelids. Say good morning to the dying by habit, as if he'd met them before. Pass Eli by.
- **Would never:** greet Eli as anyone special, take the money in front of the queue, or let anyone kiss his hand. When someone tries, he takes the hand back: "Don't. I've had it in a goat."
- **Voice:** "That one. And the coat can carry him; it's the least the coat can do. Now."
- **No veneration check:** the queue is tired and sour, not reverent. One woman mutters as they're taken in front of her. Plant that. It's the seed of the families.

## Scene 3 · The healing; someone dies behind the curtain (Doc, Eli, Seffa)

**DOC**
- **Want:** to do it quickly and cleanly while everyone looks at the right thing.
- **Would do:** give Eli something to hold. A lamp, a basin, anything to keep his eyes busy. Start a goat story the moment the work starts, and keep it going through the worst. Cut the tape and grumble at whoever taped it. At one point he goes behind the curtain "to see to old so-and-so" and comes back. When he comes back he says, quietly and to nobody, "Three." Then he's back to the goat.
- **Would never:** explain anything, glow, chant, or say "soul." He won't show strain. The cost is invisible, which is the opposite of the master's thumb of blood. Keep that contrast and don't name it.
- **Voice:** "...so my brother says, it's a goat, what did you expect, and I said, I expected a *fence*, and he said—hold still, Teo, you've a lovely jaw, I'd hate to lose it—he said a fence is only a suggestion to a goat."
- **The hand:** while Eli holds the lamp, Doc's fingers close once round Eli's bad wrist, turn it, and let go. "Hm." Nothing else. Don't fix it (that's saved).

**ELI**
- **Want:** to be useful and to be right about the gnome.
- **Would do:** hold the lamp badly. Laugh at the goat, too much. Take the red line going back as proof: "You see? The best one there is. I said." He doesn't hear the curtain go quiet.
- **Would never:** look behind the curtain. He doesn't ask what "Three" means. He'll think Doc is counting stitches.
- **Voice:** "He's going down. The red. Teo, it's going down. I *told* you."

**SEFFA**
- **Want:** Teo up. **Underneath:** she has seen "some of them get up" before.
- **Would do:** watch the line go back. Then look at Doc a beat too long. She hears the curtain go quiet, because she's the only one who knows what that quiet sounds like here. She says nothing. Later she brings the son a cup.
- **Would never:** voice the link, even to herself in dialogue. If she has any thought on the page, keep it to her mother: "She'd been behind that one."
- **⚑ Flag:** the plan says "the narration doesn't connect them." Seffa is the biggest risk, because a POV slip into her head would connect them. Stay in Eli's POV, so Seffa's look is only seen from outside.

**TEO (brief)**
- **Would do:** come back to himself and look at the curtain. If he says anything, it's flat and to an object: "It's gone quiet." **⚑ Risk:** even that may connect the two. The safer call is a look and nothing more. The author decides.

## Scene 4 · The ask, the refusal, the families (Doc, Eli, Seffa)

**DOC**
- **Want (notes only):** to leave. He uses these three as the door: a boat already going, a yard on Ithe with a strong old man, and a boy whose wrist he has just felt.
- **Would do:** take nothing for Teo. Wave the money away and insult it: "Put that back in your trousers." Then, idly, as if it had just occurred to him: "I've had a mind to travel." He'd make it sound like a whim, with a goat in it.
- **Would never:** beg. He doesn't ask twice, and he doesn't argue when Eli says no. He shrugs and goes back to work, because his pride won't let him plead.
- **Voice:** "I've had a mind to travel. Fifty years I've had it. Longer. It keeps, a mind like that, like cheese."

**ELI**
- **Want:** to repay Doc grandly, later, on his own terms.
- **⚑ Flag, the biggest:** a flat *refusal* fights the calibrated Eli. Eli says yes to everything. He arranges futures. **Fix that keeps the beat:** his refusal comes out as a promise. "Not now. We're going back to a yard, there's no room, Teo needs the— But when I'm set up, I'll send for you. I will. You'll have a whole wing." It's a no shaped like a kindness, softer because he likes Doc, and it fits the "no bunk / Teo needs the room" fix. Doc hears the no inside it.
- **Voice:** "You'll come later. Properly. I'll send a boat. Honestly."

**THE TROUBLE (the families)**
- The son comes out from behind the curtain. He saw Teo carried in ahead of his father, and now he sees Teo sitting up. He says it plainly, then louder: "You took him first. Off-worlder in a coat, and you took him first." Others in the queue join in. The woman who muttered in Scene 2 is one of them. They aren't a mob with torches. They're sick, poor people who've waited, and one grief tips the rest.
- **Doc:** says the line once, without any performance: "I wasn't able to save him." Then he lets them shout. **Never** cowering, never pleading, never hitting back.
- **Eli:** reaches for the steward voice: "If there's some sort of— I'm happy to pay for the—" It makes things worse. That's what his money does here: it proves the son's point.
- **Seffa:** the one who handles it. She takes the son's arm, says his father's name if she knows it, and brings him a cup. She's the reason no one gets hurt.
- **Why Doc goes:** nobody drives him out. He looks at the room and picks up his bag, and he could stay and outlast them. What makes it his own choice is that he goes *now* instead of in a month: "Well. That's my mind made up." **The cost:** he leaves the clinic and the queue behind. Someone in that line won't be seen. Let one face register it. That's what keeps it from reading as a quest marker (BANK 11-10).
- **Eli's refusal is mooted, not overturned:** Doc doesn't ask again. He walks to the ore boat and pays his own passage, and Seffa's debt only covers them. He does it before Eli can object. Eli finds him already sitting there. Nobody owns him and nobody has to defer to him.

## Scene 5 · The end: Teo eating, Doc on his bag

**TEO**
- **Want:** food. And to not be thanked at.
- **Would do:** eat with the good hand, steadily, saying nothing. Look once at Doc, the way he looked at chip scars. Then: "Right."
- **Would never:** say thank you out loud to Doc. He might push the last of the bread toward him.

**DOC**
- **Would do:** sit on his bag, not on the bench Eli offers. Start another goat story nobody asked for. Fall asleep in the middle of it, or seem to.
- **Voice:** "Did I tell you about the second goat? No? Worse than the first. The first one had *principles*."

**SEFFA**
- **Would do:** feed all three of them out of her bag, Doc included, without asking. The hook from the fix goes here, as a hint and not a decision: she looks at the three of them eating and says to the hull, "Somebody's got to feed you." Nobody takes it up. The payoff comes when they leave the master.

**ELI**
- **Would do:** lean back, pleased. Narrate the arrival: "Bring him back breathing. Well. He's breathing. He's *eating*." He takes it as his own win, says the master will say yes now, and includes Doc in the plan he just refused him: "The old man'll like you. Everyone likes you."
- **The letter:** check it's dry inside his shirt and touch the soft corner. No coat.

## Summary of flags (where the plan makes people act for the plot)
1. **Doc taking Teo out of turn** has to be Doc's choice, not Eli's pull. Otherwise he reads as servile to the coat.
2. **Eli's "refusal"** against his calibration. Write it as a future-tense promise that means no.
3. **Doc joining** needs a cost: the clinic and the queue left behind. He pays his own boat, so it isn't a quest marker or a favour.
4. **Seffa's long look:** stay out of her head or the clue gets connected.
5. **Teo noticing the curtain:** a look, not a line.
6. **The families' anger** must be fair grief, not villainy. The son is right by his own lights.
7. **Never on the page:** Doc's real reasons for taking Teo first, for leaving, and for choosing these three. Same rule as for Jon.

**Future-book seed (spoken spook):** an old woman in the queue: "You never know who your neighbour is. Might be two. Might be three." She says it about Doc's luck, as a saying. Nobody takes it up. It sits close to his "Three." Keep both, and don't link them.
