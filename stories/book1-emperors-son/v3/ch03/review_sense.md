The holes are all in the dawn kitchen and the logic of how Jon got in. The Jon rule mostly holds: four small slips, all cuts.

Ranked by how much each breaks belief.

## 1. Eli isn't afraid of a big stranger in his kitchen the morning after "He's asking"
> "He glanced up at Eli in the doorway and smiled. / 'Morning,' he said. / … / Eli opened his mouth." (L295–301)

**Category:** a person acts for the plot (a leap).
**Why:** Eli had a cutter in his ear three nights ago, and the King's threat came up the stair yesterday. A large man he has never seen, sitting in his chair at dawn, would frighten him before "Vosk" could disarm him, but the page goes straight from the doorway to an open mouth.
**Fix:** add one body beat before "Sorry about Vosk": his grip shifts on the boots, or his eyes go to the yard door, where the steward is. Then the name is what stops the fear, which is the charsim's cause.

## 2. The count runs outside a lit kitchen window with a stranger in it, and the house never sees him
> "The kitchen light was on… There was a man at the table… Out in the yard the steward read a number, and somebody answered." (L291–299, L359)

**Category:** the world wouldn't produce it.
**Why:** in TWO the steward reads the count "up at the kitchen light" (2:L589), so he is facing a lit window with a man eating plums in it. The cook comes in at seven (L3), and Jon sits there until "the count long done" (L513). Nobody in a house that runs on numbers registers him.
**Fix:** one shown line, with no comment: "On the step the steward looked up at the kitchen light, and read the next number." That makes it a Jon seed rather than a hole. Or seat Jon out of the window's line, and get the three of them out before seven.

## 3. "Nobody came in" rests on a log invented on cue, and the page has already shown other ways in
> "It was the gate. The Ninth Terrace gate's own page, the one any house on Ninth could open…" (L455) / "Nobody came in, Eli." (L457)

**Category:** convenience, and it weakens Teo's warning #2.
**Why:** the gate page first appears at the moment Teo needs it. ONE has also shown flyers settling at the kerb inside the Terrace (1:L179), Eli offers to send a flyer at L25, and this chapter's slot lets humans through unread. So Eli's "So there's another way" (L463) is simply right, and the warning goes soft.
**Fix:** plant the page earlier. For example, at four on Day 3 Teo thumbs it while Eli talks, or the steward checks it after the call. Then let Teo's slate also show nothing landing at the kerb, so the only open door is one neither of them knows.

## 4. A human-sized hole the enclave doesn't watch (note for Patrick, not a fix)
> "The disc in the wall stayed dark. It didn't look at him." (L553)

**Category:** the world wouldn't produce it (a clash to note).
**Why:** a Terrace that greets every resident by the hour leaves a door that reads no humans at all, and Eli and Teo walked through the biting front gate in TWO without ever thinking of it. The NOTES say Patrick accepted this rule, so I'm not proposing a change.
**For Patrick:** if the rule stands, item 3 needs the fix above. If not, a disc that is normally lit and is dark this one morning gives the same beat without the hole.

## 5. The fist opens with no step in between
> "Eli's hand closed. It went all the way… and he held it shut at his side." (L333) → "'Who?'" (L339) → "Eli sat down." (L353)

**Category:** a leap.
**Why:** Jon has just said, smiling, that Vosk's body "goes by weight". Eli's rage is in the fist, and then he answers questions and sits down as if it had never happened.
**Fix:** keep the fist shut through the Rauk lines, and give one clause at L353: "His hand opened. Eli sat down."

## 6. Eli confesses his reach failure when nothing has asked about it
> "'I can't do it,' Eli said. 'When people are looking.'" (L391)

**Category:** a leap (a missing cue).
**Why:** Jon has offered "a crew" and "a job". Nothing on the page ties the job to the reach, so Eli is answering a question that only the author has heard.
**Fix:** add Eli's "What would you want me for?" and, as action only, Jon's eyes going to Eli's hand on the table. Then Eli's outburst follows.

## 7. Jon strictness: places where the narration or Eli reads him
- **"He said it round the plum, plainly"** (L305). "Plainly" is the narrator judging his manner. Cut "plainly".
- **"The man turned the plum to find the next bite."** (L315). The purpose clause gives him a reason. Make it "turned the plum and bit it." (L413's "turned it to the next bite" is fine.)
- **"The man was looking at Eli, not at the watch."** (L349). The narrator is pointing out what he doesn't do, which is the reading the charsim warned against. Make it "The man was looking at Eli."
- **"He was very clear."** (L463). This is Eli's verdict on Jon, and the charsim says Eli mustn't interpret him either. Cut it; the quoted terms after it already carry the beat.
- **These pass:** "The man waited" (L371), "still smiling" (L523), the single look at the letter's corner (L427), and the walk without turning (L541). All of these are action.

## 8. The watch strap's soap points to a plant that isn't there
> "the strap smelled sharp, of a soap with something in it that stung" (L253)

**Category:** a cool moment with nothing under it.
**Why:** the NOTES call this Velk's soap, but TWO gives Velk a brush and a basin, never a smell, so no reader can make the link.
**Fix:** cut it to the grey dust in the links, or tie it to something TWO does show (her "cold pad", 2:L245).

## 9. "Three mornings" is four
> "Teo had run it back with his thumb, three mornings" (L455)

**Category:** a clipped join (timeline).
**Why:** the log runs from Eli's Day 2 dawn line to Day 5 dawn, which is four mornings.
**Fix:** use "four mornings", or "back to the morning they came up".

## 10. The new orc's sacks come from the wrong place
> "the new one came back up the culvert with a sack on each shoulder" (L259)

**Category:** a clipped join.
**Why:** the Spur crews leave the sacks stacked at the top of the slot (1:L163, and L139 here), so there is no reason to climb the culvert with them.
**Fix:** use "came back through from the stack at the slot with a sack on each shoulder".

## 11. Small telling and body points (low)
- **"the voice for a fee, and let it"** (L463). This is the narrator's judgement inside a naive Eli, who doesn't watch himself. Use "the steward voice" (2:L341 already set it), or cut it.
- **"the swelling gone yellow and green"** (L169). On Day 3, about 60 hours after the slap, a bruise would still be dark. Use "gone dark", or let the yellow wait for Day 5.
