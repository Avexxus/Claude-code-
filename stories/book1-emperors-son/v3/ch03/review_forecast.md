# Forecast review: Ch03 "Company"

What I read: only `BOOK1_ch01-02_STORY_ONLY.md`, then `ch03_STORY_ONLY.md`. No notes, no codex.
Sections are split at the `---` breaks, which gives 10. I made each guess before reading the section it covers.

---

## Before S1 (end of Ch02: the steward reads "Vosk?" and makes a mark)

**Guess:** It's the morning after. The 1,100-mark call brings the "father's people" or the woman in grey to the door, or Eli finally opens the letter. Confidence 3.
**Curious:** Does the King come up the stair after Eli? What is in the letter?

## S1: Kitchen at seven (the brush, the cold eggs, Teo goes home)

**Grade: NEAR.** The aftermath is right, but nobody arrives and the letter stays shut. The pink brush in the bin and the eggs scraped on top of it do the grief without anyone saying it. Teo's line "They log it" sets up both the hand and the account as a trap. The section is short and quiet, and that's correct for it.

**Next guess:** Eli does something. He rings the account, rings a clinic, or tries to go back down. Confidence 2.
**Curious:** Is his power gone? On the stair his hand wouldn't make a fist.

## S2: Bed, bath, the barracks sings

**Grade: MISS (good).** He does nothing at all, and the scene is about that. The setup is there: the hand "closed partway and stopped" picks up "it wouldn't go" from the stair, so the broken power reads as earned. The Hurrak song with the voices leaning on the name is a strong payoff of the Ch01 song thread. Tension drops here. The one forward hook is the half-closed fist.

**Next guess:** At the morning count the wait for Vosk's number doesn't come. Eli goes to the bunk and gets the forty-one marks out of the sock. Confidence 4, since the sock has been planted twice ("at the back, against the wall").
**Curious:** Does Eli take the money, and what does he think it's for?

## S3: The count, the barracks, the sock

**Grade: HIT at confidence 4.** By the rule, the scene may need a turn. It does carry two small ones:
- The steward reads straight through with no pause. Vosk is erased, not mourned, and that lands.
- The little one asks "Did they catch him?" That ties Vosk to Hurrak, and the smaller child hitting him for it is good.

The "Seeds. Unknown." twist of paper is a fine new plant. Overall, though, the beat goes where the reader expects. It could be sharper if the steward's inventory held one thing we didn't know Vosk had (the seeds come close), or if handing over the sock cost Eli something.

**Next guess:** The woman rings. The steward reports the missing orc, and maybe Eli. Confidence 3.
**Curious:** Who is "madam," and is the steward working for her?

## S4: The house bell

**Grade: HIT/NEAR.** It's the call, and the steward reports "Nothing drawn. Not since." and "One short." The turn is that the steward has plainly reported on Eli for years, and when Eli asks "Was that her?" he gets only "Sir." "One up from the depot for the count tomorrow" is a clean plant. It is short and effective.

**Next guess:** Eli goes after Ilder about Vosk's dying story (the fob, the dance, the bite). Confidence 3.
**Curious:** Is Ilder's crescent scar Eli's bite? (I'm fairly sure it is now.)

## S5: Teo at four, "put back to the spring," the coat

**Grade: NEAR.** The Ilders' party is set up here, not played. This is the weakest section for tension. It mostly joins S4 to S6:
- Teo's hand gets a "winced," but there's no escalation yet.
- "Put back to the spring" exists to feed Eli's cruel line in S9. It works there, but here it just lands flat.
- The brown thread of blood on the cleaned cuff is the best beat in the section.

It's a candidate for compression or for a new pressure (see the list at the end).

**Next guess:** At the party Dace jokes about Vosk, and Eli confronts Ilder or hits out. He might try his power on the young Ilder's fob and fail. Confidence 4.
**Curious:** Will Eli say the Ilder memory out loud?

## S6: The Ilders' lawn

**Grade: NEAR (good).** The confrontation starts and gets cut off. Dace repeating "Eighty-eight years" in the voice Eli invented is the chapter's sharpest mirror. Eli sees his own cruelty played back to him, and the reader is a step ahead of Dace.
- "Your yard." followed by Dace's interruption is a strong deflection.
- The glass left upright on the grass, which the Ilders' man can't step out to fetch, is an excellent closing image.
- The feed showing TERRORIST CELL quietly widens the world. Lisane's absence pays off "we go down."

Risk: readers will now be waiting for Eli and Ilder to settle the bite scar. If that doesn't come back within a chapter or two, it will read as dropped, not withheld.

**Next guess:** The replacement orc arrives. The King's people send something up, or someone comes for Eli. Confidence 3.
**Curious:** Is the King alive? He was digging out at the end of Ch02.

## S7: The new one, "He's asking," the watch, the late

**Grade: NEAR, mostly HIT.**
- The new orc is a HIT. Him picking dirt out of the cracks echoes Vosk's hands in the soil.
- The watch coming back up through the Ilders' man is a strong turn, and earned. The watch went into Velk's apron, so its return quietly says Velk was caught or sold out. The stinging soap smell and the grey dust in the links are good tactile proof of where it has been.
- Eli taking the new one's late off repeats the lates he had taken off for Vosk in Ch01. That's ironic and earned: he still believes the gesture is kindness.
- "Walk well" from the Ilders' man repeats the Ch01 farewell. Good.

**Next guess:** Eli rings the account for protection, or tries to bargain with the King, or the woman in grey turns up. Confidence 2.
**Curious:** Will Eli leave the Terrace?

## S8: Jon in the kitchen

**Grade: MISS (mostly good).** I didn't see a recruiter coming. Looking back, the setup partly holds:
- The letter under the bowl sits there through the whole scene, and Jon glances at it.
- "Ends at the top of the Tower" lands on the Tower that Ch01–02 built up, and on Vosk's "It's right there."
- The teacher offered is exactly the failure from Hookline.
- The plum stones as a visible clock are lovely. The "Everything's got a price, son" terms and Eli's "So it's for something" are the hinge of the book, and they're earned.

The weak spot is that Jon knows everything and offers exactly what Eli needs. It's deliberately too neat, and S9 flags it, but there is no earlier trace of him. One small plant before this would turn "came from nowhere" into "oh, that was him."

Eli's fist closing fully "the first time since the stair" is an earned answer to the S2 hook.

**Next guess:** Teo says no, then comes anyway. He raises a doubt about Jon first. Confidence 4 that he comes, 3 that he doubts.
**Curious:** How did Jon get in? Is "the father's people" Jon?

## S9: Teo's room

**Grade: HIT at confidence 4, with the turn already built in.** The gate log showing "Nobody came in" is concrete and turns the stranger into something uncanny. "You've nowhere to be" is Eli at his worst, and it's the right moment for it. The stakes rise quietly:
- The hand is infected, with a sweet smell and a yellow dressing.
- His mother says "a man there who doesn't write things down."
- Eli says "I'll tell him" twice and never does.

Teo taking the stone and leaving the cup is perfect wordless characterisation. The outcome (Teo comes) is predictable, but the cost is not, so it holds.

**Next guess:** They leave with Jon, Eli takes the letter, and the woman rings or arrives as they go. Confidence 4.
**Curious:** Does Jon get past the readers? Does Eli open the letter?

## S10: Departure through the slot

**Grade: HIT at confidence 4,** saved by one real turn: the reader disc stays dark for Eli. He has left the system, or Jon switches it off. Either way it's a clean, eerie close that turns the "Good morning" motif inside out.
- Eli putting on the second-best coat with the sock in its pocket inverts "Not in the coat." Good.
- The letter taken, the house bell ringing with no answer, and "Who let you in?" / "Jon" without an answer all pull forward.
- The brown smear in the slot (Vosk's head, Ch01) is a fine callback.

The shape is right for a grief-then-pivot chapter. The only real worry is the long flat stretch from S2 to S5 before the watch arrives.

## Top three places to add a turn or a setup

1. **S5 (sag): compress it or add pressure.** Fold Teo's visit into S4 or S6, or give it something new: an early sign the hand is going bad (the smell, a hot flush), or a second unanswered call from "madam." Right now S5 carries the invitation, the "spring" line and the coat thread, and nothing changes in it.
2. **Plant Jon before S8.** Use one small trace in S6 or S7. Options:
   - plums appear in the fruit bowl that the cook didn't buy;
   - a big grey-bearded man at the edge of the Ilders' lawn whom nobody introduces;
   - leave "He's asking" ambiguous between the King and someone else.

   Any of these makes the Jon arrival read as earned uncanny rather than convenient.
3. **S3 (HIT at confidence 4): make the sock cost something.** Have the steward resist one beat longer, or let Eli find something in Vosk's things he didn't know about Vosk (the seeds could carry this if Eli reacts to them). Tie it to the S6 Ilder confrontation so the withheld Ilder/bite memory stays alive, and pay it off soon after this chapter.
