## Take
1. **Jon knows a little less.** On first meeting he knows Vosk's name, the chip and "the King's asking". Cut one or two of the exact figures (nine years, the dwarf in Hookline, the two extra), or show that he's been watching longer than Eli realises. Pick the stronger version.
2. **Jon's stock-mentor lines** (panel): rework "There's a teacher" (said once, not twice), "Everything's got a price, son" and "Good view, I'm told" so they sound like him, not like a mentor out of stock. Keep "I'd love ya to come" word for word.
3. **Eli's motive is messier.** Cut the neat "It's for Vosk… he said it was right there." Eli can't sit with the fact that it was eleven hundred marks and a chip. He goes because he can't stay, not because he has a mission.
4. **Pressure on the gap left by Vosk, earlier and harder.** The steward, the house or the Terrace presses on the missing orc sooner: a form, a replacement fee, a question Eli can't answer.
5. **The Ilders' party:** tighten it. Keep Ilder's admission and the mark, and Dace's "Eighty-eight years" with Eli not laughing. Cut some of Dace's performance, and give Eli a rougher exit.
6. **Teo's wound** presses on the choice to leave. One beat where it costs him or scares him: the smell, a fever, or the clinic he's skipping. Doc pays it off later, so no resolution here.
7. **The letter:** taken, heavy and unexplained. No hint of what it says.
8. **Clarity for a casual reader, without explaining** (panel): make "madam" (the account woman), who "he's asking" means, the empty gate log and why the disc is dark legible through context, not exposition.
