# Originality check: FIVE · The Teacher (v3)

🌊 Claude review. Read against `codex_v5` (`08` THE MASTER cc R075, `01` BURNOUT / WILL, `MAGIC_SYSTEM_EXPLAINED` v5-cc, `06` chip) and `v3/ledger_rules.md` R-042, R-043. The text is not changed.

**Patrick's own material in this chapter (protected; not flagged):** "Most masters barely teach", "Don't use more than you can control", students who "got the chip" (BANK 11-16, cc R075); the master teaching Eli to "listen and be slow" (R075).

## Ranked findings

### 1. Dagobah lift (Star Wars, *Empire*). Recognisable. FIX
- **What matches:** wet, muddy yard, and the student lifts something too big for him. He loses it, and a small, old, unassuming master who has been sitting there all along catches it and sets it down gently. Then L229 adds the master's line: "You lifted it blind. You opened your eyes and it fell." On Dagobah Luke lifts with his eyes shut and drops the load when his focus breaks, so that line is the beat that makes a reader think "that's Yoda." L201 ("Did you see the size of that?") brushes "size matters not". It's a passing nod on its own, but it adds to the effect.
- **What already differs:** this is a rescue, not a demonstration. The boys are underneath the pallet. The master pays for it: the nosebleed, the rubbed eyes, the shaking cup by lamplight. Codex fatigue and burnout are paid on the page, and Yoda pays nothing.
- **Fix (codex-rooted):** rewrite L229 so the diagnosis comes from R075 and the Skill dial, not from the eyes. He tells Eli he held it by grabbing, not by listening: Will without Skill. That way the line sets up the crane-listening lesson (R-043) instead of the Jedi eyes-closed beat. Cut or change L201 so "size" isn't the word.

### 2. Name "Hollin". Passing nod. FIX (cheap)
- Hollin is Tolkien's common name for Eregion (*Fellowship*, "the land of Hollin"). It's a place there and a person here, and most readers won't notice. But it's a known Tolkien proper noun in a saga that already has drow and Tolkien-adjacent races. Rename it as a LOCAL name in the human-loader register.

### 3. The gate vigil: refused, waits outside in the weather, accepted (Shaolin gate / Huike in the snow / *Kung Fu*). Trope. WATCH, no fix
- The stock sequence runs in order: refusal (L237–265), waiting days at the gate (L275 onward), acceptance (L577). There's even an unplanned Huike echo: in the legend Huike's arm is sacrificed at the gate, and here Teo's arm goes bad there.
- **Why it isn't on autopilot:** the chapter turns the trope over. Eli thinks he's passing the master's test ("He wants to see who stays"), and the book says he isn't: "Waiting's not why." The master is moved by Teo. Seffa does the real work. The test exists only in Eli's head, and that is the lost-prince charm being shown up.
- **Guard:** a later chapter must never have the master admit it was a test. That would turn the trope back into the stock version.

### 4. Nosebleed as the psychic cost (Stranger Things, Akira, Scanners). Common device. WATCH
- Three nosebleeds here: Eli, the master and Hollin. It's the default image for this kind of strain. It isn't infringement, but it is the autopilot choice.
- **The saga's own signs are already on the page:** Eli's bad hand clenching and not opening (L197, L373, L611); the cup tilting upright by itself; the master's cup shaking; and for the chipped, the laugh that goes on too long and stops all at once (R-042). Keep the nosebleed on Hollin, where the chip and burnout make it diagnostic, and let the hand carry Eli's.

### 5. Disgruntled student: "We sit… Listen to the cranes" (Karate Kid's wax-on). Passing nod. CLEAN
- The shape is "the teacher makes us do something pointless." But the content is Patrick's "listen and be slow" (R075), and "tell me which one's carrying" is sensing-as-Skill (v5-cc). It's a concrete thing the cranes of Ithe give you. Keep it.

### 6. "Don't use more than you can control" / "I know." "You don't." Stock mentor cadence. CLEAN
- The maxim is Patrick's. The exchange around it is ordinary, but L249–253 ("I've buried the ones I taught… went down to the cranes. Got the chip.") ties it to the flesh-and-chip economy straight away. It's no riddle.

### 7. Time-stop rain. Common image (Matrix, X-Men, Doctor Strange). CLEAN
- It belongs to no single work. The details are the saga's own: the wasp shut gently in a hand against a pane, Eli feeling "the size of the hand. There was no end to it", the single word "One.", and the pallet "set down like a cup".

### 8. Other names: Seffa, Pask, Ferrel, Hesk, Ammet, Ithe, the Cut. CLEAN
- Searches found no matching famous character or place. Ammet is faintly close to Ammit, the Egyptian devourer of the dead. It's a passing nod and apt for the island where the dying go, so no change is needed. "The Cut" is a plain descriptive name.

## Clearly original (protect)
- Humans chipping themselves to work as loaders: a shaved square behind the ear, lifting without looking, the too-long laugh that stops dead (R-042).
- The master's hidden bill: the shaking cup at night, the hand pressed under the other hand.
- "Waiting's not why." The acceptance is earned by Teo, not by Eli's vigil.
- Pask's dead yard across the lane, the chain knocking, the boy's shoe; the board chalk washed away to a single P.
- Seffa: the stove-rim scar, "He says no to the weather", the bag packed since the night before, "He eats off my pot".
- The gnome on Ammet who sits with the dying: "Some of them get up."
- The pilot's board light, the watch, and the letter going to pulp against Eli's ribs.

**Verdict: 2 items to fix** (the L229 Dagobah line, and the name Hollin); 2 to watch (the gate vigil must stay subverted later; cut back the nosebleeds).
