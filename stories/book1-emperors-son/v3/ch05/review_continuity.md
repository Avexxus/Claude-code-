## A. Contradictions (fix before lock)

### A1. The headache "for four days" (timeline). MEDIUM
- **Before:** "He'd had it every time he came down, and gone home up the stair and not had it" (4:L7). Eli was on the Terrace from Day 2 to Day 5 dawn (THREE; no headache anywhere in `ch03/draft_v5`). It came back on the morning of Day 5 at the Spur: "It was the headache. It sat in there behind the eye" (4:L7).
- **New:** "He found the place behind his eye where it had lived for four days, and there was nothing in it." (5:L327)
- It lived about one day (Day 5 morning through the flight), not four. "Four days" seems to have come from the hand ("the way it had not shut in four days", 4:L311).
- **Fix (5:L327):** "where it had lived for four days" → "where it had lived since the Spur".

### A2. Where the board is (factual detail). MEDIUM
- **Before:** "Ahead of her was glass… and below the glass her board, lit" (4:L341); "The pilot came up the ramp with her board under her arm" (4:L321). The board is handheld or up front at her chair. It is never on a wall.
- **New:** "The board, on the wall by the hatch. Its little light." (5:L33). FIVE also has it at her chair: "The pilot said something to her board" (5:L7).
- **Fix (5:L33):** "The board, on the wall by the hatch. Its little light." → "The board, up past the hanging door. Its little light." (The beat, Eli stopping a name on her ramp, still works: 4:L219.)

### A3. The pills: white ones were never in a twist (factual detail). LOW-MEDIUM
- **Before:** "There were two kinds in it. White ones, flat and small. And three yellow ones, longer, in a twist of paper of their own. She counted white ones out into his palm, six, and then took the twist with the yellow ones and put that in his palm too, on top." (4:L451). The white pills are loose. The only twist holds the yellow.
- **New:** "found the paper twist, damp, and opened it in his lap. Two white. Three yellow in their own twist inside." (5:L281). Also: "Teo took the last two pills out of the twist himself" (5:L359).
- **Fix (5:L281):** "found the paper twist, damp, and opened it in his lap. Two white. Three yellow in their own twist inside." → "found the pills loose at the bottom, and the twist, damp, and put them in his lap. Two white. The three yellow in their twist."
- **Fix (5:L359):** "out of the twist himself" → "out of the coat pocket himself". The coat is on Teo by then (5:L331), so this also explains how Teo has them.

### A4. "a cutter at someone's ear" (factual detail). LOW

- **New:** "the next time somebody held a cutter at someone's ear—" (5:L345)
- **Fix:** "at someone's ear" → "at his ear".
