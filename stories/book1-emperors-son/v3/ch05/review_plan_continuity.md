## HIGH

### 1. "The name Jon gave": Jon gave no name (P§2)
- **State:** "Ithe," he said. "There's a man there'll find you." (4:L239). Eli asks "Is that the teacher?" and gets no answer (4:L241). The only teacher line is "There's a teacher… You'll not like it much." (3:L465). No trainer's name is on the page.
- **Plan:** "The name Jon gave leads to a trainer who has been dead for months."
- **Smallest fix:** build the wrong steer on the line that exists. Either nobody finds them, so Eli asks round Ithe for "a teacher" and is sent to the dead trainer's shut yard; or "the man" does find them and takes them to the shut yard. Don't invent a name Jon said.

## MEDIUM

### 2. The reach goes wrong "in front of them" (P§3) against R-016
- **State:** "I can't do it… Not when people are looking." (3:L463). The chair and Hookline fail under eyes (1:L419–423; 2:L403–407). The crate works because no one is looking (4:L295–297).
- **Plan:** "Eli's clumsy, loud reach goes wrong in front of them."
- **Fix:** have Eli reach when he believes he's unseen: the students' backs are turned, or he's round a corner, and the master watches from where Eli can't see him. The rule then holds as Eli's own sense of being watched. If the reach is to work while watched, that breaks R-016, and it needs a stated reason and Patrick's call.

### 3. Teo's clock doesn't fit a three-day wait (P§1, P§5)
- **State:** the red line is already "up past the elbow" on Day 5 (4:L399–401). The pills are "Two white now. Two tonight. Two in the morning." Six white were given and two taken, so **four white are left, enough for two doses** (4:L451–453, L465). After that only the three yellow are left, "if he goes off his head". "It'll hold him a few days. Then he wants a proper one." (4:L457). Doc is in SIX, "the next world over" (P§6).
- **Plan:** arrival, the dead trainer, the real teacher, then "three days in the rain or the cold", with the arm "worse in the cold".
- **Fix:** fix the flight time first (unknown on the page; "tonight" falls in flight or on Ithe). Then do one of these: cut the wait to one night and a day; or make the white pills run out on the page and use the yellow ("off his head") on day two; or have the new character or the master re-clean and dress the hand so the clock is reset on the page. Otherwise Teo should be near collapse by day three, which the plan's "Teo is cool and feverish" doesn't allow.

### 4. "Students… chipped" / "get the chip" is new on the page (P§2, P§3)
- **State:** on the page, chips are orc things. "With an orc the chip in the neck woke up" (1:L43). Readers and discs read orc chips only (3:L615–623; 4:L157, L173). The Legion chip is "Soldier's" (2:L261). Nothing shows a human with a chip.
- **Plan:** the dead trainer's "students are scattered or chipped"; the master has watched boys "get the chip".
- **Fix:** if these students are human, the first use needs one concrete plant, such as a former student with a stitched neck and a vacant face, or a disc that lights for him. Keep it to what's shown. If they're orcs, say so in the cards. (The codex source is BANK 11-16; canon-checker should confirm the human-chip reading.)

### 5. The stern knock is owed to FIVE and not in the plan
- **State:** "something in the stern went bang and went on banging" (4:L357); "something back there loose and swinging" (4:L507); the last line, "Aft, in the dark, the loose thing knocked, and was quiet, and knocked." (4:L529). The ch04 NOTES say "It's owed to ch05" (S-041).
- **Fix:** add one beat to P§1: the landing, where the knock is fixed, fails, or turns out to be cargo. The pilot leaving "without a goodbye" can carry it.

## LOW

### 6. "Loader" now has two meanings (P§3)
- **State:** a machine in TWO ("held a runaway loader off the Ilders' gate… the whole weight of it leaning on the air", 2:L401); orc labourers in FOUR ("loaders, most of them in canvas", 4:L153).
- **Fix:** in FIVE, use "crane" or a named rig, not "loader". If the yard's machine is a loader, it echoes 2:L401, which may be wanted, but then make it plainly a machine.

### 7. "use R-016's cost": the cost rule is R-014, and the hand is part of it (P§3, Seeds)
- **State:** cost at crate scale: a nosebleed, nausea, the headache beating, and **the bad hand locked shut**, prised open "one and then the next, the last one hardest" (4:L311–313). At fifteen, a loader held for a count of nine left him "sick for a day after" (2:L401).
- **The recovery pace to match:** "closed partway" on Days 2–5 (3:L53, L409); "most of the way" round a light strip, "Further than yesterday" (4:L69); after the crate, back to "partway" on the hook and the wrist (4:L345, L471). So: slow daily gain, set back by every big reach.
- **Fix:** relabel as R-014. A "clumsy, loud" reach bigger than the crate should cost more than the crate did, and it should lock the hand again. The "further than yesterday" seed can then show the hand gaining back over the wait.

### 8. The pilot's exit and her rules (P§1)

### 9. Money and phones on Ithe (P§5)
