- **D2 · Pask's gate.** "That's all. That's just the weather." became "That's all it is. Something's happened." The weather line didn't make sense in his mouth and stepped on Seffa's "no to the weather". Repeating himself to calm down is Eli's habit.

- **D5 · The rain.** "I've never seen anyone do anything like that." became "I've never seen anyone—". He's too eager to finish the sentence, and the cut breaks up a run of clean sentences.

"I've buried the ones I taught" still carries the fact, and the reader joins the two. "Most masters barely teach." is untouched.
- **D7 · "I'll wait, then."** Cut the tag "He said it easily." It was a beat explaining the line.

Also cut "That's the thing about me, Teo" (Eli explaining himself). It ends "And I'm terrible at going home. Ask anyone." That echoes his own "Ask anyone" from a minute earlier. He still talks over Teo's "Eli."
- **D9 · The pot.** "I've got a pot. It's not kind, love. It's a pot." became "It's a pot, love." One flat fact, not a two-beat riposte.

- **D11 · "Show me, love" → "Show me."** Three "love"s fell within two scenes, two of them back to back. The one kept is in "That's not a cut, love", the charsim line.

That's an accusation, not a recap of what he knows. Ferrel and the year's money are kept, and so are "nose going like a tap" and "Go and lift something".
- **D13 · The tall boy.** Trimmed the cranes speech: "On the floor. Eyes shut. Listen to the cranes, he says. Tell me which one's carrying. That's it, that's all of it." Fragments read as a teenager complaining, not a paragraph.
- **D14 · The gnome.** Cut the doubled "He sits with the dying… He takes them in, them that are going, and he sits with them", plus "He was very good" and "He doesn't sit with all of them till the end". It now runs: "He's not a doctor. Some of them get up." Same facts (Ammet, next world, ore boats, her mother, died well, some get up), with less speech.
