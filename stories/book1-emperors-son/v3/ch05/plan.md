# Chapter Five plan (v3): Claude's calls
🌊 Story clay. Builds on locked ONE–FOUR. Sources: CATCHUP §4 [P] (the master is the teacher, "Most masters barely teach", and he dies in the rule-beast room) and the codex (BANK 11-16: the master's raw power, his black-hole sacrifice, "Dont use more than you can control", the students who got the chip).

## FIVE · The Teacher (about 7–8k words)
**Job:** find the teacher. Jon's steer is wrong (the first of the times he's wrong or late). Eli earns a refusal, then a chance. Teo's arm is the clock. The beloved ordinary person comes in here.

1. **Ithe.** A rim world where the beater drops them. Poor and industrial, under its own sky, nothing like the capital. The pilot leaves without a goodbye. Teo is worse; the pills are holding, just.
2. **Jon's steer is wrong.** The name Jon gave leads to a trainer who has been dead for months: his yard is shut, and his students are scattered or chipped. Don't explain it. Eli doesn't ask why Jon was wrong.
3. **The real teacher.** Eli finds him by nearly killing his students. Their training yard is near a loader or a crane, and Eli's clumsy, loud reach goes wrong in front of them (use R-016's cost). The master stops it: one small, exact, terrifying act. He holds far more back than he lets out.
   - His refusal is flat. He has watched boys who wanted more than they could hold "get the chip".
   - "Most masters barely teach."
4. **The beloved ordinary person.** The NEW CHARACTER is one of the master's students, or the yard's cook. She's unprivileged, down to earth and not showy, and she's kind to Teo without fuss. LOCAL name, race-flavoured. She is the early death that sets the series' register (in ch7–8). Make her specific and alive. Don't flag her as doomed.
5. **Waiting him out.** Eli sits outside the yard for three days in the rain or the cold. He is warm about his idea of the master ("he'll teach me everything") and blind to what the master actually says. Teo's arm gets worse in the cold. The master's answer comes because of Teo's arm, or because of what Eli does for a student, not because Eli waited. That's a real cause.
6. **End.** The master takes him on, on hard terms: listen, don't push. Or he takes Teo to someone first. Seed the healer in the next world over, "a gnome who sits with the dying", which leads to Doc in ch6.

## Seeds (cap: about 6 Book 1 seeds and 2 future-book seeds)
