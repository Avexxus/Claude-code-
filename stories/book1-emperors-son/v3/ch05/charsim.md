## The new character: SEFFA (LOCAL name)
- **Race: human, Ithe-born.** Picked for story fit. Ithe is a poor human rim world, so a human here is the most ordinary person there is. Her brother is one of the chipped humans the plan plants, so she is the master's "got the chip" with a face on it. *Alternatives:* a bonded gnome (the dwarves' indentured class, `07` t568) would tie her too closely to Doc and make her death look like his doing. An orc would spend Eli's mid-book orc turn too early. Name alternatives: Ivet, Dosha.
- **Who:** the yard's cook, about thirty. She also sits in on lessons at the back, has a small reach and will never be any good. The master lets her stay because she feeds them. She has big forearms and a burn scar across the back of one wrist from the stove. She keeps her hair in a net.
- **Want:** to keep the yard fed and her brother Hollin (LOCAL) working. Under that, unspoken, she wants one thing that's hers. It isn't a dream of leaving Ithe. It's smaller and more real: a stove that draws properly.
- **Wound:** Hollin trained here. The master told him no more, so he bought a chip off a loader boss. He lifts for the cranes now, has a scar behind his ear, and laughs at the wrong times. She still brings him dinner. She doesn't blame the master out loud.
- **Voice:** practical, warm without softness, rim-plain. She gives food as a fact, not a favour. She asks real questions and waits for the answer (unlike Eli and Jon). She teases flatly. She's the only character who says "love" casually, as a rim word for anyone.
  - "Drink that. Not fast."
  - "He said no to you? He says no to the weather."
  - "That's not a cut, love, that's a smell."
  - "You've got money and nowhere to put it. That's a Terrace thing, is it?"
- **In this chapter:**
  - She comes round the corner with the students, carrying the pot (scene 2).
  - She brings water to Teo. **This pays off FOUR's "Is there water" (4:L499).**
  - Nobody asks her to, but she feeds them through the three days, through the gate.
  - She sees the line on Teo's arm and asks Teo, not Eli, how long it's been there.
  - She names the healer: "the gnome over on [next world] who sits with the dying". She took her mother there; her mother died, but well.
- **Why she'd come along:** not for adventure. Someone has to know the way to the gnome and the yard can spare her least of all, so it's her. Teo is the reason. The master's yes (scene 5) needs a guide, and she says "I'll take him" before anyone asks. Down-to-earth: she'll be back in four days, she says.
- **Never:** she never fusses over Teo and never flatters Eli. She doesn't foreshadow and isn't marked as doomed. Give her one joke and one ugly moment of temper (at Hollin, or at the master), so she's a person and not a saint.

## The master: voice
- **Few words, exact. He names the thing, not the feeling.** No questions unless he means to learn the answer. No comfort. He is sharp only when pushed. He looks out of doors, not at people, when he says hard things. He wipes his nose with his thumb and rubs his eyes after.
- **Unlike the others:** Jon is warm and sideways, the pilot gives orders with a "not" tail, Teo is flat. The master states what is so, like a measurement.
  - "Put it down."
  - "You lifted it blind. You opened your eyes and it fell."
  - "Don't use more than you can control."
  - "Most masters barely teach."

## Scene 1 · Ithe. Nobody comes (Eli, Teo, the pilot)

**The pilot**
- **Want:** to get her down before the coupling goes, then get the meal off.
- **What I'd do:** set her down hard at the first pad that will take her, short of the yard she was paid for. Hand them off at the ramp with the crates. Mark the board. I'm fixing the knock before they're down the ramp.
- **Never:** say goodbye, give back the watch, or say a name aloud on my ramp.
- **Say:** "Coupling. Off here." / "Not my pad. It's what there is."
- **Plan friction:** none. The stern knock gives a real cause for "early" (plan fix).

**Eli**
- **Want:** the man who'll find us, and a proper doctor for Teo, today.
- **What I'd do:** stand where everyone can see the coat so the man finds us. Ask at stalls, brightly, with the steward voice. When I hear he's dead, I'll find the next best.
- **Never:** say Jon was wrong, even to myself. He said a man would find us. So something happened to the man.
- **Say:** "We're expected. There's a man." / "Then who taught them?"
- **Plan friction:** "Doesn't ask why Jon was wrong." That's true to him, because he's committed to the idea of Jon (CATCHUP §4). No cost needed.

**Teo**
- **Want:** water, and to sit down.
- **What I'd do:** read the stencils and the signs. Notice the chip scars behind the ears of the human loaders before Eli does, and say one word about it.
- **Never:** say "I told you". Count C hasn't started.
- **Say:** "Behind his ear." / "Nobody's coming, Eli."
- **Plan friction:** none.

## Scene 2 · The reach goes wrong; the master stops it (the master, Eli, Seffa, a student)

**Eli**
- **Want:** to get Teo out of the rain. The shed under the crane is stacked full.
- **What I'd do:** the yard is shut and empty, so nobody's watching. Shift the stack under the crane, or swing the hook to clear the shed. Go big, because big worked on the crate.
- **Never:** do it with anyone watching. **That's why the yard has to look empty.** Students come round the corner, he knows he's seen, and it goes wrong: the load swings toward them (R-016).
- **Say:** "It's all right. I've got it. I've got—"
- **Plan friction:** "Clumsy, loud reach" fits only if the cause is Teo, not showing off. Keep it a kindness that he does too big.

**The master**
- **Want:** the boys alive, and for this to be over in one move.
- **What I'd do:** not stand up. One small, exact act: the swinging load stops dead and sets down "like a cup". **Terrifying:** for a count of one, the rain stops falling everywhere in the yard, and Eli feels his own reach shut as if someone has closed a hand on it. Then the rain comes back. Thumb to my nose. Rub my eyes.
- **Never:** show more than that. Never explain, and never say what I am.
- **Say:** "Put it down." Then, once it's down: "Who sent you."
- **Plan friction:** none. Seed: the held-back power. Only Eli sees the thumb and the blood (cast §1.5).

**Seffa**
- **Want:** the pot not spilled, the boys in, out of the wet.
- **What I'd do:** get the boys behind me before I know why. After, look at Teo on the crate, not at Eli.
- **Never:** be impressed by the coat.
- **Say:** "Is he yours? He's grey."

**The student (a boy of about sixteen, LOCAL, unnamed for now)**
- **Want:** to have seen it. A lift that size.
- **What I'd do:** forget the fright in a minute. Stare at Eli the way boys stare at a chip man.
- **Never:** thank him.
- **Say:** "Did you see the size of that?"
- **Note:** he is the master's fear in one look. Keep him for scene 5.

## Scene 3 · The refusal (the master, Eli)

**The master**
- **Want:** him gone before the boys start copying him.
- **What I'd do:** answer in a line or two, looking out of the gate. Name what happened, then the rule, then no.
- **Never:** say "come back later". Never show pity, and never mention his own past or his master.
- **Say:** "No." / "Most masters barely teach. I've buried the ones I taught." / "The ones who wanted more than they could hold went and got the chip. Then I buried them."
- **Plan friction:** none. "Got the chip" was shown first in scene 1, so here it's said (plan fix).

**Eli**

- **What I'd do:** turn the no into a joke, then into an offer of money, then into "I'll wait". Hear "most masters barely teach" as modesty.
- **Never:** hear the warning. Never ask about the chipped boys.
- **Say:** "Then barely teach me. I'm quick." / "I'll pay. I can pay."
- **Plan friction:** none.

## Scene 4 · The three-day wait; the pills run out (Eli, Teo, Seffa)

**Eli**
- **Want:** to be let in. Warm, certain: "He'll teach me everything."
- **What I'd do:** sit at the gate in the cold. Tell Teo the plan for when I'm taught: the call out, the best man, the room with a window. Give Teo the coat at night, and this time let it go.
- **Never:** leave the gate to find a clinic himself.
- **The real block:** a clinic needs a name, and the pilot logged them as "meal". **Teo refuses it again ("They log it")**, so the wait is partly Teo's choice. That's a real cost, not Eli's blindness alone.
- **Say:** "Day two. That's nothing. He's seeing if I mean it."
- **Hand:** "Further than yesterday" on day one. On day two, after the cold, it won't close at all on the cup Seffa gives him.

**Teo**
- **Want:** water, warmth. That Eli would look at the arm instead of the gate.
- **What I'd do:** count the pills out loud once. Hide the arm when Seffa looks, then let her see.
- **Never:** beg. Never say "I'm dying".
- **Say:** "Two left." Then, on day two: "None." And: "It's past the shoulder."
- **Plan friction:** fix the dose count. Four white is two doses ("Two tonight. Two in the morning", 4:L453). If "tonight" fell in flight, they run out on the morning of day one, not day two. **Decide the flight length:** less than a day gives day two, as planned.

**Seffa**
- **Want:** not to have a boy die on her step.
- **What I'd do:** water, bread, a blanket that smells of the stove, through the gate. Ask Teo how long the line's been there. Tell Eli straight, once: "He wants a healer, not a gate." Name the gnome who sits with the dying.
- **Never:** argue Eli into going. She says it once, like Teo does.
- **Say:** "Who's minding him while you're minding the gate?"

## Scene 5 · The master's yes and its cause (the master, Eli, Teo)

**Real cause (pick):** **Teo's arm, and Eli asking for someone else.** On day three Teo goes down in the mud. For the first time Eli doesn't ask for himself: "Not me. Him." The master's yes is **to Teo**. He sends Seffa to take Teo to the gnome, and the teaching comes after, on terms. Eli hears all of it as a yes to him, and stays blind.
- *The second leg, if it's wanted:* earlier on day three the student from scene 2 tries a lift to show Eli, and Eli says "Don't", Teo's word. Eli doesn't reach. The master sees him not reach. This is the cast's "Eli's restraint earns him" (cast §Eli↔master). Use one leg, not both, or it reads as a montage.

**The master**
- **Want:** no more dead boys on his yard. A sick one dying at his gate is the same thing.
- **What I'd do:** come out in the rain. Look at the arm, not at Eli. Give terms, one per line.
- **Never:** say he was moved, that Eli earned it, or "you waited well". **Waiting earned nothing.** Say so if Eli claims it.
- **Say:** "Seffa takes him. Now." / "You stay. You listen. You don't push." / "Waiting's not why."
- **Plan friction:** none. The cause is real, and it's Teo.

**Eli**
- **Want:** Teo saved, and his yes. He wants both and can't tell them apart.
- **What I'd do:** say thank you, too much. Promise Teo the best room. Start to go with Teo, and be stopped.
- **Never:** see that the yes was to Teo.
- **Say:** "Not me. Him." / Later: "He said yes. I told you he would."
- **Plan friction:** **Eli staying while Teo goes** is a cost Eli will have to pay. It's a strong hook into SIX, and Teo goes with Seffa. If the plan needs Eli to reach Doc in SIX, the master sends Eli after two days with "Go and get him back", or he puts off the lessons until Teo is back. Patrick's call; flag it at gate 2.

**Teo**
- **Want:** to lie down somewhere dry.
- **What I'd do:** let Seffa take the good arm. Look at Eli once, and hold it.
- **Never:** thank the master, and never ask Eli to come.
- **Say:** "Right." (His first "Right" since THREE; it marks the turn.)
