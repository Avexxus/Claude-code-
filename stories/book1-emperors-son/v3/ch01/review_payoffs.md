## Verdict
The planted seeds are strong and mostly light. Of the plan's 15 seeds (8 Book 1, 7 FB), none is missing. One is too faint (S-010's cause). Two are slightly heavy (S-003, S-101). Seven accidental seeds need a yes or no from the plan. Two rule slips.

## Ranked: fix or decide

1. **ACC: the shaved-head girl (S-015).** Teo flags her twice, Vosk watches her, and she runs past Eli. Readers will expect her to matter. Decide now: either she is the King's eyes (one ch02 line) or she returns. If neither, cut "She was on the bridge" and Vosk's look (L295).
2. **ACC: Teo slows at the gate, "never asked him why" (S-016), plus Teo reading the salvage stamp (S-017).** Said twice. It reads as a Teo secret the brief doesn't have. Decide Teo's origin (the notes' open point) and pay it before the throne room. If there's no secret, keep one mention and drop "never asked him why".
3. **FB S-106, the Velk tune Eli "half knew".** It's tiny, as asked, but if this is Derrek's tune, Eli knowing it implies contact with his father, and readers will expect Derrek to hum it in the throne room. Fix: cut "Eli half knew it… snagged" so only the tune remains, or accept that Derrek hums it once, unexplained.
4. **S-010, Vosk's aim and why it fails: FAINT.** The escape aim reads (Hurrak, "on my own", "nobody pulls on him"). The cause, that Eli's presence brought the King, rests on "You said you'd come on your own" and the boy leaving for "Tax". Add one beat: the dwarf boy looks at Eli's coat or purse before he goes (L397).
5. **S-002, Teo's warnings: count and pre-spend.** There are five warnings, not three (adding L261 and L389), and "I told you" at the sink (L603) spends the later line early. Either trim L389's "Eli" or let "like 3 times" stay loose. The later payoff must *turn* (for example, Eli says it himself), not repeat L603.
6. **ACC: the King survives and wants Eli (S-019).** "He'll have that" plus the moving rubble leaves an open threat that no beat in the brief closes. Promise it (the King returns) or close it in ch02 (news of his death or wounding).
7. **ACC: cores made from orcs (S-020).** The lift core that tightens like a hand, the cores "called something else", and the "orc-grey core" in the soup add up to a big implied horror. Check it against the codex (pool cores). It is a natural lever for the brief's §1 horror.
8. **ACC: Vosk "trying to place" Eli's face (S-018)**, right after he can feel the Tower. Strong and light. Adopt it as a father-reveal plant. It needs one echo (another sensitive) before the throne room.
9. **ACC: "Tell Hurrak's. No." (S-021).** Hurrak's what? It could be the bridge to the unchipped orcs. If adopted, it adds an open question (say so).
10. **S-009, the 41 marks for the cook.** Very visible, so it's a promise. It needs a ch02 beat: Eli keeps it, forgets it (on theme), or botches it.
11. **FB S-105, the ticking 94-year-old dwarf.** Readers may take him for Book 1's old ship-owning dwarf. Either give the ship-owner no rig or make the link deliberate.
12. **Rules:** R-003. "Six hundredth landing" is impossible with a reader every 100 steps on a 4,000-step stair, so make it step 600. R-004: "with everyone else's" includes orcs, who get no light.
13. **R-012 freshness risk.** The King wears harvested chips, so readers may decide his young men are de-chipped. Keep "unchipped" later as *never chipped*.
