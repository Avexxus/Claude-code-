# Grok's hard notes on Chapter 1 v3, "The Gift" (pasted by Patrick 2026-11-17)
Summary; the full text is in Patrick's message.

**Protect:** the Scald and the Long Stair; Vosk's death and the childhood memory; the night count with the missing number; Eli's hand raised and empty; the chip removal; the orcs kneeling and the wire-stitched boys; "I don't remember" (don't decorate it).

**Top fixes:**
1. Vosk's spike must be uncontrolled over-amplification that destroys his body as he uses it, not a level-up.
2. Kill or weaponise the soft Eli beats: the kittens line, "Thank you" to the bin, buying Teo the coin mid-procedure, the easy "I'm coming".
3. The Orc King must come as a consequence of the chip and the place, not as a boss arriving on cue.
4. Eli's failed reach is the emotional centre of the climax.
5. The 1,100 marks must have weight; they're a thread.

**Also:**
- Teo's competence is announced ("always gets the route"); give him private texture.
- The wire-boys vanish in the fight.
- The money has no friction.
- A hot Legion chip gets fitted too easily.
- The mid-levels read as generic sci-fi; they need the capital's own wrongness.
- Literary-pretty images.
- The fight's spatial chain is hard to follow.
- Eli's inner voice is too writerly.
- The emotion is explained.

**Grok's "radiation" note is wrong for Book 1.** Patrick ruled the radiation cleared long before Book 3, so Book 1's wrongness is the rift bleed and the Tower, not radiation. Use the bleed instead.

**Claude's note on balance:** Patrick's beat is that this is the first death where an orc has agency over how he is used up, and he's proud to die for the man the boy became. Keep that pride real. Harden it with the asymmetry (Eli doesn't remember), not by cutting the pride.
