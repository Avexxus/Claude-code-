## Timeline and plot logic

**1. The Terrace is dark at count time, but yesterday the Ilders were at breakfast (LOW)**
- **Earlier:** L67: "Vosk was fourteen minutes past the count." L73: "In the first-floor window of the third house, the Ilders', a boy of about nine was eating breakfast in a white shirt".
- **Later:** L615: "with the house windows dark on both sides and the sky over the Tower going grey." Then L627: "the barracks bell was going for the morning count."
- **Problem:** Eli gets home at the count. Fourteen minutes later the day before, the Ilders had a lit window with breakfast in it. The gap is small and it can stand, but the two shots of the same hour don't match.
- **Smallest fix:** L615 "with the house windows dark on both sides" → "with the house windows only now lighting on both sides". Or leave it.

**2. Three crewmen come out of the dust, but only two are accounted for (LOW)**
- **Earlier:** L581: "three of the crew staggered back out of the dust at the base of the heap".
- **Later:** L587: "[he] went to the heap and got down on his knees with the others"; L589: "The last one crossed to where Vosk lay".
- **Problem:** "the others" means more than one, but of three, one stopped, one is "the last one", and only one is left to be "the others". The count reads loosely.
- **Smallest fix:** L587 "with the others" → "with the second".
