# Forecast review: Book 1, Ch01 "The Gift" (v3)

Source read: `ch01_STORY_ONLY.md` only. No notes, no codex. First chapter, so no earlier chapters.
Method: I stopped at each `---` break (10 sections). For each break I give the guess I made before reading on, my confidence, and the open question. Then I grade the guess against the next section.

---

## Break 1: after S1 (Scald walk, Pole Street kneel, "He's eating tonight")
**Guess:** Vosk climbs out of the Scald to the Terrace and starts the double shift. We learn what the 1,100 marks are for, probably something to do with the chip. Confidence **2**.
**Open question:** What costs 1,100 marks, and what is the thing behind the plastic strips?
**Grade on S2 (Long Stair, mid-levels, Tower):** **HIT on the action, MISS on the reveal.** He climbs, but the price stays unexplained. S2 is a travel section and the readers on the landings make it work as a tour of the chip. The flesh market and the twitching lift core are strong. Nothing turns, though, and the question from S1 just waits. "He didn't feel anything" about the Tower turns out to be a quiet setup, and a good one.

## Break 2: after S2 (staring at the Tower)
**Guess:** He reaches the Terrace late and the chip punishes him for it. Confidence **3**.
**Open question:** Who on the Terrace is he going to ask for help?
**Grade on S3 (gate shock, Ilder boy with the fob, gravel at the window):** **HIT** on being late and punished. **NEAR** on the gravel thrown at a window: it's the right direction, and a nicer shape than I expected. The Ilder boy looking "interested" is the best cold beat so far, and the ending pays it off.

## Break 3: after S3 (gravel at the third window)
**Guess:** The friend in the window, a human (maybe the emperor's son of the title), comes down. Vosk asks him for the money and the friend says yes. Confidence **3**.
**Open question:** Will the friend pay, and what will it cost Vosk to ask?
**Grade on S4 (Eli's kitchen, purse, letter, Teo's floating core):** **NEAR, and a good surprise.** The time jump past the asking is smart: the money is already on the table. Two **MISSES, both good:** (a) Teo floats the apple core without noticing, the first hint of power, done quietly. (b) The letter carries the blank oval seal, which was planted on the stair landings in S2. Earned. Eli's "They'd have the boots off him before the second landing" is confident and wrong, and you can feel that.

## Break 4: after S4 ("Good." Eli ignores Teo's route)
**Guess:** They go down at night. The stitched-neck crew from Pole Street spot Eli's coat and catch them before they reach the dwarf. Confidence **4**.
**Open question:** Is Eli's Terrace confidence going to get Vosk hurt, and what's in the letter?
**Grade on S5 (gate splits them, the shaft, the wrong-sized street, the bridge "warm like a horse", Hookline, the girl slipping through the strips):** **NEAR.** The danger builds but nothing breaks yet. They get there. The warnings pile up: the crowd parts around him, "was you mine", Teo's coat warning, the girl going through the strips. They all pull the same way, and the reader is ahead of Eli the whole time, which is the point. The giant-sized street and the living bridge are the best world images in the chapter.

## Break 5: after S5 (into the alley)
**Guess:** The dwarf does the chip removal, and the crew bursts in partway through. Confidence **4**.
**Open question:** Will the surgery work, and what does the new chip do?
**Grade on S6 (Velk, the price going up plus the watch, the boy leaving for "Tax", extraction, LEGION chip, "Leash is out", Vosk feels the Tower):** **NEAR, and the best section.** The crew holds off, and the surgery has its own turns instead: the price rises, the first chip won't take, the stolen soldier's chip, and the awakening. The awakening is a **MISS, and a good one**: S2's "He didn't feel anything" sets it up and Velk's "Everybody, first time" confirms it. "And I didn't fit it" is a perfect exit line. Teo saying "We go. Now." and being shaken off is clean dramatic irony.

## Break 6: after S6 ("It's gone quiet.")
**Guess:** The crew and the big thing behind the strips come out. Vosk uses the new power, Teo uses his, and the Terrace coat or the letter comes into play. Confidence **4**.
**Open question:** What is the King, and can Vosk control what's in him now?
**Grade on S7 (the King, Vosk's uncontrolled blast, the cutter at Eli's ear, the broken arm, Eli's power failing, Teo's ladle, Vosk walking it at the King, the arch falling):** **NEAR overall.** Vosk's power: HIT. Teo using a ladle instead of his power: **MISS, good.** It's human and desperate. Eli reaching for a power and getting **nothing**: **MISS, half earned.** Eli having power at all ("Fifteen. The lake, the flyer") is revealed in the same beat as its failure. S4's chair-through-the-window and the flyers point at it, but only in hindsight. The King's design (chips sewn on like buttons) is earned by the stitched necks in S1. The arch falling is earned one paragraph earlier by the sagging ceiling. Fair, but the setup is close.
**Open question raised:** Why doesn't Teo float anything? A reader who saw the apple core will ask.

## Break 7: after S7 (Eli pulls free and goes back)
**Guess:** Vosk is dying. He gets last words, the heap shifts, Teo drags Eli out. Confidence **5**.
**Open question:** What will Vosk say?
**Grade on S8 (dying speech: the Ilder yard, "He's mine. Leave him", "Nobody spent me", the hook in his neck):** **HIT at confidence 5, so this needs a turn.** The *event* is fully predictable. The *content* carries the turn: the reveal that Eli, as a small boy, saved Vosk from the Ilder fob. It's set up by both Ilder scenes and by "was you mine". It flips the debt, and "I don't remember" / "I know. That's all right" cuts deep. "Nobody spent me" lands, though it's slightly opaque. The crew man hooking out the new chip is an ugly, earned coda. Teo turning the crew with "You want to be up here with us when he comes out?" is a good small turn.

## Break 8: after S8 (across the warm bridge)
**Guess:** They climb out, and Eli shakes and goes over it. Confidence **4**.
**Open question:** Will Eli open the letter?
**Grade on S9 (the Scald all climbing, the readers go green on blood, the ribbon unchanged, Teo sick):** **HIT.** It's a short come-down, which is right. Going green with blood on his hands is the section's whole point, and it works.

## Break 9: after S9 ("Right," and they walked on)
**Guess:** He gets home and opens the letter, and it says he's the Emperor's son. Confidence **3**.
**Open question:** Does the chapter end on the letter or on Vosk?
**Grade on S10 (the gate greets him, Teo scrubs his hands, the letter goes back under the bowl, the night count with one number unanswered):** **MISS, good.** Not opening the letter is earned: "Tomorrow" in S4, and his state now. The unanswered number at the count is the strongest ending available, and it rhymes with "Late. Logged." Teo's line about the brush is exactly right.

## Top three places to add a turn or a setup
1. **S4 or S5: seed Eli's power before S7.** Right now the reader learns Eli had power at fifteen in the same breath it fails. One earlier beat would turn the failure into a payoff instead of a reveal: a flyer at the lake, Teo glancing at Eli's hand, Eli's "nobody's putting a knife in anybody with me stood there" carrying a private certainty. While you're there, add one line on why Teo doesn't use his float in the fight (it's small, or involuntary, or he's afraid of it). Otherwise the apple core in S4 leaves a question the fight seems to dodge.
2. **S2: give the Long Stair one turn, or tie it to the price.** It's the only long section where nothing changes, and the 1,100 question just waits. Options: a reader on a landing pulses at him, a hint of what the dwarf will do to his neck, or a beat where he weighs the 41 marks against the price. Keep the texture and add one hinge.
3. **S8: the death is a confidence-5 HIT.** The Ilder memory saves it. Consider planting one more sideways signal earlier so the speech's reversal hits harder: in S3 or S5, Vosk reacting to the Ilder fob, or the Ilder yard man's boots off the grass in S5 echoed in Vosk. Or let the King's "cook looking at a bird" glance at Eli become a thread that S8 or S10 touches. That would give the escape a forward pull beyond grief.
