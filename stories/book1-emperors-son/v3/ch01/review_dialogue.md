# Ch01 dialogue pass: draft_v2 → draft_v3

> 🌊 Claude review, story layer, not canon. Dialogue only; every event, every reveal and every fact a line carries is kept. Voices come from CATCHUP §4–5 and the draft itself: Eli runs on, repeats himself and talks over people; Teo is short, dry and exact; Vosk speaks in one or two words until he dies; Velk is curt and grumbles sideways; the crew uses yard slang. Jon is not in this chapter, so there are no dodges to count.

## Overall verdict
The draft's dialogue was already mostly sound: short lines, plenty of misses ("Wall.", "He asked was you mine.", Eli's wrong guess about the head cut), with Eli's not-listening shown and never stated. The weak spots were four. Eli explained the plot to Teo, who already knew it. Velk explained the world twice. A few lines landed as neat ripostes. Vosk's dying speech was broken by full stops but still ran as one tidy monologue.

## Changes (one line each)
1. **Kitchen, "He asked you for a loan." → "For a loan."** Teo's echo was a written-sounding repeat; a clipped echo is drier and more his.
2. **Kitchen, "It's what it's for" → "That's what it's for."** Smoother as speech.
3. **Kitchen, Eli's rant.** Cut "going down to the Scald on his own, at night, with eleven hundred marks on him, to see some dwarf in a back room about his neck". Teo knows all of it, so it was for the reader. It is now broken questions ("On his own? At night? With that on him…") that assume Teo knows. The facts that matter (alone, at night, money, a dwarf, his neck) stay.
4. **Kitchen, cut "It's what friends do."** It stated the book's thesis about Eli. "Well. He's still soft." ends the exchange on Eli's dodge instead.
5. **Letter, "Later. … Tomorrow." → "Tomorrow."** The double answer read as the author hedging; one word is Eli's brush-off.
6. **Step, "Eleven hundred marks in the Scald at night. You'll get a knife in you for the purse." → "With all that on you? Somebody puts a knife in you for the purse…"** Eli was repeating his rant to Teo word for word as a set speech. Now it's a quick jab, and the route triplet and Teo's "I haven't got the route" undercut it.
7. **Kitchen floor, cut "She's got eleven hundred reasons."** A neat quip, and wrong: Eli still has the money. "So she waits." keeps the choice to be late.
8. **Hookline coat, Teo's line trimmed** ("I know what's in it. Every stall, Eli. Nobody's looking at him. They're looking at the coat."). It was two sentences longer than Teo talks, and "Every one." was a rhetorical repeat.
9. **Flesh market, "Because it costs more." → "Costs more, doesn't it."** Same joke, less of a clean punchline; it's a stallholder's shrug now.
10. **Velk's grumble trimmed** ("Depot takes a third now. A third. Then his cut. Then whatever he sends up to whoever." / "And they wonder."). It's still the same three layers and the same unanswered "Whose cut?". It now sounds like a woman muttering at her sink rather than a briefing.
11. **Velk on the Tower: "That's the Tower… Everybody gets that the first time. You'll stop noticing. Like having the sun in the next room." → "Tower. Everybody, first time. You stop noticing. Sun in the next room."** Same facts and the same canon image (BANK 11-16), in her clipped register instead of an explainer's.
12. **Coin, Velk's "It just stands there" → no reply ("Velk went on wiping.").** The deadpan echo was a riposte. Her silence is colder, and Eli's next offer then lands on nothing.
13. **Teo's third warning: "Pay her and we go. Now." → "Leave it. We go. Now."** Logic fix. Velk has already been paid, so "Pay her" made no sense; Teo means drop the coin. It is still warning #3, and he still gives up after it.
14. **Alley, "Back to the bridge" → "Bridge."** Teo's clipped form, which he repeats twice more in the fight. The repetition is his voice under stress.
15. **Crew, Teo's "You want to be the one up here with us when he comes out?" → "You want to be up here with us when he comes out?"** Shorter and less polished. It's the same threat and the same outcome.
16. **Vosk's dying speech, re-broken** (the one place a speech is allowed):
    - Clauses arrive out of order ("You were." … "This big."), and words go missing ("They had the. His dad's. The fob.").
    - A breath sticks halfway, and the tense slips ("you come down off the step").
    - Eli interrupts with "Don't talk. Vosk. Don't—", which again shows him not listening, since Vosk asked him to listen. Vosk talks over him.
    - "He's mine" is said after Vosk's eyes go to the wall and come back.
    - "I'm proud" now starts on the bare word "Proud." and loses its object ("Of the. Of what he. The boy.") before getting there ("What he grew").
    - "I'd do it for him. I'd do it for you." is cut to "I'd do it for him. For you. I'd."
    - "Nobody spent me." is his last clear sentence, said barely aloud, with Eli bending close to hear.
    - Every beat is kept, in order: Ilder, the fob, the dance, wetting himself, the laughing, the bite, the steward, "He's mine. Leave him. He's mine.", "I know you don't. That's all right.", proud of the man the boy became, "I picked it", "Nobody spent me", then "Go. Walk." / "It's right there."

## Checked and left alone
- "Wall." / "He asked was you mine." / "I know you go down there. That's how you got your head opened." These are good misses: Eli gets the cause wrong.
- "Eli," / "It's done. Eat your bird. When have I ever not come?" Vosk is cut off before he can say it, which is the scene's subtext.
- "Thank you," to the bin. "She'll want to know what happened to her brush." These are lines that miss on purpose.
- Velk's "You understand what you've bought him." / "Yes." This is the right want: she's asking without asking.
- The crew's slang ("Terrace", "King don't need his ears") and the old woman's "Eating. He's eating tonight." / "No," she agreed.
- "If it's his people, they've had twenty-four years." It carries sense fix #7 (whether Eli knows his father), and it's a dodge, not an explanation.
- Same-voice test: with the tags covered, every speaker stays identifiable. Eli runs long and repeats; Teo's lines are under eight words; Vosk's are under four until the death; Velk drops her subjects.
