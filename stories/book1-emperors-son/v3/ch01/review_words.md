## Main changes
- **Looked (52 → 12).** Replaced with concrete verbs, or with the eye doing the work: stared up, checked, glanced, eyed, studied, "found him", "Her eye went to the tin", "the reader… read them". Rhythm triples were trimmed to "She looked at Eli. At Teo. At Vosk."
- **Negations turned into the positive fact.** "She kept her eyes on the light", "Velk let it run", "Vosk stayed in the chair", "it covered about half of him", "Eli's legs gave", "the wrist was bare", "every building kept a mile back from its foot", "the front was blank", "Every head stayed down", "Teo kept hold of Eli's wrist and held to a walk". Kept the withholding lines that carry voice: "That you didn't get used to.", "Dogs didn't last.", "He didn't feel anything.", "Eli didn't look up." (the core), "Eli didn't look at it.", "It bit them.", "He didn't say anything." (the King).
- **Something (34 → 11).** Named the thing where the meaning allows: "a slick", "filth", "a line about rates", "a pot", "black spray", "three words", "paid him". Kept the menace uses: the thing behind the strips, the thing through the arch, the side of meat, the speech.
- **Then (53 → 19).** Cut where the order was already clear, and merged short pairs ("put his hand on it as he stepped up, stopped, and put his hand back").
- **As if / the way (23 → 10).** Cut the non-simile "the way"s ("all the way up", "on the way down"). Kept 5 similes: cut meat, good wine, buttons, the stove, the peg.
- **Openings.** Folded "It was…" into fragments ("Bigger than the old one", "A birthday present", "Heavy, for a letter") and merged "He was" sentences.
- **Phrases.** Some "young men" became "the crew", the reader is "a disc of dark glass", "the wall over his shoulder", "the Spur's head", "at the shaft head".
