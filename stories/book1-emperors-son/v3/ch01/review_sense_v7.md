## Flags, ranked by how much they break belief

**1. Vosk walks into the falling arch and is thrown back out, alive enough to speak.**
> "Vosk walked in under the arch. / The arch came down. … and Vosk came back out of the front of it on the wave of the fall, thrown, and hit the market floor and slid" (L487–491)
- **Category:** Convenience / a cool moment with nothing under it.
- **Why it reads wrong:** he chooses to die under the stone, and then the stone hands him back for a full speech. The rescue reads as the author's, not the physics'. (The NOTES admit this: "a mercy of physics".)
- **Fix:** he gets only as far as the lip. The belly comes down on the King, and the edge takes Vosk across the legs and pins him, so Eli has to drag his top half clear. This keeps the choice and adds a cost. (L501's "on his back on the market floor" would change to suit.)

**2. Vosk is suddenly "across the floor" when he was a step behind them.**
> "Vosk was a step behind." (L391) … "Vosk took a step toward him … and stopped." (L409) … "He found Eli across the floor with the cutter under his ear." (L443)
- **Category:** A clipped join (space).
- **Why it reads wrong:** the key beat of the chapter, Vosk looking at Eli under the cutter, needs distance between them, but one involuntary step doesn't make a market floor's worth.
- **Fix:** after the King's look, add one line: "Vosk walked out from the wall, away from them, onto the open floor." That also explains how the gap-toothed one gets behind Eli unseen.

**3. A soft Terrace boy climbs down the full height of the Long Stair on a ladder.**
> "It was a long way. The rungs were slick and warm … Partway down, the shaft opened on one side onto the sumps" (L197)
- **Category:** The world wouldn't produce it (bodies, distance).
- **Why it reads wrong:** the stair is "four thousand and some steps" with the sumps halfway (L41–43), so this ladder is hundreds of metres. Eli gets off with burning forearms, and it seems to take minutes.
- **Fix:** add a crews' cage to the sumps first, and use the ladder only for the last stretch: "a cage down to the sumps, then the ladder." Or pay the cost: stops on rest grilles, and Eli's arms shaking at the bottom.

**4. Eli thinks of sending "men to go down and dig", but Vosk isn't under anything.**
> "He could ring the account. A clinic, a flyer, men to go down and dig. … He sat with that, and left it." (L623)
- **Category:** A clipped join (state).
- **Why it reads wrong:** Vosk's body is lying in the open with a crewman hooking out his chip (L589). "Dig" points at the King, and the reader stops to work out why.
- **Fix:** "men to go down and bring him up." The "left it" works as shame, and works better once the line points at Vosk.

**5. Vosk sees the stitches on a standing man's nape while his own face is to the wall.**
> "he saw from the corner of his eye a pair of shins … Up the arm, at the nape of the young man's neck … the stitches wept." (L19)
- **Category:** The world wouldn't produce it (sightline).
- **Why it reads wrong:** a man kneeling face to the wall can see shins, but not the back of the neck of the man standing over him.
- **Fix:** move the nape description into L23, when the young man "leaned down and smelled" the collar and his neck comes level with Vosk's eye.

**6. Vosk goes out through the service slot at 11 p.m., after the count, at no cost.**
> "Vosk had said, 'It bites,' and gone down to the culvert, and they found him at the bottom of the public ramp twenty minutes later" (L189)
- **Category:** A broken promise (R-005: the slot reads the chip and punishes; the opening made that cost vivid).
- **Why it reads wrong:** the slot shocked him for fourteen minutes' lateness this morning, yet it lets him out at night with nothing.
- **Fix:** one clause on the cost. Either Eli has squared it ("I've told the steward you're out with me"), or Vosk turns up with fresh spit on his chin. The first also sets up the steward's "Vosk?" at L631.

**7. "Find us a way down that isn't the stair" has no reason.**
> (L173)
- **Category:** A person acts for the plot.
- **Why it reads wrong:** Eli has used the mid-levels since fifteen, and nothing says why the stair is out. Teo has the rota ready, so it reads as the route the scene needs.
- **Fix:** give Eli a reason he'd say: "Four thousand steps? Find us a way down that isn't the stair." Or a reason tied to the readers: "that doesn't log us every hundred steps."

**8. Velk says "Don't" with her back turned.**
> "'Don't,' said Velk. She was at a basin in the corner with her back to him" (L265)
- **Category:** Convenience.
- **Why it reads wrong:** she can't see the coin move, so either she senses reach (which would be new, and unsaid) or the line comes on cue.
- **Fix:** "She was watching him in the dark side of the tank." Or she hears the coin ring on the saucer.

**9. The Ilder father's fob worked on Eli's house's orc.**
> "'His dad's. Fob.' … 'Made me. Dance.'" (L531)
- **Category:** The world wouldn't produce it (R-008 shows fobs on a house's *own* yard orc, L73, L183).
- **Why it reads wrong:** if any Terrace fob drives any Terrace orc, that's a large unstated rule. If not, a reader asks how this one worked.
- **Fix:** have Vosk place himself: "Ilder's lot. Their yard. You were. Small." Or plant earlier that a Terrace fob works on any orc inside the gate.

**10. Teo's ladle comes to hand on cue.**
> "A big iron ladle off the wrecked stall" (L463)
- **Category:** Convenience (space).
- **Why it reads wrong:** the soup stall is across the floor toward the arch (L387), and Teo is on the far wall.
- **Fix:** "a ladle the blast had flung against the wall at his feet". The pots are already flying past Eli (L431).

**11. The King's reason is a crewman explaining, and "in it" is unclear.**
> "Terrace boy walks into Hookline in a coat like that. Worth more than any chip in it." (L413)
- **Category:** Telling (the author calls collide: 5 asks for the reason on the page, 6 says no crewman explains).
- **Why it reads wrong:** "in it" can mean the coat, which is nonsense. The line also lands a beat after the coat has already been shown to draw eyes (L239–241).
- **Fix:** "Worth more than any chip down here."

**12. Smaller things.**
- L551, "on the breath he'd been keeping for it": Eli can't know Vosk saved it. This is telling inside close third. Fix: "It came out whole, all at once."
- L427, "the pan man, the two women, the children by the cut" all sag: neither the pan man nor the net children are marked as orcs, and the sag works through the chip. Fix: "the pan man" → "the orc at the pans" (or drop him).
- L585, "You want to be up here with us when he comes out?": the bluff is right but "up here" is unclear (they're on the floor). Fix: "You want him to come out and find you over here with us?"
