# Sense check: Book 1 v3, Chapter One "The Gift" (draft_v1)
🌊 Claude review (sense-checker). Judged against `v3/ch01/plan.md`, `00_brief_v3.md`, CATCHUP §4–5, `BANK_passed_2026-10-31_to_11-17.md` and codex_v5 (cc R061/R063/R071/R096/R143/R144). There are no approved beats before this one. Story text only; the NOTES were read as context.

**Overall:** the chapter holds together. The causes mostly run cleanly: the King's crew clocks Vosk the night before, Velk names "his cut", the boy leaves for "Tax", and Eli stalls over the coin. The body pays for the draw, as canon says (R096), and the chip reads as a collar (R143). Most of what fails is local: one henchman who acts for the plot, one power that is told only when it fails, and a few joins and numbers.

---

## Flags, ranked by how much they break belief

**1. The cutter attack has no motive.**
> "He pulled Eli back against him. The cutter came round."
- **Category:** A person acts for the plot.
- **Why it reads wrong:** the King has just said "he'll have that" about Eli, so the King's own man slashing at the prize's face makes no sense. It exists only to set up the freeze.
- **Fix:** make Eli a hostage. The man puts the cutter to Eli's throat and shouts at Vosk to stop. The freeze then happens under leverage, and Vosk can't turn round to help.

**2. Eli's reach appears for the first time at the moment it fails.**
> "He could reach. He'd always been able to reach; he'd known since he was six… lifted Teo's flyer out of the lake…"
- **Category:** Convenience / a leap.
- **Why it reads wrong:** the power is explained in the same paragraph that cancels it. "Nothing came" has nothing earlier to fall from.
- **Fix:** plant it once, early and offhand. In the kitchen, for example, Eli could tip the purse toward himself across the table without touching it, or Teo could say "don't do the thing" when Eli reaches for the fruit bowl. Keep the flyer story for the freeze.

**3. Eli calls him "the King", but nobody ever says the title.**
> "The King lifted one hand, palm up…"
- **Category:** A clipped join (a POV leak).
- **Why it reads wrong:** Eli's close-third narration uses a title that no one has said where he could hear it. Only "he", "his cut" and "Tax" have come up.
- **Fix:** give the title to the smiling young man: "The King'll have the new one. And he'll have that."

**4. The landing numbers can't be true.**
> "On the six hundredth landing, or the seven hundredth…"
- **Category:** The world wouldn't produce it.
- **Why it reads wrong:** the chapter set the stair at "four thousand and some steps" with a reader every hundred, which makes about forty landings.
- **Fix:** "On the twentieth landing, or the thirtieth…"

**5. Eli could not have missed the cut that morning.**
> "he had a cut on his forehead that Eli hadn't seen that morning"
- **Category:** A clipped join (continuity).
- **Why it reads wrong:** Vosk got the cut in the slot before he threw the gravel, so Eli saw him with it.
- **Fix:** "that Eli hadn't noticed that morning". This also suits Eli, who doesn't look.

**6. The shaved-head girl has nothing under her, and she weakens Teo's second warning.**
> "She was at the Stoop. She was on the bridge. Now she's here."
- **Category:** A cool moment with nothing under it.
- **Why it reads wrong:** she later kneels with the market and flees like everyone else. Teo's warning therefore points at nothing, and the ambush comes from the tax boy instead. The pattern is "Teo is right three times", and here Teo is wrong.
- **Fix:** make her the King's eyes. Teo sees her slip through the plastic strips before the young men come out. Alternatively, she is the one not kneeling when they come out of the alley.

**7. A reader can't tell whether Eli knows his father is the Emperor.**
> "It's kept twenty-four years, it can keep tonight."
- **Category:** A clipped join.
- **Why it reads wrong:** the letter carries the Emperor's seal and Eli treats it as his father's first letter. If he knows, the oval stamped on every landing he walks past should touch him at least once. If he doesn't, "twenty-four years" makes no sense.
- **Fix:** add one line in the kitchen that settles it. One option: Eli has always been told "your father's people", and the seal is the first time "people" has had a shape.

**8. The hopping yard orc reads as puppetry, not a shock collar.**
> "the family's yard orc, a grown man, was hopping… with his arms tight to his sides"
> "The other way, the readers could tell the chip to do things."
- **Category:** The world wouldn't produce it (R143: a shock collar, full control only when the wearer is unruly).
- **Why it reads wrong:** a nine-year-old steering a man's legs for fun is full control used for play.
- **Fix:** make it pain. The fob pulses into his feet, he hops to get off the ground, and he lands and jerks again. Ilder's "made me dance" then means the same thing.

**9. Vosk never slept.**
> (Vosk comes up before dawn, works a shift and goes down again that night.)
- **Category:** The world wouldn't produce it (bodies).
- **Why it reads wrong:** he climbs four thousand steps twice in one day with no sleep, and his body never shows it.
- **Fix:** give him one beat of it, such as nodding off on the step over the bird, or Eli noticing that his eyes are red.

**10. Nobody follows the prize.**
> "Out of the dust round its foot the young men in yard trousers were coming back…"
- **Category:** A person acts for the plot.
- **Why it reads wrong:** the King wanted Eli, yet his crew lets both humans walk away across the bridge.
- **Fix:** "the others went to the heap and dug with their hands". The King comes first.

**11. The descent is a seed parade.**
> (The Stoop door, the ship-bridge, the 94-year-old dwarf and Velk's five-object shelf, back to back.)
- **Category:** Too much at once.
- **Why it reads wrong:** on the walk down, each stop is a look-at-this with no pressure behind it, so it reads like a tour.
- **Fix:** keep them all, but put the clock on them. Teo says "we're late" or checks the slate between the bridge and Hookline, so the wonders are seen while the group is moving.

**12. Some lines tell.**
- "He had the feeling he got at the edge of the clubs, that he'd found something nobody up top had any idea about. He wanted to tell someone." The laugh and the hand on the door already show this. Cut the second sentence.
- "Eli laughed out loud, mostly from relief." Cut "mostly from relief".
- "Nobody ever. Said it like that… You looked at me like I was. Something." This explains "He's mine". It is part of the brief's protected speech beat [P], so trim it rather than cut it, at Patrick's call.

## Clashes for Patrick (no change proposed)
- **The Orc King's scale.** `09` (t532) has the Orc King and "his plague army moving across the galaxy". Here he is a den-boss in the Scald. The rework is held (NP L30, #38). The ribbon "PLAGUE HOLD" half-bridges the two.
- **The King's draw.** Dwarves sag along with the orcs, including the old dwarf, whose box stops. That suggests he draws from anyone near him, not only through chips, and it leans toward settling his nature, which is held.
- **Eli and the Tower.** Eli is strong enough to lift a flyer, yet he lives facing the Tower and feels nothing ("you'd feel the tower like the sun", BANK 11-16). Sensing falls under Skill (R095), so an untrained Eli works, but one line should own it.
- **The Queen ribbon.** "INQUISITION ESCORT INCLUDES DUELIST KNOWN AS 'THE QUEEN'" places her with the Inquisition. That may fix her role before Gate 1 decides on the "sent by Mika" carry-over.

## Rules this chapter sets on the page (for the payoff-keeper's `ledger_rules.md`)
1. **The Scald.** The old ground is warm and nobody knows why. The old walls have no seams, and to people with the knack they give a "just-stopped-talking" feeling. The psychics press their foreheads to them. Living on the old ground deforms people.
2. **Drift.** It floats, casts no shadow and lights nothing. You never touch it with skin. Children catch it with wire nets, their arms wrapped in rag.
3. **The orc chip.** It sits in the back of the neck under a pale scar. Readers wake it, and the answer runs cold down the arms. Readers and fobs can make it act. Lateness at the slot brings a shock from the jaw to the back muscles, then "Late. Logged."
4. **The Long Stair.** It has 4,000-odd steps and a reader every hundred. Humans get a light, and so does Eli, green every time.
5. **Gates.** The Terrace front gate reads humans green with "Good evening" and no name. It doesn't read orcs: it bites them, so orcs use the culvert slot, one orc wide. Orcs don't ride lifts.
6. **Orc customs.** A thumb pressed to the scar is the greeting and the comfort. "Walk well" is the farewell. Nobody sings the Hurrak song all the way through.
7. **The Emperor's seal.** An empty oval stamped on every landing, and on the letter. The tutors say "the face is there for the loyal."
8. **Cores.** Lifts run on certified pool cores of grey wet flesh that tighten under load. Cheap Scald cores are "called something else".
9. **Flesh goods.** Cases are stamped "CLONE STOCK — NO SOUL". "Real" costs more and can't be told apart. An orc arm sells for less than a goat's.
10. **The Tower.** Nothing is built within a mile of it, and the traffic lanes bend round it. The rich say it is warm on the face. Unchipped, Vosk felt nothing. With the network, "the sun in the next room": everybody gets it the first time, and the feeling fades.
11. **The account.** It has never said no, and it produced 1,100 marks in cash in a day.
12. **Money.** Marks (dwarven) and imperial. Vosk saved 41 marks in nine years.
13. **The Stoop.** A street at giant scale with a curb chest-high to a man. Its doors have no seams and nobody has opened one. Every drill hole is no more than a thumb deep.
14. **The bridge.** A ribbed girder, warm "like a horse", with a dwarven salvage stamp. "It was a ship." Don't touch it.
15. **Velk's stock.** It "came out" and doesn't go back. The depot takes a third, and "his cut" comes on top.
16. **The Legion chip.** Its leash is removed, so "it'll pull for him, nobody pulls on him". It is on the Legion net, and nobody looks for an orc there. The King's crew pulls it from the body.
17. **The King's crew.** Young orcs with wire on their knuckles and necks opened and stitched with wire. They carry strapping cutters and call enclave orcs "Terrace".
18. **The King's presence.** Heat, then hot metal with a sweetness behind it. Everyone kneels facing the walls and nobody looks. People say "he's eating".
19. **The King.** Orc chips are sewn into his skin. When he draws, everyone kneeling sags. He breaks bones by closing his hand. He survived the arch: the heap moves and gives off heat.
20. **The cost of drawing.** The body pays: steam, skin split like cooked meat, blood in the eyes and ears, knuckles split open. Vosk: "Nobody spent me."
21. **Eli's reach.** He has had it since he was six. It failed him under threat, and his hands won't stop shaking.
22. **Teo.** He slows at the gate, and Eli has never asked why. He breathes slowly under pressure. Afterwards he is sick once, and his hands shake.
23. **The letter.** Delivered by hand into Eli's own fingers, never opened, its seal now cracked.
24. **The watchers.** Grey-coat men with a seal on the collar "don't look" at Eli.
25. **The night count.** The steward reads numbers off a slate. Vosk's number goes unanswered and gets a mark.
26. **Vosk's sock.** It holds 41 marks, which he asked to go to the cook.

## Verdict: **FIX FIRST**
1. Make the cutter a hostage threat against Vosk, not a slash at the King's prize (#1).
2. Plant Eli's reach once before the freeze (#2).
3. Have someone say "the King" before Eli's narration uses it, and fix the landing count (#3, #4).
