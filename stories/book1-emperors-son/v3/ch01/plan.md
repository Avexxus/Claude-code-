# Chapter One plan (v3): Claude's calls, made without asking (Patrick 2026-11-17)
🌊 Story clay. Built from `00_brief_v3.md`, CATCHUP §4–5, BANK 11-16/17 and the codex as passed (R001–R116+).

## Calls I made
- **No flight from the house.** Eli lives in a rich enclave outside the palace: a sealed upper district of the capital, at the Tower's foot on Earth. He has been raised by his father's reach and never by his father. He has no idea how far that reach goes.
- **The chapter opens on the world before Eli,** through the orc friend's eyes (LOCAL name to come). This is the only POV break in the book. It puts the orcs on the page as people before Eli can see them that way, and it is an orc's view of the far-future capital. Then the POV goes to Eli, close third, for the rest of the book.
- **Why they go down:** Eli's idea of a gift. The orc friend has already met a Returned dealer, who sells a premium chip with its control removed. Eli has decided to pay for it and to come along, because "it's what friends do". He never asks whether his friend wants him there. Teo warns him three times and Eli doesn't hear it.
- **The Orc King** rules the undercity's orcs. He is huge, and he draws on stolen Remnant. Keep his nature open and keep him off the page as much as possible: a presence first, then the fight.
- **The fight:** the orc friend, with the new chip, draws on stolen Remnant against the King. He loses, but buys them time. Eli freezes. Teo is cool-headed and gets Eli out. The orc's last speech: he remembers a boy defending him from bullies, and he's proud to die for the man that boy grew into. That is the first death, and an orc choosing how he is used up.
- **End of the chapter:** Teo and Eli surface, and the enclave's biometric gate reads Eli and opens with no question. That is his father's reach, unseen. The letter is on the table at home, unopened. Jon is NOT in this chapter. He appears in ch2 (one small glimpse at most here: a stranger eating something on a stair, never named).

## The world to show (the codex, Book 1 era)
The far-future stalemate: packed worlds, a galaxy-spanning AI reading biometrics, the Empire's grey efficiency, the faceless oval seal, clone stock "NO SOUL", flesh goods as ordinary goods. The chip works like a shock collar, and the orcs are people with traditions, heroes and tales. The Tower is felt like a sun by anyone sensitive. The capital has a wrongness: radiation ghosts in the oldest ground, an unease nobody can explain. The rift bleed sits nearest the Tower. Grit, smell and heat throughout.
