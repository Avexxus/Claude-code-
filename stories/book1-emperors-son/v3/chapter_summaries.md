# Chapter summaries: Book 1 v3 (The Emperor's Son)
🌊 Story clay. Kept by continuity-keeper. Facts, not interpretation. **Rebased 2026-11-17** onto the locked split. ONE and TWO replace `ch01/draft_v9` and the old single Chapter One summary.

## ONE · The Gift
*Text: `v3/ch01/new_ch01_v2.md` (about 8,200 words). POV: Vosk (L3–235), then Eli in close third (L239–643).*

**What happens:**
- Vosk, an orc of Eli's house, climbs up from the Scald with a dwarf's price: eleven hundred marks by tomorrow night, against the forty-one he has saved. On Pole Street he kneels while the King "eats". He is fourteen minutes late, and the slot shocks him.
- At Eli's window he asks for a loan, to be paid back at a mark a month. Eli laughs, waves off the terms, and hears "the Scald, tonight". "Leave it with me."
- Vosk works the trees. The Ilder boy's fob drops the Ilders' man, not Vosk: "His dad's does anybody's."
- Eli wakes at eleven. A grey-coated courier hands him a sealed letter, and he puts the fruit bowl on it. The account pays at once, and a woman rings to check.
- On the Vennicks' roof Eli turns Vosk's terms into a joke and says he'll go too. The chair won't lift while everyone watches; the server's tray jumps when he flicks it unwatched. Ilder says Vosk "could dance."
- He talks over Lisane, who is losing her house, and over Teo, whose place off-world is on hold. He has Vosk's lates struck and picks the long coat.
- In the kitchen Teo warns him, "He didn't ask you to come." Vosk says "Not in the coat" and falls asleep. Teo finds a crews' shaft.

**What changes:** the loan becomes a gift and an outing, and Eli will carry the money.

**Left hanging:** the letter, unopened; the woman who rang; the forty-one in the sock; Lisane's move; Teo's held place.

## TWO · The Scald
*Text: `v3/ch02/new_ch02_v2.md` (about 8,600 words). POV: Eli, close third, throughout.*

**What happens:**
- The gate lets the two humans out. The slot bites Vosk. On Ancel Row the doorman greets "Mr Eli" and laughs at "Down". A sump cage takes them below and stalls by the sumps. Eli won't give Vosk the purse; Vosk tells him where the forty-one is, and Eli mocks the terms to his face.
- In the Scald everyone watches the coat. Eli flicks a stuck drift light into a girl's net, and he crosses a warm, ribbed bridge to Hookline. He ignores Teo's second warning and buys soup; the girl slips into the arch.
- Velk takes the money and his watch, cuts out Vosk's chip and fits a leash-free Legion chip. Vosk feels the Tower and "all of them". Eli keeps him sitting, over Teo's third warning, and a tax boy runs.
- The crew and the King come for the chip and the coat. Vosk draws. A crewman holds a cutter at Eli's ear, and Eli begs Vosk to put his hand down; the King breaks Vosk's arm. Eli's reach fails under every eye. Teo frees him with a ladle.
- Vosk walks into the arch and the arch falls on the King. Dying, Vosk says Eli once came off a step to defend him, that he is proud, and that he picked it. The King's hand comes out of the heap. A crewman hooks at Vosk's chip.
- The Scald climbs the Long Stair. The gate says "Good morning". Eli leaves the account unrung and the letter under the bowl. At the count Vosk's number goes unanswered.

**What changes:** Vosk is dead. Eli has lost the money and the watch and has failed to reach. Teo's palm is cut.

**Left hanging:** the living King; the Legion chip in the crewman's hook; Velk's tin and the watch; the forty-one under the bunk; the letter.
