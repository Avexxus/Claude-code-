## ONE · The Gift
*Text: `v3/ch01/new_ch01_v2.md` (about 8,200 words). POV: Vosk (L3–235), then Eli in close third (L239–643).*

**What happens:**
- Vosk, an orc of Eli's house, climbs up from the Scald with a dwarf's price: eleven hundred marks by tomorrow night, against the forty-one he has saved. On Pole Street he kneels while the King "eats". He is fourteen minutes late, and the slot shocks him.
- At Eli's window he asks for a loan, to be paid back at a mark a month. Eli laughs, waves off the terms, and hears "the Scald, tonight". "Leave it with me."
- Vosk works the trees. The Ilder boy's fob drops the Ilders' man, not Vosk: "His dad's does anybody's."
- Eli wakes at eleven. A grey-coated courier hands him a sealed letter, and he puts the fruit bowl on it. The account pays at once, and a woman rings to check.
- On the Vennicks' roof Eli turns Vosk's terms into a joke and says he'll go too. The chair won't lift while everyone watches; the server's tray jumps when he flicks it unwatched. Ilder says Vosk "could dance."
- He talks over Lisane, who is losing her house, and over Teo, whose place off-world is on hold. He has Vosk's lates struck and picks the long coat.
- In the kitchen Teo warns him, "He didn't ask you to come." Vosk says "Not in the coat" and falls asleep. Teo finds a crews' shaft.

**What changes:** the loan becomes a gift and an outing, and Eli will carry the money.

**Left hanging:** the letter, unopened; the woman who rang; the forty-one in the sock; Lisane's move; Teo's held place.

## TWO · The Scald
*Text: `v3/ch02/new_ch02_v2.md` (about 8,600 words). POV: Eli, close third, throughout.*

**What happens:**
- The gate lets the two humans out. The slot bites Vosk. On Ancel Row the doorman greets "Mr Eli" and laughs at "Down". A sump cage takes them below and stalls by the sumps. Eli won't give Vosk the purse; Vosk tells him where the forty-one is, and Eli mocks the terms to his face.
- In the Scald everyone watches the coat. Eli flicks a stuck drift light into a girl's net, and he crosses a warm, ribbed bridge to Hookline. He ignores Teo's second warning and buys soup; the girl slips into the arch.
- Velk takes the money and his watch, cuts out Vosk's chip and fits a leash-free Legion chip. Vosk feels the Tower and "all of them". Eli keeps him sitting, over Teo's third warning, and a tax boy runs.
- The crew and the King come for the chip and the coat. The King is an orc twice Vosk's size with a grafted body: mismatched pieces sewn with wire, riveted metal plates, bare bone, a second row of knuckles, and one chip at the back of his neck. His face is his own. Vosk draws. A crewman holds a cutter at Eli's ear, and Eli begs Vosk to put his hand down; the King breaks Vosk's arm. Eli reaches, remembering a loader he held off the Ilders' gate at fifteen, and nothing comes under every eye. Teo frees him with a ladle and his palm is cut.
- Vosk walks into the arch and the arch falls on the King. Dying, Vosk says Eli once came off a step to defend him, that he is proud, and that he picked it. The King's patched hand comes out of the heap. A crewman hooks at Vosk's chip.
- The Scald climbs the Long Stair. The gate says "Good morning". Eli leaves the account unrung and the letter under the bowl. At the count Vosk's number goes unanswered.

**What changes:** Vosk is dead. Eli has lost the money and the watch and has failed to reach. Teo's palm is cut.

**Left hanging:** the living King; the Legion chip in the crewman's hook; Velk's tin and the watch; the forty-one under the bunk; the letter.

## THREE · Company
*Text: `v3/ch03/draft_v5.md` (story text L1–625). POV: Eli, close third, throughout. Days 2–5.*

**What happens:**
- Day 2: the cook bins her bloodied brush. The steward shows Vosk's amber line with an empty CAUSE box and leaves it open. Teo refuses a clinic ("They log it") and goes home. That night the barracks sings.
- Day 3: the steward strips Vosk's bunk. Effects "go up" unless there's a claim, so Eli logs Vosk as owing him eleven hundred and takes the sock. The account woman rings: "Nothing drawn… One short… No cause." Teo reads the gate's page. At the Ilders' party Dace mocks Vosk's terms. Ilder tells his side of the bite and learns Vosk is dead. Eli leaves his glass on the grass.
- Day 4: a new orc takes the count. The Ilders' man sets the watch on a wall: "He's asking… Dwarf paid her cut in it." The new one is shocked at the slot, which lights for him and not for Eli. Eli has the late struck.
- Day 5, dawn: a man eats plums in Eli's kitchen. "Sorry about Vosk." The King is asking. A crew, a job that "Ends at the top of the Tower", a teacher. The terms: come today, don't come back, do it when told. Eli says yes. Teo, his hand going bad, warns three times, then pockets his stone and comes. The man gives his name to Teo: "Jon." Eli takes the letter unopened and wears the second-best coat. The house bell rings as they go. The slot doesn't light for any of them.

**What changes:** Eli leaves the house and the Terrace, outside the gate's sight, on Jon's terms. Teo skips his clinic.

**Left hanging:** the letter; the King's asking; how Jon got in; Teo's hand; the sock; the untold part of Jon's price; who rang last.

## FOUR · The Beater
*Text: `v3/ch04/draft_v5.md` (story text L1–529, about 7,940 words). POV: Eli, close third, throughout. Day 5, from the culvert to off-world.*

**What happens:**
- Jon leads them out along the Spur. Eli notices the headache and the lean for the first time. Teo is feverish, has no coat and stops three times. A young orc with crew wire on his knuckles and neck watches the coat and goes down a side stair. Eli says "Jon"; Jon walks on.
- At a fry stall Eli's bad hand closes "most of the way" on a strip, "Further than yesterday."
- At nine, at the berth checkpoint, a notice reads TERRORIST CELL with blank face boxes. The guard sees Teo's dressing and refuses him. Jon says "Wait there", and Eli obeys. They go round the back, through the orc gate, where the disc drops a loitering old orc and stays dark for the humans.
- At the last berth sits the beater, a patched hauler loading bone meal stencilled ITHE. The pilot logs them as "meal" and wants no names. Jon says "Got elves to see" and "Ithe. There's a man there'll find you", and goes into the crowd.
- A crate falls toward a young loader. Unwatched, Eli catches it and sets it back. He hides a nosebleed, and his hand locks on the sock. A man in house colours on a Seventh ship lifts a slate at him. Eli steps into the hold.
- The beater climbs past the Tower, which has no top, and out into black. Teo reads "Earth" off the board. The wound has a red line past the elbow. Nothing rings out. The pilot trades pills for Eli's watch. Eli cleans the wound with a kit blade and tapes it. Teo asks for water.

**What changes:** Eli is off-world without Jon, the watch or the account. Teo's wound is cleaned but spreading.

**Left hanging:** Ithe and "a man there'll find you"; Jon's return; the house-colours slate; the Spur watcher; the pills, "a few days"; the water; the stern knock; the letter.

## FIVE · The Teacher
*Text: `v3/ch05/draft_v4.md` (story text L1–611, about 7,950 words). POV: Eli, close third, throughout. Ithe days 1–3 (Days 6–8). Added 2026-11-21.*

**What happens:**
- The knock is the coupling going. The pilot puts them off on an ore pad, which Eli takes to be Ithe, and flies off with the watch. Human loaders unload, one with a chip scar behind his ear. Nobody comes.
- A stall woman says Pask "met the boats" and is dead. The clinic has a reader ("They log it"). Pask's yard on the Cut is chained shut.
- In the yard across the lane, Eli lifts a pallet of pig iron, thinking no one is looking. A woman and four boys come under it, and it tips. An old man in the far shed holds Eli's reach, stops the rain for a count of one, and sets the pallet down. His nose bleeds. Eli is sick, and his hand locks. The old man refuses him: "Don't use more than you can control." "Most masters barely teach." His students "got the chip."
- They wait outside the wall. Seffa, the yard's cook, brings water, broth and a blanket. Eli puts his coat on Teo. On Day 2 the last pills go, and Teo's line is past the shoulder. Oskel, who is chipped, lifts a bar and bleeds, and Seffa sends him off. The tall boy says the old man has never done anything in two years. Seffa names a gnome on Ammet who "sits with the dying."
- On Day 3 Teo collapses, delirious, and Eli gives him two yellow pills. Eli goes in: "Not me. Him." The master: "Seffa takes him. Now." / "Bring him back breathing. Then we'll see." / "Waiting's not why." Seffa takes them on an ore boat for nothing.

**What changes:** Jon's man is dead. Eli has met the master and been refused, has no coat and no pills, and believes he has a yes. Teo has a line up his neck. Seffa is with them.

**Left hanging:** "Then we'll see"; whether Pask was Jon's man; the master's shaking cup; who stood the cup on Eli's palm; Oskel and Ferrel; the pie Thursday; Seffa's four days; the soft letter; the account call.
