## Chapter One · The Gift
*Text: `v3/ch01/draft_v6.md` (about 9,550 words). POV: Vosk (L3–81), then Eli in close third.*

**What happens:**
- Vosk, an orc of Eli's household, climbs home from the Scald with a dwarf's price in his head: eleven hundred marks. On Pole Street the undercity kneels while the King "eats". Vosk climbs the Long Stair past the chip readers and arrives fourteen minutes late; the gate shocks him.
- That evening, Eli has the money from his father's account and a sealed letter he won't open. He insists on carrying the money down himself. Teo warns him three times. With a rota for a sump shaft, they reach the Scald.
- At Hookline the dwarf Velk takes the money and Eli's watch, cuts out Vosk's chip, and fits a leash-free Legion chip. Vosk feels the Tower and the other orcs.
- The King's crew and the King come. Vosk draws against him. A crew member holds a cutter to Eli's ear. Vosk looks back, and the King breaks his arm. Eli raises his hand and nothing comes. Teo frees him with a ladle.
- Vosk lets it all out. The arch comes down on the King.
- Dying, Vosk tells Eli about a boy who came off the step to defend him from an Ilder fob. Eli doesn't remember. Vosk says: "I picked it."
- Teo drags Eli out. They climb the Long Stair, and every reader gives Eli green. The Terrace gate says "Good evening". At home Eli puts the letter back under the bowl, and at the night count Vosk's number goes unanswered.

**What changes:**
- Vosk is dead.
- Eli has lost the watch and the money, and has his first failure to reach.
- Teo has a cut palm.

**Left hanging:**
- the letter;
- whether the King survived;
- the Legion chip, being hooked out;
- Vosk's 41 marks;
- whose mark is on Velk's tin;
- the woman who rang the house.
