## Chapter One · The Gift
*Text: `v3/ch01/draft_v7.md` (about 9,800 words; replaces v6). POV: Vosk (L3–79), then Eli in close third.*

**What happens:**
- Vosk, an orc of Eli's household, skipped the night count to see a dwarf about a chip: eleven hundred marks, against his forty-one. Climbing home past Pole Street, kneeling while the King "eats", he arrives fourteen minutes late; the slot shocks him.
- That evening Eli has the money from his father's account and a sealed letter he won't open. He flicks Teo's apple core to the door. He insists on carrying the money; Vosk objects and falls asleep. Teo finds a crews' shaft.
- At Hookline the dwarf Velk takes the money and Eli's watch, cuts out Vosk's chip, and fits a leash-free Legion chip. Vosk feels the Tower and the other orcs.
- The King's crew and the King come; Eli is "worth more than any chip". Vosk draws. With a cutter at his ear, Eli begs Vosk to put his hand down; Vosk looks, and the King breaks his arm. Eli's reach gives nothing. Teo frees him with a ladle.
- Vosk walks himself under the arch and lets it all out. The arch falls on the King.
- Dying, Vosk tells of a boy who came off the step against an Ilder fob, and says he is proud to die for the man that boy grew into. Eli doesn't remember.
- They climb the stair at dawn; the gate says "Good morning". Eli leaves the letter under the bowl. At the morning count Vosk's number goes unanswered.

**What changes:** Vosk is dead. Eli has lost the watch and the money and has his first failure to reach. Teo has a cut palm.

**Left hanging:** the letter; whether the King survived; the Legion chip, being hooked out; Vosk's 41 marks; Velk's tin; the woman who rang.
