## SEVEN · Listen (about 7–8k words)
**Job:** back to the master; the training actually starts; Jon checks in by relay (he doesn't return); Seffa chooses to come further, at the cost of the yard and Oskel; the next destination is set (the door on the ninth: the rift and the prison, ch8).

**Calendar.** Arrival on Ithe is **Day 10** (C4). Training Day 10 to Day 11. Jon's call and Seffa's pie on **Day 12, Thursday** (see C4). Leave the Cut Day 13. Night 9 is the barge going back; SEVEN opens at dawn on Day 10.

1. **Back at the yard on Ithe.** Rain, frost in the lane, horns, the small red sun. Teo walks the last of the lane with Seffa and Eli. Teo breathing ("Bring him back breathing"). The master stands in his gate. He looks at Teo's hand (held up, closed and open) and then past Teo to Doc, and his eyes stay on Doc. His face does not move. He takes Eli on, on his terms: no lifting until told, no questions, the hours are the cranes'. Eli: "That's a yes. Isn't it?" Master: "Yes." Then the terms.
2. **Training, in scenes, not a montage.** The crane rows at row four, day shift.
   - "Listen, don't push." The cranes, timing loads. The tall boy shows the swing: wait for it, go with it, not against it. Teo says "It's swinging." Eli says he's listening.
   - Eli lifts a bundle of pig iron on the wrong swing, shouting, and the reach goes out wide. The guy-line whips, the tall boy's arm goes under the load, the bundle comes down on the rig frame and two bars slide. Eli's hand locks shut. The master, from the crate, eyes shut: "Don't use more than you can control." **(P2: the second time. It is marked as the second.)** Eli prises the hand open with the other.
   - **The moth lesson** (night, the shed lamp): find a moth blind, by listening. A moth comes to the lamp (A1: the lamp draws them). Eli hears the wings, points at the lamp, points at the wrong wall. The master: "Listen. Don't look." Eli closes the hand round it. It dies. Eli is warm about it: "I felt it go." The master: "Again." (Record the cold; no comment.)
   - Small, uneven progress with the hand, matched to the ledger: after the lock, "partway" on the crate strap by morning; "most of the way" on the moth by night. Cold sets it back in the morning frost (Day 11).
   - Eli is warm about his idea of the master, and doesn't hear what the master tells him: "He's wonderful, Seffa. A proper teacher." The master: "Listen." Eli: "I am listening."
3. **Jon's check-in** (Day 12, midday). Oskel up the Cut at his loose fast pace: "Seff. Seff. Listen. There's a call for you down on row four. For the one in the coat." Eli: "That's me." Oskel: "It said the coat." Teo gets up in the coat. Eli goes too.
   - The relay: a tin hut at the foot of crane four, a handset on a cord, a box with a dial, a lamp in a cage. No one in the hut. The row man has put it through and gone.
   - Teo takes the handset. Jon: "Is that the coat?" Teo: "Yes." Jon: "Thought it might be." Behind Jon, water going over something steady, and men a long way off.
   - Jon to Teo: "That's come right, then." (He knows the hand. Nothing on the grid recorded the healing. Eli does not say how Jon knows. Teo says nothing.)
   - Eli takes the handset. "Jon. It's me." "Son." Eli: the yard, the teacher, "He's said yes." Jon: "Good." Eli: "Are you coming?" Jon: "Ya don't need me for the next bit." Eli: "What's the next bit?" Jon: "The door. On the ninth." Eli: "Which ninth? Jon, what door—" Jon: "Mind yerself." The line goes.
   - Teo holds the handset to his ear after Eli has let go of it, and hears the end. Then he hangs it on the hook.
   - Walking back up the Cut, Eli talks about the cranes, the boys, what the master will say about the door. The account does not come up (C1).
   - **Jon's presence.** He is heard, not seen, and never interpreted. No one says where he is. Nobody asks him a question he answers.
4. **Seffa's choice** (Day 12, Thursday, evening). The pie was due today (Oskel, Day 7: "Bring the pie Thursday… The one with the onions," `5:L435`). Seffa has the onions cut on the board in the yard kitchen and no pie. Oskel is at the stove with a spoon. "You said Thursday." "I said."
   - She tells him he stops on: he has his shift, and the chip. He asks if she's coming back. She doesn't answer that. She ties the onions up in a cloth for the boys and puts the board on the sill. The cost is shown: Oskel's hand on the spoon, the chip square behind his ear, the laugh that starts and goes too long and stops. No speech. "Somebody's got to feed you," she says to the pot. Then she goes to the shed.
   - The master, on the crate, with the strap, eyes shut. Seffa: "I'm going with them." He waits. Then: "The boys eat at dark."
   - **Seffa does not say "brother".** Oskel is not called her brother on the page (C8).
5. **The master won't come.** Night, Day 12. Eli asks him to come to the door: "You'd be— there'll be the rest of it, the cranes, whatever it is. The door's on the ninth. We'd need someone who can—" The master: "I don't leave the Cut." Eli: "Not even—" Master: "No." (P3.) Eli goes on to talk about the room, the boys, the wing, and the master has gone back to the strap.
6. **End.** Leaving the Cut, Day 13, dawn. Eli, Teo, Doc and Seffa. Oskel at the foot of crane four with his shift lamp, not waving. The tall boy at the gate. The master at his gate, eyes shut, listening to them go. The gate is open a hand's width. Teo does not look back. Doc does not look back either, and Eli does. Stop on the sound.
