## Items, ranked by how much they break belief

**1. HIGH · convenience + broken rule (R-017)**
- **Line:** L217 "There's a call for you. Down on four. The row man put it through. He says it's been on the box since the bell." / L297 "There was a line now, down at four, and the row man could have put a call through to anybody in the city if you asked him nicely." / L263 Jon: "Are you on Ithe?" (Jon asks where Eli is; he already knows the coat is on the line, L241–245).
- **Problem:** Jon finds them on cue, through a relay that did not exist a chapter ago, asking for "the one in the coat". No step shows how Jon learned the Cut, the yard, or the coat. Eli's "There'll be a way to ring from here. There always is" (L67; 5:L489) is paid off the same morning. R-017 says the account is watched and logs; this call is logged by no one on the page, and "the row man put it through and gone" (L235) waves that away.
- **Fix:** The row man writes the call down before he puts it through: "He wrote the gate down. Said who asked for the coat." That gives a cost (someone at the gate gave them up) and a log.

**2. HIGH · leap (the walk-out)**
- **Line:** L389 "They went out of the Cut at first light, before the horns. … Teo walked in front, alone…"; L381 Eli: "Well. Then we'll see."
- **Problem:** Eli has just been taken on ("Yes… On what I say," L53) and begged for the job for two days. The next dawn the whole party walks out on an undated call ("On the ninth," L275) with no decision scene. Nobody says yes to the door. Missing step: the choice itself, and what the master's yes costs.
- **Fix:** One exchange in the far shed where Eli says he'll come back after the ninth and the master answers, "Then you're not taken on." The walk becomes a choice with a price.

**3. MED · cool moment with nothing under it (the master's look at Doc)**
- **Line:** L25 "His eyes came up off it, past Teo's shoulder, past Eli, and found Doc at the back of them with his bag. They stopped there, and stayed."
- **Problem:** The master, who has never seen Doc (story_state: "He has not seen Doc"), reacts to him as to a known figure. The reader gets a meaningful look and no reason for it.
- **Fix:** Seffa glances at Doc first, or the master's eyes follow her look: she took her mother to that man last winter (5:L511). One clause of cause.

**4. MED · telling / explained look**
- **Line:** L131 "Eli watched him watch, and felt the warm thing in his chest go up, the same as at the gate. The old man would see it. He'd see what Doc was."
- **Problem:** It names Eli's feeling and states what the master will understand, right after the look has done the work. Explaining a look is banned (RI §9; rubric Q2).
- **Fix:** Cut "He'd see what Doc was." and "felt the warm thing in his chest go up." Keep "The old man would see it" or cut the whole sentence.

**5. MED · convenience and a Doc-status line (H2/H3)**
- **Line:** L29 "He's a healer. He's the— Everyone likes him. He sits with the—"
- **Problem:** Eli announces Doc's standing. Patrick has ruled that no line may say Doc is respected (BANKp 11-11, H2/H3). Eli has known Doc for one day. Eli's warmth is design; the status claim is the problem.
- **Fix:** Cut "Everyone likes him." Keep "He's a healer." and let the yard see the rest.

**6. MED · broken on-page rule (R-016, the reach fails watched)**
- **Line:** L79 "You're not. You're looking at the iron." / L85 "He watched the hook." / L93–97 the reach "found the hook, and the hook came round the wrong way."
- **Problem:** Eli's own rule is "Somebody looks and it just goes" (5:L235), and the reach fails watched. Teo is watching the hook; the tall boy is watching Eli until L79. The page never says the watchers have turned away, so the reach working reads as a rule bent.
- **Fix:** One line before L93: the tall boy's eyes go to the iron, and nobody is looking at Eli when he reaches.

**7. MED · clipped join (the day skip)**
- **Line:** L303 "It was Thursday."
- **Problem:** Oskel's noon call (L209–L271), the walk to the relay, the lamp, the trough, and the morning all sit between two section breaks with no count. The reader cannot tell how many days have passed since the yard, or whether the 9th is near. Story_state puts Thursday at Day 12 only by inference.
- **Fix:** Name the gap in one line: "Two days on the crate, and then it was Thursday." (Or give a day count.)

**8. MED · a cool moment with nothing under it (the door and the calendar)**
- **Line:** L275 "The door," said Jon. "On the ninth." / L295 "They're all on the month, the rows, the cranes."
- **Problem:** Jon gives a date and a place with no cause for him to speak now (he never interprets; R-034). The door is never located, so Eli's plan and the party's walk have nothing under them. "On the month" is a new calendar fact that no earlier beat set.
- **Fix:** Jon names one thing Eli can act on (a gate, a street) in a single clause. Log the month as LOCAL or cut it to "the ninth."

**9. MED · leap (Seffa)**
- **Line:** L347 "I'm going with them," she said.
- **Problem:** Seffa leaves ten years of cooking, the boys, and a yard she has just been given. Her only reason on the page is "Somebody's got to feed you" (L339), said to the pot. The turn from "Are you coming back?" (L319) to "I'm going with them" has no step.
- **Fix:** Give her one reason she can state to the master, e.g. she is going to see the tall boy's arm through, or to see Teo fed on the road. A cost (the master says nothing, lets her go) is already there at L345–355.

**10. MED · too much at once**
- **Line:** L209–L397 (the last third).
- **Problem:** The call, the Thursday pie, the onions, Seffa's exit, the master's refusal, Eli's wheedling, and the dawn departure all land inside one unmarked stretch. The door is the chapter's turn but gets the least room.
- **Fix:** Cut the pie exchange to one line, or move Seffa's exit to the dawn beat so the call and the walk share a scene.

**11. MED · a clipped join (Doc's position)**
- **Line:** L117 "Doc had come up the rail from the shed. Eli hadn't seen him leave the bench."
- **Problem:** Doc sat on the yard bench until the bell (L65), then the group is at row four. The walk between them is missing, so Doc's arrival at the cranes reads as a jump.
- **Fix:** Say where he came from: "Doc came down the rail behind them with his bag."

**12. LOW · telling (the tall boy)**
- **Line:** L101 "He didn't think about it. Eli saw that. He was quicker than Eli had ever been, and quicker at everything…"
- **Problem:** The narration reports what Eli sees and then sums the boy up.
- **Fix:** Cut "Eli saw that." and "and quicker at everything."

**13. LOW · telling (Jon's voice)**
- **Line:** L261 "…warm, and easy, and not in any hurry."
- **Problem:** A gloss on a voice the reader already hears.
- **Fix:** Cut the gloss; keep "It was the one word."

**14. LOW · ambiguous knowledge line (Jon)**
- **Line:** L249 "That's come right, then," Jon said.
- **Problem:** Reads as Jon knowing about Teo's hand, which he has not been shown to know. R-034 says Jon is never interpreted.
- **Fix:** Make it "That's sorted, then," or cut it. Either way, no reading of the hand.

**15. LOW · untracked injury**
- **Line:** L115 "It cracked at the knuckle when it went."
- **Problem:** A new injury to the bad hand that is never mentioned again and is not on the hand clock.
- **Fix:** Cut the crack, or add it to the hand clock as a cost.

**16. LOW · unshown habit**
- **Line:** L307 "…had come up again at dark, the way he always came for his supper…"
- **Problem:** Oskel's supper habit is new and never set up.
- **Fix:** Cut "the way he always came for his supper."

**17. LOW · echo**
- **Line:** L339 "Somebody's got to feed you," Seffa said, to the pot.
- **Problem:** The same line is spent in ch06 (6:L499), to the hull. Seffa's ch7 use is a callback, but it is the second use in two chapters and it lands with no new cause.
- **Fix:** Keep it only if the cause in item 9 is added; otherwise cut it.
