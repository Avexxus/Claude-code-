## Flags, ranked by how much they break belief

### 1. The checkpoint: Jon queues Eli for a reader arch (Convenience / the world wouldn't produce it)
> "There was a queue. Jon walked down the side of it to the end and stopped, and stood there eating, and Eli and Teo stood behind him." … "Teo was next after Jon."

- **Why it reads wrong:** THREE took them out through the slot so that no reader would see Eli (3:L621; R-029, S-005 "his first step outside the reach"). Here Jon walks them straight into a reader arch, and Eli goes unlogged only because Teo's fever happens to fail the guard. The reader sees luck doing the plot's job, or Jon making a mistake.
- **Smallest fix:** Jon doesn't join the queue. He walks down the side of it **to the booth window**, and the guard sees Teo while Jon is at the glass. "Round the back" then comes from Jon's word plus the guard's laziness, and the arch never gets a look at Eli. The page still doesn't say why.

### 2. The coat: a "Done" deal that doesn't happen (A clipped join / continuity)
> "I could run her three months on that coat… You keep it in the hold. I don't want it stood on my ramp at the other end." / "Done," said Eli.

- **Why it reads wrong:** "Done" after a price reads as a sale. Then Eli wears the coat for the rest of the chapter (the sock and the letter are in it, and Teo's pus is on its cuffs). Was it sold or not? The charsim said to price the coat and not sell it.
- **Smallest fix:** make her line plainly about hiding it: "You keep it in the hold. You and it." Then change "Done" to "Absolutely." R-028 is still priced, and nothing changes hands.

### 3. The crate: where it falls, and what it costs (The world wouldn't produce it / broken promise R-016)
> "went over the side rail at the ramp's edge and hung there on its corner, tipping out over the drop past the edge of the cradle, and under it on the berth floor the young orc…"

- **Why it reads wrong:** the crate can't be going **over the drop** and falling **onto the orc on the berth floor** at once, so the stakes blur. A full crate of bone meal, stopped in mid-fall and lifted back, is a big lift. R-016 says a big lift makes Eli sick, and THREE set the scale for that as "sick for a day". The cost here is only a headache spike, a taste and a wet palm.
- **Smallest fix:** pick one. (a) The crate tips **inward** off the ramp toward the young orc's back, and Eli only checks the tip and lets it settle back, which is a small push that fits the small cost. Or (b) it goes over the drop, there's no orc under it, and the cost goes up a notch: he is sick over the hatch lip, or his nose bleeds once.

### 4. Water runs "uphill" toward the Tower, but the floor leans toward the Tower (A broken promise / the world)
> L3 "carried it off along the kerb toward the Tower, uphill" · L31 "went uphill with everything else, pink" · L23 "walking out along it you were walking very slightly uphill"

- **Why it reads wrong:** if walking away from the Tower is uphill, the floor tilts toward the Tower, so water running toward it is running **downhill**. ONE's rule is "ran off toward the Tower whichever way the street went" (1:L51). As written, the page contradicts itself inside a few paragraphs.
- **Smallest fix:** drop "uphill" at L3 and L31 and say "against the street" or "toward the Tower, whichever way the kerb ran". Keep the ankles at L23.

### 5. The orc gate bites a man ten places back (The world wouldn't produce it / R-024)
> "About ten from the front an orc had his eyes shut… he must have stood one step too long… The disc had gone on looking."

- **Why it reads wrong:** every reader so far bites the orc who loiters **at** it (1:L47, the stair landing; 3:L333, the slot). A disc that punishes a gap ten bodies away needs a reason the page doesn't give.
- **Smallest fix:** make him the **front** orc. He shuts his eyes in the disc's light, stands one beat too long, and goes down at the gate. The line then has to step round him to reach the disc, which sharpens "Nobody bent down."

### 6. Two hours go missing between the culvert and the checkpoint (A clipped join)
> 3:L583 "twenty to seven" → L73 "The watch said nine."

- **Why it reads wrong:** the walk is written as a few streets. Nothing tells the reader the Spur is two hours long, or that a fevered Teo walked all of it. The nine is a sting (his mother's man "on the Spur at nine"), so the time has to stay.
- **Smallest fix:** add one line in the berths approach: "It was a long way out. They stopped for Teo three times, and the third time he sat."

### 7. The headache and the lean: why does he notice them now? (A leap / telling)
> "He'd had it all his life. Everybody had it." · "He'd never once noticed. He noticed now"

- **Why it reads wrong:** there's no cause on the page. Eli is a Terrace boy, and ONE puts the headache in the mid-levels (1:L51), so "all his life" is doubtful. TWO already had "the walkway leaned" on this street (2:L31).
- **Smallest fix:** give the cause in four words: "He'd never walked it before," since his flyers came to the kerb (which L149 already says). Or tie it to the Scald: "Since the Scald he could feel it."

### 8. Teo reads "Earth" off a board he can't see (Convenience)
> "Teo had his head back against the wall and his eyes half shut. He read it off the board from the bench, past her shoulder, without lifting his head."

- **Why it reads wrong:** Eli has to lean forward against his strap to see a slice through the hanging door. Teo, slumped back with his eyes half shut, reads a line of text through that same slice.
- **Smallest fix:** let the board **say** it ("the board said something back"), with "Earth" in its flat voice, and Teo repeats it. That's the charsim's own option.

### 9. Jon's exit behind the cart (A cool moment with nothing under it; strict Jon)
> "A cart came across between, piled with sacks, and when it had gone by he wasn't on the walk any more"

- **Why it reads wrong:** the narration doesn't interpret Jon, but the **staging** does. It's the vanish trick, and it reads as uncanny, which is a "Jon knows / Jon is more" beat. S-029 said to add none in FOUR. Patrick's "odd" test applies.
- **Smallest fix:** give him a plain exit that Eli sees. He turns down the side stairs at the landing, or steps onto the hauler's ramp and is gone into her. The absence is the beat, not the disappearance.

### 10. Teo looks at the booth at nine (An unintended pointer)
> "he saw Teo look at it too, at his wrist, and then away, at the booth."

- **Why it reads wrong:** at nine, on the Spur, Teo looks at a man who then refuses to fill in a form. A reader may take the guard to be his mother's "man there doesn't write things down" (3:L503).
- **Smallest fix:** "and then away, back up the Spur." If the link is wanted, it's a new thread, so log it.

### 11. Eli's first time off-world gets no beat (A leap)
> "…and then the sky going black." → "Teo was sick before the hour was out."

- **Why it reads wrong:** he has never left Earth, and the black sky passes without a breath of his own.
- **Smallest fix:** add one line before the break: he puts his palm to the cold wall of the hold, or says "Teo. It's black," and gets nothing back.

### 12. The seal: "Teo." / "Nothing." (A cool moment with thin cause; low)
- **Why it reads wrong:** the beat lands only if the reader links the oval to the letter, and the page doesn't touch the letter here. The "Teo." / nothing pattern is also used twice (the plants, the seal).
- **Smallest fix:** at the seal, the letter's corner digs into his ribs as he walks. Then move the S-001 touch from L379 to here, or keep both if the payoff-keeper allows.

## Strict on Jon

They came back." The italic recall at L107 is Eli's memory of the term, not a reading of Jon. That's fine.

The brief calls him "autopilot warmth". One "son" at the handover ("Do as she says, son.") would keep him the same man. The cap allows two.

## The writer's doubts, ruled
**Eli cleaning the wound himself: keep it. It doesn't steal from Doc.**
- It's crude field work under a stranger's flat orders (blade, scrape, tape), done because nobody else will. It costs (the pus on the coat, Teo's tears, the watch already gone) and has a consequence (the clock).
- It treats the **local** wound and leaves the **spreading** one: the red line past the elbow, and "a few days… Then he wants a proper one". Tape can't touch that line, so Doc's job in SIX is untouched and is set up as the one thing Eli couldn't do.
- It also gives Doc something to undo: a tape job taped shut over an infection, which Doc can look at and fix.
- The one risk is "His hands had stopped shaking." It hints at a hidden healer's knack. Keep it, but don't repeat it.

**"Clone or the other?": right. Not too grim. Keep it as is.**
- It's the world's ordinary voice, already set in ONE ("Real! Real, not clone" / "Costs more, doesn't it", 1:L55–57; R-019).
- Jon's "Whatever's hot" is indifference, not cruelty, which is exactly Jon.
- It lands three days after Jon's "Rest of him goes by weight, down there" (3:L407). Eli eats and asks "What is that?" because he doesn't listen. Teo heard: he looks at the plate and refuses. The joke and the horror sit in different people, which is the chapter's whole method.
- Don't add a reaction from Eli. Don't explain "the other".
