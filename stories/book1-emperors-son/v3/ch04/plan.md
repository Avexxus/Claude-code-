## FOUR · The Merchant (about 7–8k words)
**Job:** leave the capital and the world Eli knows, gain a ship, and meet the old dwarf.

1. **Through the capital below the Terrace,** led by Jon. This is the city at the Tower's foot as a working machine:
   - the Tower's pressure, which only Eli feels;
   - the lean of the old ground;
   - flesh goods as ordinary goods;
   - the faceless seal;
   - a checkpoint where the Inquisition's "terrorist cell" notice is pasted, with no faces yet;
   - Jon walking through it with no trouble.
   Use scenes, not a tour. One turn: the checkpoint reads Teo's fever, and something has to be done about it.
2. **The dwarf's dock.** A private berth in a dwarven quarter. The old dwarf (LOCAL name, race-flavoured) is rich and old, and owns the ship; he won it, or took it as repayment. Money means nothing to him. He wants adventure, and he insisted on coming. He's the only one who understands its imperfect engine.
   - He talks brave and moral, but shows fear, selfishness and contradictory morals.
   - He never names a ledger, but he always asks for something, and the asks grow. **Plant the first small ask here.**
   - His stories about himself crack under pressure.
   - He owns orc labourers on the ship. Show them; Eli doesn't see them as people yet.
3. **Jon's handover.** Jon introduces them and doesn't come. He's "got elves to see" or something like it, and he's gone. He's always absent. He leaves one instruction: where they're going (the teacher), and nothing about why.
4. **Departure.** The ship lifts. Eli sees the Tower from above for the first time, and the capital around it. Earth falls away. Teo's fever: the dwarf has a "fix" that costs Eli the first thing he gives up. The letter is still unopened in his coat.
   - **End:** in transit. The engine sounds wrong, and only the dwarf can tell how wrong.
