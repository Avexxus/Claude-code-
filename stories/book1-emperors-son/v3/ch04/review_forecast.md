# Ch04 "The Beater": forecast pass

Read cold, STORY_ONLY only (ch01–03 skimmed first). No notes, no codex.

**How clean these forecasts are:** I read the chapter in chunks, and three chunks ran past a scene break before I stopped (at S2, S4/S5 and S7). Those forecasts were written after the fact and are marked *(after the fact)*. The guesses at the end of ch03, inside S3, inside S6 and inside S8 were made before I read on.

---

## S1 · Culvert to the Spur (the headache, the lean, the plants)
**Forecast (from the end of ch03):** Jon walks them down to the Spur and on to some kind of transport. Teo's hand gets worse in plain view. Eli starts to see the city differently now that the gate has stopped reading him. Confidence 3.
**Worry:** Is Teo's hand going septic, and will anyone act before it's too late?
**Grade: HIT.** Teo is grey and sweating with his hand buttoned into his coat. Eli now feels the headache and the lean of the street. That was the expected shape. It's a quiet scene that works mostly through mood.

## S2 · Flesh market, Jon's cone *(after the fact)*
**Forecast:** A walk through the market. Jon shows who he is through some small habit. Confidence 3.
**Worry:** Where is the King's "asking"? Ch03 promised "He'll come up."
**Grade: HIT.** The cone over the shoulder is a nice bit of Jon. But this is the second place in a row that re-walks ch01 (lift core, eyes sorted by colour, hands in pairs). Eli's tour-guide patter adds some irony, but nothing in the scene moves.

## S3 · Feeds, seal, booth (notice, guard refuses Teo)
**Forecast (made at "The watch said nine"):** The arch reader stays dark for them, as at the slot, and that causes trouble. Nine o'clock is Teo's missed appointment, and that will hurt. Confidence 3.
**Worry:** Will Teo say anything at all about the clinic he's skipping?
**Grade: NEAR, good surprise.** The trouble comes from the guard, not the reader. He sees the yellow on the dressing and won't let Teo through. The setup is right there (the dressing, "it'll close"). Eli catches himself starting the steward voice, and the line "When I say do a thing" pays off in the moment. The 9:00 beat lands as one shared look. Strongest scene so far.

## S4 · The orc gate (the old loader's fit) *(after the fact)*
**Forecast:** "Round the back" means the orcs' gate. Something there shows what the chip does. Confidence 3.
**Worry:** Will Eli finally stop for an orc?
**Grade: HIT.** Jon stepping over the fallen loader is a strong image. Eli goes round wide and steps in the gutter, which is a good small failure. The plot doesn't move, though, and it echoes the slot scene from ch03.

## S5 · Berths, the beater, the pilot, Jon leaves *(after the fact)*
**Forecast:** A shabby ship and a hard-nosed pilot. The coat is the fare. Jon comes along. Confidence 3.
**Grade: MISS, mixed.** Jon leaving ("Got elves to see." "Ithe.") surprised me. It's good in one way: it strands the boys and pays off "you'll not like it much." It's bad in another: Ithe and the elves come with no setup in this chapter. The only earlier seed is one line in ch01 (ELVEN DELEGATION RECALLED). "No names" and "after this I don't know you" are good. The long ribbed ship that Teo looks at plants a question and leaves it open.

## S6 · Hold, loading, the crate
**Forecast (made when the crate tips):** Eli catches it with his gift, and it works because nobody is watching. Then someone notices. Confidence 3.
**Worry:** Does his gift only work when he isn't seen?
**Grade: HIT.** The line "Nobody was looking up the ramp" makes the rule from ch03 explicit. The turn I didn't predict is the young orc folding the canvas, and Eli's "Good—" dying in his mouth. That is the chapter's best line of character change, and it's earned.

## S7 · Launch, the Tower through the glass, "Earth" *(after the fact)*
**Forecast:** Wonder at take-off. The Tower seen from above. Confidence 3.
**Grade: HIT on the wonder, MISS on "Earth."** The Tower outrunning the top of the glass is good. "Earth" read off the board is a big reveal dropped in one word. The setup partly exists (four worlds, plague holds, systems), but the reader can't tell whether Earth is where they're leaving or where they're going. Decide which it is and make that readable.

## S8 · Teo's wound, the kit, the watch
**Forecast (made at "Name it"):** She wants paying. Eli reaches for the sock, which is Vosk's forty-one marks, and the cost is moral. Confidence 3.
**Worry:** Will he spend Vosk's money?
**Grade: NEAR, good surprise.** She wants the watch instead. It was set up heavily in ch02–03 (the four faces, the moon, the King sending it back). Teo's "Don't" makes it cost something. Earned. This is the peak of the chapter.

## S9 · Cleaning the wound on the hold floor
**Forecast:** He does exactly what she told him to, and Teo is barely holding on at the end. Confidence 4.
**Grade: HIT at 4.** The scene is predictable by design, because the pilot laid out every step. The turns inside it hold it up: Eli's hands stop shaking, he reaches for the time and finds a bare wrist, and he asks "Ithe?" and gets no answer. It's a good soft ending, but the chapter closes on no new question, only the loose thing knocking.

## Tension curve
- S1 Culvert/Spur: 2. Mood, with Teo fading.
- S2 Flesh market: 2. Re-treads ch01.
- S3 Booth: 4. The guard turns Teo away; "Wait there."
- S4 Orc gate: 3. Strong image, no forward motion.
- S5 Berths/Jon leaves: 3, spiking at the exit.
- S6 Crate: 4. The gift works unseen; "Good—".
- S7 Launch: 3. Wonder, plus an unclear "Earth."
- S8 Wound/watch: 5. The peak.
- S9 Cleaning the wound: 4. Release, with nothing set up for the next chapter.

## Top three places to add a turn or a setup
1. **S1–S2: two flat sections back to back.** Ch03 ended on a threat ("He's asking," "He'll come up"), and ch04 never follows it up. Cut the market walk down. Add one sign that the King is still looking: wire on someone's knuckles on the Spur, a stitched neck in the queue, or the notice moved earlier and made personal. That gives the walk to the berths a sense of pursuit.
2. **S5: Jon's exit and Ithe.** Plant Ithe or the elves earlier in the chapter, for example on the S3 feed ribbon, which already exists. Also give Eli one real choice to go or turn back before the ramp comes up. Right now he is carried along the whole chapter.
3. **S7/S9: give the chapter a hook to end on.** Make "Earth" readable, and/or bring the unopened letter in at the end. It digs into his ribs once in S8 and is never touched again. One touch of the letter at the close, or a pilot line about Ithe, would give the next chapter its pull.

No forecast at confidence 4–5 hit in a way that flattens a scene. S9 is predictable on purpose and is held up by its small turns.
