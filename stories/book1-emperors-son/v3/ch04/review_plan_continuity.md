## Contradictions (both passages quoted)

### 1. World rules · "the Tower's pressure, which only Eli feels" · MEDIUM
- **State:** "Everybody had the headache. It sat behind the eyes and in the ears, all day, every day" (1:L51). "The rich said it was warm on your face." (1:L63) Velk, to an unleashed orc: "Tower… Everybody, first time. Sun in the next room." (2:L281)
- **Plan §1:** "the Tower's pressure, which only Eli feels;"
- **Problem:** the city already feels it: humans as the headache and the warmth, and Vosk, once the leash was out. "Only Eli" breaks all three.
- **Smallest fix:** "the Tower's pressure, which everyone has stopped noticing and Eli now can't." Or "which Eli feels as more than the headache."

### 2. World rules · the checkpoint "reads Teo's fever" · MEDIUM (R-004, R-007, R-029)
- **State:** a reader gives a human "only a light, a name maybe, a soft chime" (1:L43); "Every human face that passed under a reader got its light." (2:L17). Teo refused the clinic: "They log it." (3:L37). The slot doesn't read humans at all (3:L621).
- **Plan §1:** "the checkpoint reads Teo's fever, and something has to be done."
- **Problems:**
  - Reading a human's body is a new reader power, and nothing on the page sets it up.
  - A reader that reads Teo also reads Eli. That's his first read since he left by the slot, and it would log him leaving (R-007: hour, no name). This breaks the step outside the father's reach that THREE has just set up (ledger S-005: "hold it").
- **Smallest fix:** make the check something other than a reader: a guard who sees the sweat and the hand, or a hand-held thermal wand. Or keep the reader and decide on the page what Eli's light does there. Log the choice as a rule.

### 3. Plot logic · "Eli sees the Tower from above for the first time, and the capital around it" · MEDIUM
- **State:** "the Tower stood up out of the grey… pale, up and up into the cloud, where you couldn't see the end of it" (3:L445); "so big that the clouds broke round it" (1:L63). Jon's job "Ends at the top of the Tower" / "Never been." (3:L443, L453). Eli has had "both his flyers" (1:L565) and "pulled the whole flyer up out of the lake" (2:L399).
- **Plan §4:** "Eli sees the Tower from above for the first time, and the capital around it."
- **Problems:**
  - Above the Tower means above the top, which is the book's goal. Seeing it now spends the ending's image.
  - A flyer owner has seen the city from the air, so "for the first time" doesn't hold for the capital either.
- **Smallest fix:** "Eli sees the Tower from higher than he's ever flown, and it still goes on up past them into the cloud."
