# PART A · Chapter FOUR, re-carded

## A.1 What changes, in one screen

- **The door is a rift.** A seam in a wall that isn't there, finer than a needle's eye. Eli can prise it. It will not stay open: it shuts the instant nobody is holding it.
- **Nobody knows where they are.** No dinner briefing. Jon parks them on a dead moon ("Nobody goes there. Wait for me. Six days.") and doesn't come. Nobody on the page says "prison" or "hell" in this chapter. The people who live behind the seam call it home.
- **Teo holds it** (A.2). Eli and Doc go in.
- **Inside is a real place:** a hill road, a fed village with a tithe house, a crossing-ground where a human transit hull sits, a pit the crews bet into from above.
- **The hill woman** has walked up that road every tenth day for thirty years and always come down into the village again. Some people don't come down. She says so plainly. That's how Eli knows the seam is the place she means.
- **She follows them out.** Nobody asks her. She thanks nobody. At the first port she walks off the ship, and comes back on her own terms.
- **The rift shuts for good.**
- **Kept:** the raid, the missed gun and the nine; Krell, the grease pencil and the water ration; the Inquisition station; "When you've earned it"; the galley nights; "There's a sun down there"; the strap; Jon's "Good for you". Her name is never printed. The bond is a sense in the body.

## A.2 Who holds the rift: Teo

**Why Teo, not Doc.**
1. **The strain has to be real.** Doc is the strongest thing on the crew and hides it. If he held the gap his strain would be an act, and the cost would be nothing. Teo has almost no reach. He holds it through a condemned amplifier rig, and the rim has already taught the reader what a rig does to a boy who holds too long (v1 L221–223).
2. **His own reason puts him there.** Teo is the one who thinks something is wrong with Doc. He will not leave Doc holding Eli's only way back. "I'm not leaving him on the door." So his suspicion sends Eli into that country alone with the thing he suspects.
3. **It turns the friendship.** Eli has overruled Teo three times (Doc, the knife in Seluvane, the raid). This is the first time Teo overrules Eli.
4. **It puts Doc inside a soul-thief farm,** unwatched by the one person who watches him. That is the best plant for the throne room the book has.
5. **It rhymes with the Tower** without a footnote: a room that needs one person to stay and hold while the rest pass (7.3), and Teo walking somewhere alone on his own nerve (7.5).

**What it costs him, on the page (4.6):** the rig cooks onto his spine and comes off in strips; his left ear fills with blood and never clears; he bites through his lip; he has lost the count and his water. He held it with a grey coat's gun at his neck, because the coats wanted it open too.

**Body track from here (replaces the thigh wound, v1 L461):** deaf on the left; a wet dressing down his spine for a month, a ridged scar after; a shake in both hands that goes in a few weeks. The rig is dead and he never wears another. The left hand still burns at the moon dragon (6.8) and still never closes right.

**Plants needed earlier (two lines, in TWO):**
- 2.5: the rig boy who burns out was "holding the lip" of a well, keeping a well-mouth open while the pump crew worked. (v1 L221 already has "holding a line on the north wells".) Well-mouths close if nobody holds them. Everyone on the rim knows it.
- 2.6: Teo comes up the temple steps one night with a condemned rig off the company docks that he has patched. The master, without looking up: "Throw that in the glass." Teo keeps it in the flyer.

## A.3 What the narration calls her, and the place

- **Before she speaks (4.4):** "a woman", "the thin woman in the pit".
- **From 4.5:** **the hill woman.** It's what the village calls her ("the hill woman's gone up again; she'll be down by dark"). It's a neighbour's word, mildly mocking, and it has no jailer in it. She doesn't object to it and doesn't use it.

- **The place:** "the country behind the seam", "the hill", "the village"; later "the crossing" for the transit. v1 L941 becomes "They crossed once by a human transit hull, because there was no other way."

### 4.8 · The Board
**REACTION** · a human port, then the ship's galley, four months · Eli, the hill woman, Teo
- **Reaction:** on the third day in port Eli passes the legal exchange. She is standing in front of the price board.
- **Dilemma:** speak to her, and make it an invitation; or walk on. Later, one good night, he thanks her by reflex.
- **Decision:** he walks on. He says nothing after the thanks. He keeps sitting up.
- **Beats:**

# PART D · Grit brief

**Rules.** Smell and touch before sight. The body first: Eli's own, then the one nearest him. One exact ugly thing per paragraph, then move on. Nobody gloats and nothing lingers. Orcs and victims get a human gesture each time they appear. Nothing here is ever arousing. Heavy nouns and wet verbs; no clinical nouns ("fluid", "tissue", "remains").

## ONE · the yard
1. **The pens.** Hundreds standing in their own waste because the chip doesn't let them step aside. Ammonia. The scars at the skull base weep in the cold.
2. **The ramps and presses.** Hot fat in the mouth is people. A gutter under each press runs grey with a skin on it. The yard hands' gloves are stiff to the wrist.
3. **The plate.** Warm, sweating in the cold. A stack that settles like something breathing out.
4. **The CORES shed.** No window. A drain under the door. The boy's rag has dried into the cut.
