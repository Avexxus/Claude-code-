## Plan: eight beats (LOCAL)
1. **Jon** reads the floor, palms a table edge, and holds one loose cup in the air, then sets it down. Rung: Read (foundation), Hold. Dial: narrow, steady, sustain. Cost: none yet. Reader sees the cup hang, then land.
2. **Derrek** lays a field on the room. Rung: Hold, sustain, precise. Cost: a sheen. Reader sees doors and chairs settle a finger's width, nothing thrown.
3. **Jon** braces against the field with a steel rod he bends into a grip. Rung: Rearrange, held by Hold. Dial: narrow, craft, sustain. Cost: knuckles split. Reader sees the rod hold its shape under pressure, nothing flung.
4. **Derrek** cuts one point: a chair leg splits clean. Rung: controlled Shatter, steered. Reader sees a clean split, not a blast. **Jon** catches the splinter in the air with Hold and drops it on the floor, a hand's width from his cheek. Rung: Hold, clumsy, short. Dial: narrow, short burst. Cost on Jon: Anchor strained.
5. **Derrek** reads Jon's breath and pulse and presses there. Rung: Hold, precise, no chemistry shown (Q43 open). Reader sees Jon's chest go tight, no explosion. **Jon** answers by holding his own body steady with Anchor and Steady. Dial: narrow, sustain. Derrek laughs when Jon's hand stops shaking.
6. **Jon** goes still and sets two loose objects in the air, held by Steady, and offers nothing more. Rung: Hold, sustain. **Derrek** lets his field go slack, then pulls his attention off the room. Neither lands a decisive hit. Reader sees a standoff, not a win.
7. **Derrek** ends it with one tiny steered cut at the floor. **Jon** holds it, Rearrange under Hold, so the floor stays one piece. Rung: Rearrange over Hold, craft. Dial: narrow, exact. Cost: Jon's hands. Reader sees the split line stop at Jon's boots.
8. **Aftermath:** the table is split clean, Jon's hands are bloody and stiff, Derrek is still laughing. Nobody dies (R105). Reader sees the costs and nothing else.
