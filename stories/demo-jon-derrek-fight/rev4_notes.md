## What changed from rev. 3
1. **Similes.** Cut "wood under ice", "as if they were full of water", "decided, without asking him, to stop". Kept comparisons a reader can see: "like a man shaking out a sheet", "still as a lid on a pot", "the way a boy holds up a stick", "a smith... with a fire going".
2. **Actions.** The laugh is a sound; the arm flings and the laugh goes on. The bar is a bent bar with a crook his fist sits in. Jon's left hand comes up, palm out, and the splinter stops in the air a hand from his cheek. "The splinter turned with the air" is gone. The aim is spelled out: "You saw me aim it. Right at his face."
3. **Narration.** "The bend it was about to take" and "a seam nobody had pointed at" are gone. The narration only reports what the potman sees and feels from the doorway.
4. **Continuity.** Floor is boards throughout; "flags" removed. Bar: right fist; it drops and rings on the boards when Jon opens the hand. Dice: two objects, raised by Jon's left palm, one each side; the candlestick stays on the sill and rolls off when the sill shakes. Left hand presses the right at the end. No loop is said to be held after it leaves the hand.
5. **Negations.** "Didn't mean the door" is the one "didn't" in the scene. "Nothing" appears once ("on nothing"), and "Jon said nothing" once. The other "didn't"s and "no X, no Y" lines were rewritten.
6. **Single-use phrases.** "Said nothing" once. "Head back" once (beat 3). "Bark" once (beat 1). "On nothing" once (beat 1).
7. **Derrek's turns.** Each one now has a visible cause on the page: the chairs he just named; the "Watch this one" he just said; the "You're holding it" he just said; the blood on Jon's knuckles. Two turns from rev. 3 were cut ("to see whether Jon had heard it", the look out the window).
8. **Dialogue.**
   - "I never meant the door" → "Didn't mean the door, Jon. Door was just there."
   - "You've got it held" → "You're holding it."
   - "Bit of a handle, that" → cut.
   - "Breathe for me. Let me hear it." → kept, with a setup: Jon's breath goes long and even, the potman sees it, then Derrek asks for it and the hand shakes.
   - "Same time Thursday?" → cut.
   - "Christ" → cut.
   - "Ya" → once, in Jon's opening line. Habit set before it: he said it at noon to the barman, the dog and the potman.
9. **Stagey phrases.** "Off his mark" → "Jon had moved." "The room waited" → "The room held still."
10. **Derrek's pull on the leg.** New beat: Derrek picks up a half of the chair leg and holds it by his face. It is over-the-top and visible; it fits the rev. 3 "Look how clean that is" line.
