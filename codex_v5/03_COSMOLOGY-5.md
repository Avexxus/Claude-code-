## The One — origin of CONSCIOUSNESS, not the universe 🔒

The physical universe already existed — matter, energy, and the four forces, running by their own laws with no author. **The One was the birth of *experience* entering that material universe.** Once a singular consciousness whose ultimate desire was to **know what it was not**, it chose to know itself by becoming other — and split. That self-knowing split scattered it into all consciousness: every mind, soul, and spark of experience that has ever been.

🔒 **The One is subject to the forces.** Consciousness arose *within* physics and obeys it. The One is a resident of reality, not its author. Even the divine is inside the physics.

🔒 **All beings are descended from the One; matter is not.** A living being = a physical body (physics) + a fragment of the One (consciousness/mind) inhabiting it. Inanimate matter holds no fragment of the One — it is only physics, and isn't alive. [REV v5-wk] — **unless a soul is held in it: enchantment = a soul held in matter.** Matter can hold Remnant because bodies are inanimate matter made animate; body-like substrate holds best; matter itself does no magic, a held soul in matter does (wk W12; Constitution 0c). ## One shatter, not staggered creation 🔒 (reconciled — read before "generations")
All consciousness came into being in the **single** shatter at the beginning. **Every race — elder and latecomer alike — coalesced in that one event; none were "made later."** The old "generations" framing (`07_BEINGS.md`) is recast: the tiers are a spectrum of **fragment-SIZE (→ body-durability → lifespan → generational distance to the origin)**, NOT waves of creation at different times. Bigger fragments got bodies durable enough to persist as individuals across history (dragons, giants, elves) — so the beginning is only a few long lifetimes back, near and vivid, still in living cultural memory. Small fragments got short-lived bodies that cycle through death and reproduction constantly (humans, orcs, the rest) — so thousands of generations stack between them and the origin, and it decays into dim myth they're too consumed by the present to regard. **Same age as peoples; wildly different generational depth.** (This removes the contradiction the old model smuggled in — a second creation event for latecomers, which the single beginningless shatter forbids.
