## LIFE / DEATH 🌊 (a soul-magic flavor)
- Growth, decay, fertility, disease, the living process; necromancy, ghosts, soul-passage. Sits on the **soul-magic** branch (acting on the living fragment, not on dead matter). Likely the most evocative use of soul magic on the page.
- **Now partly built (v5) — see `04_MAGIC_SUBSTRATE.md`, soul magic:** echoes vs. ghosts vs. changelings are distinct (t498–502); a ghost is Remnant bound together by magic, and every true ghost is a soul-binding crime; soul-binding is **concentration** — the holder pays, and killing the holder frees or destroys the ghost (t504–505); long-lived ghosts are held by **warden-objects**, deliberately hand-wavey (t506–509); **necromancy = the holding + the vessel** — body, amulet, armor (t510–513).
- Not soul magic: the Orc King's dead are empty flesh animated with no soul in it (t522; `08_CHARACTERS.md`).
- [NEW v5-jul] A held soul with no working brain feels only vague "existential dread," like "a soul that was stolen and trapped inside an artifact... without the processors to actually understand" (jul08 t341; `04`).
- [NEW v5-wk] **Ruled:** a bound soul feels dread, without a brain to understand it (wk W13). [REV v5-wk2] (was listed under "Ruled") 🌊 *Proposed, unconfirmed:* objects hold souls without a holder's concentration; ghosts need a holder (wk W14 — Claude's proposal, chat t82; Patrick moved on and never addressed it). **Enchantment = a soul held in matter** (wk W12).
- [NEW v5-wk] **Undead types:** plague (biotech); animated flesh; souls bound to corpses; souls in machines; changeling leftovers (wk W69).
- [NEW v5-wk] **Resurrection is possible in principle** — decoherence: nothing destroyed, odds near zero; Derrek, who sees time, could trace the fragments (wk W51).
- *Status:* rules above are banked; everything else discovered per scene; no pre-spec. 🌊 Whether a self survives death, and where it goes, stays open by design (t499).
