# 14 — PHYSICS REFERENCE (optional flavor palette — NOT a rulebook)

## 1. THE FUNDAMENTAL FORCES (the four real ones)
- **Gravity** — attraction between all mass-energy
- **Electromagnetism** — all charge, light, magnetism, and their interplay
- **Strong nuclear force** — binds quarks into nucleons, nucleons into nuclei
- **Weak nuclear force** — governs flavor-change/decay (beta decay)

## 2. MOTION & MECHANICS
- Velocity, speed, direction; acceleration
- **Momentum** (mass × velocity) + conservation
- Angular momentum / rotation / spin + conservation
- **Inertia** (resistance to change in motion)
- Force (mass × acceleration); torque (rotational force)
- **Friction** (static, kinetic, rolling); drag
- Tension, compression, shear, strain, stress
- Elasticity / restoring force
- Centripetal / centrifugal effects; buoyancy
- Equilibrium / rest / balance

## 3. ENERGY (the universal currency) & CONSERVATION
- **Conservation of energy** — the master law (energy transforms, never created/destroyed)
- Kinetic energy; potential energy (gravitational, elastic, chemical, nuclear, electric)
- Work (force × distance); power (rate of work)
- Mechanical advantage (levers, pulleys, ramps)
- Energy transfer & transformation between forms
- **Conservation of momentum**; **conservation of angular momentum**
- Conservation of mass-energy (E=mc²); conservation of charge

## 4. HEAT & THERMODYNAMICS
- **Temperature** (avg kinetic energy of particles); **heat** (thermal energy in transfer)
- Conduction, convection, radiation
- Thermal expansion/contraction; specific heat; latent heat (phase-change energy)
- **Entropy** (disorder; the tendency toward it)
- Thermal equilibrium (heat flows hot→cold)
- Laws of thermodynamics (energy conserved; entropy increases; absolute zero unreachable)
- *[NEW v5-wk] Flavor hook:* the appendage **shaking particles = heat** (wk W02; canon in `04_MAGIC_SUBSTRATE.md`).

## 5. STATES OF MATTER & PHASE
- Solid, liquid, gas, plasma (+ exotic states)
- Melting, freezing, boiling, condensation, sublimation, deposition, ionization
- Phase transitions + their energy costs (latent heat)
- Density, compressibility; crystallization/ordering

## 6. FLUID & GAS BEHAVIOR
- **Pressure** (force/area); hydrostatic pressure
- Flow, current, viscosity; turbulence vs. laminar
- Surface tension, capillary action
- Cohesion (like-to-like) & adhesion (to other)
- Diffusion (high→low concentration)
- Gas laws (pressure/volume/temperature)
- Sound (pressure waves through a medium)

## 7. WAVES & OSCILLATION
- Oscillation / vibration / resonance
- Wave propagation, frequency, wavelength, amplitude
- Interference, diffraction, reflection, refraction
- The electromagnetic spectrum (radio→light→gamma)

## 8. ELECTROMAGNETIC PHENOMENA (detail)
- Electric charge (static, current); electric fields; potential (voltage)

- Magnetism, magnetic fields, poles

## 14. TOPICS FROM THE PHYSICS TUTORING (v5 — flavor hooks only)
*From the opening of the physics chat (t8–21). The tutoring itself changed no canon (t10–21). Real-physics ideas in plain words; each hook says where (if anywhere) canon borrowed the grammar. Flavor, not law.*

### 14.1 Fields are real
- In modern physics the **fields** are treated as the real, fundamental things — not just bookkeeping for probabilities — and particles are **ripples/excitations** in them (an electron is a ripple in the electron field; a quark in the quark field).
- **"Empty" space isn't quiet.** Even a vacuum has field activity; the **Casimir effect** (two close plates in a vacuum pushed together) is the standard illustration.
- *Canon hook:* **the One is described LIKE a field** — a soul is a resonance/excitation on the One's field, as a quark is on the quark field; fragment "size" reads as the resonance's strength; conception is diffuse field coalescing into a stable resonance (t8–9). **Guardrail:** this is borrowed *grammar* for the consciousness domain only — a separate field from the physical ones, "same grammar, different noun." It is NOT a physical field, and Constitution 0b/0c stand. Canon: `03_COSMOLOGY.md`.

### 14.2 Why fields exist — unanswered
- Physics describes how fields behave; it does not say **why there are fields at all.** That question is open in real physics.
- A speculative idea discussed in the tutoring: a **"digital"/information-like universe** — reality as something more like computation. Speculation, not established physics.
- *Canon hook:* none. (In-world, science can explain WHAT mages do, never the CAN — Constitution Law 1.)

### 14.3 Expansion of space vs. the light-speed limit
- Nothing travels **through** space faster than light. But **space itself can stretch**, and across great distances that stretching can carry far regions apart faster than light — no law broken, because nothing is moving through space that fast.
- *Canon hook:* none directly; a useful nod for far-future travel talk (14.6).

### 14.4 Decoherence — "the forgetting horizon" (flavor term, placeholder)
- **Decoherence:** a quantum system's delicate "both at once" state washes out as it tangles with its surroundings; the information isn't destroyed, it spreads out until it can't practically be gathered back.
- *Canon hooks:* the **forgetting horizon** [REV v5-jul] (placeholder — Claude's gloss at t167, carried along in later discussion but never coined or confirmed by the author as a name; t630 ruling) (was: "our in-world flavor term") is the flavor term used for a being that has come apart so far it cannot pull itself back together — the Law of the Regress (every plane is a being that decohered past the forgetting horizon; the One let go completely) and the in-betweeners' coherence and splitting (t164–172). The Returned learned "decoherence" and phasing in the maze (t148–150). Canon: `03_COSMOLOGY.md`, `06_EXTERNAL_POWERS.md`, `07_BEINGS.md`. Physics flavor only.
- *[NEW v5-wk] Canon hook:* **resurrection is possible in principle** — decoherence: nothing destroyed, odds near zero; Derrek, who sees time, could trace the fragments (wk W51; `06_EXTERNAL_POWERS.md`, `08_CHARACTERS.md`). Flavor only.

### 14.5 Black holes and event horizons
- A **black hole**: matter crushed to a point so dense that nothing, not even light, gets back out. Its boundary is the **event horizon**.
- Seen from far away, anything falling toward the horizon appears to slow and stretch, as if taking forever to arrive.
- *Canon hooks:* the master's sacrifice in the Tower — "he just creates a single point so dense and feeds it himself and his remnent to make it strong enough to engulf the space around it" (t432; `09_STRUCTURE.md`). The time-gods' ordering — each later pair's birth is "always infinity after," stretched "like looking at an event horizon" (t644; `03_COSMOLOGY.md`). 🌊 Claude's idea that the rule-beast "falls forever, never arriving" at the black hole's edge (t433) was never confirmed.
