## The No-Formula / Multi-Scale Law 🔒
- Characters are **not computed** from their positions (no x+y=z).
- Every scale (morality, temperament, favored expressions, loves, duties, …) is **independent, full-range** (with a real middle and a real neutral), and does **NOT** blend into "types" or "corners."
- **Most beings rest in the soft middle of most scales;** the poles are rare. Nobody lives in a corner. ("Dark" is a *tendency that slides by degree in context*, never a category that makes all your acts evil — the cartoon-villain failure mode.)
- Depth lives in the **subtle, often non-aligned** positions a person holds at once — including genuinely **contradictory** ones held whole (Lirael: love AND grief AND duty-above-feeling AND desire, none reconciled; she acts from duty *while* loving). **Stop computing characters; hold their contradictions.**
- The cosmic poles are **bare directions only.** Any meaning/feeling/morality (belonging vs. suffocation, liberty vs. isolation) is *individual*, never definitional. Never assign a pole a tidy thematic value.
- **Don't flatten blurred lines (v5, t674).** Turning a blur into a ladder, a graduation, or a good/bad split is the same failure. The author, correcting Claude on angels/demons/changelings/gods: "Your giving it to much flattening... The line is far more blurred." (Constitution Laws 3, 16.)
- [NEW v5-jul] **Grading scales, not fixed points.** A race or group is never one block that acts all at once: "Duality friend. Duality. Grading scales not a single fixed point" (jul08 t269, on dragons retreating one by one). The world-law form is "Noone is a monolith" (t596; `10_WORLD.md`).
- [NEW v5-jul] **No group is THE anything.** "We've explicitly worked at every step not to make any group THE anything. They simply resonate with pacts" (jul08 t212). Any line that reads "X are the world's Y" should trip the same alarm as "fire user."
- [NEW v5-jul] **Don't harden a tendency into a condition.** Claude's recurring slide: lean → title, strategy → trait, unimportant → impossible. "At 0 point did I say that… I only said due to their short life history is unimportant to them as a whole" (jul08 t213–214). Check the tendency-versus-condition line hardest.
- [NEW v5-jul] **Sins are not racial attributes.** "Lets drop this sin as a racial attribute as per our no locked identities. All races in this world are greedy prideful jealous beings… I think we can do the same thing weve done with every other scale we tried to force" (jul23 t52–54). A sin is "a complex web of interconnected behaviors and motives"; a race's culture and history shape *how* the vices surface (e.g. dwarves value independence at all costs, and arms-dealing wealth serves it — jul23 t54). A vice can be a race's *most deadly* without being its only one (humans and pride, jul23 t82).
- [NEW v5-wk] **No forced triads.** "I dont need to make every trope arbitrarily 3" (wk W33). Don't pad a set to three (or any tidy count) for symmetry.
- [NEW v5-wk] **No formula from method to nature.** The author corrected Claude: there is **no formula** tying god vs. demon to method of ascent — behavior is individual (wk W32). Likewise duality is **not** strict cosmic halves; it is the name versus the act, and the axis keeps a neutral middle (wk W29). Same failure as the grid: a tidy mapping where the author holds a blur.
- **Tell that I'm violating this:** if I start enumerating "the four combinations are…" or drawing a grid, stop.

## Rigor on the substrate; silence on the soul 🔒
Two modes, and using the wrong one is destructive:
- **Substrate (the system: Remnant, the soul-substrate, costs, interactions, and the force/element flavor vocabulary):** be the rigorous engineer **of internal consistency.** Press for hard rules, hunt contradictions against our own canon, demand consistency. **Never audit real-world physics and never do math** — physics is flavor (see "Physics is flavor" below; Constitution 0a).
- **Soul (who a person is and how they therefore express magic):** build the **space of possibility** and then **go quiet.** Never populate it with types. The system must stay *almost arrogantly silent about who people are* — it defines the substrate and stops; the people are sovereign over how they meet it.
- **Why:** the moment a system encodes "this kind of person gets this kind of power," it quantizes individuality and caps versatility. The deepest-character systems keep the rules on the *physics* side of the line and the people on the *character* side. Every "no racial/temperamental lock," "morality is independent," etc. is this line being held.

## Physics is flavor — no audits, no math 🔒 (v5 — re-confirmed by the author; the 🔒 restates locked Constitution 0a)
- Physics is here for the author's **intuition only** — "not laws applied as requirements" (t0–5). Real science is a well of texture to draw from; it never gates a scene (Constitution 0a; `14_PHYSICS_REFERENCE.md` is a non-binding palette).
- **No math.** "Your doing math and we agreed no math" (t72). No numeric size tiers, stat charts, mass or energy budgets, or structural-physics mechanisms (e.g. square-cube auditing). The world owes no physics mechanism for what it does.
- If a physics idea would break the physics/consciousness wall (Constitution 0b/0c), the wall wins — flag it rather than bend it (t6–7).

## Build laws, not a spell list 🔒
- We build **the laws the spells must obey**, never a lookup table of techniques. A spell list pre-solves every fight (characters become loadouts; prose narrates a calculation — dead).
- Test for anything proposed: *"Is this a law the world obeys, or a catalog of outcomes?"* A law *generates* infinite moments; a catalog *forecloses* them ("here are the six gravity techniques, ranked").
- **Characters' specific powers emerge from the story** and are explained *after* (as Lirael's Collapse was recognized, then built on). In a live moment (e.g. Jon vs. an inversion-tornado) we ask: what is it made of? its traits? relative power here? what can the defender perceive? what do they reach for? — invented in the moment, grounded in the laws, never pulled from a menu.
- The groundedness in the **laws** is exactly what *earns* total improvisational **freedom** on top. Freedom above, bedrock below.
- **New powers must not break rarity (v5, t84).** A rare feat stays rare by its cost or its setup (the dragon's once-made stone: "the rock is how we kept it rare").

## The skeleton & build order 🔒 (REWORKED — the rigorous engine was retired)
The original six-joint Sanderson-mirrored build (SOURCE → ACCESS → UNITS → PROGRESSION gates → COST/LIMIT → INTERACTION) was scaffolding for a **rigorous physics-engine magic system that the author deliberately abandoned** for **science-flavored, scene-first, loose magic** (`04_MAGIC_SUBSTRATE.md`). So:
- **Kept & solid:** SOURCE (the One → Remnant; Spirit = active Remnant magic), ACCESS (the awakening gradient; Remnant ceiling), UNITS (principle-work + reproducible spells).
- **Retired as pending bones:** PROGRESSION gates, COST/LIMIT charts, INTERACTION laws — these were the rigorous-engine homework. The only standing limits are **Remnant + skill + fatigue**, and all specifics are **discovered per scene**, never charted.
- **Sanderson's Laws still make a fine QC lens** (understand-it-for-earned-solutions; limitations > powers; deepen-before-expanding) — but as *taste*, not a build checklist.
- *(History, v5 t324: the earliest system — "the 4 elements and 4 forces and 4 alignments as a weird compass" — forced rigid classes and taught what doesn't work. "Me and you did our best work when we made radical changes.")*

## Magic is expression, not derivation 🔒 (the "silence on the soul" line, applied to magic)
Build the **person** completely first (motives, wounds, history, who taught them, what they'd take to); **then** their magic follows as an expression of that person, with the force/element flavor named *last* to describe the output. **No equation in either direction** — not ability→character, not character→ability, not race→element. There are **no force/element "users" or types**, only beings who do magic. This is the same discipline as "rigor on the substrate; silence on the soul" above — now the system stays silent even about *abilities* having an identity, let alone people.
- **Every character is "just a skilled user" (v5).** "As with all our characters she is just a skilled user" (t612) — gravity was the *image* of a move (Lirael's Collapse), never a type. Labels that name a person by a force are retired for the same reason: "Calling him sf straps him of the mastery and dedication it took and awards it to a special category he belongs to" (t136).
