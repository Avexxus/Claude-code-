## What magic IS 🔒

- **REMNANT = the chunk of the One that you ARE.** Your fragment of consciousness; your capacity — how much of reality you can grip at once. Innate, fixed in the living, raisable only by harvesting (which fragments you). It is **identity, not fuel** — you can touch the world *because you are a remnant of the One*. Not mana, not a pool, not spent per cast. [NEW v5-wk] **Remnant cannot be burned or spent** — "we cant burn remnant" (wk W10). Soul = Remnant (wk W07).
- **REMNANT IS AGELESS.** (t678 — Patrick: "I think remnant is ageless.") The fragment of the One does not age; **flesh does.** More Remnant makes the flesh resist age more strongly (Law 4a) — resistance, never immunity. *(This retires the old changeling "founder's clock" aging line — that line lives in `07_BEINGS.md`; see routing.)*
- **SPIRIT = the name for active Remnant magic** — a being's faculty for **consciously manipulating matter** (and, rarely, other consciousness). Spirit is *the system itself*: having it is what distinguishes a conscious being from inert flesh. **Flesh is just flesh; Spirit is what reaches out and acts.** (This replaces the old "Spirit = the consciousness-half of a two-part system." There is no two-part system anymore. Spirit = using your Remnant. Full stop.)
- **MAGIC = inherited mastery, science-flavored.** The One mastered the universe (wielder-in-chief, not author); a being's magic is a lesser expression of that same mastery — a fragment doing what the One does. [REV v5-wk] **The grip is now mechanized; the sense stays mysterious** (wk W06) — see *The appendage and the inner sense* below. (was: "There is **no interface mechanism** to explain; the capacity is identity ('how?' bottoms out at 'you are a piece of the One').") In-world, **"magic" and "science" are the same word** — people do not distinguish them; what we call physics they call the workings of magic. [NEW v5-wk] Humans call magic "science" because dragons were humanity's first teachers (wk W63); faith, magic and science are one act under different names (wk W57).
- [NEW v5-wk] **THE APPENDAGE — the grip, mechanized.** Remnant extends an **appendage** that grabs the universe. Its length, size, shape and intensity scale with Remnant, skill and focus (wk W01). What the grip does (flavor-level, no math): shaking particles = heat; pulling charges apart = lightning; crushing or ripping the nucleus = explosions; turning a quark = transmutation — **the hardest act**; a botched transmutation rips the nucleus; denting space = gravity (wk W02). Dragons stay four-legged; the appendage is their hands (wk W63).
- [NEW v5-wk] **THE INNER SENSE — unexplained.** An unexplained perception that comes from Remnant, **separate from the appendage**, both trainable and innate (wk W03). Patrick: "you mostly feel with a second inner sense." "Feel" returns as skill — a dial, not a gate (wk W05). It stays mysterious (wk W06). **Sensing other beings' Remnant is a rare master-level skill** — "Some very talented masters have learned to sense other beings remnant. This is rare" (wk W07). On the page soul-sensing must be **rare, felt rather than reported, and unreliable** — never "video gamey," no constant scanning or detecting (wk W09).
- **One undivided act.** A being reaches out with Spirit and makes reality do what they want. That's the whole of it. What the act *looks like* (fire, gravity, a shove, a transmutation) is description applied afterward, never a separate kind of magic.

## REMNANT + SKILL = POWER — the only gates 🔒

[REV v5-wk] **Three independent dials — Remnant (strength), control (skill), and the inner sense** (wk W03) — plus the bill, effort/fatigue. (was: "The entire mechanical statement of a being, and the **only** limits on magic:") The limits on magic:
- **REMNANT (how much)** — capacity; how much reality you can grip at once. Innate, fixed. More Remnant = more possible power (you can't grip more than you have consciousness to grip with). This is the natural ceiling.
- **SKILL / MASTERY (how well)** — how finely you grip, shape, and **blend**. Learned; grows with training and time. Combinatorial fluency. Distinct from raw Remnant; two beings of equal Remnant produce utterly different results. [NEW v5-wk] The rulings call this dial **control** (wk W03).
- [NEW v5-wk] **THE INNER SENSE (how finely you perceive)** — the third dial, separate from the appendage, trainable and innate (wk W03). **Basic magic needs only control; deeper levels need finer sense. This is a cost gradient, not a gate** (wk W04) [v5-wk2 attribution: substance is Patrick's — *"To start actually interfering with actual physics you must be able to feel small things"* (chat t65); the words "cost gradient, not a gate" are Claude's (t60, t66)]. Example: the **"numb titan"** — huge Remnant, poor sense (wk W03).
- **EFFORT / FATIGUE (the bill)** — working magic is work; the steward pays mentally and bodily. **Exhaustion is the universal soft limit** — a mage tires like a laborer or a grandmaster. Overreach past what your body and Remnant can pay is dangerous (🌊 whether "overdraw"/burning the self stays a named mechanic is open — the description holds either way [REV v5-wk] — though overdraw can no longer mean burning Remnant, wk W10). *(🌊 The Tower apprentice's death — pushing past his limit and burning from within, `05_POWERS_BANKED.md` — is a story instance of this description; whether it makes "overdraw" a named mechanic is still Patrick's call. That is **all** the gating there is [NEW v5-wk] (the inner sense adds cost, not a gate — wk W04). **No physics audit, ever.** We do NOT check whether an act is energetically possible, whether it "needs fusion not weak," mass-budgets, ignition arithmetic, conservation. A weak character can't casually crack a planet — not because math forbids it, but because they lack the Remnant and it would kill them to try. The three soft gates generate every limit a scene needs. **No math** (t72: "your doing math and we agreed no math") — no size charts, no square-cube reasoning, no structural-physics mechanism owed for any being; dragons are the standing proof the world owes no physics mechanism (t73).

> 🚫 **STEER-ONLY RETIRED.** Magic is no longer restricted to "manipulating energy that already exists." The "Maxwell's-demon / information-work" framing is gone. The only cost is **effort/fatigue**, never an energy ledger.

## Forces & elements = pure descriptive flavor 🔒

- **The four forces (Strong, EM, Weak, Gravity) and the four elements (fire, water, earth, air) are a shared VOCABULARY for describing what magic looked like — applied AFTER the fact, never a system that constrains.** You watch someone unbind an atom and an explosion follows → you call it nuclear. You watch them collapse matter together with great force → you call it gravity. You watch them reshape a thing → EM. You watch them warp *what a thing is* → the weak force. Fire, lightning, freeze, rot, transmutation — all just folk-and-science names for effects.
- **There are NO "users."** No fire-users, no gravity-users, no EM-mages — **no types at all.** There are only **beings who do magic.** Sometimes a being's magic looks like gravity; the next moment the same being does something that looks like fire, or flings a rock with plain momentum, or hardens their skin, or something you wouldn't name. The label names the **act**, never the **person**, never a class, lane, loadout, or kit.
  - **Re-confirmed this session (t612):** "As with all our characters she is just a skilled user." A character who once uses a gravity-looking signature (e.g. the drow's tribute use of Lirael's Collapse, `05_POWERS_BANKED.md`) is **not** a "gravity type." The image names the act.

Strong-flavored feats remain the rarest, found only at the very top of the power scale (t136, t83–84; consistent with Law 7b — mythic by scale and prestige, never a class).
- **Blending is free and unstructured.** Fire + gravity + spin = a fire-gravity tornado, with no permission check. No forced paths, no locked growth trees, no reachability hierarchy, no "EM comes first," no element→force mapping anyone has to obey. Mix and combine whatever the moment calls for.

## Magic is EXPRESSION, not derivation 🔒 (the core character principle)

**Build the person completely first** — motives, wounds, the delusions they'd defend, their history, who taught them, what they survived, what they'd naturally take to. A whole being, built with no thought to magic. **THEN decide what their magic looks like, as an expression of *that person*.** Flavor (force/element) is assigned **last**, to describe the output — never to derive or explain the character.

- **No derivation in EITHER direction.** Not ability→character ("she wields gravity, so she's patient"). Not character→ability ("he's angry, so he must be fire"). Not race→element, not temperament→affinity, not nature+ability+element+race as any equation. All retired.
- **The "why this magic" is ALWAYS the individual's life** — what this specific person learned, leaned into, and felt drawn to — **never the element's supposed temperament.** Fifty people whose magic sometimes looks like "earth" are fifty different people for fifty different reasons; they are not all patient, because "earth" has no temperament to impose.
- **The same effect can mean opposite things in two beings.** One person's gravity is grief (heaviness, surrender, letting go); another's is dominance (crushing, pinning). Same described effect, opposite souls — because the magic is downstream of the person. A type-system can't do this; ours gets it for free.
- This is the No-Formula / "silence on the soul" discipline (`12_METHOD.md`) applied to magic: the system defines what magic IS and then **stays silent about who people are.** It does not grant *abilities* an identity, let alone people.
- [NEW v5-jul] **Every character develops their own powers; gods are the ultimate expression.** Patrick (jul23 t106): "Characters developing their own unique powers uses our system to its fullest degree... create truly unique flavor for every being. God's take this to the ultimate expression in the series." A god's magic is not a different system — Patrick rolled back his own idea that gods "practice magic that is different to remnant" (jul23 t104); it is the same Remnant magic, carried to its furthest individual expression. (A god's signature technique offered through a pact: `06_EXTERNAL_POWERS.md`.)

## People are not types — but they ARE individuals 🔒 (the texture)

Loosening the system does NOT make everyone an identical infinitely-fluid blank. People have **histories, tastes, strengths, and specialties** — they're just not *classes*. (Wheel of Time is the cleaner comp than Harry Potter: one undivided Power, but every channeler has natural strengths, favorite weaves, things they never learned, things their gift makes easy — individual expression of a single source, real differences in *kind and scale*, without anyone being a "Fire person.")
- **Learned repertoire:** a being knows the workings they bothered to learn; leans on what comes naturally; reaches for something else when forced. Repertoire flows from **their life and choices**, not a label the system stamped.
- **Natural inclination (in kind and scale):** talent suits some things to some people; some workings come easily, some never. Culture/disposition may *color* what someone gravitates toward (a war-forged person reaches for war-shaped magic) — but it's **pressure, never a wall, always individual** (statistical flavor at most).
- **Chosen specialty:** mastery, preference, study, and "what came naturally" are all real and differentiating — they're just downstream of the person.
- **Magic isn't the only learnable art.** Potions, enchantment, craft, lore, the non-magical disciplines sit alongside it (a "potions master" isn't a different kind of mage — just someone who went deep on a craft). This gives low-Remnant and non-prodigy characters somewhere to be excellent, and the world schools/trades/depth. (Enchantment as a craft → `06_EXTERNAL_POWERS.md`.)

## The Awakening Gradient 🔒 (kept, simplified)

Magic ability is **"how far along am I?"**, never on/off — a continuous gradient of **control + understanding**: involuntary leaks (power escaping unaimed, loudest under emotion) → crude intent → control through understanding → engineered reproducible workings → mastery.
- Your **Remnant (fragment size) sets the CEILING**; practice sets how far you travel; it dulls with neglect, deepens with use. Sapience is the visible readout of having enough fragment to begin.
- Individual, never racial (only statistically race-flavored, since fragment-size distributions differ).

## Spells 🔒 (kept)

**Spells** = specific, complex, **reproducible** workings — engineered by understanding deeply enough to combine effects precisely (the magical equivalent of a repeatable experiment). Taught by teaching fundamentals + how they combine, then drilling reproduction. **NOT slotted/plug-and-play.** Capped by Remnant, accessed by skill, no hard cap on number known. Makes authored signature moves, schools, lineages, hoarded/lost spells, and "she knows X but not Y" all structurally real.

### The two roads of soul theft ✅ (t676)

## Power scale 🔒 (pure continuum)

Power is a single graded scale of **Remnant × skill × time-to-learn**. "Demigod" / "god" are descriptive points on it, NOT gated tiers. A weak god vastly exceeds mortals but ranks low among gods. Given enough Remnant and time, a mortal simply becomes a god — which is exactly what eternity made of Jon & Derrek.
- **Gods are what happens when ANY user becomes incredibly powerful** (t668) — by either road: matter ("a being who perfected gravity magic to harden their body beyond stone or an em specialist who can twist mater at a whim," t670) or soul (angels and demons are soul-magic users, t668).
- **No graduation.** Changeling, angel/demon and god are blurred regions on one continuum — "You dont just graduate from changeling school and your now an angel and where the line again becomes god is murkier still" (t674). Angel and demon are one kind of being (t674). Detail: `07_BEINGS.md`.
- [NEW v5-wk] **Roads and no formula.** All gods are born of flesh, on one world (wk W30). Humans reach godhood only by **time-fusion** (Jon, Derrek) **or theft**; dragons, giants and elves can grow singular (wk W31). Singular gods revile changelings, but power earns respect regardless; behavior is individual — **no formula ties god vs. demon to method of ascent**; a changeling who ascended is not a "god" in the singular sense (wk W32). The greatest giant god is a million minds in unison (wk W17).
