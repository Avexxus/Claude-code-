## THE THEME — DUALITY 🟡 (strict definition)
The saga's thematic core is **DUALITY**, defined strictly: **a SINGLE subject holding TWO POLAR-OPPOSITE truths AT ONCE, both true, unreconciled.** The test (all three must pass): *genuine opposites? one subject? both true at once?*
- [NEW v5-wk] **Duality = the name versus the act** (wk W29, W57): faith, magic and science are one act under different names. Duality is **not** strict cosmic halves; the cosmic axis gets a neutral middle, and the duality theme stays as it is (wk W29). How the three-part test above sits with "the name versus the act" → CONFLICTS in `changes/C_story_wk.md`.
- **NOT** mere pairs / two things opposed (Jon-vs-Derrek *as opponents* is two subjects → fails on its own).
- [REV v5-wk] (was: "**NOT** synonyms / two words for one thing (magic/science is the same effect renamed → fails; it is NOT duality).") The renaming of one act — magic/science/faith — **is** the duality's own shape: the name versus the act (wk W57, W29).
- Cleanest exemplars: **THE ONE** (everything *and* nothing at once); **JON** (the ever-present watchful master *and* the unknown figure never there when you look again); **DERREK** (steers the entire narrative *and* has zero control over his own — flung randomly); **JON'S ENDING** (his ultimate victory *is also* the beginning of his infinite cycle — he gets no ending). Kin to the No-Formula method-law's "contradictory positions held whole" (Lirael) — `12_METHOD.md`. The lens every major choice can answer to.
- **Jon and Derrek are the same** (t239–240, Patrick: *"The point has always been duality. Jon and derrek are the same. They always have been. In every way that matters."*). Not a villain-flip: neither man is the answer; the contradiction is held, never resolved. The pair passes the duality test as ONE subject — the story's center is the two of them together (t702: *"I dont think my story has a hero... its both of them together which drive the conflict... Its not easy to say one is the villain one is the hero."*). **Both are right for their own reasons; there is no hidden correct answer at the window** (t724).
- **THE THESIS (Patrick's wording, t466):** ***"it doesn't matter in the grand scale, but everything matters to someone."*** Both halves are true at once (the cosmic half: even Jon and Derrek are one pair among infinite pairs; the human half: meaning is real to the one who holds it). Companion lines Patrick confirmed: *"what matters most is what matters to you"* (t660); the saga also explores **what happens when you don't have a choice** — the elves inside the bond, the souls inside a changeling, the pull of reunion (t662).
