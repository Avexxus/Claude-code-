## Threshold-beings / In-betweeners (Tier-3) 🔒 lineage · ⚖️ mechanics
*An ancient lineage pre-dating the One (not descended from it), in their own bubble between the worlds; obey physics, run the Rule-OS, not Remnant.*
> [REV v5-jul] (was: whole section stamped 🔒) **Stability tier (Patrick, jul08 t365):** the lineage and OS above stay locked (Constitution 15). The power mechanics below — Phase-step, Unfixing, the Hold, the emergency ladder and Cascade, the Afflicted/Victor split — are Claude Code's formalization of Patrick's general description ("rule beasts moving outside of our reality because they exist outside it"): "they sound good and can always be adjusted later for scenes." They are an approved, **adjustable foundation (⚖️)**, not fixed law. The Void, Chain, two states and splitting are Patrick's own later descriptions (physics chat) — canon, but never locked.
[NEW v5-wk] **In-betweeners stay alien and separate; they are the Qu-like remakers** (wk W37).
**Fuel = transition (change/becoming). Price = coherence.** (v5: the death that coherence buys is **splitting too far to come back** — see Splitting.) They operate **outside** the Remnant/force system (they predate it) — they don't out-power anyone, they **sidestep** the rules. Powers are narrow, specific, **rule-shaped.**

**THE VOID** (v5; 🔒 removed v5-jul — never locked) [t126–131, t430] — **the Void is the space around the in-betweener** (Patrick): "they arent moving through our dimension at all. They are moving their void to where they want it." An in-betweener is never actually standing in our dimension; what looks like one present in a room is its Void brought to that place ("they bring the bubble to you") [t130–131]. **The Void is their little pocket where you play the Rule** [t128]. **It exists on top of the real world** — laid over real rooms or real land — so those inside may **not realize at first** that anything has changed [t430]. Outsiders can't simply break into it: "its an ambiguous moving zone attached to a in betweener" [t146].

**CHAIN** (v5; 🔒 removed v5-jul — never locked) [t126, t130] — **the ability to bind;** Chain **drags the victim into the Void.** Distinct from the Hold (below).

**CHAIN VOID** (v5; 🔒 removed v5-jul — never locked) [t126] — "the natural result of fusing the two." **Void, Chain and Chain Void are GENERAL in-betweener powers,** not one being's signature ("I like it being a general power") [t126].

**TWO STATES** (v5; 🔒 removed v5-jul — never locked) [t132] — "inside void they have no true form. They are as ethereal and unreal as the void dimension they live in. However perhaps they can choose to leave the void momentarily and assume a physical form." *(Session reading, accepted by continuation [t133]: this is the domain Constitution 4b means — deathless within its own Void; a manifested body outside it obeys physics and can be pinned — fits "Unfixing countered by being pinned.")*

**SPLITTING** (v5; 🔒 removed v5-jul — never locked) [t164] — "A rule beast can split into as many forms as they like however the more they become the more decohered they are until they choose to reassemble. Go too far form too many and they can never remember who they were to return." Each split thins coherence; pieces can reassemble while enough self remains to remember; past that point the pieces drift on not knowing they were one being — **their true death** [t164–165]. A keeper can leave its seat this way, sending out pieces while its core stays [t164–165]. *(Patrick: this is the same mechanic the One uses — it decohered into everything and can't pull back together; the Law of the Regress lives in `03_COSMOLOGY.md` [t166]. A shared law of selves, not shared lineage — in-betweeners remain a separate lineage [t167].)*

**Universal kit (true in-betweeners)** ⚖️ [REV v5-jul] (adjustable foundation, jul08 t365):
- **Phase-step** — transition-based movement (cheap short, costly far). *(v5 reading, accepted by continuation: phase-step is the in-betweener repositioning its Void's point of contact with our world — far contact costs more than near [t131].)*

- **Threshold-sense** — passive perception of transitions/seams/instability and of other transitional beings (incl. Jon & Derrek); free.
- **Minor reshaping** — cheap small-scale state-change.
- **The Hold** — force something to *stay* mid-transition (their trap/prison signature); bleeds coherence continuously → the encounter clock. *(Hold arrests a state; Chain drags a body into the Void.)*
- **Void / Chain / Chain Void** — above.

**The Rule** ⚖️ [REV v5-jul] (was 🔒; jul08 t365 — the concept is Patrick's, its formal mechanics adjustable) — a **binding condition/law imposed on a bounded space that traps everyone inside it — the in-betweener included.** Releases only when its condition is met (e.g. "only one may leave"). **(v5) The bounded space is the Void** — the Rule is played inside the in-betweener's pocket, laid over real rooms or real land [t128, t154, t430]. The in-betweener is bound by its own law and must win its own game to survive it. The fight is a **puzzle-prison:** deduce the wording, navigate/survive it, defeat or escape the hunter who works the same rules against you.
- **Bound, not trapped** (v5, Patrick) [t160]: for an in-betweener master of its own condition, "leaving is not hard. He is here because its his web. It is hard for a fly to leave the spiders web while the spider dances around preparing for dinner."
- **They choose their contests** (Patrick) [t160]: "rule beasts tend not to take challenges they do not feel confident they can beat."
- Those who meet the condition are simply released — "naturally excused by the rule beast so they arent kept" [t622].

**Emergency moves (tiered)** ⚖️ [REV v5-jul] (jul08 t365): Lash-Out (overload unfixing) → Sacrifice-Step (tear free of a pin by abandoning coherence; escape diminished) → Snare-Reversal (hold the attacker's pin mid-transition; coherence-vs-Remnant race) → Anchor-Theft (transition the reference point a pin needs; can't be caught by the same trick twice) → **Cascade.**
- **CASCADE** ⚖️ [REV v5-jul] (was 🔒; jul08 t365) = spend **100% of remaining coherence** to set the Rule's condition to its **most lethal value** ("one"→"zero"). The Rule's final form, not a generic explosion. **Dishonorable** (a sore loser flipping the board to deny an earned win, at the cost of self-erasure). Subtlest counter: resolve the condition against it *before* it panics into Cascade. Dramatic tell: when an in-betweener goes **still / stops trying to escape**, Cascade is coming.

**Honor code (rooted in nature):** only a **fair, genuinely-uncertain contest** produces rich transition (their food). So they demand **equal duels** (1v1, 2v2, always matched), bind themselves by their own Rules to keep it fair, and **cultivate worthy opponents** — imbuing mortals with Rules and setting them to fight, watching for any mortal worthy of a personal duel. Clean loss = honorable; Cascade = shameful. Jon & Derrek (living transition) = the ultimate coveted opponents.

**Two categories of Rule-bearer** ⚖️ [REV v5-jul] (was 🔒; jul08 t365) (the second was added for the Book-3 protagonist):

Anchored by mortal coherence (blurry/shaken but rarely fully dissolves). Each is a flawed *preview* — a demi-boss implying a true in-betweener "master" who granted the Rule. (Two-stage villain/ally template: the haunted amateur, then the alien master.)
- **The VICTOR** — a mortal who **defeated a threshold being in fair contest** and was *awarded* the Rule as the honorable prize. Because they *won it through understanding*, they can **wield it with intention and skill** (the afflicted cannot). Legendarily rare. *(This is the Book-3 protagonist — see `08_CHARACTERS.md`.)* 🌊 *(v5: the book structure has since changed — Book 1 is now the far future with Derrek's son as hero, and whether that hero carries a Rule is open [t333/t345, t380]; this reference awaits Patrick.)*
