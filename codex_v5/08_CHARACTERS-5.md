### JON — FINALE RECAST 🟡 (older session; supersedes "hidden pride" as the ROOT, and the older certainty framing [RESTORED v5-verify]; v5 notes inline)

The realization that breaks the trap: **he is the fault line — his jumps are why time is breaking** — which is the same as realizing the perfect run he chased was destroying everything, and he never needed it.
