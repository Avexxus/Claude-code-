### The codex is built in TWO LAYERS. This is the most important thing to understand.

> **VERSION:** v5 — Oct 2026. See *v5 — how this version was made* at the bottom. Lines this version overturned are kept in the 🚫 RETIRED list at the bottom, never silently deleted.
>

Several summary lines in *Core world premises* and *History & story* below predate that pass; the note at the top of each section lists which ones, and the files themselves are current.

> ## 🔵 WORLD / SYSTEM LAYER — rigid bedrock
> The physics, the magic-system, the forces, cosmology, the mythology, the method, the ability-mechanics. **This is the part that stays rigid.** 🔒 LAWs here are real and load-bearing. We hold these to the letter; they do NOT bend to serve story. This is the engine everything runs on.
>
> ## 🟡 STORY LAYER — fluid / work-in-progress
> Characters, names, plot, book structure, scenes, who-does-what. **Nothing here is fixed.** All names are placeholders (Jon, Derrek, the elf — ALL placeholders). Beats and roles move freely. **No 🔒 applies to story** — story is clay, by design, so new ideas can reshape it without breaking anything. The ONLY constraint on story is that it must not violate the WORLD bedrock. **Story-vs-story "conflicts" are not conflicts — they're just the story being unfinished.**
>
> **Why split this way:** the world must be rigorous (it's the science/magic that has to hold up). The story must be flexible (you never know when a new idea reshapes a character or plot, and if every line were locked, nothing could move). Build the world hard; let the story float on top.

## THE STABILITY TIER SYSTEM (applies to WORLD-layer content)
- 🔒 **LAW** — invariant. Never broken without explicit renegotiation. (WORLD layer only.) Only Patrick locks.
- ✅ **CONFIRMED, NOT LOCKED** — *(v5)* canon Patrick stated or accepted but never locked. Binding, but he may promote it to 🔒 or revise it. This is what most new v5 material carries.
- ⚖️ **FLEXIBLE** — adjustable with care (e.g. the rule-beast kit and Cascade — "they sound good and can always be adjusted later for scenes," jul08 t365).
- 🌊 **FLUID** — open clay, actively being shaped; includes ideas Claude proposed that Patrick hasn't ruled on.
- 🟡 **STORY** — provisional story content; movable by default; never law.
- 🚫 **RETIRED** — a tripwire; an idea we killed. Never reintroduce.
- *(⏳ = pending / not yet written.)*

## CORE WORLD PREMISES (the rigid spine, in one place)
> **[NEW v5-wk2] Superseded summary lines below (the files are current):** "No interface mechanism" and "REMNANT + SKILL = POWER" → the grip is mechanized as the appendage, the inner sense stays mysterious, three dials (Remnant, control, inner sense) (`02` Laws 1–2; wk W01–W06). "Inanimate matter never gains a soul" → matter can hold a soul; enchantment = a soul held in matter (`02` Law 0c; wk W12). "The Blight names only those who seek maximum dissolution" → keeper / consumer / Blight are titles at every level (`02` Law 13; wk W36). "Any user grown vast" becomes a god → humans only by time-fusion or theft (wk W31). Changelings "infiltrate" → largely monsters (wk W19). "A god asks for your devotion…" → a tendency, not a formula (wk W32; NP L15). Add: Remnant can never be burned or spent (wk W10).
- **The One = origin of CONSCIOUSNESS only.** The physical universe (matter, energy, the four forces) pre-exists it and is pure science, untouched. *(v5: "the One" is colloquial — what each world calls its own, "much like we say the sun," t192.)*
- **The Law of the Regress (v5).** Infinite and beginningless, the same story at every level: every plane of reality is a being that came apart so far — past the "forgetting horizon" (placeholder term) — that it became a new plane, and some part always clings together (t172). Our level: one level up a being came apart; the piece that let go completely is our One, the pieces that held on are the **Unified Ones**, and the other pieces that let go became the **sibling worlds** — siblings, not copies (t166–191). `03`.
- **The level above (v5).** A varied population. **The Blight** names only those who seek maximum dissolution (t176–178); the **Unified Ones** hold together and built the barrier that keeps it out. **Jon and Derrek's portal did not create the One — it opened that shield (the breach), and every reset rips it wider** (t184). The resets also swept the invaders back each loop. `02` Law 13, `03`.
- **Magic = inherited mastery.** The One *mastered* the universe (wielder, not author); a being's magic is a smaller expression of that mastery. No interface mechanism — the capacity is identity. **REMNANT + SKILL = POWER.** **SPIRIT = the name for active Remnant magic** (a being's faculty to consciously manipulate matter; having it is what separates a being from inert flesh). One undivided system; **the Body/Spirit "two kinds" split is retired.**
- **No steer-only; effort-gated; no physics audit.** Magic **may add energy** when the scene calls for it (steer-only retired; conservation no longer enforced). The only gates are **Remnant (how much) + skill (how well) + effort/fatigue (the bill)** — never a feasibility check. Rare second branch: **soul magic** (Spirit turned on another's Remnant — will-vs-will, self-risk). Remnant never transfers.
- **Forces & elements = pure descriptive FLAVOR, named after the act — never a power-class or a kind of person.** There are **no force/element "users," no types, no builds.** You watch the act, then call it gravity/fire/EM/weak. **Magic is EXPRESSION, not derivation:** build the person first; their magic follows. *(v5: "As with all our characters she is just a skilled user," t612.)*
- **All BEINGS descend from the One; matter is just physics.** Inanimate matter never gains a soul (t116).
- **AGING/LIFESPAN (bedrock):** every being = flesh + a piece of the One; more Remnant → more resistance to age (longer life) but **never immunity** — everything ages and dies. "God" = a larger chunk of Remnant in flesh — any user grown vast; no qualitative god/mortal divide. ✅ **Remnant itself is ageless; only flesh ages** (t678). Ladder: orcs < humans < dwarves < elves < **dragons (second-longest, t272)** < **giants (longest mortal)** < great gods (seem eternal). **NO true immortals among the aging.** True immortality is a separate MECHANISM (Jon, Derrek, rule-beasts). **No mortal can kill Derrek — another god can, and Jon is a god** (t370); whether the same holds for Jon is open. *(Elves & giants are ANCIENT MORTALS — not "near-immortal"; giants live MUCH longer than elves.)*
- **Soul theft belongs to no race (v5).** ✅ A **changeling is a crime, not a race** — a self-made soul thief of any people, a possessor, not a shapeshifter (t484, t680). Two roads: move your own soul into another body, or consume (t676). **Angels and demons are one kind of being**, soul mages of the changeling kind; a pact is a harvest run in reverse (t666, t674). Changeling, angel/demon and god are blurred regions on one continuum (t674).
- **Pacts (v5).** A pact grants **technique, never Remnant**, through a soul-magic tether that fades on the holder's side, never the patron's; the holder pays the usual effort/fatigue from their own limited Remnant (jul23 t107–108); taught abilities remain as the link fades. "A god asks for your devotion. A demon offers you a deal" (t548).
- **In-betweeners** are an ancient lineage that pre-dates the One, run the Rule instead of Remnant, and still obey physics. *(v5: the bounded space is the in-betweener's **Void**; **Chain** drags a victim into it, t128–130.)*
- **Design stance:** a **science-FLAVORED, SCENE-FIRST** magic story (*not* "mechanisms rigid, magnitudes bend," which is retired). Magic explains the *can* ("they are users"); physics is **light flavor** on the *what*, never an engine. **The science serves the cool; the instant it makes something less cool or adds homework, it loses.** Mechanism-policing is GONE; "we agreed no math" (t72). Rigor lives ONLY in internal consistency, never in matching reality's math. `14_PHYSICS_REFERENCE.md` is a non-binding flavor palette.
