## 2. Answers to open questions

None of these is a clean answer. Each is a July quote that leans one way, and later rulings may outweigh it.
- **Q22** (how elves feel resets). A P86: "Elves face constant existential dread from the resets wiping out chunks of their life and loved ones and radically shifting the reality around them. This would be constantly disorienting." A P89: "The ability to feel the fluctuations in time as a wound." This backs the A-32 pick (an ache of absence). The P86 line isn't in the repo.
- **Q33 / L4** (does Jon count as a threat to Derrek). A P32: "Derrek never had to be afraid of non time gods… However the ONE person who could defeat him and he knew it." That is Jon. It backs t370 ("jon is a god"). Not in the repo verbatim.
- **Q36 / A-27** (does the tether fade). A P185: "it never once changes the fact derrek is pulled with him." A P186: "the tether does not care why they jumped. derrek goes with jon. end of story." This backs the pick "the curse-tether never fades". The quote is already in the repo; the Q is still open.
- **Q40** (how literally Jon causes the Blight). A P37: "The resets are what broke open the barrier." A P33: "He learned that he is why time is breaking. His jumps are the fault line." Read literally, his resets opened the breach. The substance is already in `03`/`08`.
- **Q58** (where the First Jump origin lives). A P181: "book 4 is a rough place holder for the idea of the story where jon and derrek first broke open the portal. then the story of them first jumping. the story where derrek shows jon the window." So in July the answer was Book 4. The later R089/R100 reorder leaves Q58 open.
- **Q29** (human hunger: need or want). A P48 is where the old line comes from: "They grow not only from pride but from necessity with pride operating as the mask they hide behind." It dates from 07-01, before t480 ("never actually d[one]…"), so it's evidence for the older side, not an answer.
- **Q30** (can a changeling father children). A P74 is a lean only, and the mechanic it rests on is retired: changelings become "a perfect copy… down to every cell… Otherwise how would two changelings even breed?" Under host-body canon that suggests yes, but it was never ruled.
