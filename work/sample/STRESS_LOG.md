## One: Property (moment 8–10, base 4)

1. **S1 · the run to the wire** (8 · animal fear)
   - Now reads: "Wire ahead. Past it the yards: slag, cranes, open sheds, pens. Hot fat and ozone in his mouth. Once, over his shoulder, the towers going up white out of the bedrock without a seam in them, and then he didn't look back again."
   - Was: "Behind him the towers went up white out of the bedrock without a seam in them. Ahead, wire. Past the wire the yards, slag flats and cranes…"
   - Why: a running man doesn't survey the skyline behind him. He sees what's ahead in pieces, and the towers come in one snatched look.
2. **S2 · the pens** (8 · he sees parts, not the system)
   - The one long paragraph is now four: the orcs and scars ¶ the presses and warm plate ¶ "The orcs on the ramps could see the stacks." ¶ the young ones by CORES. "Orcs stood in rows" became "Orcs in rows". G1 kept word for word.
   - Why: it read like a composed survey mid-chase. Breaking it lets each horror land as a separate glimpse, and the line about the orcs seeing the stacks stands alone.
3. **S3 · Jon on the crane** (9 · too frightened to take a person in whole)
   - Now reads: "A man on the counterweight of the nearest crane, twenty feet up. Eating a peach. Two stones on the iron beside him already. A long coat, too warm for the weather, and a face that looked pleased to see Eli before it knew who he was."
   - Was: full sentences ending on a narrator's generalisation ("the kind of face that looks pleased to see you…").
   - Why: in the middle of the fight he catches Jon in pieces. The face line stays, but now it's Eli's impression, not a narrator's aside.
4. **S4 · the kill** (10 · the first death by his own hand)
   - Now reads: "He reached. Forty yards of slag, and then the guard's throat, and he closed his grip on it and held. It took longer than he'd thought it would." P6 follows unchanged.
   - Was: "He reached across forty yards of slag, closed his grip on the guard's throat, and held it."
   - Why: the old sentence was neat and efficient, like an order carried out. This is the worst moment of the chapter, so the sentence stumbles.
- **Unchanged:** the hip slip (P3), shaking hand (P7), the stone (P8), and the counting while he waits for Teo. They already read as a mind in shock. The opening "That was the whole problem" stays as Eli's own wry idiom.

## Two: The Master (base 4 dropping to 3: the first dip)

- **Unchanged:** the poster (7). It's already broken in the right places, and Grok singled out the warrant beat. The burnout boy (P10) and the refusal are unchanged too.
