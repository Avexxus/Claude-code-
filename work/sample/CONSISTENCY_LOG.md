## For Patrick (older than v9, not changed)

1. **The dragon section (ch 6 opening, "The dragon knew his crew would die.")** is in the dragon's POV, and "From the surface it looked like…" plus the cooked crew is omniscient. It breaks close third on Eli. This is deliberate in every version since the draft (P22 was cut for being "the dragon's POV"). Keep it as a set-piece, or reframe it through the display?
2. **"Derrek" is never introduced.** The name first appears at the unison line in ch 7 with no "his father, Derrek". Readers can infer it, but you might want one earlier mention (Eli would know his father's name).
3. **Eli's ribs in ch 6** ("took four months to knit") have no stated cause; the cutter capture is implied. It reads as a gap, maybe a good one.
4. **Ch 5 ending:** "After that she stopped calling him *the human*…" sums up weeks of sparring, then "The yard wanted the berth back by morning. They left." The time order wobbles (v8's "the next morning" had the same problem). One fix is to move the berth line before the summary paragraph.
5. **"stood there while they did it" … "Eli stayed until it was done"** (ch 5) says the same thing twice. It's left alone as roughness, and the second sentence keeps P20's "his" pointing at Eli.
6. **Ch 6 freighters:** the arms freighter, the human transit hull (hell), then "the freighter" at the dragon moon. Which ship is which is loose.
7. **Log housekeeping:** `NATURAL_LOG.md` still describes C5 (broken feet) and C8 (two days on the bag) as applied. Both were reverted by the guard, and the book is correct.
