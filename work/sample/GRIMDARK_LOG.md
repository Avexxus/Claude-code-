## Glimmers kept (5), and how each is crushed

1. **Teo's loyalty** (the flyer that came late, the code he forged, his care for the crying elf woman). Crushed: Derrek kills him mid-sentence, without looking (ch Seven). His stiff hand lies open on the floor (G27), and his parents are still in the palace.
2. **The dark elf telling Eli her name** (ch Six), and her pressing his broken fingers before she goes. Crushed: Derrek turns her own Collapse back on her. Nobody else ever heard the name, so it dies with her.
3. **The master coming back** (ch Six, up the ramp in the rain). Crushed: Eli won him with "a cheap trick", using the man's grief for his own master, and the master dies in the beast's room.
4. **The colony peace** (ch Five, the line in the riverbed, 400 saved). Crushed twice: it cost the colonist boy's life (G16), and the elves later cleared both camps for a landing field. "The line in the riverbed had held; nobody on either side of it was left to cross it." (new, ch Six)
5. **Doc healing the feverish girl** (ch Three, "Thank me when she's grown"). Crushed: it was never a kindness. He took her soul and told her mother he couldn't save her (G8, G28).

Not counted as glimmers: Jon's warmth (the hand-holding in the yard, comforting Teo, "I knew you would"). It is never decency. The narration already shows it spending people (the warrant, the discount, the spar he didn't move), so it stays.

## Glimmers cut

1. **Eli's quiet decency about the word "dark elf"**: "Eli had stopped using it some time before and couldn't have said when." It was easy and went unpunished. Replaced by G15.
2. **The Queen's praise after the master's death**: "That was well done… Your master." Now reads: "The Queen went past him to the door without a word, holding her shoulder, and didn't look at the place on the floor."
3. **The rescue at the crater, as a clean win.** The beat stays, but it is no longer a glimmer: Eli's missed gun killed nine (G12), and the freed ones were stranded with the Inquisition due back (G13).
