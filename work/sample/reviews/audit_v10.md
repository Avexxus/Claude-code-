## 2. The micro-contradiction sweep

Quote A ↔ Quote B → fix (beats).

**Real (fix recommended)**
1. **The peach and two hands** (ch 1). "came across the slag still chewing" (49) and "Then two at once, a hand for each" (51) ↔ "finished the peach" (71). He's holding a peach while using both hands. → He pockets the half-peach, or sets it on a post and picks it up after. (This is the same kind of slip as the old "grip slid off the cages… tore the bars".)
2. **Woken up vs awake** (ch 2, 105, same paragraph). "The rest would have woken up in the pens" ↔ "They're awake in there the whole time… The chip doesn't put you out." → Change the first to *walked back to the pens* / *gone back up the ramps*.
3. **Nine dead vs two dead** (ch 4). "walked… through the end of the prisoners' line, nine of them" (343, **⚑G12**) ↔ "The two at the end of the line lay where the gun had left them" (351). → Keep G12. Fix 351 instead: *the nine at the end of the line*, or *the two at the very end were still chained to each other* with the others named as lying near them.
4. **Escaped just now, but fifty years on the road** (ch 4). "Thirty of us, when we started… I was a child" (385) and she falls *out* of the door (369) ↔ "She'd trained on the road, fifty years of it… sold her to a pit on a human world" (405, **⚑G** per GRIMDARK_LOG). If she has never been outside, she can't have fought in a pit on a human world. → Keep the G-item's horror and move it: a pit *down there* (humans drop through hell: 335, BANK 11-01 NP#5), or say she was out once before and sent back.
5. **"The lights along the walls"** (ch 8, 885) ↔ "The light came from nowhere" (ch 7, 717). → *The light dimmed all at once.*
6. **The ruined arm** (ch 8). "his right arm hanging. The skin of the forearm was gone" (943) and "one arm hanging at his side" (997) ↔ "Then the ruined one as well" (957) and "brushed it aside with the back of his ruined hand" (973). → Make it cost: he lifts the ruined arm once, it falls back, and it hangs from then on.

**Minor**
7. **Cold vs too warm** (ch 1). "steaming in the cold" (23) ↔ "A long coat, too warm for the weather" (45). Not a logged P-item (it predates v8). → *a long coat, buttoned to the chin* or similar, or drop "in the cold".
8. **No tremor, then a tremor** (ch 7). "No sound came through, and no tremor" (813) ↔ "the whole Tower shuddered once" (817), right after the oath that nothing leaves. A reader may think the blast leaked. → Make it lights only, or set it plainly *far above* as the Emperor's reaction (the dais is upstairs).
9. **Nothing leaves, not even light, yet a picture shows** (ch 7, 805). The text pre-empts it ("the beast's picture, painted for them"). → Fine as is; flagging it only because a hostile reader will poke it.
10. **Station vs "would be back"** (ch 4). "Inquisition station on one side" (305), and the last inquisitors run *for* it (371) ↔ "The Inquisition would be back on the ninth" (399). → *would send more on the ninth*.
11. **"I asked them to come. All of them."** (ch 8, 1023) ↔ the Queen was sent by Mika and never asked (469), and the dark elf came out of a door. → Cut "All of them." (Eli's floor speech is protected work; Patrick's call.)

**Patrick's call (⚑, or intended)**
12. **"It never goes out… They let us keep that"** (409) ↔ in hell "The light went out" (597). She means outside, but "let us keep" suggests the prisoners had it down there. → *It never goes out. Not out here.*, or leave it.
13. **"Her ruined arms came up one last time, and she threw"** (893) after "arms bent where arms don't bend" (889). Intended as heroic, but it is the "tore the bars like paper" shape. → One beat: she throws with her whole body, from the knees.
14. **Vacuum sound** (ch 6). "the suit seal hissing" (639) while Teo sits outside on the airless moon. → *hissing in his helmet*, or move it into the hold.

**Ledger drift (aside):** STRESS_LOG S3 says the crane line now reads "a face that looked pleased to see Eli before it knew who he was". v10 line 45 still has the narrator's "the kind of face that looks pleased to see you…". The log and the text disagree; this is the drift the fresh review warned about.
