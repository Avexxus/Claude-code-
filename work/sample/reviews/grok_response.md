## The points

**1. Pacing too tidy: APPLIED (partly).**
- Evidence: Teo's flyer landed the moment the yard went quiet; the crater raid "took three minutes", clean except for the enemy; the colony peace ran in a straight line; the master's bag was "already packed"; "We'll come back for him."
- Already some friction: three hungry days, the warrant, the lost ship, the four months for the ribs to heal.
- Changed: Teo comes forty minutes late; a drone finds the back road; the colony talks walk out and stall; the master throws Eli out, the ship waits four days for a part, and he arrives as the ramp lifts; nineteen days adrift in a lifeboat; three weeks stranded after the dragon.

**2. Jon's competence is cartoonish: APPLIED.**
- Evidence: he is on the crane at the right moment (canon, kept); he "hadn't been there a second ago" on a crate in the Queen's attack and then gives the perfect speech; "neither of them had heard him come aboard"; he has the perfect words for the master on the lander; the token works without question.
- Changed:
  - He is wrong twice: "He's not coming", and the back roads.
  - He is absent for the Queen's attack. Eli argues for the relay himself and pays for it with his cheek.
  - His token is taken from him.
  - He misses a spar in the fall, and it opens the Queen's shoulder.
  - On the lander the master cuts him off.
  - Asked "How?" after "I knew you would", he says "I don't know." That is late-run memory he can't account for, not a pose.
- Canon kept: the warmth on autopilot, the orc-hold discount, his coldness in the Tower.

**3. Eli senses Jon's reach: ALREADY FIXED.**
"Eli couldn't feel souls; nobody had ever said he could" (ch 4). In ch 8, the barrage reaches him as weight, per BANK 11-07, 5. No change.

**4. The Inquisition folds: APPLIED (partly).**
- The "I'm the Emperor's son" fold isn't in this draft. Eli uses that line on the giant, who refuses him, and the Queen nearly kills him for it.
- Two softer folds remained: the gate inquisitor stamped the papers "without another word", and the crater left no consequences.
- Changed: the inquisitor keeps Jon's only token, caps the pass at three days with a red bar, logs their names, and has them tailed. At the crater the station beacon is already lit. A cutter hunts them for months, takes the Returned ship back, and leaves them to drift in a sector with no shipping lanes.
