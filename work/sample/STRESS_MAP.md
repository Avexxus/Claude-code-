# Stress map: Book 1 sample (2026-11-08)

> 🌊 Claude's working notes, not canon. A scene-by-scene map of Eli's stress, used to set the prose texture in `SAMPLE_BOOK1_v10.md` (changes in `STRESS_LOG.md`). Patrick: "Make it feel more emotionally accurate for each scene. But apply a growing stress as his overall journey gets worse so we still see it grow, but less evenly."

**How to read it.** *Moment* = how hard the scene presses on Eli right now (1–10). *Baseline* = what he carries into it from the journey so far (1–10); it rises unevenly and ratchets, so each calm sits higher than the last. *Truth* = what he is actually feeling under the scene, which isn't always what the scene "should" make him feel.

**Texture rule.** Moment 1–4: full sentences, connective tissue, room to notice and think. Moment 5–7: mixed; plain, some cuts. Moment 8–10: fragments, skipped steps, snagged details, short paragraphs. If the baseline is 6 or more, even a low-moment scene keeps damage (an intrusive image, a short attention span, flat affect).

**Baseline curve:** ch1 spike 9 (base 4) → ch2 dip, 3 at the temple → ch3 holds at 3 → ch4 crater spike 9, ship dip 4 → ch5 base 5, giant's hall 5, corridor spike 9 → ch6 base 6, strike 8, the name 5, dragon 9, base 7 by the frozen orcs → Earth eve false calm 6 (base 7) → ch7 Tower 8–10 → ch8 10 → ch9 flat 9.
