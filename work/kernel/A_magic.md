## A. HARD RULES

### What magic is
- 🔒 Magic = a conscious being reaching into reality with its own Remnant and making it do what it wants. One undivided act; fire, gravity, a shove are descriptions applied afterward, never kinds of magic. (04 What magic IS; 02 Law 1)
- 🔒 Remnant = the chunk of the One you ARE: your soul, your capacity, "a measure of how much of the one is held together". Soul = Remnant. (R021; 04 wk W07)
- 🔒 Remnant is identity, never fuel. Never a pool, never "mana", never spent per cast, never burned. "We cant burn remnant." (02 Law 2; 04 wk W10)
- 🔒 Remnant cannot be destroyed. When the body can't hold it, it releases back into the environment. (R024, R096)
- ✅ Remnant is ageless; only flesh ages. (04 t678; 02 4a)
- 🔒 "Spirit" = the name for actively using your Remnant. Flesh is just flesh; Spirit is what reaches out. (04)
- 🔒 Magic and science are the same word in-world. Humans call it "science" because dragons were their first teachers. Faith, magic and science are one act under different names. (04 wk W57, W63)
- 🔒 Matter is just physics and does no magic. A machine with no soul does no magic; it can only mimic magic's effects mechanically (an EM-field generator throws what looks like EM magic), described in the same words, never the same act. (02 Laws 0c, 1; jul08 t339)
- ✅ An ensouled machine is a held soul; with receptors that soul can work magic. Simple machines need no Remnant; complex machines need flesh with Remnant held inside. (R130, R030)

### The grip (the appendage) and what it does
- ✅ Remnant extends an appendage that grabs the universe. Its length, size, shape and intensity scale with Remnant, Skill and focus. (04 wk W01)
- ✅ Grip vocabulary (flavor, no math): shaking particles = heat; pulling charges apart = lightning; crushing or ripping a nucleus = explosions; denting space = gravity; turning a quark = transmutation, THE HARDEST ACT; a botched transmutation rips the nucleus. (04 wk W02)
- ✅ Dragons stay four-legged; the appendage is their hands. Using magic to build or hold tools is tiresome for them, so dragons design and others build. (04 wk W63; R036, R090)

### The three dials (the only gates)
- 🔒 Power = Remnant (how much) + Will (how much you mean it) + Skill (how well, including sense and touch). Plus the bill: fatigue, and burnout past it. Nothing else gates magic. (02 Law 2; R095, R096)
- 🔒 REMNANT: innate, capped by the body's physical capacity. Training and age never add Remnant: "unlike some games you dont gain mana over time". (R097, R022)
- 🔒 WILL: conviction, moral weight, wanting it. "The part of me that really believes I can produce this flame." A timid giant throws nightmare fireballs he's too scared to make obey; a trained, willful gnome still can't quite beat a dragon. (R095)
- 🔒 SKILL: training. "How many times have yiu sat down and tried to find the nucleus of an atom… How many boulders have you lifted." Covers sensing and touch. Two beings of equal Remnant produce utterly different results. (R095; 04)
- ✅ Basic magic needs only control; deeper workings need finer sense. That makes deep work costlier for the dull-sensed, never forbidden. Example: the "numb titan": huge Remnant, poor sense. (04 wk W03–W04)
- 🚫 Never "the inner sense" as a separate third dial. (04 RETIRED v5-cc)

### Costs, fatigue, burnout
- 🔒 The everyday bill is fatigue: a mage tires like a laborer or a grandmaster. Exhaustion is the universal soft limit. (02 Law 2)
- 🔒 Burnout = the BODY breaks when made to carry more than it can hold, usually through amplifiers. Magic vibrates and charges the matter of your own cells; overdo it "and you literally destroy your own body". The Remnant is fine and releases. "Imagine god channeling his magic and the suit he used is burnt." (R096; BANK 11-16 [REMNANT · never spent])
- ✅ Bodies normally hold about what their owner can control. Hooking up to external Remnant (Tower, chip, held souls) lets a user work at "2 3 5 50 100000x" normal ability, far past the body's breaking point. That's where people destroy themselves. (R023; BANK 11-16 [THE MASTER])
- ✅ Canon burnout deaths: the Tower apprentice amplified from the Tower's soul-mass and his body burst into fire from inside and went to cinder. The master warned chipped students: "Dont use more than you can control." (R135, R075; 05)
- 🔒 No physics audit, ever. No energy ledger, no feasibility math, no "needs fusion not weak", no size charts or square-cube reasoning. Magic may ADD energy. A weak being can't crack a planet because they lack the Remnant and it would kill them, not because math forbids it. (02 Laws 0a, 2; 04)
- 🚫 Never mana bars, pools, recharge, or Remnant as fuel or currency. (04 RETIRED; R131)
- 🚫 Never "steer-only" / conservation as a rule / Maxwell's-demon framing. (04 RETIRED)

### Capacity: holding more than yourself
- 🔒 Your own fragment can't be given, lent, shared or siphoned. Only knowledge or technique passes between beings. (02 Law 2; jul23 t108, t114)
- ✅ You CAN hold more than your own: devices and grafted flesh add capacity; soul magic crams extra Remnant inside you, kept together only by your Will and your own Remnant's coherence. (R097)
- ✅ Held Remnant is an AMPLIFIER, never a battery. It boosts your own source or the held soul is forced to do the work. Nothing drains; an item never runs dry. (R112, R035, R133)
- ✅ The public believes held Remnant is a battery; that's their myth. (R034, R112)
- ✅ Taking another's Remnant = "stealing their production", worked as an amplifier. (R132; 04 wk W11)
- ✅ Giants, the largest living bodies, have the most capacity. Strong users naturally have bodies that can hold what they control. (R023)
- ✅ Loose Remnant drifts everywhere and gathers in latent pools mined "like oil reserves". Rifts are the richest sites; at the Tower rift, chunks of raw floating Remnant leak in. Rifts are reservoirs, never the source of anyone's magic. (R025, R014; 04)
- ✅ A soul cannot be sold and is not a currency. You sell a being, or a soul bound into something. Money is the imperial sheckle, the dwarven mark, the elven silver crown (placeholder names). (R033, R054)

### Flesh vs metal; enchantment; devices
- ✅ Enchantment is one rule: Remnant held inside something, in many uses and forms. Imbued gear, living metal, chips, the Tower: "It is all the same thing." Reservoirs, attunement objects and bound effects are uses, not kinds. (R099, R074; 06 ONE RULE)
- ✅ Organics hold Remnant better; "It prefers them. So itll stay in it." Metal must be bound by a learned magic. That's why Book 1-era tech uses flesh. (R148)
- ✅ Body-like substrate holds best; younger flesh is higher quality for high-end work; another elf is very high-quality material. (06 wk W12; R028)
- ✅ Flesh augments keep the hold on your Remnant: "They make your augmentation from flesh so it retains ability to hold your remnant." (R032)
- ✅ Grafted flesh adds capacity. The Orc King: grafted, grotesque body (mismatched skin, bone pushing out, metal plates), ONE chip only, so he holds more. Body horror, never identity. (BANK 11-18; R097)
- ✅ Focusing chambers channel and amplify; only dwarves can build them (a monopoly). Dragons buy them. A captured soul can be bound into a core, never drained. (R043, R134)
- ✅ Implant form: a human-sized chamber implant lets a user "project their entire force into a single powerful attack". The max one-shot is self-destructive. Other uses: longer draws, strength and durability, cognitive speed, magnetic fields against EM attacks. Strength rises only relative to the user's own baseline. (06 jul08 t259–265)
- ✅ The chip is a shock collar: one universal chip sold in different boxes for different classes; full control only when the wearer is unruly; can control anyone chipped. Not full mind control. (R143)
- ✅ The bond-spoof chip is a miniaturized focusing chamber, made by dwarves, perfected by dissecting live and dead elves. It is a key, not a teacher: it works only for someone already capable of soul magic, and lets them pass as an elf / break a bond-protected elf. (06 jul23 t151–156)
- ✅ Cathedral ships are built by giants (via Convergence) from dead flesh: less Remnant, but they hold a lot. Dragons don't care whose dead are in the hull. (R027, R045)
- ✅ Magic weapons: dwarves made the Book-3-era masterwork gear, magic swords, enchanted necklaces; enchantment is a learnable craft, and a master enchanter need not be a strong user. (BANK 11-16 [DWARVES]; 06)
- ✅ Drones can't feel souls in general; only specific spoofing or reading chips can. (R031)

### Soul magic
- 🔒 Soul magic = Spirit turned on another fragment of the One (mind, memory, emotion, will, soul). Same Remnant, different rules: will against will, coherence against coherence, no forces or elements. (04 Soul magic)
- 🔒 It is two-way: what you grip grips back. The limit is self-risk, not fatigue: you bleed the other's nature into yours, fracture, lose pieces of self. (04)
- 🔒 Rare, forbidden, scarcely known; belongs to no race. Everyone knows souls can be stolen, which makes it tempting and taboo. (04 t484, t544, t680)
- ✅ It can override a living soul and take the body. (04 jul08 t329)
- ✅ Two roads of soul theft: TRANSFER (force your soul into a younger body) or CONSUME (take souls into your own body). A crime anyone can commit, not a race: "Its your father its your uncle its the priest at the church." (04 t676, t680)
- ✅ All changelings are incredibly powerful from the start; no weak changelings. Sustainable pace is 1–2 souls at a time. The tell is soul-body mismatch; smart ones shield it or hop bodies. Failures: the giant's decay, the young dark elf's rupture. (04 t544; wk W16, W18, W20)
- ⚖️ Wholesale absorption: a weaker soul loses coherence in the swirl of a far greater one. Needs at least changeling-level power; a god-scale being can do it to many at once. Jon and Derrek can. (04 jul23 t116–120)
- ✅ Binding a ghost is constant concentration: the holder pays every day. Kill the holder, the binding lapses, the soul is released (never destroyed). (04 t504–505; R096)
- ✅ Echoes = impressions left by great death, no one there, fading over centuries. Ghosts = Remnant bound together by magic: every true ghost is a crime, every haunting a crime scene. Changelings = many souls in one living driver. (04 t498–502)
- ✅ An ancient ghost needs a warden: a second soul bound into an object to hold the first. Two victims; breaking the object frees both. Mechanism deliberately hand-wavey. (04 t506–508)
- ✅ The vessel varies (own body, amulet, armor); necromancy = the holding + the vessel. (04 t510–513)
- ✅ A held soul feels only vague, general things, maybe some strangeness. "Sensation comes from sensory organs." Dread is flavor, not the rule. (R128)
- ✅ Not every walking corpse holds a soul: the Orc King's dead are empty animated flesh. (04 t522)
- ✅ Resurrection possible in principle, odds near zero; no one can trace a dead soul's scattered Remnant. (R122)
- ✅ Pressure fields: a powerful being exerting itself presses on everyone near; to keep conscious thought you need trained shields against types of pressure ("soul pressure"). Physical immunity is a continuous psychic force barrier needing massive Remnant and control. (R067)
- ✅ Top-tier fine control reaches body chemistry: tiny explosions in every cell, reading hormones, pheromones and blood pressure as mood, nudging them. Defenses resist it. (R066)
- ✅ The elven bond is an extrasensory organ, NOT soul magic, not a tether, shares no Remnant. Reach scales with Remnant and distance: one far elf = a pinprick, the homeworld = a beacon, up close they feel each other plainly. Elven crews coordinate; they don't pool. (R098)
- ✅ Sensing scales with the source: powerful users feel the Tower "like the sun"; the mildly psychic feel ghosts; sensing one being's Remnant is a rare master's skill. (R129; 04 wk W07)
- 🚫 Never soul-sensing as constant scanning or detection ("video gamey"). (04 wk W09)

### Convergence and the Tower
- ✅ Convergence works on bodies. The funerary rite releases the soul; the emperor's version used his city's bodies as material and trapped their Remnant in the stone. (04 wk W15)
- ✅ The Tower = the giant emperor converging his entire capital into himself to survive the dragon king's nuclear breath; he dissolved into it, everything outside vaporized. The largest Remnant source on Earth; a giant channeling device and amplifier; everyone near it is at their absolute peak. (R136, R026)
- ✅ The Tower is mindless: "Its not thinking. It isnt feeling. Its not speaking." (R111)

### External powers
- 🔒 Two umbrellas plus the Rule: PACTS (any deal with a being), ENCHANTMENTS (held Remnant), THE RULE (in-betweeners' alien OS). Thin frames, specifics built per scene. (06)
- 🔒 A pact grants TECHNIQUE, never Remnant. The holder pays every use from their own limited Remnant. "A pact doesn't make you strong; it makes you unrepeatable." (06 jul23 t108–109)
- ✅ Each patron's technique is its own signature. Taught abilities remain as the link fades; the tether fades on the holder's side, never the patron's: "You can stop attuning. You can't stop being findable." (06 jul23 t106, t114–115)
- ✅ "A god asks for your devotion. A demon offers you a deal." A pact is a harvest reversed. For gods, the tether feeds the god; a religion is a recruitment network of pact champions. (06 t548, t666; wk W57)
- ✅ Angels and demons are one kind of being, the same as changelings: superior soul mages. Changeling → angel/demon → god are blurred regions; no graduation. (06 t666–674)
- ✅ A patron strong enough can puppet a lesser being's will; on the page that's a plot point, never framed as an earned rescue. (06 jul23 t122–126)
- 🔒 In-betweeners predate the One, run the Rule instead of Remnant, obey physics. Fuel = transition; price = coherence. True death = splitting too far to remember the whole. (06; 02 Law 15)
- ⚖️ Their kit (Void, Chain, Chain Void, phase-step, Unfixing, the Hold, Cascade) is an adjustable foundation. A Rule traps everyone in its bounded space, the in-betweener included; it releases when its condition is met. (06 jul08 t365)
- ✅ The Returned's powers (phasing, decoherence, compression) are new Remnant magic learned from the maze, not stolen Rule-magic. (06 t148)

### Who people are, and how magic expresses
- 🔒 No types: no fire users, gravity users, EM mages, classes, builds, loadouts. The label names the act, never the person. "As with all our characters she is just a skilled user." (04 t612, t136)
- 🔒 Build the person first; magic is expression of their life, assigned last. No derivation either way (not race→element, not temper→element). The same effect can mean opposite things in two people. (04 Magic is EXPRESSION)
- 🔒 Blending is free: fire + gravity + spin = a fire-gravity tornado, no permission check. (04)
- 🔒 People still have repertoire, inclination and specialty, just not classes. Spells = reproducible, engineered workings, taught and drilled, never slotted. (04 Spells)
- 🔒 Awakening is a gradient: involuntary leaks (loudest under emotion) → crude intent → control → engineered workings → mastery. (04 Awakening)
- ✅ Every character develops their own powers; gods take this to the ultimate expression with the same Remnant magic. (04 jul23 t106)

### Power scale with concrete reference points
- 🔒 One continuous scale (Remnant, Will, Skill × time). "Demigod"/"god" are points on it, not tiers. Any being of any size can become a god with enough time and practice; theft and the keeper's pact are shortcuts. (04; 02 Laws 3–4; R107)
- 🔒 Lifespan follows Remnant (resistance, never immunity): orcs and dwarves < humans < elves < dragons < giants < great gods. No true immortals among the aging. (02 Law 4a; R093)
- ✅ Races on the axes: giants = sheer Remnant, little skill, will sapped after their fall; dragons = less than giants, so they perfected Skill, with belief in their supremacy; elves = high Remnant + high Skill + the bond; humans = lower Remnant, cannot match giant, dragon or elf one on one, so they fight in combined arms, groups, and eventually chips, with "indomitable" will and numbers; dwarves = low Remnant, highest Skill of all races, built systems and tools that use Remnant. (R068–R070, R093, R149; BANK 11-16)
- ✅ Reference points: a timid untrained giant's uncontrollable fireballs terrify other races. (R095)
- ✅ The dragon king's breath: nuclear fuel made with magic, "a literal nuke fired out of a dragons mouth", that destroyed the giant capital. Its radiation poisoned the land for hundreds of years. (BANK 11-16 [BETRAYAL DAY]; R061)
- ✅ The master, near the top of lesser beings: amplified by the Tower, he held his cohesion with his whole body to press a single point until it became a black hole. (BANK 11-16 [THE MASTER])
- ✅ Derrek: microscopic clarity, cell-level explosions, chemistry reading; pours out soul pressure; stolen souls held inside him as magnifiers. Only a god armed with power from beyond the regress could threaten him. (R066; BANK 11-16 [DERREK · unleashed]; 02 4b wk W38)
- 🔒 Time is off-map: Jon's and Derrek's time powers came from the Unified One; all their other magic they built themselves over endless time. Not a learnable force. (R109; PROPERTY #70)
- ✅ Strong-force-flavored feats are the rarest, found only at the very top of the scale. (04 t136)

## B. FLAVOR / AURA

**The core feeling**
- Magic is a physical act, the body reaching into matter: you grip, shake, pull, crush, dent. Felt in the flesh, never cast from a menu. "Your body is what is spent. As you harness magic you are doing things like vibrating or charging. This would have consequences on the structure of your cells" (BANK 11-16).
- Power isn't spent, it's carried, and the danger is the body that carries it. The signature image is a scorched suit: "Imagine god channeling his magic and the suit he used is burnt" (R096). Overreach ends in flesh rupturing or burning from inside, never in an empty gauge.
- Science and wonder are one thing. A reader who knows physics recognizes nuclei and charges under the folk words, but nobody does the math: "we agreed no math" (t72). "The science serves the cool" (02 Law 0a).
- Grimdark economics: Remnant is "stored into an organic or inorganic form. Organics just hold it better" (BANK 11-16). So the far future runs on flesh: grown augments, cores holding bound souls, cathedral ships of dead flesh, clones sold as real. "Anyone who hates it is crushed by the weight of people who will use it anyway" (R064). Horror at industrial scale, shown concretely and never hinted at (BANK 11-08 grimdark standard).

**How it looks in a fight**
- "These are wizards. Big and tough doesnt mean throws punches." "He is compressing matter ripping space sure but describe how that would look" (R038). Show space bending, matter compacting, light going wrong. No thrown balls of fire.
- Pressure as atmosphere: great beings press on a room and lesser minds struggle to keep thinking. Patrick's touchstones: "haki" and HxH-style shields to "certain types of pressure" (BANK 11-16). "He continues to pour out his soul pressure… Each one a magnifier he can hook up to. He really turns up the pressure" (BANK 11-16 [DERREK · unleashed]).
- The terrifying is intimate as well as huge: "tiny explosions in every cell of your body… read each hormone or feremone… He can read your blood pressure" (BANK 11-16).
- Top-end imagery he returns to: a black hole pressed into being by a man holding himself together with "all of his body" (BANK 11-16 [THE MASTER]); "a literal nuke fired out of a dragons mouth" (BANK 11-16); holding matter in a gravity well while igniting a star at its heart (MSE §10); the Tower felt "like the sun blazing in the center of our solar system" (BANK 11-16).
- Joy in power, for the right monster: "Derrek loves this. He is having the most fun he has ever had… marvels… about the sheer beauty of the destruction he has sown" (BANK 11-16).

**Soul magic's register**
- Quiet, domestic and wrong. The changeling is "your father… your uncle… the priest at the church" (t680); the village healer "sucked out their soul and lied" (R145). The threat is "so obscure and also inevitable and noone protects against it… it cant stop daily life" (R037).
- Love as crime: ghosts bound "out of love"; a son forged into an amulet to keep his dead father cohered because the mother couldn't let go (04 t510); a brother whose death drops both brothers at once (04 t504). Every haunting is a crime scene.
- Held souls aren't screaming. They feel "vague general things and maybe some strangeness", because "sensation comes from sensory organs" (R128). Near the Tower the mildly psychic catch "vague otherworldly feelings they can't explain" from voices with "no brain or mouth to speak" (BANK 11-16 [CAPITAL · wrongness]). Eerie, not loud.
- Soul-sensing stays rare and unreliable, never "video gamey" (wk W09).

**Taste markers (what makes it Patrick's)**
- No classes, ever. "As with all our characters she is just a skilled user" (t612). "Hes just a master" (t136). A label "strips the person of the mastery and dedication it took."
- The dials are emotional, not only mechanical. Will is "the part of me that really believes I can produce this flame" (R095). Cowardice limits a giant; conviction lifts a gnome. That's still not enough to beat a dragon.
- Races differ in *how* they wield, not in what they're allowed: brute giants, scholarly dragons with "deep schools of magic" (R068), human swarms and chips, dwarves who "built systems for building tools that used remnant" (BANK 11-16).
- Pacts are teachers, not batteries: "A user in book 1 using a gods remnant would be undefeatable. A user using a gods power with their own limited remnant keeps them grounded" (jul23 t108). Patrons appear "in the ether as the bhudda" to those who attune (jul23 t112). (He used that word for the image; keep it out of page text as a real-world reference. inferred)
- Comps he names: Wheel of Time (one Power, individual strengths, MSE §7); One Piece and HxH for pressure (BANK 11-16); Warhammer 40k for tone, "draw heavy inspiration" without copying (13 jul23 t96, 🌊 not a register law).
- The flesh-weapon instinct: "Yea I like flesh weapons. Lots of fun there" (BANK 11-01; the weapons question stays open, B-26).
- Body horror with power behind it: the Orc King as "a more grotesque shape. Flesh and bone sticking out. Metal patches. Grafted skin not the same color" (BANK 11-18). Power you can see bolted on.
- Recurring contrasts: identity vs fuel; flesh holds vs metal must be trapped; huge-and-clumsy vs small-and-trained; the universe-scale working vs the cell-scale one; a god's power in a body that burns like a suit.
- Restraint as wisdom: the master teaches Eli "to listen and be slow" because he watched chipped students destroy themselves (BANK 11-16). In the master's last stand, caution is what he finally lets go of. (inferred: the drama of magic lives in the decision to exceed the body)
