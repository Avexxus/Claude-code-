## A. HARD RULES

### The stance (how to write any magic or cosmic scene)
- 🔒 Scene first, science-flavor after. "The science serves the cool." The moment science makes a scene less cool or adds homework, science loses. (02 Law 0a)
- 🔒 No physics audit, ever: no energy budgets, no "that needs fusion, not weak," no feasibility math, no stat tables or size charts. "We agreed no math." (02 Law 0a, Law 2; t72)
- 🔒 Rigor means internal consistency only. Never contradict our own canon; reality's math doesn't matter. (02 Law 0a)
- 🔒 Magic may add energy. Conservation isn't enforced. Steer-only is dead. (02 Law 2)
- 🔒 The four forces and the four elements are words for describing an act after it happens. Nobody is a "fire user," a "gravity type" or a "force user." Watch the act, then name it. (02 Laws 5, 6; 01 FORCE)
- 🔒 Magic is expression, not derivation. Build the person first: wounds, teachers, history. Their magic follows from that life. No race→element, temperament→affinity or ability→character equation. (02 Law 18b)
- 🔒 Personality shapes how someone learns and how their magic looks, never what they can reach. (02 Law 10)
- 🔒 Everything is a grade on a scale: power, awakening, mastery, godhood. No hard tiers, no "graduating" from one kind to another. (02 Law 3; t674)
- 🔒 Combining forces means wielding them side by side. Forces never fuse into a fifth force. Mastery = how much of that space you can weave. (02 Law 9)
- ⚖️ Transmutation, gold-making and the worst releases are the mythic top: rare arts of the mightiest, high on the scale, never a class. "Transmutation transmutes, full stop." (02 Law 7b)
- ⚖️ The grip, as flavor: shaking particles = heat; pulling charges apart = lightning; crushing the nucleus = explosions; turning a quark = transmutation, the hardest act; a botched transmutation rips the nucleus; denting space = gravity. (02 7b; wk W02)

### The bedrock: two domains
- 🔒 Physics (matter, energy, the four forces) existed before the One and has no author. The One created consciousness only, never matter. (02 Law 0b; 03 The One)
- 🔒 The One lives inside physics and obeys it. Even the divine is inside the physics. (03)
- 🔒 Physics and consciousness touch at exactly one point: magic. (02 Law 0b)
- 🔒 Between planes, nesting by scale is simply real, "part of the magic," and is never explained. Inside a plane, the 0b wall holds. Both images are allowed as flavor: a universe inside every iota of Remnant, and our universe as "1 tiny particle in something much larger." (02 0b; R101)
- 🔒 Only beings descend from the One. A being = a physical body + a fragment of the One. A rock holds no fragment. (02 Law 0c)
- 🔒 "The One" can be described *like* a quantum field (a soul as a resonance on it), but it's a separate, non-physical field. It's never a bridge to physics. (02 0b; 03)
- ✅ The brain is body, not soul. A destroyed brain doesn't free the fragment. The fragment leaves only at death, which is an event, and a brainless living body still holds its person, "locked inside as a vague state." (02 0c; 03; jul08 t331–343)

### Magic: Remnant, Will, Skill
- 🔒 Power has three independent dials. **Remnant** is how much of the One you are. **Will** is conviction, moral weight, how badly you want it. **Skill** is training, including sense and touch. (02 Law 2; R095)
- 🔒 A timid, untrained giant can still make nightmares. A trained, strong-willed gnome still can't beat a dragon in raw power. (02 Law 2; R095)
- 🔒 Remnant is identity, never fuel. It can't be burned, spent, drained or used up per cast. Never write a mana bar. (02 Law 2; wk W10; R131)
- 🔒 The everyday cost is effort and fatigue: a mage tires like a laborer. (02 Law 2)
- 🔒 Burnout = the body breaks when it's made to carry more than it can hold, usually through amplifiers. The flesh fails and the Remnant releases undamaged. "Imagine god channeling his magic and the suit he used is burnt." It is never Remnant burning. (02 Law 2; R096)
- 🔒 Your own Remnant is capped by your body's physical capacity. Devices and grafted flesh add capacity. Soul magic can cram more in, and holding it takes Will plus your own coherence. (02 Law 2; R097)
- 🔒 Your own fragment can't be given, loaned or siphoned. Patrons teach technique and never give Remnant. (02 Law 2; jul23 t108, t114)
- ✅ Held or stolen Remnant amplifies. It's never a battery: nothing drains or runs dry. "Battery" is a public myth only. (R112, R133, R034)
- ✅ Remnant extends an appendage, a psychic hand. It scales with Remnant, Skill and focus. Fine work with it is tiring. (01 APPENDAGE; wk W01; R036)
- ✅ Sensing scales with the source. A strong user feels the Tower "like the sun," and the mildly psychic feel ghosts. Sensing one person's Remnant is a rare master's skill, never constant scanning. (R129; wk W07, W09)
- 🔒 Only beings do magic. Matter does none. A soul held in matter can. (02 Law 1, 0c)
- ✅ A soulless machine does no magic and only copies the look with physics. An ensouled machine is a held soul, and with receptors it can work magic. (02 Law 1; R130; jul08 t339)
- ✅ Simple machines run on electricity. They need no Remnant. (R030)
- 🔒 Science, in any era, can explain WHAT mages do. It can never explain the CAN. (02 Law 1)
- ✅ In-world, "magic" and "science" are one word. Faith, magic and science are one act under different names. Humans say "science" because dragons were their first teachers. (02 Law 1; wk W57, W63)
- 🔒 Spirit = active Remnant magic, one undivided system. Soul magic is its rare branch, will against will and limited by self-risk. (02 Law 18)

### Enchantment, souls, death
- 🔒 Enchantment = Remnant held inside something, in many forms: imbues, living metal, chips, the Tower. Organics hold it best. Metal must be bound by a learned craft. (02 0c; R099, R148)
- ✅ A held soul feels only vague, general things, maybe some strangeness. "Sensation comes from sensory organs." Dread is flavor, not the rule. (R128)
- 🔒 Soul-binding (interrupting a soul's natural death process) and soul-harvesting (absorbing another fragment) are the deepest violations. Harvesting fragments the thief. (02 Law 14)
- ✅ Soul-binding is concentration: the holder pays constantly. Kill the holder and the soul is freed, its Remnant released, never destroyed. (03; R096)
- ✅ Absorption is gated by power, through coherence: you need changeling-level strength, and gods do it to many at once. Soul magic can also override a soul and steal a body. (02 Law 14; jul23 t118; jul08 t329)
- ✅ Soul theft belongs to no race. A changeling is a crime, not a race: "Its your father its your uncle its the priest at the church." Changelings take 1–2 souls at a time. (02 Law 14; t680; wk W16)
- ✅ You can't sell a soul. You can sell a being, or a soul bound into something. Souls aren't currency. (R033)
- ✅ Echo, ghost and changeling are three different things. An echo is loose Remnant left at a site of great death, with no one in it, and it fades. A ghost is Remnant bound by magic, so every ghost is a crime with a holder somewhere. A changeling is many souls in one living driver. (03; t498–501)
- ✅ When a body dies, its Remnant releases back into the world. (R024)
- ✅ Resurrection is possible in principle, but the odds are near zero. Dead fragments can't be traced: "vague specs of remnant long since swirled back into the world." (03; R122)
- 🚫 Never have anyone gather or trace a dead person's fragments (e.g. Derrek collecting Lirael's). (R110, R122)
- ✅ New beings are born when diffuse Remnant gathers into the child at conception. No reincarnation: no character is secretly a past self. (03 How new beings are born)
- 🔒 All races coalesced in the one shatter. Long-lived races stand a few lifetimes from the origin, and short-lived ones thousands of generations away. (03 One shatter)

### Lifespan and gods
- 🔒 More Remnant means flesh that resists age and injury: resistance, never immunity. Everything that ages dies eventually. (02 Law 4a)
- 🔒 The lifespan ladder: orcs and dwarves (short) < humans < elves < dragons (second-longest) < giants (longest of the races) < great gods (they seem eternal, but aren't). (02 4a; R093)
- ✅ Remnant itself is ageless. Only flesh ages. (02 4a; t678)
- ✅ Dwarves are hardy and low in Remnant. They desperately extend their lives through craft, tech and magic. (R093)
- 🔒 Elves and giants are ancient mortals. Never call them "near-immortal." (02 4a)
- 🔒 No god/mortal divide. A god is any user grown vast, the same kind of being as everyone else. A weak god still dwarfs lesser beings. (02 Law 4; t668)
- ✅ Any being of any size can become a god with enough time and practice. Theft and the keeper's pact are only shortcuts. (R107)
- ✅ All gods are born of flesh, on Earth. (wk W30)
- ✅ Usage rule: "gods are mortals." Don't use "mortal" as the opposite of "god." Say "lesser being." (02 Law 4; jul23 t118)
- 🔒 The only true immortals are the rule-beasts (inside their own domain: they "cannot die, but can feel"), plus Jon and Derrek by mechanism (see OPEN). (02 Law 4b)
- ✅ STORY rule: Jon's and Derrek's bodies can be destroyed in-world, and Derrek has killed two time gods. The story never lets Jon or Derrek die. (R105)
- 🔒 No lesser being can kill Derrek. Only a god armed with power from beyond the regress, "if they are powerful and outsmart derrek," could threaten him. (02 4b; t370; wk W38)

### The regress and the level above
- 🔒 The Law of the Regress: every plane is a being that came apart so far it forgot it was one and became a new plane, and some part always clings together. Same story at every level, infinite and beginningless. (02 Law 11; t172)
- 🔒 Our One came apart completely and can't pull back together. The pieces that held on are the **Unified Ones**. The other pieces that let go became their own Ones: the **sibling worlds**, siblings, not copies. (02 Law 11; 03)
- ✅ Every world calls its own One "the One," "much like we say the sun." (03; t192)
- 🔒 The level above is a varied population (predatory, benevolent, indifferent), made of the same Remnant, only larger and older. (02 Law 13; jul23 t100)
- 🔒 The Unified Ones hold together and built the barrier (the bubble) that keeps our level safe. (02 Law 13)
- ✅ **The Blight** is a placeholder name for the enemies of our reality, those who want to consume or destroy it. It's never a single maw of hunger. (R102; 03)
- ✅ A Unified One that decoheres becomes a whole reality. One is strong enough to handle a kin's fragments. (R072)
- 🔒 The One stays in the background, never followed on the page. It shows up only as texture: a raving street-priest, a villain's monologue, human zealots calling themselves the One's anointed. (02 Law 12; 03)
- 🔒 Light and dark are variables (morality, aesthetics), never cosmic powers or beings. (02 Law 12)
- ✅ Madness is the base layer, the One dissociating. Subtext only, with no visible metaphysical cause, and it touches good and bad alike. (03; wk W70)
- ⚖️ Our universe is dead beyond Earth: there's no native life anywhere else, ever. The far-future races settle empty worlds. Earth is everyone's homeworld. (03 DEAD UNIVERSE; wk W58)

### The puppeteer, the pair, the breach
- ✅ The puppeteer is a Unified One at universe scale, "a potential universe who never decohered." It's unnamed on purpose. Call it "the keeper" (plain word) or "the unified one." Never name it. (03; t196; R102)
- 🔒 Jon and Derrek's only *given* power is time, from the keeper's pact. Every other ability they learned over eternity. (02 Law 17; R109)
- ✅ They are "one with time," in control of it and inextricably linked. Never say "time-fusion." (R108)
- ✅ Jon jumps (resets). Derrek sees time and aims the jumps. He has "seen how it could go, not lived it," so he never slips. (wk W39; R019)
- ✅ The tether: whenever Jon jumps, Derrek goes with him. "The tether does not care why they jumped." (02 Law 17; jul08 t369)
- ✅ The keeper could reset the world itself, and forcing them to do it is part of the punishment. Jon's gift is a sentence, not a reward. (R010, R117)
- ✅ How a reset works: nobody inside time-travels. The universe is rewound from outside like a recording. Jon's soul is carried through and his body is rewound. (R012)
- ✅ Jon wakes blank at each reset, and his memory returns over the run. He knows patterns, never outcomes: there's no script. (R118, R119)
- ✅ They are beyond every god in the story: "0 beings in our story who come close." Derrek is quiet and respectful only before a Unified One. (03; t315)
- ✅ Derrek never supported the Blight. He holds the world together against the breach his portal opened. (02 Law 13; t180)
- ✅ The portal didn't create the One. It tore open the Unified Ones' shield (the breach), and every reset rips it wider. (03 THE BREACH; t184)
- 🔒 Resets do two opposite things. They widen the breach, and they rewind the invaders back out. Derrek's "reset or reality ends" is literally true. (03 reset's two functions)
- ✅ A rift forms wherever Jon jumps, even off Earth. Rifts leak because the level above bleeds through. That isn't water or any substance, and it can't be drained. (R013, R002; t186)
- ✅ Once the bleed is in, it stays. Only Jon and Derrek can undo it, by reversing time. Others must seal fast. (R003)
- ✅ Rifts are reservoirs people fight over, never the source of magic. Every being's magic is its own. (03; t188)
- 🚫 Never make the portal or the rifts the origin of magic, the One or the world. (03 portal-bootstrap; t184)
- ✅ Breaks are local and vary by universe: many had no pair, some had singles, some had groups. (R103)
- ✅ The time-god society's Citadel stands in the outer region between realities. Both founders built it. Its law: watch, never join, or lose the society's protection. No one enters Jon and Derrek's war. (R127, R113; t650)
- ✅ The time gods' shared toolkit: sense (find a rift), slice (open it), hold a portal, stitch (close), brace (steady the area). Never "drain." (R001)
- ✅ In the outer region every pair's power is limited. Derrek's sight broke there. (R008)
- ✅ The Arcavist, the Historian and Barnaby are one man, the fourth time god. Derrek killed his master, the third, and later kills him. (R104)
- ✅ "Only two men at the window" is a literary device: whoever else is there, it comes down to those two. (R121)

- ✅ "The final run" is Jon's lie to himself. A run is about 10,000 years, there have been 1,000+ runs, and both men are eldritch and broken in ageless bodies. (02 Law 19; wk W42)

- ✅ Time travel and the loops stay. (R011)

### The Tower
- ✅ The Tower is the giant emperor's last Convergence. He drew the city's bodies into himself and dissolved, everything outside was vaporized, and the city's Remnant is trapped in the stone. (R136; wk W15–W16)
- ✅ The Tower is mindless: "no brains or eyes or skin. Its not thinking." (R111)
- ✅ It's the largest store of Remnant on Earth and a massive amplifier, where everyone is at their absolute peak. It's living stone made from fresh Remnant (cathedral ships are the opposite: dead flesh). (R026, R027)
- ✅ It sits on the first and largest rift, Derrek's portal, which is the most torn by Jon's jumps. It leaks raw floating Remnant from the next dimension. (R014; t66)
- ✅ It's fused into all eras, and the window sits at its center. It's the nexus of *their* family of universes, not of the regress. (01 THE TOWER; t456, t636–638)
- ✅ Derrek built additions to it (elevators, laser arrays), not the walls. The city never moved, only its ruler: giants, then dragons, then Derrek. (t62, t64; wk §J)

### Pacts, angels, in-betweeners
- 🔒 A pact grants technique, never Remnant. The holder pays fatigue from their own limited Remnant. Taught abilities remain as the link fades. (02 Law 16)
- ✅ The pact tether fades on the holder's side, never on the patron's: you can stop attuning, but you can't stop being findable. The tether feeds the god. (02 Law 16; jul23 t115; wk W57)
- 🔒 Angels and demons are one kind of being, soul mages of the changeling kind. Good and evil aren't the line between them. (02 Law 16; t666, t674)
- ✅ "A god asks for your devotion. A demon offers you a deal." It's a tendency, not a formula. (t548; wk W32)
- 🔒 In-betweeners are a lineage older than the One and not descended from it. They run the Rule, not Remnant, and still obey physics. (02 Law 15)
- ✅ A rule-beast has no true form inside its Void (the bubble it carries). It can split into many forms, but split too far and it can never remember itself and return. That's its only true death. (02 4b; t132, t164)
- ⚖️ The rule-beast kit (Phase-step, Unfixing, the Hold, Cascade) is adjustable. (02 Law 15)
- ⚖️ Inside a severed Rule-domain you fight with only what you are and what you brought in. (02 Law 7c)

## B. FLAVOR / AURA

- **Grimdark with one light.** Patrick: "Its grimdark. So were doing haunting evil world with a kind hearted light we follow" (BANK 11-21). The cosmos is the haunting part: vast, indifferent, and stocked with things that eat realities.
- **Cosmic smallness and human weight, together.** "Among our spoke of the wheel we are the central point where still only being one wheel of one car on one world in one galaxy etc etc" (t638). The thesis: "it doesnt matter in the grand scale but everything matters to someone" (t466). Scenes should be able to hold both at once.
- **The divine is just bigger.** Gods are flesh with more Remnant: "gods are what happens when ANY user becomes incredibly powerful" (t668). There's no holy glow that makes them a different kind of thing. Awe comes from scale, never from kind (inferred).
- **Duality, not halves.** "Duality friend. Duality. Grading scales not a single fixed point" (jul08 t269). "Your giving it to much flattening" (t674). Patrick pushes back whenever things sort into neat categories.
- **The theme line of the One:** "The greatest one wished to be everything and that includes things which try to kill each ither" (BANK 11-01). The cosmos is a god that wanted to know what it wasn't, and got murder too (inferred).
- **Madness under everything.** "Madness is base layer kts nondiscriminate good and bad all are mad" (t365). It's felt as subtext, never explained.
- **Wonder that refuses to be explained.** On nesting: "Its one of those things we just have to say is real. Thats part of the magic" (BANK 11-02). He wants the mystery kept, not footnoted.
- **Names that carry dread.** "The name the blight is a dark fettid term that implies malice and harm. Blights kill. They leave things barren" (t178). Cosmic words should smell of rot and threat, not wonder-park (inferred).
- **The puppeteer is too big to name.** "So the being is unnamed intentionally to grand to be understood by people in our universe" (t196). Before it, "the world cannot look away," and even Derrek goes quiet (t321).
- **Burnout as image:** "Imagine god channeling his magic and the suit he used is burnt" (R096). The body is a garment the soul can scorch.
- **Wizards, not brawlers.** "these are wizards. Big and tough doesnt mean throws punches" · "Try not to make it so slingy… describe how that would look more than just he hurled ot at him" (R038). Describe what reality does when it's gripped: heat shimmer, charge, crushed nuclei, dented space (inferred from wk W02).
- **Pressure you can feel.** Strong beings carry a soul-pressure field, and the Tower is felt "like the sun" (R067, R129). Power presses on the air before anything happens (inferred).
- **The Tower:** a mindless mass of a dead city's souls in living stone, "just a mass of the one with no real way we can understand it" (BANK 11-16). Radiation sickness and animals that avoid the land around it (t88; R061). Sacred, toxic and coveted, all at once (inferred).
- **Body horror, quietly metaphysical.** A brainless body still holds its person, "locked inside as a vague state. They feel whats happening" (t341). Patrick took that as "the beautifully grimdark interpretation" (t343). Held souls feel only "vague general things and maybe some strangeness" (R128). The horror is in the dim, not the scream (inferred).
- **Evil is ordinary.** "Some people are so evil they will steal everything yiu have including your soul" (t680). "Its your father its your uncle its the priest at the church" (t680). "Everyone knows you can steal and transfer souls" (t680), yet daily life goes on: "it cant stop daily life" (R037).
- **Eldritch, tired, ageless men.** Jon and Derrek: a thousand-plus runs of about 10,000 years each, "eldritch and broken in ageless bodies" (wk W42). "I still think this is the final run. That part seems load bearing in a way I simply cannot move" (t328), which is now Jon's lie to himself.
- **Fate as a leash.** "the tether does not care why they jumped. derrek goes with jon. end of story" (jul08 t369). The cosmic layer is a punishment, never a reward (R117).
- **The window.** "Its only 2 men at a window. Dont forget that" (t458). Every book narrows to that image.
- **Heaven and hell as propaganda.** Heaven is "Utopia. A perfect place wrapped in light," yet "Beautiful and empty" (t228, t604). Hell is "full and abundant… But its angry noise" (t604), and really "a normal world like ours. They just name it such" (BANK 11-07). Patrick's world keeps undercutting its own myths: the name versus the act (wk W57).
- **Science-fantasy register.** Words like quark, field, decoherence and resonance show up as poetry, never homework. "we agreed no math" (t72). Folk eras call the same act fire or air (02 Law 6–7).
- **A dead universe.** Planets everywhere, life nowhere but Earth. Settlers in the far future find it "weirdly dead" (03). Loneliness at galactic scale (inferred).
- **What makes it Patrick's:** consciousness is the only magic, and physics is left alone; the soul is a measurable chunk of a shattered god; souls are an industrial material (amplifiers, hulls, chips) while selling a soul stays impossible; gods are flesh; the cosmic war comes down to two old men and a door they opened as young men (inferred from 02, 03, R033, R027).
