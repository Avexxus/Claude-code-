# Shared brief for the far-future tech batches (2026-11-02)

Patrick wants the far future filled out with "like a thousand types of tech": space travel, cloning, daily technology, teleporters "or whatever". Explore what science fiction has created over the years and adapt the genre-wide conventions to this world. Never copy a specific named device, faction or setting; use only general conventions.

## Settled rules you must build on (decisions/BANK.md, 2026-11-02 entries)
- **Layered tech.** Simple machines run on electricity and ordinary energy and need no Remnant. Only complex machines with an advanced AI need Remnant, held in flesh.
- **Soul-cores.** A flesh core grown around a measured draw of pool-Remnant; a bigger core means a more capable machine.
- **Remnant wells.** Latent pools mined like oil, with prospectors, wildcat wells and wars over fields. Rift sites are the richest.
- **Amplifier rigs.** Gear holding pooled Remnant extends a wearer's reach. Burnout (the body wrecked by overuse) is the soldier's death.
- **AI comes in three kinds:**
  - held minds: beings made purely of Remnant, living in the digital
  - Remnant-infused AI: holding a chunk of Remnant in focus, like a magic weapon
  - plain digital constructs: no soul
  Only ensouled AI can cross into the physical world. Placed in a body, or a robot with real receptors, it can likely do magic.
- **What ensouled machines are:** robots, complex machinery, advanced computers, weapons, satellites, drones.
- **Tainted cores.** Orc-soul cores are sold cheap and mislabeled as pool cores.
- **Grown structures and hulls are Convergence**, the same process that made the Tower, shaped into the modern age's towers.
- **Clone organs and flesh exist.** Clone bodies are considered lower quality but are identical, so they're often sold as "real".
- **Soul-cores feel nothing real.** Only vague things; sensation needs sense organs.
- **Machines can NOT sense souls.** That's the elves' gift. Only very specific devices built on the bond-spoof chip or reading chips can.
- **Fast interstellar messaging exists without the bond.**
- **Augmentation.** Metal parts have adverse effects on your magic, because you can't fully control your Remnant without your whole body. Flesh-grown augmentations keep that ability.
- **Money.** A soul is not currency and can't be sold. You can sell a being, or a thing with a soul bound into it. There are several currencies, the most trusted being an imperial coin, a dwarven mark and an elven silver crown (names are placeholders).
- **The public doesn't know Remnant is souls.** They think it's a material found in pools that makes machines smarter and more alive, gives power, and works as a "battery" (a misconception; it's really an amplifier only). On the fringes, people know it can be taken from others. Every iota of Remnant is a universe further down; nobody knows.
- **Machines copy magic's effects but never the act** (jul08 t339). Magic is a soul gripping reality.

## Existing canon to respect (see notes/D_beings.md, notes/E_world.md, notes/C_magic_powers.md)
- **Each race crosses the stars its own way:**
  - humans traverse hell (the elven prison realm)
  - dwarves squeeze dimensions
  - elves shrink one of their dimensions
  - dragons power jumps with crews strapped into focusing chambers
- **Earth** is a planet-wide city, the human capital, with the Tower at its heart.
- **The galaxy has no aliens**, only dead worlds settled by the known races. The Galactic Council sits in human buildings.
- **Ships:**
  - dragon ships: a focusing-chamber beam, a few shots
  - elven ships: the crew's bond-pooled souls
  - human ships: gun crews
  - most ships: machinery only
- **Other existing tech and trade:**
  - cathedral ships are grown from converged dead
  - the orc device (a kill switch, a drain, a puppet function)
  - the bond-spoof chip
  - the organ trade
  - clones have souls (everyone pretends they don't)
  - the rich live on far colonies, and drones do the labor
- **CUT domains:** space-folding as a wieldable magic, entropy, probability. The Returned's warped-space craft and the races' FTL sit on that seam, so any teleporter idea must say how it relates.

## Output format (Patrick reads on a phone and answers in batches)
Write `work/ideas/tech_<domain>.md`:
1. **Settled already:** one short paragraph listing what's already decided for your domain.
2. **The catalog:** 25–40 technologies grouped by subtopic. Each one gets:
   - **name**: a plain descriptive label, no proper names
   - **the sci-fi convention** it comes from, in a few words
   - **how it works here:** 1–2 lines using the rules above (does it need a soul-core? flesh? is it plain machinery?)
   - **a tag:** FITS, TWIST or RISKY (name what it would bend)
3. **Avoid:** genre conventions that would break canon, one line each.
4. **Questions for Patrick:** 6–8 short lettered-option questions, each with "Claude's pick", covering the real decisions in your domain.
5. **Top 10** for the domain.
